**Title: The Path to Liberation Through the Eightfold Path and the Four Noble Truths**

**Summary:**  
The Eightfold Path serves as a practical guide for achieving liberation, rooted in the understanding of the Four Noble Truths. The complete eradication of craving and attachment is essential, building upon the profound realization of these truths.

**

1. The Eightfold Path as a Path to Liberation**  
The Eightfold Path is identified as the pathway leading to liberation, emphasizing its role as a structured approach to spiritual practice and ultimate freedom from suffering.

**

2. Connection Between the Eightfold Path and the Four Noble Truths**  
The Eightfold Path is directly linked to the realization of the Four Noble Truths, highlighting its function as a means to embody and actualize the insights provided by these truths.

**

3. Eradication of Craving and Attachment**  
The cessation of craving and attachment is presented as a critical component of spiritual progress, achievable only through a deep understanding and application of the Four Noble Truths.
