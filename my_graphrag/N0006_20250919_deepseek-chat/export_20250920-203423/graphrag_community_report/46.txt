**Title:** The Role of Meditation and Sustained Practice in Achieving Liberation  

**Summary:**  
Meditation and sustained practice are essential for individuals seeking liberation. Meditation fosters focus on the present moment, supporting continuous spiritual growth. It serves as a foundation for subduing thoughts and achieving complete enlightenment. Sustained practice is crucial for mastering liberation, as it enables individuals to uproot the causes of suffering. Non-attachment, a key characteristic of liberated individuals, is cultivated through consistent practice, allowing one to transcend worldly achievements and perceive the illusory nature of phenomena.  

**Meditation as a Foundation for Liberation**  
Meditation, or Chan Ding, is a vital practice for spiritual growth, emphasizing focus on the present moment. It aids in sustaining continuous practice and serves as a crucial tool for subduing thoughts. This deliberate focus is foundational for achieving complete enlightenment.  

**Sustained Practice and Uprooting Suffering**  
Continuous spiritual practice is indispensable for mastering the path to liberation. Through constant awareness, individuals can eliminate the roots of suffering. This enduring effort ensures the gradual dismantling of psychological afflictions.  

**Non-Attachment and Liberation**  
Non-attachment is a defining trait of those who attain liberation. By transcending attachment to achievements, individuals can sever the causes of suffering. This detachment enables a profound understanding of the illusory nature of all phenomena.  

**Cultivating Non-Attachment Through Practice**  
Sustained spiritual practice leads to the state of non-attachment. Through consistent effort, individuals gain insight into the transient and illusory essence of worldly experiences. This realization fosters a liberated state of mind, free from entanglement with transient phenomena.
