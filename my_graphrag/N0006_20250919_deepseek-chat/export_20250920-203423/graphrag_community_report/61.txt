**Title: The Interplay of Purified Conduct and Wisdom in Cultivation**

**Summary**  
The text emphasizes the importance of two key traits in a practitioner's journey: purified conduct (清净戒行) and wisdom (般若智慧). These qualities work in harmony to help practitioners restrain their minds, dwell in tranquility, and eliminate the root causes of afflictions.

**Purified Conduct Restrains the Mind**  
Purified conduct is a fundamental trait for practitioners, enabling them to discipline and control their thoughts. It serves as a foundational practice to stabilize the mind and prevent distractions.

**Wisdom Fosters Tranquility**  
Wisdom is equally essential, guiding practitioners to abide in a state of effortless serenity. It provides the understanding necessary to transcend superficial concerns and maintain inner peace.

**Synergy of Conduct and Wisdom**  
Purified conduct and wisdom are interdependent, each reinforcing the other. Together, they create a powerful framework for practitioners to address and eradicate the deep-seated causes of suffering and distress.
