**Title: Key Principles of Spiritual Liberation Through Practice**  

**Summary:**  
The text outlines two fundamental principles for spiritual liberation: transcending the cycle of birth and death through dedicated practice, and the importance of relinquishing self-attachment to achieve this liberation.  

**Transcending the Cycle of Birth and Death**  
Dedicated practice enables individuals to rise above the perpetual cycle of birth and death, suggesting a path toward ultimate spiritual freedom.  

**Relinquishing Self-Attachment for Liberation**  
Letting go of self-attachment is presented as a critical step in distancing oneself from the cycle of rebirth, emphasizing the role of detachment in spiritual progress.
