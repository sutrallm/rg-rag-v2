# **Characteristics and Achievements of the Saint (聖者)**  

## **Summary**  
The Saint represents the pinnacle of spiritual attainment, embodying discipline, wisdom, and liberation. Through rigorous practice, the Saint transcends suffering, eliminates afflictions, and attains a state of unshakable peace. Their presence serves as a guiding light for others, revered by celestial beings and humanity alike.  

## **Key Findings**  

### **1. The Path of the Saint**  
The Saint achieves liberation through strict adherence to moral discipline (戒律) and the cultivation of wisdom (智慧). By eliminating greed, hatred, and delusion, they attain Nirvana—a state free from suffering and rebirth.  

### **2. Liberation Through Insight**  
Through meditation (禅定), the Saint gains profound understanding of impermanence, freeing the mind from attachment (心解脱). This wisdom allows them to remain undisturbed by worldly temptations or adversities.  

### **3. Reverence from Celestial Beings (諸天)**  
Celestial beings hold the Saint in high regard due to their purity, wisdom, and spiritual achievements. The Saint’s disciplined conduct and enlightenment earn them universal respect across both human and divine realms.  

### **4. Beyond Dualities**  
The Saint transcends binary distinctions such as good and evil, existing in a state of natural harmony. No longer bound by karmic actions (业行), they embody effortless wisdom and compassion.  

### **5. Role as a Spiritual Guide**  
As exemplars of the Dharma, the Saint inspires others to pursue liberation. Their life demonstrates the transformative power of ethical conduct, meditation, and wisdom in overcoming suffering.  

### **6. The State of Nirvana**  
The Saint’s ultimate attainment is Nirvana—complete freedom from cyclic existence. This state is marked by inner stillness, clarity, and the cessation of all afflictions.  

### **7. Contrast with Ordinary Beings**  
While ordinary individuals chase sensory pleasures, the Saint seeks wisdom and liberation. Their detachment from worldly pursuits distinguishes them as models of spiritual excellence.  

### **8. Universal Veneration**  
The Saint is regarded as a supreme field of merit (福田), worthy of reverence and offerings. Their presence benefits all beings, fostering spiritual growth and ethical living.  

### **9. The Middle Way**  
The Saint abides in the Middle Way, free from extremes. Their balanced perspective avoids rigid views, embodying harmony and equanimity.  

### **10. Final Liberation**  
Having eradicated all defilements, the Saint attains the cessation of birth and death. Their existence reflects the highest realization—unyielding peace and boundless wisdom.
