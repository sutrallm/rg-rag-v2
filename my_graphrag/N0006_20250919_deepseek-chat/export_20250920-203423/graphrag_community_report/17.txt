### Title: Understanding the Cycle of Birth, Death, and Rebirth in Buddhist Cosmology  

#### Summary:  
This report explores the concept of 轮回 (the cycle of birth, death, and rebirth) in Buddhist cosmology, its underlying causes, and the methods practitioners employ to transcend it. Key themes include the role of ignorance, attachment, and spiritual practices such as wisdom, meditation, and ethical discipline in achieving liberation (解脱). The report highlights the significance of eliminating afflictions (烦恼) and delusions (无明) as essential steps toward breaking free from the cycle of 轮回.  

#### The Role of Ignorance and Attachment  
轮回 is driven by ignorance (无明) and attachment (爱取). These factors perpetuate the cycle of existence by causing beings to cling to delusions and desires. Overcoming these root causes is essential for transcending 轮回.  

#### Liberation as the Ultimate Goal  
The primary objective for practitioners is to achieve liberation (解脱), a state free from the cycle of birth, death, and rebirth. This liberation is attained by eradicating the attachments and misconceptions that sustain 轮回.  

#### Spiritual Practices for Transcendence  
Practitioners employ various methods to transcend 轮回, including cultivating wisdom (智慧), practicing meditation (禅定), and adhering to ethical precepts (持戒). These practices aim to eliminate the root causes of 轮回 and lead to enlightenment.  

#### Wisdom as a Path to Liberation  
Wisdom plays a crucial role in understanding the true nature of 轮回. Through deep spiritual practice, practitioners gain insight that allows them to break free from the cycle of existence. Wisdom is not just comprehension but a transformative realization that leads to liberation.  

#### Eliminating Afflictions and Delusions  
轮回 is rooted in afflictions (烦恼) and habitual tendencies. Practitioners focus on dissolving these through meditation and spiritual practice to achieve liberation. Removing these obstacles is essential for breaking the cycle of 轮回.  

#### The Interconnectedness of Key Concepts  
Wisdom, meditation, and ethical discipline are interconnected practices that collectively address the causes of 轮回. Each method contributes to the practitioner’s ability to transcend the cycle and attain liberation.
