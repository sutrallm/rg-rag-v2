**Title: Overcoming Death’s Dominion Through Diligent Spiritual Practice**  

**Summary:**  
The core principle of 修行者 revolves around the practice of 不放逸 (diligence and vigilance) to achieve spiritual progress and liberation. This practice helps practitioners avoid distractions, faults, and the influence of 死亡魔王 (the Lord of Death), who symbolizes the cycle of death and rebirth. Through sustained effort in meditation and mindfulness, practitioners aim to overcome spiritual negligence, transcend the cycle of life and death, and attain a state of purity and liberation.  

**Principle of Diligence and Vigilance**  
不放逸 emphasizes relentless effort in spiritual practice to prevent regression, avoid distractions, and overcome attachments. Practitioners must guard their minds, maintain mindfulness, and focus on progress to escape suffering and achieve liberation.  

**Death’s Cycle and Spiritual Liberation**  
死亡魔王 represents the forces of death, fear, and spiritual negligence. Through diligent practice, practitioners can transcend the cycle of death and rebirth, free themselves from the Lord of Death’s control, and aspire to a higher state of spiritual transcendence.  

**Practical Methods: Meditation and Mindfulness**  
禅定 (meditation) is a key method within 不放逸, enabling practitioners to cultivate mindfulness, detach from cravings, and remain focused on spiritual advancement. This practice serves as a tool to avoid distractions and maintain progress on the path to liberation.  

**Guidance from Buddha**  
The teachings of Buddha underscore the importance of 不放逸 and diligent practice as essential pathways to spiritual liberation. Following these principles ensures continuous progress and the ultimate goal of transcending life and death.  

**Freedom from Death’s Control**  
Through unwavering diligence, practitioners can avoid falling under the dominion of 死亡魔王, thereby escaping the cycle of death and rebirth. The practice of 不放逸 is a means to overcome the influence of death and achieve lasting spiritual freedom.
