### Title: The Path to Liberation and Wisdom through Buddhist Practice  

#### Summary:  
The text emphasizes the importance of Buddhist practice in achieving both present happiness and ultimate liberation. It highlights that practitioners attain pure wisdom through diligent practice, with the ultimate goal of transcending the cycle of birth and death. The rarity of human life is underscored as a reason to cherish the opportunity for spiritual practice.  

#### Key Points:  

1. **Attainment of Pure Wisdom through Practice**  
   Diligent engagement in Buddhist practice enables practitioners to achieve pure wisdom, which is a fundamental aspect of spiritual growth.  

2. **Liberation from the Cycle of Birth and Death**  
   The ultimate objective of Buddhist practice is to break free from the cycle of birth and death, achieving true liberation and transcendence.  

3. **Rarity of Human Life and the Opportunity for Practice**  
   Human life is described as a rare and precious opportunity, underscoring the importance of utilizing it for spiritual practice to attain liberation and wisdom.  

4. **Interconnection of Practice, Wisdom, and Liberation**  
   Buddhist teachings emphasize that consistent practice leads to the realization of pure wisdom, which is essential for achieving liberation from the cycle of existence.
