**Title: The Role of Śamatha-Vipaśyanā in Meditation and Liberation**  

**Summary:**  
Śamatha-Vipaśyanā, a core practice in meditation, serves as a pathway to liberation by calming the mind, eliminating afflictions, and cultivating wisdom. This practice emphasizes sustained focus and insight, enabling practitioners to transcend attachments and penetrate the true nature of phenomena.  

**Insights:**  

**1. Śamatha-Vipaśyanā as the Foundation of Meditation**  
Śamatha-Vipaśyanā is the central method in meditation practice. It helps practitioners develop concentration and insight, leading to the cessation of afflictions and the attainment of liberation.  

**2. Cultivating Focus to Eliminate Distractions**  
A key aspect of Śamatha-Vipaśyanā is fostering sustained focus. By directing the mind to a single point, practitioners can eliminate distractions and stabilize their mental state.  

**3. Maintaining Mindfulness to Curb Attachments**  
The practice requires practitioners to remain anchored in mindfulness. This helps dissolve clinging and misguided thoughts, paving the way for inner peace.  

**4. Insight into the Nature of Phenomena**  
Śamatha-Vipaśyanā includes the development of insight, allowing practitioners to see through the superficial layers of phenomena and grasp their underlying essence.  

**5. Liberation Through Elimination of Afflictions**  
By consistently practicing Śamatha-Vipaśyanā, practitioners can eradicate the roots of afflictions, ultimately attaining liberation and wisdom.
