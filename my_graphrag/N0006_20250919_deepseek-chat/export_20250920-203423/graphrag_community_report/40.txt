**Title: The Importance of Inner Purity in Spiritual Practice**

**Summary:**  
The text emphasizes that true spiritual practice demands inner purification, as external adornments cannot mask inner impurities. It highlights the significance of cultivating a pure heart and distancing oneself from worldly distractions.

**The Necessity of Inner Purification**  
Spiritual growth and practice are rooted in the cleanliness of one’s inner self. External appearances or decorations are insufficient to conceal internal flaws or impurities.

**Focus on Inner Purity and Detachment**  
Achieving a state of inner purity requires a deliberate effort to remain untainted by worldly influences. This involves distancing oneself from distractions and maintaining a heart free from contamination.

**The Role of a Pure Heart in Spiritual Practice**  
A pure and unblemished heart is essential for meaningful spiritual advancement. It serves as the foundation for genuine practice, enabling individuals to transcend superficiality and focus on deeper, more authentic cultivation.
