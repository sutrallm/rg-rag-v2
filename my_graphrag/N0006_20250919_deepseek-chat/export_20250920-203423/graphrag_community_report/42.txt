**Title: The Compassionate Acts of the Rabbit in Spiritual Teachings**

**Summary:**  
The text describes two significant actions by a rabbit that symbolize profound spiritual principles. The rabbit’s self-sacrifice to provide for a sage and its offering of its fur to an exemplarity monk demonstrate deep compassion and the essence of selfless giving. These acts highlight the rabbit’s embodiment of sacrificial devotion and its role in advancing spiritual attainment for others.

**Insights:**

**The Rabbit’s Self-Sacrifice for Spiritual Enlightenment**  
The rabbit’s act of leaping into a fire to provide sustenance for a sage reflects the ultimate expression of selflessness. This sacrifice enables the sage to attain supernatural abilities, emphasizing the profound impact of altruistic actions in spiritual growth.

**The Rabbit’s Offering of Fur as an Act of Compassionate Giving**  
By willingly offering its fur to an exemplarity monk, the rabbit exemplifies the principle of compassionate giving. This act underscores the significance of selfless contributions as a means of fostering spiritual progress and nurturing virtues such as kindness and empathy.
