**Title: Consequences of Disregarding Buddhist Teachings**

**Summary:**  
Individuals who fail to adhere to the teachings of the Buddha not only cause themselves suffering but also risk severe spiritual consequences, including potential descent into negative realms.

---

**Disregard of Teachings Leads to Suffering**  
Those who do not follow the Buddha's teachings in their practice experience personal suffering as a direct result of their actions.

**Behavioral Misconduct Has Spiritual Consequences**  
Engaging in improper behavior due to a lack of adherence to the Buddha's principles can ultimately lead to rebirth in unfavorable or harmful realms.
