**Title: The Path to Liberation: Practitioners and the Cycle of Life and Death**

**Summary:**  
Practitioners aim to transcend the cycle of birth, death, and rebirth through disciplined practice. By eliminating greed, anger, and ignorance, and cultivating wisdom, they achieve liberation. Meditation and insight into the nature of this cycle enable them to reach a state beyond birth and death, freeing themselves from suffering and perpetual reincarnation.

---

**Wisdom as a Tool for Transcendence**  
Wisdom enables practitioners to see through the illusions of birth and death. It provides clarity about the true nature of existence, allowing them to break free from the cycle of reincarnation and attain liberation.

**Elimination of Negative Traits**  
Practitioners focus on eliminating greed, anger, and ignorance, which are the root causes of suffering and entanglement in the cycle of life and death. This purification is essential for achieving liberation.

**Meditation and Insight**  
Through meditation and deep contemplation, practitioners gain a profound understanding of the cycle of birth and death. This insight helps them overcome the cycle and attain a state of no birth and no death.

**The Pursuit of Liberation**  
The ultimate goal of practitioners is to escape the perpetual cycle of birth, death, and rebirth. By cultivating wisdom and practicing diligently, they seek lasting freedom from suffering and reincarnation.
