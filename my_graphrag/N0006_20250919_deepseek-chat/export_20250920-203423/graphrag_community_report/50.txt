**Title: The Path to Liberation Through Non-Attachment and Tranquility**  

**Summary:**  
This report explores the principles of non-attachment and tranquility as pathways to liberation from suffering. It highlights the impermanent nature of all phenomena and the wisdom that arises from understanding this truth. Key elements include the relationship between non-attachment and tranquility, and how these practices lead to freedom from worldly distractions and the cycle of rebirth.  

**The Impermanent Nature of Phenomena and Liberation**  
The understanding of impermanence is central to achieving liberation. Recognizing that all phenomena, including spiritual and worldly achievements, are impermanent, practitioners detach from them. This detachment leads to freedom from the cycle of rebirth and suffering. The wisdom of liberation stems from this realization, enabling individuals to transcend attachment and achieve true freedom.  

**Non-Attachment as a Foundation for Tranquility**  
Non-attachment and tranquility are deeply interconnected. Both practices involve freedom from worldly distractions and desires. Non-attachment arises from the wisdom of liberation, fostering a state of peace and clarity. Tranquility, in turn, reinforces non-attachment, creating a harmonious cycle that supports spiritual growth and liberation.  

**The Role of Wisdom in Non-Attachment**  
Wisdom is the driving force behind non-attachment. By understanding the impermanent nature of all things, practitioners cultivate liberation wisdom. This wisdom allows them to release attachment to transient phenomena, paving the way for spiritual liberation and inner peace. The integration of wisdom into practice is essential for sustaining non-attachment and achieving tranquility.
