**Title**  
User‑Provided Text Required for Translation and Reorganization  

**Executive Summary**  
The passage states that the assistant cannot begin any processing until the user supplies the original text. Once received, the assistant will translate the content, reorganize it into a new structure, and format the result. The process hinges on the user’s submission and the assistant’s defined tasks of translation, reorganization, and formatting.  

**Action Requirement**  
The user must paste the source material. Without this input, the assistant is unable to carry out translation, structure, or formatting work.  

**Scope of Assistant Functions**  
Upon receipt of the original text, the assistant will produce:  
- A translated version of the content.  
- A reorganized arrangement of the same material.  
- Formatting adjustments appropriate to the new structure.  

**Data Flow**  
1. The user provides the original text.  
2. The assistant translates the text.  
3. The assistant reorganizes the translated material into a new form.  
4. The final output is formatted and presented.
