**Title**  
Plain Language Conversion and Doctrinal Reorganization Process

**Summary**  
The described method transforms original content into a simplified, everyday‑language version. During this conversion, references to specific individuals and geographic places are identified and removed. The resulting material is then restructured into a doctrinal exposition, and the exposition receives a new title that reflects its revised focus.

**Key Findings**

**Standardized Simplification**  
The method replaces literary devices with clear, ordinary expressions, improving readability for a general audience.

**Targeted Removal of Identifiers**  
During processing, mentions of characters and locations are flagged for deletion, ensuring that the final version contains no personal or place identifiers.

**Doctrinal Reorganization**  
After removal of identifiers, the content is reorganized into a doctrinal exposition, shifting from narrative or explanatory style to an instruction‑oriented presentation.

**Title Renaming**  
The doctrinal exposition is given a new title that aligns with its revised content, indicating a deliberate rebranding of the material.
