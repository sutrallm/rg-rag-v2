1. Receive the format specification from the user.

The text explicitly requires that the output be a readable, plain Chinese rendition. Formal or complex phrasing is to be replaced with straightforward language.

All formatting instructions are supplied by the user. The assistant’s responsibility is to adhere strictly to those instructions for translation and organization.

The translated content must be rendered in the formatting style specified. No deviations or additions are permitted beyond what the user’s format dictates.

#### Role of the Assistant
