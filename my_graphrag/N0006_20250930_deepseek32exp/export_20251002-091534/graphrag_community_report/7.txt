**Title: Pathways to Transcendence: The Practices of Practitioners and Saints**

**Summary:**
This report details the spiritual objectives and methods used by practitioners (修行者) and saints (圣者) to achieve liberation. The primary goal is to transcend the cycle of birth and death (生死流转), which is perpetuated by afflictions (烦恼). The core methods for achieving this are diligent spiritual cultivation, the attainment of inner stillness through meditation, and the application of wisdom. These practices culminate in a state of true freedom, characterized by a mind free from attachments and a permanent state of tranquility, even within the cycle of existence.

**The Goal of Transcending the Cycle of Birth and Death**
The fundamental objective for practitioners and saints is to transcend 生死流转, the cycle of birth and death. This cycle is sustained by afflictions (烦恼), which bind beings to it. The ultimate success of their spiritual endeavors is measured by breaking free from this cycle.

**The Central Role of Inner Stillness**
Achieving a state of inner stillness or tranquility (寂静) is a central theme. This state is both a method and an outcome of spiritual practice. Meditation (禅定/禅修) is the primary practice for attaining this inner peace. This tranquility is not temporary; it is described as a state in which one can permanently reside, even amidst the cycle of birth and death.

**The Liberating Power of Wisdom**
Wisdom is identified as a direct cause of liberation. It enables a complete and thorough解脱 (liberation) from all attachments. This specific form of wisdom is free from defilements and leads to true freedom. A key realization within this wisdom is the thorough certification of non-self (彻证无我), which results in自在解脱 (spontaneous liberation).

**Diligent Cultivation as the Path to Realization**
Liberation is not passive; it requires active and diligent spiritual cultivation (精进修行). This involves diligently training the mind, continually purifying one's intentions, and not following the illusory appearances of the world. Through this sustained effort, practitioners can sever the very root of afflictions, which is the source of suffering and bondage.

**The Embodiment of Liberation in Saints**
Saints or enlightened beings represent the successful culmination of this path. They have attained a state of true freedom and liberation, characterized by a mind completely free from attachments. Their spiritual realization allows them to transcend the cycle of birth and death. Their existence is marked by the ability to abide in stillness and to have completely severed the root of afflictions.
