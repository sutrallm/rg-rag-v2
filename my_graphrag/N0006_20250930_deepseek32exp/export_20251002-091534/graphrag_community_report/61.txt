**Report on the Observation of Physical and Mental Phenomena in Practice**

**Summary**
The core of the practice described involves the continuous and correct observation of physical and mental phenomena. This sustained observation is the primary method for developing wisdom, applying diligence, and progressing on the path to liberation. The fundamental nature of these phenomena is identified as impermanent, unsatisfactory, and empty.

**The Role of Observation in Developing Wisdom**
Wisdom arises specifically through the continuous observation of physical and mental phenomena. These phenomena serve as the direct object for the development of insightful understanding.

**The Necessity of Diligent Observation**
Diligence in practice is defined by the constant and unwavering awareness of physical and mental phenomena. This persistent observation is a foundational requirement for the practitioner's effort.

**Observation as the Path to Liberation**
The path to liberation is contingent upon observing physical and mental phenomena as they truly are. This accurate perception is essential for progress toward the ultimate goal of the practice.

**The Impermanent Nature of Phenomena**
A primary characteristic of physical and mental phenomena is their impermanence. They are subject to a constant process of arising and ceasing.

**The Unsatisfactory and Empty Nature of Phenomena**
The essential nature of physical and mental phenomena is further characterized as unsatisfactory and empty. Correct understanding involves directly perceiving these qualities.

**The Purpose of Observation in Core Teachings**
A fundamental teaching instructs practitioners to observe physical and mental phenomena with a correct understanding of their arising and ceasing nature. This observation is central to penetrating the veil of ignorance.
