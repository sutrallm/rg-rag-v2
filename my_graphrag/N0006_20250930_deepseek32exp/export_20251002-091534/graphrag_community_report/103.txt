**Title: Stages of Practice for Overcoming the Ten Fetters**

**Summary**

This report outlines a structured spiritual path designed for practitioners to systematically overcome ten specific defilements, known as the ten fetters. The process is defined as gradual and requires progression through a complete, sequential series of practice stages.

**Detailed Findings**

**The Gradual Path to Overcoming Defilements**
A defined and complete sequence of spiritual practice exists to guide individuals. This path is not immediate but is designed for the progressive and systematic removal of defilements that bind practitioners.

**The Ten Fetters as the Designated Obstacles**
The specific obstacles to be overcome are ten in number and are identified as fetters or bonds. These fetters are characterized as defilements that practitioners must cut off through their dedicated practice.

**A Defined Sequence of Practice Stages**
The method for achieving liberation from these fetters is a structured and ordered progression. This is described as a complete sequence of practice, indicating that each stage builds upon the previous one and all are necessary for the ultimate goal of cutting off the ten fetters.
