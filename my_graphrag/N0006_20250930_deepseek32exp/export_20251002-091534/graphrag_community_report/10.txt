**Title: The Cessation of Karmic Force Through Spiritual Attainment**

**Summary**

This report details the mechanism of karmic force (业力) and the path to its transcendence. Karmic force is accumulated through sensory attachment and results in rebirth and suffering. Its generation is rooted in ignorance and desire. However, through spiritual practice aimed at eradicating defilements and gaining wisdom, a practitioner can cease producing new karmic force. For a fully liberated being, such as a sage or one who has attained nirvana, past karmic forces become inert and no longer produce effects, resulting in a state of freedom from the cycle of birth and death.

**Mechanism and Consequences of Karmic Force**

Karmic force is the power accumulated through beings' sensory attachment to external objects, representing the retributive force of good and evil actions according to the law of cause and effect. This potential force causes rebirth, suffering, and binds beings to the cycle of birth and death. The generation of new karmic force creates bondage and perpetuates this cycle. Ignorance is identified as the fundamental root from which karmic force arises.

**The Path to Cessation Through Spiritual Practice**

The generation of new karmic force can be stopped through spiritual cultivation. A key method is the eradication of fundamental defilements, specifically greed, hatred, and delusion. Furthermore, the attainment of wisdom, characterized by insight into the true nature of reality and freedom from defilements, prevents the creation of new karmic cycles. Achieving freedom from desire also prevents the accumulation of new karmic force.

**The Liberated State Beyond Karmic Influence**

A practitioner who has achieved complete enlightenment, liberation, or nirvana is considered a sage. Such a being no longer accumulates, produces, or creates any new karmic force. They have transcended the dichotomy of good and evil and are no longer bound by karmic retribution. For these liberated beings, past karmic forces are likened to scorched seeds that can no longer sprout or produce any effect. Consequently, they are not fettered by karmic forces, are no longer subject to their牵引 (influence/pull), and have surpassed all constraints of karma, existing in a state that transcends its influence.
