**Title: The Role and Outcomes of Diligent Spiritual Practice**

**Summary:**
This report details the essential components and objectives of diligent spiritual practice as derived from the provided text. The practice is defined by unwavering effort, constant awareness, and strict adherence to correct teachings, primarily those of the Buddha. Its central purpose is to achieve liberation from the cycle of birth and death, specifically by overcoming the control of the Death King and attaining Nirvana. Mastery of meditation and the path to liberation are presented as direct results of this sustained, disciplined effort.

**The Centrality of Unwavering Effort and Awareness**
Diligent practice is characterized by continuous effort and the prevention of spiritual laziness. A core requirement is maintaining constant awareness and an attitude of non-negligence. This sustained diligence is not optional but is fundamental for making spiritual progress and avoiding difficulties.

**Adherence to Authoritative Teachings**
The practice must be grounded in following correct doctrines and teachings. Specifically, it requires strict adherence to the guidance of the Buddha and the correct Dharma. This provides the necessary framework and direction for the practitioner's efforts.

**Mastery of Meditation and Liberation**
A primary function of diligent practice is to enable the genuine mastery of meditation. Furthermore, the path to liberation itself is mastered through this unwavering effort. Constant awareness is specifically identified as the factor that enables true mastery of the path to liberation.

**Liberation from the Cycle of Birth and Death**
The ultimate goal of this practice is to transcend the bondage of the cycle of birth and death. This liberation is the definitive purpose of engaging in the practice of the path to liberation.

**Overcoming the Death King**
Diligent practice is the direct method for avoiding and escaping the control of the Death King. By practicing diligently, one can avoid falling under the Death King's dominion, break free from his bondage, and ultimately transcend his realm entirely.

**Transcending Worldly Attachments**
The practice leads to freedom from worldly defilements and attachments. It allows practitioners to move beyond the shackles of afflictions and to gain a true understanding of the impermanent and unsatisfactory nature of the five aggregates.

**Attainment of Nirvana**
The final and supreme result of diligent practice is the achievement of Nirvana. This is presented as the ultimate fruition of the entire spiritual endeavor.
