**Report on the Attainment of Non-Action Nirvana**

**Summary**
The text establishes the state of Non-Action Nirvana as the ultimate spiritual achievement. An individual who attains this state is identified as a Saint and is considered the supreme field of merit, making them the most worthy recipient of offerings.

**Key Findings**

**The Ultimate Goal of Spiritual Practice**
The text defines the attainment of Non-Action Nirvana as the final liberation and the ultimate state of解脱 (liberation). This presents it not as an intermediate stage, but as the conclusive and highest objective of the spiritual path.

**The Saint as the Supreme Field of Merit**
An individual who has achieved Non-Action Nirvana is designated as a Saint. This status carries a specific, practical implication: such a Saint is described as the "究竟福田" (supreme field of merit). This means they represent the most fertile ground for generating positive karmic results, establishing them as the preeminent and most authentic recipient for devotional offerings and support.
