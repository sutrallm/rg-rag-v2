**Report on the Purification of Body, Speech, and Mind through Noble Practice**

**Summary**
This report details the process and outcome of spiritual practice centered on the purification of one's actions. The core objective is the complete cleansing of the three karmas—body, speech, and mind. Practitioners achieve this through the disciplined observance of Noble Practice, which involves constant introspection and the cultivation of pure conduct. The ultimate result of this full purification is liberation, a state of peace and freedom from suffering, as exemplified by enlightened saints.

**The Process of Purifying the Three Karmas**
The fundamental activity for a practitioner is the continuous purification of the three karmas, which are defined as the actions of body, speech, and mind. These three karmas are the direct object of purification in spiritual practice. The path to a state of peace and freedom from suffering is reached specifically through this ongoing process of cleansing these three aspects of being.

**Noble Practice as the Method for Purity**
The prescribed method for achieving purity is the adherence to Noble Practice. This practice consists of maintaining pure precepts and cultivating specific behaviors: gentle speech, a subdued mind, and pure physical actions. The goal of Noble Practice is to ensure these three karmas are entirely pure, making it an integral component of spiritual cultivation.

**Liberation as the Outcome of Complete Purification**
The definitive outcome of successfully purifying the three karmas is the attainment of liberation. A practitioner who has achieved true liberation has fully cleansed all actions of body, speech, and mind. Enlightened saints, having completed this cultivation of Noble Practice, embody this result, possessing absolute purity in their physical, verbal, and mental actions.
