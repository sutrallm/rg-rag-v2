**Report on Buddhist Attainment Pathways and States**

**Summary**
This report details the progressive stages and ultimate goals of spiritual practice, culminating in complete awakening and liberation. The path involves specific methods like mindfulness, contemplation, and mind cultivation to eradicate defilements and attachments. The final states of enlightenment and ultimate nirvana are characterized by the cessation of craving, the extinction of afflictions, and freedom from the cycle of rebirth.

**The Path to Complete Awakening**
Awakening is a state of complete mental stillness and the elimination of all attachments, achieved through diligent practice of the teachings. It is the result of the full development of the seven factors of enlightenment and the complete practice of the threefold training. Practitioners who attain this state eradicate all ignorance and执着, are no longer subject to karmic influence, and cease generating new karmic seeds. This state represents the ultimate liberation from all suffering and bondage.

**Characteristics of Ultimate Nirvana**
Ultimate nirvana is the state of final liberation, marked by the cessation of formations, the quenching of craving, the abandonment of clinging, and the extinction of afflictions. Those who reach this state have transcended all dualistic concepts, such as good and evil, and are beyond conceptual thought. They reside in a state of non-action and are free from future rebirth.

**The Role of Contemplation**
Contemplation is a practice within the progression of wisdom, built upon a foundation of moral discipline and concentration. It is a daily practice that begins with observing the breath and bodily sensations, gradually extending to all mental activities. Through observing the arising and passing of physical and mental phenomena, this method is a crucial pathway for developing wisdom and understanding dependent origination.

**Cultivating Unwavering Awareness**
Awareness is a state of mindfulness that practitioners must maintain in all activities. It is cultivated through continuous practice, focusing on the breath and bodily sensations while maintaining a non-attached stance toward all phenomena. This awareness should be free from clinging to the past, expectations for the future, or attachment to present conditions. Through constant practice, this awareness becomes as solid as a diamond, capable of piercing the darkness of ignorance and guiding practitioners to liberation.

**The Fundamental Practice of Mind Cultivation**
Mind cultivation is the essential method for taming the mind, comparable to breaking a wild horse using the reins of mindfulness. This practice aims to guard the mind, freeing it from the fluctuations of greed and aversion to reveal its inherent radiance. It is a gradual process of purifying coarse and subtle mental habits, ultimately revealing the innate awareness. The practice involves maintaining awareness without attachment to past clinging, future expectations, or present conditions, thereby gradually transcending attachment to the three times.

**The Nature of Bondage and Liberation**
Bondage refers to the fetters that bind practitioners, which must be severed through practice. By practicing correctly, practitioners can cut off all bonds and attain the nirvana state. Those who have reached the far shore have completely severed these fetters, achieving total liberation. The state of liberation is defined by a mind free from all bonds and transcending all negative states.

**Transcending Duality Through Liberation**
The duality of good and evil is a framework that is overcome by fully awakened practitioners and saints through their profound understanding of dependent origination and their attainment of complete liberation. This transcendence liberates them from all dualistic concepts.

**The Fruition of Practice**
The state of non-learning is the ultimate fruition of practice, indicating that all tasks are accomplished. Those in this state are free from future rebirth, have completely eradicated the three fetters and ten bonds, and have permanently escaped the four floods. The realization of the saintly fruit leads to the state of ultimate nirvana, characterized by the complete eradication of these fetters and bonds.
