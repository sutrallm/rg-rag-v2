**Report on the Path to Liberation through Realizing True Reality**

**Summary**

This report details the process and outcomes for a practitioner seeking liberation. The path is defined by the cultivation of wisdom and the practice of meditation, which enable a direct perception of true reality. This realization, characterized by understanding dependent origination and emptiness, allows the practitioner to transcend discrimination and affliction. Both sages and Buddhas are presented as having achieved this ultimate state of liberation, where their every action is in harmony with true reality.

**The Path to Eradicating Afflictions**

A practitioner can completely sever all afflictions through the continuous purification of mental activity and by abiding in true reality. This process is supported by the cultivation of moral discipline, meditative stability, and wisdom.

**The Role of Wisdom in Perceiving Truth**

Wisdom is the capacity to clearly perceive and penetrate the true reality of phenomena. It enables a direct seeing into the essence of true reality, understanding the empty nature of all phenomena and their dependent origination. The manifestation of true reality requires this thorough insight from wisdom; when discriminative thought ceases, truth naturally appears.

**The Outcome of Realizing True Reality**

Those who comprehend the principle of true reality transcend all dualistic distinctions and abide in the Middle Way. A liberated individual's every action—walking, standing, sitting, and lying down—is in accord with true reality. Sages and Buddhas, as accomplished practitioners, have all reached this ultimate truth of liberation.

**The Method of Meditation for Penetrating Phenomena**

Through the systematic practice of calm-abiding and insight meditation, a practitioner can penetrate the superficial layer of phenomena and arrive at the essential nature of true reality. This practice of observing true reality is a foundational activity for the practitioner.

**The Framework for Contemplating Reality**

The Four Noble Truths are utilized as a framework for contemplating true reality. The culmination of the spiritual journey is achieved through the perfection of wisdom, which completes the process by providing a thorough seeing of true reality.
