**Title: The Role of Wisdom in Understanding Impermanence**

**Summary:**
The text establishes that a deep understanding of the impermanent nature of all phenomena is the central characteristic of wisdom. This wisdom, cultivated through dedicated practice, enables a practitioner to observe and fully comprehend this universal principle, which is a direct and essential component of achieving a state of liberation.

**Insights**

**The Centrality of Impermanence in Wisdom**
Wisdom is defined exclusively as the profound capacity to perceive the impermanent nature of all phenomena. It is not a general intelligence but a specific, deep-seated ability to understand that all things, including all events and existences, are in a constant state of flux and lack any permanent, fixed entity.

**Cultivation of Wisdom Through Practice**
This specific form of wisdom is developed through dedicated practice. Practitioners engage in observation and contemplation to realize the principle of impermanence directly. The process of "wisdom观照" (wisdom observation/illumination) is the method by which this understanding is achieved.

**Impermanence as the Path to Liberation**
A thorough and penetrating cognition of the impermanent nature of worldly phenomena is a fundamental element of the liberated state. Specifically, one who achieves "心解脱" (mind liberation) does so by gaining a clear and complete insight into this impermanence, identifying it as a crucial aspect of liberating wisdom.

**The Universal Application of Impermanence**
The principle of impermanence applies to all aspects of existence without exception. This includes the five aggregates (the components of a sentient being), all feelings, and all physical and mental phenomena, all of which are understood to be subject to change.
