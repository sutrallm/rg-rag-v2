**Report on the Interdependence of Wisdom, Meditative Concentration, and the Fruition of Bodhi**

**Summary**
The analysis reveals that the complete attainment of Bodhi, or supreme enlightenment, is the ultimate outcome of a specific spiritual path. This path is fundamentally characterized by the balanced cultivation of wisdom and meditative concentration, known as "Samadhi and Prajna Equally Sustained." Furthermore, the foundational training in moral discipline, concentration, and wisdom serves as the sequential process to achieve this goal. A key mechanism within this process is the use of wisdom to transform mental afflictions into enlightenment itself.

**The Path to Bodhi Relies on Balanced Cultivation**
The achievement of Bodhi is not the result of a single practice but depends on the integrated and balanced development of two core components: wisdom and meditative concentration. Both are described as essential and equally important parts of the practice of "Samadhi and Prajna Equally Sustained." Practitioners who maintain this balance are assured of ultimately attaining complete, supreme Bodhi.

**Moral Discipline, Concentration, and Wisdom Form a Sequential Training**
A structured, sequential path exists for realizing the fruit of Bodhi. This path is the progressive training in the threefold practice of moral discipline, meditative concentration, and wisdom. The culmination of this entire training regimen is the "Bodhi path fruit," which represents the final and successful outcome of the discipline.

**Wisdom Transforms Afflictions into Enlightenment**
A specific function of wisdom within this path is its role in metamorphosing obstacles into attainment. Wisdom realization is the direct means through which mental afflictions are converted into enlightenment. This transformation is a critical process for achieving the final goal.

**Bodhi Represents the Ultimate Fruition**
The ultimate objective of all these practices is the complete and perfect achievement of Bodhi. The "Bodhi path fruit" explicitly includes this state of圆满成就, or perfect accomplishment. The very emergence and development of wisdom itself ultimately leads to the realization of Bodhi, confirming it as the definitive end point of the spiritual journey.
