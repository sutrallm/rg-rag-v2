**Title: Buddhist Path to Liberation through Dependent Origination and Wisdom**

**Summary**
This report outlines the Buddhist path to liberation, which centers on understanding the principle of dependent origination—the law that all phenomena arise and cease due to causes and conditions. Practitioners employ the method of Calm-Abiding and Insight to develop wisdom, which manifests as the Wisdom of the Middle Way and the Wisdom of Emptiness. This wisdom allows for a direct seeing of dependent origination and the empty nature of all things, enabling enlightened beings to sever ignorance, eradicate defilements, and attain the ultimate state of Nirvana.

**The Centrality of Dependent Origination**
Dependent origination is the universal law and essential nature of all phenomena, describing how they come into being through the confluence of causes and conditions and cease with their dispersal. It is the core doctrine of the Dharma, representing the fundamental pattern of existence and the true reality of all things. A complete and direct understanding of this principle is the key to liberation.

**The Path of Calm-Abiding and Insight**
The simultaneous cultivation of Calm-Abiding and Insight is the primary method of practice. Calm-Abiding stabilizes the mind, while Insight investigates reality. Used together, this practice gives rise to wisdom, allowing practitioners to thoroughly perceive the truth of dependent origination and selflessness. The ultimate outcome of this practice is the attainment of Nirvana.

**The Role of Wisdom in Realization**
The development of wisdom is the direct result of practicing Calm-Abiding and Insight. This wisdom culminates in the Wisdom of the Middle Way, which transcends extremes of existence and non-existence and maintains equanimity, and the Wisdom of Emptiness, which understands that all phenomena lack inherent existence. This wisdom directly sees dependent origination, severs the root of ignorance, and realizes the ultimate truth that all phenomena are empty of an independent self.

**The State of the Enlightened Being and Nirvana**
A sage or enlightened being is one who has thoroughly realized dependent origination and the empty nature of phenomena. Such a being has severed all bonds of cyclic existence, eradicated defilements and attachments, and abides in the Wisdom of the Middle Way. This state of liberation is Nirvana, the ultimate realization attained by directly recognizing the nature of dependent origination, where one is free from conceptual discrimination and clings to no state.
