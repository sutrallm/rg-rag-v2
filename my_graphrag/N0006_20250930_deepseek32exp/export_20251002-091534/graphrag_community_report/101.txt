**Report on the Attainments and Characteristics of the Accomplished Practitioner**

**Summary**

This report details the defining characteristics and ultimate state of the accomplished practitioner, known as a Saint or Sage. These individuals have achieved liberation through the complete cultivation of moral discipline, meditative concentration, and wisdom. They have eradicated all mental afflictions, transcended the cycle of birth and death, and are no longer bound by karmic forces. Their state is marked by profound stillness, impartiality, and a mind that is unshaken by worldly conditions. They serve as a refuge and field of merit for the world, receiving respect even from celestial beings.

**Characteristics of the Accomplished Practitioner**

**Cultivation of Moral Discipline, Concentration, and Wisdom**
The Saint is defined by the perfect achievement of moral discipline, meditative concentration, and wisdom. These three elements are the foundational and necessary conditions for this attainment. Through their complete cultivation, the practitioner severs all fetters and roots of mental afflictions, transcends suffering, and achieves liberation. Moral discipline provides the stability for spiritual progress, while wisdom allows for the clear perception of reality and the destruction of ignorance.

**Eradication of Mental Afflictions**
A primary achievement of the Saint is the complete elimination, uprooting, and extinction of all mental defilements, specifically greed, hatred, and delusion. By cutting off the stream of these afflictions, the practitioner overcomes the bonds of suffering and is freed from cyclic existence. This state of being free from defilements is a key reason they are revered.

**Transcendence of Karmic Conditioning**
The Saint no longer accumulates, produces, or creates new karma. Past karmic forces are rendered ineffective, like scorched seeds that cannot sprout. Having transcended the dichotomy of good and evil, the Saint is no longer bound or fettered by karmic retribution, which is the force that perpetuates the cycle of birth and death.

**Liberation from the Cycle of Birth and Death**
Through spiritual realization, the Saint has gone beyond the cycle of birth and death, having reached the far shore. They are no longer bound by the causes of rebirth and have transcended the suffering of this cycle. While they may still appear in the world, they are not contaminated by it.

**Attainment of a Still and Unshaken Mind**
The Saint abides in a state of inner stillness and peace, achieved through diligent mental cultivation and meditative practice. This mind is firm like a diamond or Mount Sumeru, remaining unmoved by the eight worldly winds: gain and loss, fame and disgrace, praise and blame, and pleasure and pain. They maintain equanimity in all circumstances.

**Embodiment of Impartiality and Non-Attachment**
The Saint dwells in the Middle Way, without clinging to extremes such as existence or non-existence. They do not grasp at or cling to any phenomena, states, or realms. Their mind is free from attachments and hindrances, like a bird flying in the sky. They are compared to a lotus flower, which grows from mud but remains unstained, symbolizing their presence in the world without being polluted by it.

**Recognition and Respect from Celestial Beings**
Celestial beings, inhabitants of the heavenly realms, respect, revere, and offer protection to practitioners who possess pure moral discipline and wisdom. They honor the virtue and merit of those who have severed the roots of afflictions and achieved liberation.
