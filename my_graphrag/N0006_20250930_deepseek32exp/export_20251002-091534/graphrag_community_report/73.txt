**Report on the Nature of Complaint and Its Antidote**

**Summary**
This report details the relationship between a specific form of distress, identified as complaint, and its direct countermeasure. The analysis confirms that complaint is a manifestation of a deeper affliction and that a specific contemplative practice is prescribed to address its root cause.

**Complaint as a Manifestation of Distress**
The text defines complaint as a specific, tangible form of a more general affliction or vexation. This indicates that complaint is not an isolated phenomenon but a symptom emerging from a foundational state of distress.

**The Contemplative Antidote to Complaint**
A specific method, identified as the contemplation of dependent origination, is presented as the dedicated remedy for complaint. This establishes a direct, targeted relationship where this practice is designed to neutralize and overcome the underlying condition from which complaint arises.
