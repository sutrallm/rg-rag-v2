**Report on the Attainment of Ultimate Realization and the True Nature of Phenomena**

**Summary**
This report details the state of ultimate realization, known as the 'ultimate fruit', and the 'true nature of phenomena' that is perceived within it. The ultimate fruit is a state of complete accomplishment and liberation, characterized by the destruction of ignorance and afflictions. Practitioners in this state possess perfected wisdom and meditative concentration, which allow them to perceive and naturally align with the true nature of all phenomena, a reality that is beyond all conceptual distinctions.

**The State of Ultimate Realization and Accomplishment**
The ultimate fruit represents the final state of a completely awakened being, embodying a state of full accomplishment and nirvana's perfect liberation. In this state, practitioners have destroyed the root of ignorance, utterly eradicated all craving and attachment, and eliminated all afflictions. They have fully perceived the true nature of phenomena and attained a mind without obstructions. This results in a state of great autonomy in all realms, unconstrained and able to guide beings according to circumstances. Their teachings are always in accord with truth, reflecting an unshakable state of liberation.

**The Relationship Between Meditative Concentration and Wisdom**
Moral discipline serves as the foundation for developing the capacity for meditative concentration. This meditative concentration, in turn, gives rise to perfected wisdom. This wisdom illuminates reality and is crucial for perceiving the true nature of phenomena. The development of wisdom is directly related to understanding the dependent origination and emptiness of all phenomena.

**Direct Perception of the True Nature of Phenomena**
The true nature of phenomena is the ultimate reality that is naturally aligned with by those who have attained ultimate realization. It is the real character of all phenomena, which can be directly perceived by a mind in a state of meditative concentration. A mind abiding in meditative concentration can clearly and accurately perceive this true nature. This reality is an expression of the nature of dependent origination, transcends all dualistic concepts, and is free from all conceptual elaborations. It is compared to a sky untouched by dust or a mirror clearly reflecting all things.

**Characteristics of the Accomplished Practitioner**
Practitioners who have reached the ultimate fruit are fully awakened beings who have attained complete accomplishment. They possess ten powers of wisdom and their mind is boundless like space. A key characteristic is their non-attachment; they do not cling to any phenomenal signs, yet they naturally align with the true nature of all phenomena. They have achieved a state of mind without obstructions or attachments.
