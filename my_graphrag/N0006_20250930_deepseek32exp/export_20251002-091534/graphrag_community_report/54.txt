**Title: Contrast Between the Negligent and the Diligent Practitioner**

**Summary:**
The text establishes a fundamental contrast between two types of individuals: the negligent, who are unaware of their own spiritual indolence, and the diligent practitioners, who are purposeful and progress from a state of ignorance to one of understanding.

**Contrast in Awareness and Spiritual State**
The text presents a clear dichotomy. One group is characterized by negligence and a lack of self-awareness, being described as "unaware of their own indulgence." The other group is defined by diligent practice and is likened to "those with clear vision." This contrast is not merely behavioral but is rooted in a fundamental difference in awareness and spiritual condition.

**The Path from Ignorance to Understanding**
The diligent practitioner's path is defined by purposeful progress. The text explicitly states that through their diligence, they "move from darkness towards light." This signifies a transformative journey, where "darkness" represents a state of spiritual ignorance or confusion, and "light" represents enlightenment, clarity, and understanding. The path is active and directional, enabled by their consistent effort.
