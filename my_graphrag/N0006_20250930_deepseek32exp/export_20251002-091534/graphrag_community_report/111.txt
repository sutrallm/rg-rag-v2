**Title: The Role of Mirage in the Contemplation of Impermanence**

**Summary**

The text describes a specific meditative practice where the perception aggregate, known as 想蕴, is deliberately observed as being like a mirage. This method is employed within the framework of 无常观, the contemplation of impermanence, to facilitate a deeper understanding that all perceptual phenomena are transient and illusory, similar to a mirage.

**Detailed Analysis**

**Mirage as a Metaphor for Perception**
The text establishes that a mirage is not merely an example but a central metaphor for the nature of perception itself. Within the context of contemplating impermanence, perception is not viewed as solid or reliable. Instead, it is understood to be deceptive and insubstantial, just as a mirage appears to be water but is not.

**The Observation of 想蕴 as 阳焰**
The practice involves a direct and focused observation of the perception aggregate, or 想蕴. The method specifies that this aggregate is to be viewed specifically as 阳焰, a mirage. This indicates a targeted contemplative technique where the inherent quality of a mirage—its appearance of reality that vanishes upon closer examination—is used to analyze and deconstruct the very nature of perceptual experience.

**The Function of 无常观**
The framework for this practice is identified as 无常观, the contemplation of impermanence. The purpose of observing perception as a mirage is to directly understand the principle of impermanence. By recognizing that perception is illusory and fleeting, one gains a profound understanding that all compounded things are subject to change and are devoid of a permanent, independent essence.
