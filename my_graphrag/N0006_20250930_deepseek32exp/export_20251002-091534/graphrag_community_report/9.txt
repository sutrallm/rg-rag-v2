**Title: Foundations of Spiritual Attainment: Moral Precepts and Wisdom**

**Summary**

This report details the interdependent relationship between pure moral precepts and perfect wisdom as the core components of spiritual attainment. The analysis reveals that practitioners and enlightened beings alike rely on pure moral discipline as the foundational basis for spiritual development. For sages, this foundation results in the attainment of perfect wisdom, with both aspects being complementary and essential.

**The Foundational Role of Pure Moral Precepts**

Pure moral precepts are identified as the essential foundation for spiritual progress. Practitioners rely on them for spiritual development and wisdom cultivation. Furthermore, these precepts must reach a state of purity or清净 to be effective, indicating that the quality of moral discipline is as important as its practice. This purified moral foundation is also a fundamental aspect of a sage's spiritual attainment and virtue.

**The Attainment of Perfect Wisdom by Sages**

Sages, or saints, are characterized by their possession of perfect, or圆满, wisdom. This perfect wisdom is a key trait of a sage and is described as being perfected through their spiritual cultivation. The text emphasizes that sages are endowed with this complete wisdom, marking it as a definitive outcome of advanced spiritual practice.

**The Complementary Nature of Moral Precepts and Wisdom**

Pure moral precepts and perfect wisdom are not isolated achievements but exist in a complementary relationship. Pure moral precepts serve as the foundation for developing wisdom and spiritual light. The text explicitly states that these two aspects are complementary facets of a single spiritual attainment, indicating that one supports and completes the other in the path of spiritual development.
