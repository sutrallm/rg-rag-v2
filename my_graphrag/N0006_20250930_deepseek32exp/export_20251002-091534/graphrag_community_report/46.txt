**Report on the Path and State of Liberation in Spiritual Practice**

**Summary**
This report details the path to and the definitive state of liberation, the ultimate goal of spiritual practice. Liberation is achieved through the complete cultivation of moral discipline, meditative concentration, and wisdom, leading to the permanent eradication of greed, hatred, and delusion. It is characterized by the transcendence of the cycle of birth and death, the cessation of all suffering, and a mind that is unbound and utterly free from attachment. The path involves diligent practice, including the Noble Eightfold Path and the Four Foundations of Mindfulness, with a core requirement being the profound insight into the dependent origination and the empty, impermanent, and selfless nature of all phenomena, particularly the five aggregates.

**The Constituents of the Path to Liberation**
The complete system of moral discipline, meditative concentration, and wisdom forms the foundational and necessary path to liberation. These three trainings work synergistically to guide practitioners to the ultimate goal. Upholding moral discipline is the cornerstone, as pure conduct naturally accumulates the provisions for liberation. Meditative concentration is a vital method for achieving mental clarity and stability, leading to the wisdom that understands the true nature of existence. Wisdom, the culmination of the path, enables the practitioner to sever the root causes of affliction and attain final freedom.

**The Role of the Five Aggregates in Attaining Freedom**
Liberation requires the complete abandonment of attachment to the five aggregates: form, feeling, perception, mental formations, and consciousness. Practitioners must thoroughly observe these aggregates and penetrate their impermanent, unsatisfactory, and selfless nature. Seeing their illusory nature serves as the foundation for ultimate liberation. The state of mental liberation is specifically achieved when attachment to these five aggregates is fully abandoned.

**The Centrality of Dependent Origination and Wisdom**
A deep, direct insight into dependent origination—the law that all phenomena arise and cease due to conditions and lack independent existence—is fundamental to achieving liberation. This understanding is the basis for realizing emptiness. Wisdom is the faculty that allows for this profound seeing. It enables the practitioner to comprehend that all things are dependently originated and empty of a permanent self, thereby cutting off the root of ignorance and all afflictive knots.

**The Definitive Characteristics of the Liberated State**
A person who has attained liberation, a Saint or a Buddha, has permanently extinguished all afflictions, including greed, hatred, and delusion. This state, known as Nirvana, represents the complete cessation of suffering and the final transcendence of the cycle of birth and death. The liberated mind is completely free from all bonds and attachments, undisturbed by praise or blame, and abides in non-discriminating, equal wisdom. Such a being no longer creates new karma and is not bound by past karmic accumulations, moving through the world without being stained by it, like a lotus flower unsullied by the mud from which it grows.

**Essential Practices and Supporting Factors**
The Noble Eightfold Path is the definitive and necessary route that leads to liberation. The consistent practice of the Four Foundations of Mindfulness provides a direct path for realizing the true nature of reality. Diligence is the fundamental root and indispensable requirement for progress on the path, enabling the practitioner to overcome laziness and persevere until the final goal is reached. Conversely, spiritual negligence leads to sinking deeper into afflictions. A mindset of disenchantment, born from seeing the impermanent nature of the aggregates, naturally arises and propels the practitioner toward liberation.
