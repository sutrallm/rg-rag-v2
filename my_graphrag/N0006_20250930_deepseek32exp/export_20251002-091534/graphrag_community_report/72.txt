**Report on the Abandonment of Aversion through Loving-Kindness Practice**

**Summary**
The analysis identifies a core spiritual conflict between the problem of aversion and its prescribed solution. Aversion, characterized as a form of defilement, is identified as an obstacle on the spiritual path that must be abandoned. The practice of loving-kindness meditation is presented as the specific and direct method for counteracting and overcoming this state.

**The Nature of Aversion as a Defilement**
The text defines aversion as a specific manifestation of a defilement or affliction. This classification establishes it not as a minor irritation but as a fundamental spiritual hindrance that disrupts mental clarity and progress.

**The Necessity of Abandoning Aversion for Spiritual Progress**
A central requirement for following the correct spiritual path is the active abandonment of aversion and hatred. This is not presented as an optional practice but as an essential component of the discipline, indicating that progress is contingent upon addressing this specific defilement.

**Loving-Kindness Meditation as the Direct Antidote**
The text specifies loving-kindness meditation as the dedicated practice for counteracting aversion. This establishes a direct, functional relationship where this particular method is designed to target and neutralize the identified problem of aversion.
