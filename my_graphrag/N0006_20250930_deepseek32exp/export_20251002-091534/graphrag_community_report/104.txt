**Title: Core Tenets of Authentic Dharma Practice and Cultivation**

**Summary**

This report outlines the fundamental criteria for verifying genuine spiritual attainment within the provided framework. The primary standard is the effective diminishment of self-attachment, identified as the root obstacle. Further validation is found in the natural and unconditional expression of two key qualities in daily life: boundless compassion and profound empathy, which arise spontaneously from a deep sense of interconnectedness.

**Key Criteria for Authentic Cultivation**

**The Diminishment of Self-Attachment as the Foundational Goal**
The central and most critical measure of genuine spiritual progress is the extent to which self-attachment and its habitual tendencies have been eradicated. This self-centered focus is explicitly defined as the fundamental barrier that must be overcome during practice and realization.

**Unconditional Compassion Manifested in Daily Conduct**
Authentic attainment is demonstrated through the natural and unforced expression of boundless compassion in everyday situations. This compassion is not conditional or selective; it arises spontaneously and seamlessly as a reflection of one's integrated practice, without requiring deliberate effort.

**Empathy Rooted in a Perception of Shared Existence**
A further indicator of deep realization is the manifestation of profound empathy, which stems from a direct understanding of a shared essence with all beings. This empathetic response is not an intellectual exercise but a natural outflow in daily life, reflecting a state where the perceived separation between self and other has dissolved.
