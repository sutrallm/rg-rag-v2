**Report on the Nature and Overcoming of Self-Attachment in Spiritual Practice**

**Summary**
This report details the fundamental role of self-attachment as the primary source of suffering and the central obstacle to spiritual liberation. The path to overcoming this involves a sustained practice of mental purification and the direct realization of non-self, which is the core truth leading to freedom and peace. Individuals who have completely eradicated self-attachment demonstrate profound equanimity and abiding ease.

**The Central Obstacle of Self-Attachment**
Self-attachment is identified as the root cause of affliction and suffering, binding individuals to cyclic existence. It is a fundamental delusion and habitual tendency that must be abandoned. This deep-seated clinging to a permanent self is the principal barrier that must be overcome on the spiritual path to achieve liberation.

**The Liberating Truth of Non-Self**
Non-self is the core doctrine, signifying the absence of a permanent, independent self-entity. It is the ultimate truth that practitioners must realize, enabling them to transcend all dualistic concepts. The direct and complete realization of this truth severs the very root of all afflictions, allowing one to relinquish attachments to the past and future, thereby attaining the state of nirvana.

**The Path of Practice and Realization**
The process of overcoming self-attachment involves a sustained spiritual practice aimed at dismantling this delusion and purifying mental formations. Through this continuous purification, the habitual tendencies of self-attachment are gradually dissolved, culminating in the direct realization of non-self. The complete eradication of this fundamental clinging requires subduing both coarse afflictions and subtle, latent tendencies.

**The Outcome of Overcoming Self-Attachment**
Individuals who have severed self-attachment, known as sages or the wise, have eliminated self-centered grasping. They maintain a mind of perfect equanimity amidst both favorable and adverse circumstances, achieving a state of abiding ease and freedom. Full liberation is contingent upon the complete eradication of self-attachment.

**A Method for Weakening Self-Attachment**
Observing the illusory nature of the five aggregates serves as a method to gradually free oneself from the afflictions of self-attachment. This observation leads directly to the realization of non-self. Furthermore, a specific, non-conceptual wisdom is identified as the force that eliminates self-grasping.
