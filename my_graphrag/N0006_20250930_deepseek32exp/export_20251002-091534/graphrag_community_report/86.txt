**Title: The Path to Arahantship and the Attainment of Unborn Dharma Endurance**

**Summary**

The text describes the state of an Arahant, a fully liberated being who has eradicated all afflictions and transcended suffering. The fundamental requirement for this liberation is the complete eradication of the view of a self. This achievement leads to the ultimate state of Nirvana and the realization of the unborn dharma endurance, characterized by a mind that is unobstructed, pure, and free from all烦恼.

**Key Findings**

**The Necessity of Eradicating the View of a Self**
The complete destruction of clinging to the view of a self is presented as the essential and non-negotiable requirement for liberation. This act is described as being capable of destroying the very root of birth and death, similar to cutting down a reed.

**Arahantship as the Culmination of Practice**
An Arahant is defined as a sage who has perfected moral discipline, concentration, and wisdom, and has severed the root of all afflictions. They have fully realized the four stages of enlightenment and are described as a lamp illuminating the world.

**Nirvana as the Ultimate State**
The ultimate state achieved by an Arahant is Nirvana. This is a final condition from which there is no return to cyclic existence, marked by the complete cessation of all烦恼.

**The Centrality of Unborn Dharma Endurance**
The ultimate state of realization and liberation is consistently identified as the unborn dharma endurance. Arahants have verified this state, which is associated with the true nature of all phenomena and represents a condition beyond all dualities.

**The Role of Observing Dependent Arising**
Correctly observing the law of dependent origination is stated to lead inevitably to the attainment of the ultimate bliss of unborn dharma endurance.

**The Result of Abandoning Clinging to the Five Aggregates**
Verifying the stages of enlightenment requires the complete purification of all clinging to the five aggregates.

**The Spontaneous Nature of Realization**
The verification of an enlightened stage occurs spontaneously at the moment when the dichotomy of perceiver and perceived vanishes.
