**Report on the Attributes of Enlightened Beings and the Nature of Obstructions**

**Summary**
This report details the defining characteristics of enlightened beings, who have achieved ultimate spiritual liberation by eradicating fundamental afflictions and transcending all dualistic concepts. Their state is imperceptible to obstructive forces, specifically symbolized by the figure of Mara. The key to this liberation is a profound non-attachment to time, phenomena, and states of existence.

**Defining Characteristics of an Enlightened Being**
An enlightened being is one who has completely eradicated the three fundamental poisons: greed, hatred, and delusion. They have reached the far shore, achieving the supreme fruit of practice and ultimate tranquility (涅槃). Their attainment represents the ultimate truth as pointed to by all Buddhas, a state that transcends language and conceptual thought.

**The Realization of Emptiness and Non-Attachment**
A core attribute of an enlightened being is the thorough perception that the five aggregates are empty. This deep understanding allows them to transcend all dualistic concepts and views, including extremes of eternalism and nihilism. They are entirely free from conceptual attachments and are not bound by worldly phenomena, comparable to a lotus unstained by mud or the sky untouched by clouds.

**Imperviousness to Obstructive Forces**
The enlightened state is characterized by a complete freedom from obstructive forces, personified as Mara. Because their mental traces cannot be found, Mara is incapable of locating them. This immunity stems directly from their lack of attachment; they do not cling to the past, yearn for the future, or adhere to any state of existence, thereby breaking the power of these binding forces.

**Path to Liberation from Obstructions**
The principle that an enlightened being cannot be found by Mara also serves as the path for any practitioner to achieve the same freedom.
