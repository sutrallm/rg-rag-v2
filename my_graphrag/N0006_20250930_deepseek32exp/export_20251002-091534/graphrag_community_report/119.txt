**Report on the Cultivation of Body, Speech, and Mind**

**Summary**
This report details the fundamental role of purifying body, speech, and mind in a practitioner's spiritual path. The consistent correction of these three aspects is identified as the direct method for subduing sensory desires, severing the root of attachment, and ultimately achieving a state free from suffering.

**The Centrality of Purifying Body, Speech, and Mind**
A practitioner's cultivation is fundamentally structured around three components: body, speech, and mind. The core of their spiritual practice involves maintaining purity in physical actions, verbal expressions, and mental activities.

**The Method for Subduing Sensory Desires**
The process of subduing sensory desires is accomplished specifically through the correction and discipline of one's body, speech, and mind. This triad constitutes the primary technique for achieving this control.

**The Path to Severing Attachment and Ending Suffering**
A sustained and continuous effort to correct body, speech, and mind leads to the gradual eradication of the root of attachment. This progressive cultivation is the defined pathway that culminates in the attainment of a state without suffering.
