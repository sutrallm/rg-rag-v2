**Report on the State of the Supreme Saint**

**Summary**
This report details the definitive characteristics of the ultimate state of spiritual attainment, known as the Supreme Saint. The analysis confirms that this state represents the highest possible level of accomplishment, defined by two primary attributes: a complete transcendence of all dualistic concepts and the full accumulation of two specific forms of merit.

**Definitive Characteristics of the Highest Spiritual State**

The highest spiritual state is characterized by a total transcendence of all relative and dualistic frameworks. This indicates a mode of being that is not subject to conventional distinctions such as existence and non-existence, good and evil, or self and other. Attainment of this state signifies a fundamental shift beyond conceptual limitations.

**Prerequisites for Attaining the Ultimate State**

Attainment of the ultimate state is contingent upon the complete accumulation of two forms of merit: merit and wisdom. The fulfillment of both these reserves is presented as a non-negotiable prerequisite for reaching this supreme level of spiritual realization.
