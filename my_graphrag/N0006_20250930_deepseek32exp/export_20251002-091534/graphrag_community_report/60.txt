**Report on Spiritual Attainment and the Lotus Metaphor**

**Summary**

This report details the qualities and states of enlightened beings and dedicated practitioners, using the lotus flower as a central metaphor for purity and transcendence. Key findings indicate that such individuals achieve a state of perfect freedom, characterized by an unstained mind and non-attachment to worldly phenomena or spiritual accomplishments. This state is supported by the integrated cultivation of compassion and wisdom, the practice of equanimity, and the transcendence of dualistic thinking.

**The Lotus as a Symbol of Transcendent Purity**

The lotus flower, which emerges unstained from mud, symbolizes the state of enlightened beings and practitioners. The mud represents worldly defilements and attachments. These individuals, while actively engaging with the world, remain unaffected and unstained by it. This symbolizes their liberation from all defilements and their ability to exist without being bound by worldly conditions or phenomena.

**The State of Perfect Freedom Beyond Concepts**

Enlightened beings manifest a state of perfect freedom that transcends conceptual thinking. They dwell in a non-acquisitive wisdom characterized by not grasping at spiritual attainments or accomplishments. This represents the ultimate state of liberation, where the mind remains pure and unstained.

**The Foundational Role of Equanimity**

Equanimity is a foundational quality for the path to liberation. It is a state of mind that treats all beings equally, like the earth supporting all things. This involves not rejecting suffering and not clinging to happiness, thereby transcending dualistic opposition. For enlightened beings, this manifests as remaining unmoved by worldly praise or blame in both favorable and adverse circumstances.

**The Integration of Compassion and Wisdom**

Spiritual practice requires the perfect integration of compassion and wisdom. Compassion must be supported by wisdom to achieve the union of these two qualities. This compassionate mind, supported by non-discriminating wisdom, treats all beings equally and gradually dissolves the distinction between self and others.

**The Practice of the Four Immeasurables**

Practitioners should at all times cultivate the Four Immeasurables: loving-kindness, compassion, sympathetic joy, and equanimity. This practice is the method for developing unconditional compassion and a broad, altruistic mind aimed at benefiting all beings.

**Non-Discriminating Wisdom and the Elimination of Self-Grasping**

Enlightened beings dwell in non-discriminating wisdom, a state that eliminates self-grasping and transcends dualistic modes of thinking. This wisdom is a defining characteristic of those who have attained liberation and is the ground from which unconditional compassion arises.
