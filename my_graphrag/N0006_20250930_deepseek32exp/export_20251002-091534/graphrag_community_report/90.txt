**Report on the Path to Ultimate Liberation and Spiritual Accomplishment**

**Summary**
This report details the definitive stages and characteristics of spiritual practice leading to ultimate liberation. The path is governed by the immutable law of cause and effect and requires diligent practice that integrates teachings into daily life. The ultimate goal is to become a completely enlightened being, a state marked by the transcendence of all dualities, the cessation of suffering, and freedom from the cycle of birth and death. Spiritual accomplishment, verified through practical application, serves as the foundation for this final liberation.

**The Final State of Complete Liberation**
The ultimate liberation is a state of thorough awakening where all ignorance and delusion are destroyed. In this condition, practitioners transcend all dualistic concepts and abide in a wisdom of non-discrimination and equality. The mind is pure and unstained, analogous to a lotus flower that grows from mud yet remains unsullied. This state signifies the cessation of all suffering, the severance of all roots of affliction, and the destruction of all attachments. Consequently, one transcends the cycle of birth and death and is no longer bound by any karmic force, achieving supreme and unhindered freedom.

**Spiritual Accomplishment as a Validated Foundation**
Spiritual accomplishment is a state of fulfillment reached through spiritual practice, characterized by unwavering stability, consistency in speech and action, and wisdom that illuminates the world like a lamp. This achievement is defined by freedom from attachment and the maintenance of steadfast mindfulness. Its authenticity is verified through practical application, similar to pure gold being refined by fire to remove impurities. This process demonstrates the essence of the true teachings through personal realization, purifying body, speech, and mind so they remain untainted by worldly phenomena. It is the necessary prerequisite and foundation that progresses toward ultimate liberation.

**The Governing Principle of Cause and Effect**
A fundamental and unavoidable law states that all actions produce corresponding results. This principle requires practitioners to vigilantly guard their physical, verbal, and mental actions. It forms the basic principle for practicing the Noble Eightfold Path. Adherence to this law ensures that practitioners will undoubtedly realize the sacred fruits of their practice, a outcome that cannot be altered by any external force. Understanding and following this law is essential for spiritual accomplishment.

**The Characteristics of a Completely Enlightened Being**
A completely enlightened being is one who has extinguished all fires of affliction and abides in a wondrous state of non-action, having achieved supreme liberation. Such a being is no longer bound by karmic forces, illustrated by the metaphor of a bird flying through the sky without leaving any trace. This signifies that they accumulate no further karmic actions.

**The Necessity of Integrating Teachings into Practice**
The essence of the Dharma teachings can only be realized by integrating the doctrines into daily spiritual practice. Mere intellectual knowledge of the teachings without actual practice is insufficient to yield genuine benefit. The core meaning of the Dharma is personally demonstrated and verified by genuine practitioners through their practical application, embodying the true essence of the Buddhist teachings.
