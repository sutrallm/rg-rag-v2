**Report on the State of Enlightened Beings and Their Attainments**

**Summary**
This report details the definitive characteristics and state of an enlightened being, referred to as a Saint or one with perfect accomplishment. The core of this state is a transcendent abiding in the primordial, quiescent nature of mind, free from all dualistic concepts and craving. A key manifestation and validation of this attainment is the mastery and unimpeded use of the three insights and six supernatural powers, which are wielded without being deluded by them.

**Characteristics of a Perfectly Accomplished Being**
A being who has reached perfect accomplishment has extinguished all craving and transcended all dualistic concepts, including distinctions such as good and evil. They permanently abide in the primordial, quiescent nature of their mind. This state represents the ultimate destination for a practitioner.

**The Role of the Three Insights and Six Powers**
The state of perfect accomplishment is manifested through the unimpeded use of the three insights and six supernatural powers. These powers are not a source of delusion for the Saint but are a natural expression of their attainment. The possession of these abilities serves as one of the verification standards for a practitioner's successful achievement.

**Equanimity as Transcendence**
The cultivation of a mind of equanimity is defined as the transcendence of dualistic opposition, leading to a state of freedom. This freedom from duality is a fundamental characteristic of the enlightened state, where the Saint is not bound by any form of discrimination.
