**Report on the Role of Right Knowledge in Spiritual Practice**

**Summary**
The text describes the function of Right Knowledge (正智) as the pivotal instrument for spiritual liberation. It enables a practitioner to observe the impermanent and illusory nature of all conditioned phenomena, specifically the five aggregates and name-and-form. By fully comprehending this reality and the principle of dependent origination, one can sever the underlying attachments, thereby stopping the cycle of rebirth and achieving freedom from suffering.

**The Function of Right Knowledge in Observation**
Right Knowledge allows a practitioner to observe the arising and passing away of all worldly phenomena. It facilitates a clear seeing of the five aggregates as insubstantial and illusory, comparable to a bubble or a mirage.

**Right Knowledge as the Mechanism for Liberation**
The complete development of Right Knowledge leads to the cessation of the stream of the cycle of rebirth. It is the specific means through which attachment to the phenomena of name and form is abandoned, allowing for liberation from the suffering of birth and death.

**The Objective of Applying Right Knowledge**
The ultimate outcome of applying Right Knowledge is to achieve a true understanding of dependent origination and to realize liberation. This genuine, experiential seeing is what enables a practitioner to cross over and be freed from the ocean of suffering inherent in the cycle of life and death.
