**Title: The Plantain Tree Metaphor in the Contemplation of Impermanence**

**Summary**

The text establishes that the mental aggregate known as "mental formations" (行蕴) is systematically observed as a plantain tree (芭蕉) within the practice of the contemplation of impermanence (无常观). This metaphor is used to facilitate a deep understanding of the transient and insubstantial nature of these mental constructs.

**Insights**

**The Metaphorical Role of the Plantain Tree**
The plantain tree serves as a specific metaphor within the contemplation of impermanence. Its purpose is to provide a tangible object of observation for understanding the abstract nature of mental formations.

**Mental Formations as the Object of Contemplation**
The primary subject of this contemplative practice is the aggregate of mental formations (行蕴). The practice involves a deliberate and focused observation of this specific mental aggregate to gain insight.

**The Purpose of Observing Impermanence**
The fundamental goal of using the plantain tree metaphor is to cultivate an understanding of impermanence. The practice is designed to reveal the transient and ultimately insubstantial character of mental formations, demonstrating that they lack a permanent, solid core, much like the layered, hollow trunk of a plantain tree.
