**Report on the Attainments of the Accomplished Practitioner**

**Summary**
This report details the definitive outcomes achieved by an accomplished practitioner. The individual has successfully reached the ultimate state of Nirvana and has eradicated the fundamental cause of suffering by dispelling ignorance.

**Key Attainments**

**Arrival at the Deathless Shore**
The practitioner has completed their spiritual journey, arriving at the far shore of Nirvana, which is characterized as a state beyond death.

**Dispelling the Darkness of Ignorance**
The practitioner has overcome the core obscuration of ignorance, which is described as a state of darkness. This act of dispelling is presented as the direct method for achieving the ultimate goal.
