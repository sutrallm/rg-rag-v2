**Title: The Far Shore: Attainment of Ultimate Liberation**

**Summary**

This report details the state known as "the far shore," the ultimate goal of spiritual practice. It is characterized as a state of complete liberation from suffering, the cycle of birth and death, and all forms of bondage. Those who reach this state, known as "ones who have gone to the far shore," have eradicated the causes of rebirth and exist in a condition of permanent peace and security, free from fear and attachment.

**The Final Goal of Spiritual Practice**

The far shore is the ultimate state to be reached through diligent spiritual practice. It represents the final destination of life, a state of liberation and awakening freed from suffering. Practitioners aim to arrive at this secure and free state, which lies beyond the constraints of time and space.

**Characteristics of the Liberated State**

This state transcends the cycle of birth and death and is characterized by non-arising and non-ceasing. It is a condition of ultimate peace and safety. Those who arrive here experience the cessation of mental disturbances and verbal fabrications, having surpassed all concepts of body and mind. They are completely free, with their liberation compared to a lamp whose flame has been extinguished.

**The Path to Attainment**

The far shore is achieved through the cultivation of meditation and wisdom, and by abandoning all attachments. Diligence aids practitioners in reaching this liberation. Key practices include observing bodily and mental phenomena as they are, maintaining continuous awareness, and engaging in spiritual cultivation to cross the swamp of greed, hatred, and delusion and the torrent of birth and death.

**The State of the Liberated Being**

An individual who reaches the far shore is a saint who has attained ultimate liberation. Such a being creates no new karmic actions and holds no attachment to past deeds. Having severed all fetters that lead to cyclical existence, they are without fear and worry. The three spheres of existence are utterly destroyed for them, and they have transcended duality and all forms of clinging.
