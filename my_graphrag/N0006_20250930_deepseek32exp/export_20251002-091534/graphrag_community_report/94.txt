**Title: The State and Path of Complete Awakening**

**Summary**

This report details the characteristics of a fully awakened practitioner and the definitive path to achieving this state. The ultimate goal is a state of complete mental stillness and liberation, achieved exclusively through diligent practice of the teachings. This results in the destruction of all afflictions, the cessation of karmic generation, and freedom from all suffering.

**Characteristics of the Fully Awakened State**

The mind of a fully awakened practitioner is in a state of complete stillness, likened to an extinguished lamp flame. This state is one of unshakable stability, marked by the complete eradication of all attachment, craving, and ignorance. It represents the ultimate awakening and the culmination of liberation.

**The Cessation of Karmic Processes**

A practitioner who has achieved complete awakening has dismantled the mechanisms of karma. They have destroyed all afflictive structures, ensuring that no new karmic seeds are generated. Past karmic conditions are rendered inactive. Consequently, such a practitioner is no longer subject to the pull of karmic force.

**The Path to Achievement**

The sole path to this awakened state is the diligent and complete practice of the teachings. This involves the dedicated cultivation of the threefold training and the full development of the seven factors of enlightenment. Through this correct practice, one is certain to transcend all suffering and bondage.

**The Result of the Path**

The outcome of this path is the attainment of a complete and final awakening. This is the ultimate nirvana, a state that is the direct result of spiritual practice and represents the highest achievement for a practitioner.
