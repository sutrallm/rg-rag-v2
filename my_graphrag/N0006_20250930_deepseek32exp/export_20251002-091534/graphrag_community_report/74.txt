**Title: The Attainment of Liberation Through the Eradication of Defilements**

**Summary:**
This report details the state of an individual who has permanently ended all defilements. Such a person achieves complete liberation from the cycle of life and death, characterized by an unshakeable, supreme state of peace. This liberated state is compared to a lotus flower, which remains unstained by the mud in which it grows, illustrating the ability to exist within the world without being affected by it.

**Insights:**

**The Nature of Liberation from Life and Death**
A person who has permanently eradicated all defilements attains liberation from the cycle of life and death. This is not merely an escape but a definitive and final state of freedom.

**The Unstained Existence in the World**
The condition of such a liberated individual is analogous to a lotus flower growing from mud. The lotus emerges pure and unstained, demonstrating that one can exist within the worldly realm without being tainted or attached to its impurities.

**The Conquest Over Death and Attainment of Supreme Peace**
The eradication of defilements is equated with the destruction of death's army. The result of this conquest is the achievement of an unsurpassed, ultimate state of quietude and tranquility.
