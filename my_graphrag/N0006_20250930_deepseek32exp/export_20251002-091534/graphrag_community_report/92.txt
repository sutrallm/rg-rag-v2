**Report on Doctrinal Principles and Actual Practice**

**Summary**
This report outlines the fundamental relationship between doctrinal teachings and actual practice. The doctrine provides the essential principles and direction for spiritual cultivation, with the core objective being the complete cessation of suffering. Actual practice is the necessary, experiential method for verifying these teachings, understanding the nature of suffering, and ultimately achieving liberation. A reliance on textual study alone, without practice, is insufficient for realizing these goals.

**The Necessity of Experiential Practice for Verification**
Actual practice is defined as a personal, experiential process of verifying doctrinal teachings. It is through this direct practice, rather than intellectual debate or textual study alone, that an individual can touch truth and personally realize the ultimate state. The value of the doctrine is manifested and confirmed through this practical application.

**The Core Objective of Complete Cessation of Suffering**
The fundamental goal of all practice and the core objective of the doctrine is the complete cessation of suffering. This cessation is equated with liberation. All aspects of cultivation are ultimately directed toward this aim of utterly ending suffering.

**The Origin of Suffering in Attachment**
Suffering is identified as the first Noble Truth that must be recognized. Its origin arises from attachment and grasping. This understanding of the root cause of suffering is a central point of correct understanding within the doctrinal framework.

**The Practical Method of Observing Breath and Phenomena**
A key method within actual practice begins with observing the breath. This practice involves maintaining awareness in all activities and systematically observing phenomena. The process includes being mindful of the body's impermanence, observing the arising and passing of feelings, and recognizing the flow of thoughts. This method is designed to penetrate phenomena and directly perceive reality.

**The Role of Doctrine as a Guiding Framework**
The doctrine consists of the correct teachings and fundamental principles that a practitioner must follow and cultivate. It provides the guiding direction for the spiritual path, pointing toward the essence of overcoming suffering and attaining happiness. It outlines the methods of the path to liberation.

**The Relationship Between Mind Cultivation and the Middle Way**
A crucial aspect of practice involves the mind transcending discrimination. This transcendence allows the mind to accord with the Middle Way, which is identified as the ultimate practice approach. Actual practice provides the practical methods and essentials for this gradual process of mind cultivation.

**The Complementary Nature of Concentration and Observation**
The method of observing the breath within actual practice is closely connected to the practice of concentration, where the mind is focused on a single point. Both are important methods within the broader system of cultivation. Furthermore, the practice of wisdom observation enables practitioners to understand the nature of suffering through this systematic observation.
