By using mindfulness as a tool to guard the mind, practitioners learn to maintain awareness without clinging to past, present, or future experiences. This sustained practice purifies mental habits, ultimately allowing the innate, transcendent quality of awareness to manifest naturally, which is intrinsically connected to the unfolding of wisdom.
