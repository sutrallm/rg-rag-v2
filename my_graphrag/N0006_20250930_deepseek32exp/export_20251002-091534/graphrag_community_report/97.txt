**Report on the Attainment of the State of No-Learning (无学位)**

**Summary**
This report details the definitive characteristics of the state of No-Learning (无学位), identified as the ultimate outcome of spiritual practice. The state signifies the complete and final liberation of a practitioner, marked by the total eradication of specific spiritual defilements, including the three fetters and ten bonds, and a permanent transcendence of the four floods. This condition represents the final attainment of enlightenment and nirvana.

**Characteristics of the Ultimate State of Enlightenment**
The state of No-Learning is the final fruit of spiritual cultivation. It is defined as a condition where all necessary work has been accomplished, and the practitioner will no longer be subject to future rebirth. Consciousness in this state is described as being perfectly clear, comparable to a cloudless sky.

**Final Eradication of Defilements**
A core attribute of this state is the complete and irreversible destruction of the three fetters and ten bonds. This total elimination of fundamental spiritual corruptions is a prerequisite for and a definitive marker of having reached this ultimate stage of liberation.

**Permanent Transcendence of Worldly Existence**
The state of No-Learning involves a permanent departure from the four floods. This signifies a final and absolute freedom from the forces that perpetuate the cycle of suffering and existence, confirming the practitioner's unconditional release.

**Culmination in Ultimate Nirvana**
The realization of the Noble Fruits leads directly to the state of ultimate nirvana. The state of No-Learning is, therefore, synonymous with this supreme and final nirvana, representing the conclusive goal of the spiritual path.
