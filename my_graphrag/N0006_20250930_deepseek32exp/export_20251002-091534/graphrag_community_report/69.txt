**Title: The Role of Concentration in Mindfulness and 止观 Practice**

**Summary**

The practice of mindfulness and 止观 (zhǐguān) develops a foundational concentration power. This power is essential for overcoming mental distractions, afflictions, and defilements by directly calming and平息 (píngxī) the mind's restless thoughts and intense thought fluctuations.

**Detailed Findings**

**The Development of Concentration Power**
Both mindfulness and 止观 practice are identified as methods for cultivating concentration power. This power is not an inherent trait but is developed through dedicated practice.

**Concentration as a Defense Against Distraction**
The primary function of this developed concentration is to protect the practitioner from distractions. It acts as a mental shield, preventing external or internal stimuli from disrupting focus.

**Overcoming Mental Afflictions with Concentration**
Concentration power is instrumental in overcoming mental afflictions and defilements. It provides the mental stability required to confront and subdue negative or harmful mental states.

**The Calming of Mental Activity**
A central effect of concentration is the calming of the mind's activity. It specifically targets and平息 (píngxī) the mind's "wandering thoughts" (妄念, wàngniàn) and the "fluctuations" or "waves" of thought. This process is described as平息炽热的思绪波动 (píngxī chìrè de sīxù bōdòng), which means to calm or quell fervent or intense thought fluctuations.
