**Title: Characteristics and Attainment of Ultimate Nirvana**

**Summary**
This report details the state of Ultimate Nirvana, the final goal for practitioners. It is a state of complete liberation from the cycle of birth and death, achieved by fully awakened beings. Key characteristics include the cessation of mental formations, the quenching of craving, the extinction of afflictions, and the abandonment of clinging. Those who attain this state transcend all dualistic concepts, including the framework of good and evil, through a profound understanding of dependent origination.

**The Final Goal of a Practitioner**
The ultimate objective for a practitioner is to achieve Ultimate Nirvana. This state represents complete liberation from the cycle of birth and death, meaning no future rebirths will occur.

**Path to Attainment Through Understanding**
A practitioner who thoroughly comprehends dependent origination and attains non-outflow wisdom can reach Ultimate Nirvana. This profound understanding is the mechanism through which complete liberation is achieved.

**Transcendence of Dualistic Frameworks**
A fully awakened being transcends all dualistic concepts. Specifically, such a practitioner overcomes the duality of good and evil, liberating themselves from this and all other binary oppositions.

**Characteristics of the Liberated State**
Ultimate Nirvana is defined by several distinct characteristics. These include the cessation of mental formations, the quenching of craving, the extinction of afflictions, and the abandonment of clinging. This state is also beyond all conceptual thought and idle speculation.
