**Report on the Attributes of Fully Enlightened Beings**

**Summary**
This report details the defining characteristics of fully enlightened beings. These individuals have achieved a state of complete mental purity and freedom, operating through a wisdom that is free from attachment and conceptual limitations.

**Core Attributes of Fully Enlightened Beings**

**Mental Purity and Freedom from Attachments**
Fully enlightened beings have severed all attachments. Their minds are described as remaining unstained, comparable to lotuses that emerge clean from mud, signifying a state of purity unaffected by negative influences.

**Transcendence of Conceptual Thinking**
These beings dwell in a state of perfect freedom that transcends conceptual thinking. This freedom is not bound by conventional thought patterns and represents a higher mode of existence.

**Non-Acquisitive Wisdom**
The wisdom in which enlightened beings dwell is characterized as non-acquisitive. This means it is defined by not grasping at attainments or possessions, indicating a state of being free from desire and clinging.
