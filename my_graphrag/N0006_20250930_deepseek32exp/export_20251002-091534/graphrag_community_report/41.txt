**Title: The Attainment and Significance of Non-Outflow Wisdom in Spiritual Practice**

**Summary**

This report details the nature, attainment, and consequences of non-outflow wisdom. It is defined as the ultimate, purest form of wisdom, representing the final goal of spiritual practice. Attainment is achieved through specific mental disciplines, particularly maintaining mindfulness and awareness at the six sense faculties. The primary outcome for a practitioner who achieves this wisdom is complete liberation, characterized by freedom from karmic cycles, transcendence of the rebirth mechanism, and an unshakable mind.

**Key Findings**

**The Ultimate Goal of Spiritual Practice**
Non-outflow wisdom is the final objective and culminating achievement of the stages of spiritual practice. It is described as the ultimate result of wisdom development and the conclusive wisdom attained through cultivation.

**A State of Complete Purity**
This wisdom is characterized by its absolute and flawless purity. It is entirely uncontaminated and is a defining trait of a sage.

**Attainment Through Mindfulness and Awareness**
The method to achieve non-outflow wisdom is through diligent mental training. This involves maintaining correct mindfulness at all times and preserving vigilant awareness specifically at the six sense faculties.

**Liberation from Karmic Cycles and Rebirth**
A practitioner who attains this wisdom ceases to generate new karmic bonds. It prevents the creation of new karmic cycles, allowing the individual to thoroughly dismantle the mechanism of rebirth and transcend the cycle of birth and death.

**Achieving Ultimate Nirvana and an Unshakable Mind**
The result of attaining non-outflow wisdom is the state of ultimate nirvana, which is the final liberation. The practitioner's mind becomes immovable by the "eight worldly winds" and is free from all attachments, remaining unstained like a lotus flower emerging from mud.
