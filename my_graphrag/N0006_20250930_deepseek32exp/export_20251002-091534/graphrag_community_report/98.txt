**Report on Practitioner Methods for Liberation through Awareness**

**Summary**
This report details the established methods and progressive stages for practitioners seeking liberation. The core practice involves cultivating a continuous, non-attached awareness of bodily and mental phenomena, beginning with the breath and bodily sensations. This sustained awareness, known as mindfulness, is the essential tool for penetrating ignorance, understanding the nature of existence, and ultimately severing all bonds to achieve a state of complete freedom.

**The Path of Observation and Awareness**
The fundamental practice involves observing physical and mental phenomena. Practitioners maintain awareness during all activities, using this conscious attention to observe their physical, verbal, and mental actions. This cultivated awareness becomes unshakeable and serves as the primary method for diligence on the path.

**Foundations for Wisdom Development**
The development of wisdom follows a sequential path. The practice of observation is built upon a foundation of discipline and concentration. By observing physical and mental phenomena, practitioners gain direct understanding of the arising and passing away of all conditioned things, which is the essence of wisdom.

**The Role of Mindfulness and Continuous Knowing**
A core state to be maintained in the practice of observation is mindfulness. This practice requires the continuous application of clear knowing. The mind is cultivated by maintaining awareness without clinging to past attachments, future expectations, or present conditions.

**Systematic Progression of Observational Focus**
The practice of observation follows a structured progression. It begins with the breath as the starting point and uses bodily sensations as a primary object of observation. This awareness involves continuous attention to both breath and bodily sensations. The scope of observation is then gradually expanded to include every arising thought and mental impulse.

**The Outcome of Severing Bonds and Attaining Liberation**
The ultimate goal is liberation, a state characterized by the mind's freedom from all bonds. This is achieved through unwavering, non-attached awareness while engaging with phenomena. Such firm awareness is capable of penetrating the darkness of ignorance. Through this awakening, practitioners transcend all fetters. A practitioner who has reached the far shore is one who has severed all binding defilements, achieving a liberation as final as a lamp being extinguished, and enters a state where every bond is cut.
