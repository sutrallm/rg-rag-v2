**Title: The Integration of Compassion and Wisdom in Spiritual Practice**

**Summary**

This report details the essential relationship between compassion and wisdom in spiritual practice. The analysis reveals that wisdom is the foundational support for genuine compassion, which must be cultivated impartially towards all beings. The ultimate goal of this practice is the union of compassion and wisdom, a state that eliminates self-centeredness and leads to complete spiritual liberation.

**The Foundational Role of Wisdom in Compassion**

Wisdom is identified as the necessary foundation upon which genuine compassion must be established. Compassion is not merely an emotional state but is supported and sustained by a deep, discerning understanding.

**Cultivating Equanimity as a Prerequisite for Compassion**

A core requirement for developing compassion is the cultivation of equanimity, which involves maintaining an equal and impartial attitude toward all beings, regardless of circumstance.

**The Ultimate Goal of Spiritual Practice**

The ultimate objective of this spiritual path is to achieve the union of compassion and wisdom. This integrated state represents a complete and perfected spiritual quality.

**The Liberating Function of Non-Discriminating Wisdom**

A state of complete spiritual liberation is characterized by abiding in non-discriminating wisdom. This specific form of wisdom functions to eliminate self-grasping and self-centered attachment.
