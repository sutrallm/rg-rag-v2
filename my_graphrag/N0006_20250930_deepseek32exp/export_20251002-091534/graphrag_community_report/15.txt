**Report on the Eightfold Path and Its Components**

**Summary**
This report details the composition and foundational structure of the Eightfold Path, a framework for spiritual development. The path is initiated by Right View and consists of eight distinct components, including Right Mindfulness, Right Thought, Right Speech, Right Action, Right Livelihood, Right Effort, and Right Concentration.

**Composition of the Eightfold Path**
The Eightfold Path is explicitly composed of eight specific elements. These are: Right View, Right Thought, Right Speech, Right Action, Right Livelihood, Right Effort, Right Mindfulness, and Right Concentration. The text confirms each of these as a component.

**Foundational Starting Point**
The path has a defined starting point. The practice begins with the establishment of Right View, indicating its primary role as the foundation upon which the other elements are built.
