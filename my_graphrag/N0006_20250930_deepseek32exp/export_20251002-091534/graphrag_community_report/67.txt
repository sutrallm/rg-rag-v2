**Report on the Cultivation of Tranquility and Insight Meditation**

**Summary**
The text establishes that both Tranquility Meditation (*Samatha*) and Insight Meditation (*Vipassanā*) are essential and complementary practices for a practitioner. Their concurrent cultivation is presented as the definitive method for generating wisdom, with Insight Meditation specifically identified as the means for penetrating the true nature of phenomena.

**Key Findings**

**The Complementary Roles of Tranquility and Insight Meditation**
The text explicitly states that Tranquility Meditation and Insight Meditation are two methods a practitioner should cultivate simultaneously. It does not present them as sequential or optional, but as a unified practice. The term "simultaneously" underscores their interdependent nature in the path of cultivation.

**The Unified Practice as the Path to Wisdom**
The combined practice of Tranquility and Insight Meditation, referred to as "the concurrent cultivation of both," is identified as the direct cause for the arising of wisdom. This indicates that wisdom is not a product of either practice in isolation, but emerges specifically from their integrated application.

**Insight Meditation's Function in Perceiving Ultimate Realities**
Insight Meditation is assigned the specific function of "penetrating the true nature of all phenomena." This defines its primary role: to analyze and understand the fundamental characteristics of existence, moving beyond superficial appearances to comprehend ultimate realities.
