**Title: Principles and Outcomes of Practicing the True Dharma**

**Summary:**
This report outlines the core principles and resulting outcomes for an individual who diligently practices the true Dharma. The practice is defined by unwavering diligence, mindfulness, and adherence to the teachings, which guide the practitioner toward spiritual goals. The ultimate results of such practice include transcending the cycle of death, achieving liberation, and gaining a deep understanding of reality. Furthermore, the teachings themselves are validated through spiritual attainment and preserved through transmission.

**The Guiding Role of the True Dharma**
The true Dharma serves as the fundamental guide for spiritual practice. It consists of the laws, guiding principles, and methods that nurture all who seek the path. Adherence to these teachings directs the practitioner's spiritual work and provides the core doctrines for their journey.

**The State of Diligent Practice**
Diligence is the essential state of practicing the true Dharma. It requires a practitioner to follow the teachings and practice without negligence or regression. This involves constant vigilance and a sustained effort to abide in meditative concentration.

**The Objective of Transcending Death**
A primary outcome of practicing the true Dharma is to overcome the control and mastery of the domain of death. By diligently following the true teachings, a practitioner can transcend the cycle of death and rebirth.

**The Path of Mindful Conduct**
Practice requires integrating mindfulness and clear comprehension into all daily activities, such as walking, standing, sitting, and lying down. This continuous awareness is necessary for genuine abiding in meditative states.

**Progressive Spiritual Development**
Through practice, an individual cultivates and gradually perfects the threefold training of moral discipline, meditative concentration, and wisdom. This developmental path leads the practitioner toward spiritual maturity.

**The Result of Liberation and Understanding**
The ultimate purpose of learning and practicing the true Dharma is to guide the practitioner toward awakening and liberation from suffering. The true teachings dispel ignorance and delusion, leading to purity. A key requirement of practice is to correctly perceive the nature of dependent origination, and it is recognized as a path that conforms to the Middle Way.

**Validation and Preservation of the Teachings**
The essence of the true teachings is verified and demonstrated through spiritual attainment. Furthermore, the transmission of these teachings functions to preserve and pass on the true Dharma, ensuring its continuity for future generations.

**The Exemplary Role of the Saint**
A saint, by abiding in the true Dharma, becomes a refuge and support for all sentient beings, serving as a living example of the teachings.
