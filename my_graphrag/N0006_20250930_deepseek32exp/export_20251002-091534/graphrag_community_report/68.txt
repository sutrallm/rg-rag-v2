**Report on the Nature and Role of Compassion in Spiritual Attainment**

**Summary**

This report details the fundamental characteristics and functions of compassion as described in spiritual practice. Compassion is identified as a core quality of enlightened beings, intrinsically linked with wisdom, and is defined by its selfless and all-encompassing nature. Its primary purpose is the benefit of all beings, and it is cultivated through specific practices such as the Four Immeasurables.

**Compassion as a Defining Trait of Enlightened Beings**

Compassion is a quality that manifests in enlightened beings and is a part of their spiritual achievement. It is identified as one of the ultimate characteristics of a sage, who is defined as an awakened individual with perfect accomplishment. The presence of compassion is therefore a marker of high spiritual attainment.

**The Inseparable Link Between Wisdom and Compassion**

Wisdom and compassion together form the two foundational pillars of spiritual practice. These two qualities are not independent; they must be perfected together. Authentic wisdom naturally gives rise to immeasurable compassion, indicating that one cannot be fully realized without the other. This union is considered a sublime function of a sage's attained virtues.

**The Selfless and All-Encompassing Nature of Compassion**

Compassion is defined by its selfless quality, operating with a mind free of ego. Its scope is universal, aimed at benefiting all sentient beings without exception. This is described through the practices of "unconditional compassion to embrace all beings" and "sympathetic compassion to relieve suffering," highlighting its active and inclusive character.

**Compassion Manifested as Spontaneous and Nourishing Action**

The expression of compassion is not forced but arises naturally from an enlightened state, similar to spring water that flows forth and nourishes all things. This metaphor underscores its role as a life-giving and sustaining force. This natural manifestation takes the form of altruistic actions dedicated to the welfare of all beings.

**The Practical Cultivation of Compassion Through the Four Immeasurables**

Compassion is not merely a theoretical concept but a quality to be actively developed. Practitioners are instructed to cultivate the Four Immeasurables at all times as a core component of their spiritual training. This practice is the method for developing the capacity to embrace beings and relieve their suffering.
