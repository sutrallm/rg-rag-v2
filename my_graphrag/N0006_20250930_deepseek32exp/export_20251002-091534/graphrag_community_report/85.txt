**Title: The Path to Liberation through Dependent Origination**

**Summary:**
This report details the essential role of Dependent Origination (缘起法) and its detailed exposition, the Twelve Links (十二因缘), in achieving liberation from cyclic existence. The process requires direct realization through practices such as right knowledge (正智) and mindfulness, leading to the transcendence of erroneous views and the ultimate attainment of nirvana and the patience with the uncreated (无生法忍). The doctrine is fundamentally linked to the principles of the Middle Way and the equal nature of all phenomena (法性平等).

**Understanding the Causal Structure of Existence**
Dependent Origination is a fundamental doctrine that explains the causal chain of existence. The Twelve Links provide the specific, detailed content of this doctrine. The cycle of existence is initiated by the first link, ignorance (无明). The practice involves both sequential and reverse contemplation of these Twelve Links, and complete awareness of them is critical for reaching liberation.

**The Practice for Direct Realization and Liberation**
Practitioners must use profound contemplation and mindfulness to observe physical and mental phenomena. Through the application of right knowledge, they can comprehend Dependent Origination. This direct realization is identified as the exclusive path leading to nirvana. Such practice naturally dismantles all afflictions and bondage, ultimately resulting in the supreme bliss of the patience with the uncreated.

**The Outcomes of Thorough Comprehension**
A practitioner who fully comprehends Dependent Origination understands that all phenomena, including the five aggregates (五蕴), are devoid of an independent nature and are characterized by impermanence. This understanding allows one to overcome all delusion and darkness. Furthermore, such a person no longer gives rise to the extreme views of eternalism or nihilism.

**The Ultimate Realization of Equal Nature**
A thorough awakening to Dependent Origination leads to the understanding of the ultimate reality: the equal nature of all phenomena (法性平等). This principle indicates that all living beings possess the intrinsic potential for awakening. A sage who sees Dependent Origination accordingly abides authentically in this state of equality, having transcended all conceptual discrimination.
