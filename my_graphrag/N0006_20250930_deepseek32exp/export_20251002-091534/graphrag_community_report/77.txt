**Title: Cessation of Karmic Accumulation in the Liberated State**

**Summary**
This report details the fundamental shift in the relationship with karmic actions for individuals who have achieved spiritual liberation. A fully realized being, having reached the far shore of birth and death, ceases to create new karmic actions. Furthermore, any karmic momentum from past actions is rendered inert and incapable of producing further consequences, thereby ending the cycle of birth and death.

**Cessation of New Karmic Action**
An individual who has attained liberation, referred to as a sage or one who has reached the far shore of birth and death, stops accumulating and initiating new karmic actions. This state is characterized by a complete absence of attachment and volitional activity in all circumstances, leading to no new karmic seeds being sown.

**Inactivation of Past Karmic Momentum**
For a fully awakened being, not only is the creation of new karmic actions halted, but the residual karmic imprints from previous actions are also neutralized. This past karma loses its potential to grow or manifest, becoming powerless to affect the liberated state. The analogy is provided of a bird flying through the sky, which leaves no trace behind.

**Liberation from the Cycle of Birth and Death**
The perpetuation of the cycle of birth and death is directly fueled by karmic actions. The definitive end of this cycle is achieved upon reaching the far shore of birth and death, which is marked by the complete cessation of karmic accumulation and the exhaustion of existing karmic potential.
