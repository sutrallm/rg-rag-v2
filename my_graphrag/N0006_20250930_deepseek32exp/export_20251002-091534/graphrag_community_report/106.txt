**Report on the Attributes of a Perfectly Enlightened One**

**Summary**
This report details the definitive characteristics and attained state of a Perfectly Enlightened One. The analysis reveals that such an individual has achieved a permanent state of liberation, characterized by the complete eradication of mental defilements and an utterly unshakable mind. Furthermore, they are endowed with a specific and complete set of extraordinary qualities, including the Eighteen Exclusive Attributes, the Ten Powers, and the Four Fearlessnesses.

**The Attained State of Liberation**
A Perfectly Enlightened One is a saint who has achieved liberation. This liberation is not a temporary condition but is described as their fundamental state of being.

**Complete Eradication of Mental Defilements**
All afflictions and corruptions of the mind have been utterly destroyed within a Perfectly Enlightened One. This results in a consciousness that is compared to a diamond, indicating it is firm, indestructible, and cannot be disturbed.

**Possession of the Eighteen Exclusive Attributes**
A Perfectly Enlightened One is fully equipped with a set of eighteen distinctive qualities that are not shared with other spiritual practitioners. These attributes represent a unique and complete mastery.

**Mastery of the Ten Powers**
A Perfectly Enlightened One possesses ten specific powers. These powers signify a comprehensive and authoritative understanding or capability.

**Endowment with the Four Fearlessnesses**
A Perfectly Enlightened One is endowed with four types of fearlessness. This indicates a state of absolute confidence and absence of doubt in their own realization and teaching.
