**The Threefold Training System: A Path to Liberation**

**Summary**

The text describes a comprehensive spiritual training system consisting of three core, interdependent components: moral discipline (持戒), meditative concentration (修定), and wisdom (智慧). This system provides a sequential and progressive path for practitioners. The process begins with establishing moral purity as a foundational step, which then enables the development of concentration. This concentrated mind, in turn, becomes the basis for the ultimate emergence of wisdom. The complete and balanced cultivation of all three components is essential and leads to the eradication of all afflictions, the transcendence of the cycle of birth and death, and the ultimate attainment of liberation, the uncreated path (无漏圣道), and full awakening. A Saint (圣者) is one who has perfected this training.

**Sequential and Interdependent Nature of the Path**

The three components form a strict, progressive sequence. Moral discipline serves as the indispensable foundation for the entire path. It purifies actions and speech, providing the stability required for subsequent stages. Concentration is developed upon this foundation of moral purity and is the intermediate stage. Wisdom, the final component, arises directly from a mind that has been stabilized through concentration. The three are mutually reinforcing; none can be omitted, and each supports the development of the others within a complete cultivation structure.

**Moral Discipline as the Foundational Groundwork**

Moral discipline is the initial and foundational practice. It establishes behavioral norms and ethical purity, which purify the practitioner's body, speech, and mind. This discipline is likened to the earth that supports all things, as it is the root from which all virtuous qualities and merit for liberation naturally accumulate. A stable moral foundation is a prerequisite for successfully achieving meditative concentration and allows wisdom to grow naturally.

**Meditative Concentration as the Stabilizing Intermediate**

Concentration practice is the second component, built upon the groundwork of moral discipline. Its purpose is to collect, stabilize, and purify the mind through focused attention. This practice, which includes the application of mindfulness, serves to deepen the spiritual journey by calming mental disturbances. A stabilized and concentrated mind is the direct prerequisite for the arising of wisdom.

**Wisdom as the Culminating Goal**

Wisdom is the third and ultimate component of the system. It emerges from a mind that has been perfected through moral discipline and concentration. This wisdom involves a profound insight into the true nature of reality, which dispels ignorance. The attainment of such wisdom is the final objective of the entire cultivation process and is a necessary condition for achieving nirvana and complete liberation.

**Outcomes of Complete Cultivation**

The full and balanced practice of this threefold training leads to definitive spiritual outcomes. A practitioner who perfects it becomes a Saint (圣者), a true spiritual practitioner (梵行者) who has severed all afflictions and bonds at their root. Such a being achieves liberation, transcends the constraints of the three realms (三界), and attains the uncreated path (无漏圣道) and the ultimate fruit of the path to awakening (菩提道果). The virtue of a Saint is said to have a profound impact, capable of "shaking" the three realms.
