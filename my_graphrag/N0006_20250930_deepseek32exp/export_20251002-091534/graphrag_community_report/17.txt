**Report on the Path and State of Ultimate Liberation**

**Summary**
This report details the process and outcome of spiritual cultivation aimed at ultimate liberation. The path is defined by dedicated practice that culminates in a state of complete enlightenment. In this final state, the enlightened being transcends all dualities, karma, and the cycle of birth and death, abiding in a realm of innate purity and unattached wisdom.

**The Path of Cultivation as a Prerequisite**
Spiritual practice is the foundational process for achieving ultimate liberation. This cultivation is a form of spiritual accomplishment, and its progress is directed toward the final goal of liberation. The ultimate objective for a practitioner is to become a completely enlightened being, representing the highest achievement of this path.

**Wisdom as the Driving Force**
Wisdom is the central faculty that guides practitioners toward ultimate freedom. This liberation is specifically characterized by a release from all attachments, indicating that wisdom operates by eliminating ignorance and delusion, which are the root causes of bondage.

**Transcendence of Karmic Consequences**
A defining feature of ultimate liberation is freedom from the bondage of karma. Those who are ultimately liberated are no longer affected or constrained by karmic forces. This state is compared to a bird flying in the sky without leaving any traces, symbolizing that no new karmic actions are accumulated.

**Freedom from the Cycle of Birth and Death**
The ultimate state of liberation means moving beyond the cyclical process of birth and death. This represents a final and absolute release from the fundamental processes that govern conditioned existence.

**The State of Non-Abiding Purity**
The liberated being resides in a wondrous state of non-action, a natural and effortless abode. This condition of innate purity is symbolized by the lotus flower, which grows from mud yet remains unstained, representing the ability to remain pure amidst defilements without being affected by them.

**The Transcendence of Dualistic Concepts**
In the state of ultimate liberation, a practitioner surpasses all dualistic conceptions. They dwell in a wisdom of equality, a perspective that sees beyond the contrasts and oppositions that define conventional reality.
