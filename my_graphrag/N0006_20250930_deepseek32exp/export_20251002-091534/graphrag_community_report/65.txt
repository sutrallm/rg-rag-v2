**Title: Genuine Practice and Verification in Spiritual Cultivation**

**Summary**

This report outlines the principles and verification process for genuine spiritual cultivation. The core concept is "实证" (actual verification through practice), which measures progress by the reduction of afflictions and growth of wisdom, not by external phenomena. A genuine practitioner is defined by their unwavering mind, actions that align with the Dharma, and steadiness when facing favorable and adverse circumstances, which serve as the ultimate test of their realization.

**Key Findings**

**The Process of Actual Verification Through Practice**
The text defines a process where understanding is validated through direct experience and practice, moving beyond theoretical knowledge. This verification is an ongoing effort that requires repeatedly honing one's perspective in daily life. The outcome is genuine liberation, not a superficial attainment.

**Measuring Progress by the Reduction of Afflictions**
The primary standard for measuring progress in practice is the reduction of personal afflictions. The growth of wisdom is also quantified through this practical verification, establishing a clear, internal metric for development that is distinct from the pursuit of external signs or phenomena.

**The Defining Qualities of a Genuine Practitioner**
A genuine practitioner is an individual who has achieved actual verification through practice. They are characterized by a mind that remains constantly steady and is undisturbed by external appearances. Their words and actions naturally and consistently align with the principles of the Dharma.

**Favorable and Adverse Circumstances as the Test of Realization**
Favorable and adverse situations encountered in life are identified as the essential criterion for examining the effectiveness of one's practice. The key test is whether an individual's mind can maintain equanimity and stability when facing these circumstances, demonstrating the depth of their realization.
