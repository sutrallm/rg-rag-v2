**Title:** Bamboo Raft Metaphor: Necessity of Non-Attachment to Dharmas for Wisdom  

**Summary**  
Religious doctrines beyond their practical utility obstruct true wisdom. The bamboo raft metaphor demonstrates that teachings must be used temporarily and released once their purpose is fulfilled, emphasizing the critical role of non-attachment to dharmas.  

**Religious Doctrines Beyond Utility Hinder Wisdom**  
Attachment to religious teachings when they exceed their practical function impedes the attainment of true understanding. Holding onto doctrines longer than necessary creates barriers to deeper insight.  

**Bamboo Raft Requires Release of Dharmas After Use**  
The bamboo raft analogy illustrates that dharmas serve as transient tools; abandonment of attachment to them is essential once their purpose is achieved. This mirrors the necessity of moving beyond reliance on formal teachings to progress toward wisdom.
