**Greed as Root Cause of Suffering and Spiritual Obstacle**  

Greed is a mental defilement identified as part of the Three Poisons and Five Hindrances, generating distress, heat, and suffering when the mind is impure. It originates from attachment to material desires and incorrect perceptions of reality, symbolized by metaphors like heat haze, mirage, and snake. Its eradication requires mindfulness, impure contemplation, six roots purity practices, and understanding emptiness of all phenomena, which removes fetters and enables liberation.  

**Foundational Role as Root Cause of All Afflictions**  
Greed serves as the fundamental cause symbolized by a tree’s root, requiring complete severance to prevent rebirth of afflictions. It acts as a core component of the Three Poisons and Five Hindrances, directly responsible for suffering and obstructing spiritual progress.  

**Symbolic Depictions of Illusory Nature**  
Metaphors such as heat haze mistaken for water, mirage, and snake illustrate greed’s deceptive and unreal character. These representations emphasize its false perception as a source of emotional disturbance.  

**Obstruction in Spiritual Progress**  
Greed acts as a major barrier hindering meditation practice, blocking the holy path and liberation. Its presence impedes spiritual advancement and necessitates removal for progress.  

**Origins in Attachment and Misconception**  
Attachment to external phenomena, five desires, and gain-loss dynamics, coupled with incorrect perceptions of reality, initiate greed. Clinging behavior intensifies emotional disturbance and craving.  

**Remedial Practices for Eradication**  
Mindfulness, impure body contemplation, and six roots purity techniques effectively address greed during sensory experiences to eliminate attachment without recurrence.  

**Emptiness Recognition for Detachment**  
Understanding the emptiness of all phenomena fosters detachment from greed, enabling good dharma to manifest naturally.  

**Direct Cause of Universal Suffering**  
Greed directly produces states of suffering and distress among sentient beings, contributing to all forms of suffering through attachment.  

**Fetter Creation**  
Greed generates mental bindings that enslave beings, requiring eradication for spiritual freedom.
