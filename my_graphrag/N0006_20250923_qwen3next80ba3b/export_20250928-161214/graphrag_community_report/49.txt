SAGE Attributes and Reverence from Worldly and Celestial Realms

A SAGE has eradicated mental afflictions through spiritual practice, achieving transcendence of all suffering and liberation from the cycle of rebirth. This state, cultivated via wisdom, ethical conduct, diligence, and mental stillness, results in profound inner tranquility. Both celestial beings and worldly beings revere the SAGE for this spiritual purity, moral discipline, and enlightened understanding.

Afflictions and Suffering Connection  
Afflictions cause the suffering of birth and death inherent in samsara, as stated in "断尽一切烦恼、超越生死痛苦". Samsara inherently involves this suffering through its continuous cycle.

Wisdom’s Role in Eradication  
Wisdom contributes to the eradication of afflictions. It enables transcendence of suffering through understanding impermanence and non-attachment, forming a fundamental characteristic of enlightenment essential for overcoming pain.

Precepts’ Support for Ethical Conduct  
Precepts establish moral conduct foundations that aid in transcending suffering through disciplined practice. They support the eradication of afflictions as part of ethical discipline and spiritual conduct.

Diligence’s Contribution to Affliction Removal  
Diligence leads to eradication of afflictions through consistent spiritual practice, directly contributing to the complete removal of mental disturbances.

Stillness’s Foundation for Tranquility  
Stillness aids in the eradication of afflictions and provides the foundation for transcending suffering by removing mental agitation. SAGE embodies inner peace as a result of spiritual attainment and abides in unrivaled mental tranquility through cessation of disturbances.

Complete Liberation from Suffering  
Sages attain complete liberation from all suffering and the cycle of rebirth by eliminating all afflictions, as stated in "斷盡一切煩惱、超越生死苦痛". This transcends the cycle of samsara through attainment of mental stillness and non-attachment to all realms.

Reverence from Celestial Beings  
Celestial beings hold sages in the highest reverence for their spiritual purity and accomplishments. Heavenly realms hold SAGES in high esteem due to their virtuous and enlightened nature, and devas respect sages for spiritual attainment.

Worldly Respect for Sage’s Virtue  
Worldly beings respect and revere SAGES for their wisdom and ethical conduct, acknowledging the sage’s embodiment of moral discipline and awakened state.
