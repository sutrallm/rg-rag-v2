**Five Aggregates: Impermanence, Non-Self, and Liberation Path**  

The Five Aggregates—form, feeling, perception, mental formations, and consciousness—are impermanent components of temporary existence. Clinging to these as a self is the root of suffering. Understanding their illusory nature through non-attachment leads to liberation. Practitioners achieve awakening by observing impermanence during meditation and realizing non-self.  

**Each Aggregate's Transient Nature**  
Form is impermanent like foam, perception akin to a mirage, and mental formations hollow like a banana tree. Each component demonstrates impermanence and insubstantiality.  

**Clinging Aggregates as Self Causes Suffering**  
Attachment to the aggregates generates afflictions; this clinging is described as futile like grasping at the moon in water. Their impermanent nature makes them the fundamental cause of suffering when possessed.  

**Liberation via Dispassion**  
Dispassion toward the aggregates breaks attachment, achieving liberation. Non-attachment prevents influence from form and ends suffering.  

**Non-Self Realization**  
Analysis of aggregates reveals no inherent self. This understanding removes ego attachment, eliminating suffering's root cause.  

**Observational Contemplation**  
Practitioners engage in deep contemplation during meditation to observe impermanence and non-self. This practice is essential for awakening and transcending defilements.  

**Conditioned Suffering Basis**  
The aggregates form the basis of conditioned existence, inherently linked to all suffering when mistakenly clung to as self.  

**Six Roots Purity and Cessation**  
Six Roots Purity leads to emptiness of aggregates, resulting in cessation of suffering.  

**Saintly Beings' Perception**  
Saintly beings perceive the aggregates as impermanent illusions and transcend attachment through this realization.
