**Buddhist Practice Stages: CLEAR MIND AND SEE NATURE, CALMING AND VIPASSANA, and CONCENTRATION**  

CLEAR MIND AND SEE NATURE serves as the foundational prerequisite stage, with CALMING AND VIPASSANA building mental stability essential for CONCENTRATION practice. This sequential development progresses from initial mindfulness through advanced wisdom cultivation to ultimate liberation.  

**CLEAR MIND AND SEE NATURE as Foundational and Prerequisite Stage**  
CLEAR MIND AND SEE NATURE functions as the essential starting point within the Stages of Cultivation, needing to be established prior to any CALMING AND VIPASSANA work. It directly enables recognition of inherent true nature for subsequent practices.  

**CALMING AND VIPASSANA Enables CONCENTRATION Stability**  
CALMING AND VIPASSANA meditation develops the mental stability required for CONCENTRATION practice. This relationship demonstrates that CALMING AND VIPASSANA must precede advanced concentration efforts.  

**Sequential Progression Achieves Ultimate Liberation**  
Buddhist practice follows a defined sequence beginning with mindfulness practices (CLEAR MIND AND SEE NATURE), advancing through wisdom cultivation via CALMING AND VIPASSANA, and culminating in liberation. Each stage depends on the prior for effective progression.
