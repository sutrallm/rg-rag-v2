**SAGE's Liberation Path and Reverence from Worldly and Celestial Realms**

A SAGE achieves liberation by eliminating all mental afflictions through ethical discipline, meditation, mindfulness, and wisdom, transcending suffering and earning reverence from both worldly and celestial realms.

**Ethical Precepts Form Spiritual Foundation**  
Ethical conduct and mindfulness serve as the foundational practice supporting all subsequent spiritual development, establishing the basis for moral discipline and purification.  

**Mental Stillness Facilitates Affliction Removal**  
A calm and undisturbed mental state achieved through meditation and mindfulness is essential for cutting mental afflictions and removing disturbances, creating clarity for spiritual progress.  

**Wisdom Discerns Reality to End Afflictions**  
Wisdom discerns the true nature of reality, enabling the process of completely ending mental afflictions through deep insight and understanding.  

**Meditation Practices Develop Concentration**  
Focused meditative practices cultivate mental stability and clarity, directly contributing to the eradication of defilements and fostering inner tranquility.  

**Spiritual Virtue Earns Reverence Across Realms**  
Enlightened sages are held in high regard by celestial and worldly beings due to their attainment of moral purity, wisdom, and spiritual accomplishments.  

**Diligence Drives Spiritual Progress**  
Consistent and earnest effort is necessary to cultivate stillness, eradicate afflictions, and progress toward liberation through sustained practice.  

**Afflictions Generate Samsaric Suffering**  
Mental disturbances are the root cause of pain and dissatisfaction inherent in the cycle of rebirth and mortality within samsara.  

**Affliction Elimination Achieves Liberation**  
Complete removal of all afflictions results in freedom from suffering and transcendence beyond the cycle of birth and death.  

**Mind Cultivation Refines Mental Awareness**  
Active development of mental awareness and concentration through mindfulness and meditation supports overcoming afflictions and advancing wisdom.
