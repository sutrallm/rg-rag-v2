**Precepts, Concentration, and Wisdom for Cultivating Stillness and Ending Afflictions**  

Ethical discipline through precepts establishes foundational spiritual practice. The three trainings integrate precepts, concentration, and wisdom to cultivate mental stillness, which eradicates afflictions and transcends suffering. Sages and saintly beings consistently maintain this stillness through disciplined practice.  

**Ethical Discipline Establishes Foundational Practice**  
Precepts form the foundation for spiritual cultivation and support diligent cultivation of the mind.  

**Three Trainings Integrate Core Principles**  
Integrating ethical discipline, concentration, and wisdom cultivates mental stillness through the three trainings.  

**Concentration Cultivates Mental Stillness**  
Mental focus and stability through concentration cultivates stillness.  

**Mind Cultivation Achieves Stillness**  
Diligent mental development results in abiding in stillness.  

**Wisdom Discerns Reality to End Afflictions**  
Wisdom is essential for ending afflictions by discerning the true nature of reality.  

**Diligence Establishes Stillness**  
Consistent effort leads to mental stillness.  

**Stillness Eradicates Afflictions**  
Abiding in stillness enables the cutting off of mental afflictions.  

**Stillness Transcends Suffering**  
Stillness provides the foundation for transcending suffering by removing mental agitation.  

**Sages and Saintly Beings Cultivate Stillness**  
Sages cultivate mental calm and focus through stillness, and saintly beings abide in stillness through disciplined practice.
