**Buddhist Mindfulness and Meditation for Mental Stability and Liberation**  

The Buddhist tradition recognizes the mind as the cognitive foundation for mindfulness and meditation practices. It is observed as impermanent within the four foundations of mindfulness, requiring cultivation to stabilize restlessness and transcend ego-centric views. Practices like meditation and samadhi develop concentration and tranquility, symbolized by a rock in adversity and a candle in wind for distraction. Guarding the mind is essential for liberation, accomplished through non-attachment to external phenomena, present-moment awareness that dissipates afflictions, and diligent spiritual training.  

**Stable Mind Symbolized by Rock in Adversity**  
A trained mind remains steadfast during turbulent circumstances, represented by a rock symbolizing stability when facing danger.  

**Scattered Mind Compared to Candle in Wind**  
An unsteady mind prone to distractions fluctuates like a candle flickering in wind, necessitating mindfulness to achieve focus.  

**Non-Attachment to External Realms**  
The mind must avoid clinging to outer phenomena to maintain focus and prevent agitation.  

**Guarding the Mind Enables Liberation Mastery**  
Continuous protection of the mind is necessary to master the path of liberation, as stated in "唯有持续守护心念，才能真正掌握解脱之法".  

**Afflictions Dissipate Through Mindful Awareness**  
Present-moment awareness within the mind clears obstacles and afflictions, akin to light dispelling darkness.  

**Mind Observed for Impermanence in Four Foundations**  
The mind is examined as a core object in the four mindfulness practices, observed for impermanence to liberate from ego-centric views.  

**Meditation Develops Focused Clarity**  
Meditation cultivates concentration and clarity, described as "心志专注" during practice.  

**Saints Cultivate Mind Through Diligent Practice**  
Practitioners refine the mind via "勤修心念", emphasizing persistent effort in spiritual discipline.  

**Samadhi Cultivates Focus and Tranquility**  
Samadhi training develops both mental focus and inner peace through disciplined practice.  

**Heedful Guarding Prevents Negligence**  
Vigilant protection of the mind ensures awareness and prevents carelessness in spiritual pursuits.
