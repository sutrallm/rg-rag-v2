**Buddha's Lotus Metaphor: Purity from Impure Origins**  

The lotus symbolizes purity and enlightenment through its growth in muddy water while remaining unstained. The Buddha used this metaphor in teachings to convey the mind's ability to maintain clarity despite worldly impurities. Wet, impure earth represents defilements from which the lotus emerges untainted, highlighting spiritual transcendence of such conditions.  

Buddha Incorporated Lotus Symbolism in Sermons  
The Buddha used the lotus flower as a symbolic metaphor in his sermons to illustrate purity amid impurity.  

Impure Earth Symbolizes Worldly Defilements  
Wet, impure earth symbolizes worldly defilements and conditions from which the lotus emerges untainted.  

Lotus Represents Mind's Purity and Clarity  
The lotus serves as a metaphor to describe the purity and clarity of the mind nature, illustrating the ability to remain unstained by worldly conditions.  

Lotus Growth Demonstrates Spiritual Transcendence  
The lotus grows from muddy water but remains unstained, symbolizing spiritual transcendence of worldly impurities.
