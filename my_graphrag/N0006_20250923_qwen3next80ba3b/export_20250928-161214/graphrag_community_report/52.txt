**Ethical Precepts, Mindfulness, and Wisdom Leading to Supreme Peace and Liberation**  

The text outlines how ethical practices establish a foundation for wisdom, which, combined with mindfulness, achieves supreme inner calm and liberation. Cessation of afflictions directly produces supreme peace, while unobstructed wisdom delivers ultimate peace and freedom. Clear reality comprehension, free from delusions and hindrances, supports this progression.  

**Ethical Precepts Establish Foundation for Wisdom**  
Observing ethical precepts creates the foundation for developing unobstructed wisdom.  

**Mindfulness and Wisdom Attain Supreme Inner Calm and Liberation**  
The highest level of inner calm and liberation attained through complete mindfulness and wisdom.  

**Affliction Cessation Leads to Supreme Peace**  
Complete cessation of afflictions leads to supreme peace.  

**Unobstructed Wisdom Results in Ultimate Peace and Liberation**  
Unobstructed wisdom results in the highest level of inner peace and liberation.  

**Reality Understanding Free from Delusions and Hindrances**  
Clear and unimpeded reality comprehension, free from delusions and hindrances.
