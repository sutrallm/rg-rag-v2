**Mandatory Removal of Persons and Geographic Locations**  

The specified requirements mandate the total exclusion of all persons and geographic locations. Identical deletion instructions apply to both categories, ensuring complete removal of biographical and geographical references.  

**Uniform Deletion Rules for Humans and Locations**  
All references to individuals and geographic sites must be entirely excluded with identical processing protocols. There is no differentiation between the two categories in the deletion instructions.  

**Complete Elimination of Biographical and Geographic Elements**  
Every instance of person-related biographical data and location-based geographical information must be entirely removed. No exceptions are permitted for these reference types.
