**Buddhist Liberation Pathways: Core Practices and Realizations**

The text describes liberation as the ultimate state of freedom from samsara, suffering, mental afflictions, desire, and attachment, synonymous with Nirvana and the ultimate goal of Buddhist practice. It is achieved through spiritual practices including the Threefold Training, mindfulness, transcendent wisdom, and the realization of impermanence and non-self. Key elements involve overcoming attachment, eliminating afflictions, diligence, compassion, and adhering to ethical discipline, concentration, and truth-based insight to transcend rebirth and suffering.

**Liberation Synonymous with Nirvana**  
LIBERATION is the state of complete freedom from samsara, suffering, mental afflictions, desire, and attachment. It is synonymous with Nirvana and represents the ultimate goal of Buddhist practice. Liberation and Nirvana refer to the ultimate state of freedom from samsara, where suffering and rebirth cease entirely.  

**Awakening as Reality Realization**  
AWAKENING is a state of profound spiritual insight and realization characterized by the profound realization of ultimate truth or the true nature of reality, which leads to liberation from the cycle of samsara and is essential for achieving liberation from suffering.  

**Threefold Training Core Path**  
The Three Trainings is the threefold practice of ethical discipline (precepts), mental concentration, and wisdom that form the core path to enlightenment in Buddhism. The Threefold Training is the systematic path through which practitioners achieve liberation from suffering and the cycle of rebirth.  

**Bliss as Liberation Outcome**  
A state of profound happiness and joy experienced as a result of spiritual liberation. Liberation inherently produces BLISS as its natural outcome. Practitioners experience BLISS upon attaining spiritual liberation and inner peace.  

**Transcendent Wisdom Realizing Reality**  
Transcendent wisdom that realizes the true nature of reality and leads to enlightenment. PRAJNA WISDOM directly leads to LIBERATION by realizing the true nature of reality.  

**Samsara Cycle of Rebirth**  
The cycle of death and rebirth in Buddhist cosmology, from which liberation is attained. Samsara is the cycle of rebirth, and liberation is the state of freedom that transcends Samsara.  

**Affliction Cessation Equals Liberation**  
Conditioned by the cessation of afflictions and attachment, a core outcome of Buddhist practice. The complete cessation of afflictions is synonymous with achieving liberation. AFFLICTIONS perpetuate the cycle of REBIRTH, and their cessation enables transcendence.  

**Meditation for Mental Clarity and Insight**  
Meditation is a key practice leading to liberation from suffering and samsara through the achievement of mental clarity and insight. Calm abiding and insight is the progressive path leading to the attainment of liberation.  

**Spiritual Wisdom Direct Path**  
Spiritual WISDOM directly leads to LIBERATION from samsara and suffering by revealing the true nature of reality and providing the necessary insight, as stated in the text: "唯有真实智照才能导向解脱".  

**Practitioner's Path Through Practice**  
A PRACTITIONER strives for LIBERATION through dedicated spiritual practice, which involves understanding the true nature of reality and overcoming craving, thereby attaining ultimate liberation.  

**Mindfulness Guarding the Mind**  
Mindfulness enables mastery of liberation, as stated in 'Only by continuously guarding the mind's attention can one truly grasp the path to liberation.' Mindfulness is essential for grasping the path to liberation from suffering. Guarding the MIND is essential for mastering the path of LIBERATION, as stated in "唯有持续守护心念，才能真正掌握解脱之法".  

**Affliction Elimination for Liberation**  
Liberation is attained through the complete elimination of all mental AFFLICTIONS via realization of truth, mindfulness, and wisdom, with overcoming AFFLICTIONS leading to the realization of LIBERATION.  

**Liberation as Suffering Cessation**  
SUFFERING and LIBERATION are related such that liberation is the state of complete freedom from suffering, entailing its cessation. Liberation is the attainment of complete freedom from suffering.  

**Diligence as Primary Cause**  
Diligence enables practitioners to attain liberation from the cycle of rebirth and suffering, as diligent practice is a primary cause leading to this attainment.  

**Ethical Precepts Necessity**  
PRECEPTS are essential for achieving liberation through ethical discipline and mental purity.  

**Attachment as Obstacle**  
Attachment is a major obstacle to liberation from samsara, and overcoming it is essential for achieving this liberation. Non-attachment results in liberation. Breaking attachments to all desires and states leads directly to liberation.  

**Impermanence Understanding for Liberation**  
IMPERMANENCE and LIBERATION are related such that observing or understanding impermanence directly leads to liberation from suffering.  

**Sages Attaining Liberation**  
Sages attain complete LIBERATION from all suffering and the cycle of rebirth by eliminating all afflictions, as stated in the phrase '斷盡一切煩惱、超越生死苦痛'. A saint has fully attained liberation through the eradication of all suffering.  

**Greed Elimination Achieving Liberation**  
GREED obstructs the path to LIBERATION, and eliminating GREED achieves LIBERATION.  

**Detachment from Five Aggregates**  
LIBERATION is achieved through detachment from attachment to the FIVE AGGREGATES.  

**Concentration for Inner Peace**  
Concentration practice leads directly to inner peace and liberation. Meditative concentration is a foundational path leading to ultimate liberation from suffering.  

**Path of Non-Self Insight**  
Insight into Non-Self is a critical path to achieving Liberation, as it dismantles the root of ego-based clinging. Realizing non-self is essential for attaining liberation from suffering.  

**Three Poisons Elimination Necessary**  
Eliminating the three poisons is necessary for achieving liberation from suffering.  

**Heedlessness Obstruction**  
HEEDLESSNESS obstructs the attainment of LIBERATION by perpetuating ignorance and suffering.  

**Effortful Practice Fundamental**  
Effortful practice is fundamental to achieving liberation from suffering.  

**Truth Essential for Liberation**  
TRUTH is essential for achieving LIBERATION.  

**Breaking Fetters for Ultimate Liberation**  
Breaking all FETTERS achieves ultimate LIBERATION.  

**Compassion as Vital Pathway**  
COMPASSION serves as a vital pathway toward achieving LIBERATION.  

**Right Path Leads to Liberation**  
Adhering to the RIGHT PATH leads directly to LIBERATION from suffering.  

**Dispassion Necessary Condition**  
DISPASSION is a necessary condition for achieving LIBERATION.  

**Calm Abiding and Insight Progression**  
Calm abiding and insight is the progressive path leading to the attainment of liberation.  

**Enlightenment Ending Suffering Cycle**  
Enlightenment results in liberation, ending the cycle of suffering and rebirth.  

**Liberation Transcends Rebirth**  
LIBERATION represents transcendence over the cycle of REBIRTH. Liberation transcends the cycle of birth and death.  

**Attainment Yields Respect**  
Attainment of liberation results in respect from all three realms of existence.  

**Attachment to Awakening Hindrance**  
Attachment to the results of AWAKENING hinders true realization and progress.  

**Saints Practice Three Trainings**  
A saint embodies and fully practices the three trainings of precepts, concentration, and wisdom.  

**Deep Concentration Enables Wisdom**  
Deep CONCENTRATION enables the arising of PRAJNA WISDOM through sustained insight.  

**Three Trainings Cultivate Stillness**  
THREE TRAININGS cultivate STILLNESS by integrating ethical discipline, concentration, and wisdom.  

**Delusion Transformed via Insight**  
DELUSION is transformed into awakening through insight into afflictions' causes.
