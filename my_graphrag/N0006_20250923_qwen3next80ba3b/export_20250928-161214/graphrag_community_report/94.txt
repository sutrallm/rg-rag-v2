**Title**  
Breaking Attachment, Formless Dharma Gate, and Non-Abiding Mind for Liberation from Suffering  

**Summary**  
Breaking Attachment through wisdom of impermanence and practices like Formless Dharma Gate and Non-Abiding Mind addresses afflictions, ends suffering and cyclical existence, and achieves mental freedom. These methods avoid clinging to all phenomena, including enlightenment goals, and cultivate states of mental stability and serenity.  

**Forming All Clinging, Including to Enlightenment Goals**  
This process involves relinquishing attachments to any state or phenomenon, as indicated by the source text, which directly supports mental liberation by preventing fixation on even spiritual aspirations.  

**Formless Dharma Gate Targets Conceptual Attachments to Treat Afflictions**  
This practice method specifically addresses and dismantles attachments to conceptual forms, functioning as a direct approach to alleviating afflictions.  

**Non-Abiding Mind Practice Eliminates Afflictions Through Non-Clinging**  
By not abiding in any state or phenomenon, this method actively removes afflictions through cultivating a mind free from attachment.  

**Impermanence Recognition Facilitates Release from Transient Attachments**  
Wisdom in perceiving the transient nature of phenomena enables practitioners to break free from attachments to impermanent states, a critical element of the process.  

**Unmoving Mind Nature Establishes Natural Serenity**  
When the mind remains unshaken, external circumstances and internal thoughts naturally become serene, creating a stable mental state as per the source text.  

**Formless Dharma Gate Leads to Unconditioned State Beyond Cyclic Existence**  
This practice method ultimately achieves a state free from all conditioned phenomena, representing liberation from suffering and rebirth cycles.  

**Non-Abiding Mind Cultivates Unmoving Mind Nature**  
This practice serves as the method to develop the Unmoving Mind Nature that is free from fixation, forming a key link between practice and outcome.  

**Breaking Attachment Directly Mitigates Root Causes of Suffering**  
Through mental liberation, this process tackles the foundational sources of all suffering, achieving cessation via non-attachment.  

**Ultimate Realization Ends Suffering and Rebirth Cycle**  
The culmination of truth realization brings about the cessation of suffering and the end of the rebirth cycle, as this is the ultimate state described in the text.
