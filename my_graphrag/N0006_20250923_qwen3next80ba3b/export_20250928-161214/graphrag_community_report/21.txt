**Mind Nature Purity and the Three Realms**  

The three realms of desire, form, and formlessness form the framework of existence in Buddhist cosmology. The mind's pure essence, symbolized by a lotus and wish-fulfilling jewel, naturally dispels ignorance, illuminates all directions, and perceives phenomena as transient and illusory. Liberation brings respect across all realms, while precepts and pure actions sustain mental clarity. Recollection of the Three Jewels (Buddha, Dharma, Sangha) maintains this purity, and the mind's clarity directly influences sentient beings' states of peace or suffering while universally dispelling darkness.  

**Three Realms Structure and Navigation**  
The three domains of desire, form, and formlessness represent inhabited realms where beings exist, traversed by the mind without attachment or obstruction.  

**Lotus as Purity Symbol**  
The lotus metaphor describes the mind nature’s inherent cleanliness and clarity.  

**Wish-Fulfilling Jewel Symbolism**  
The wish-fulfilling jewel illustrates the mind’s capacity to dispel ignorance and radiate light across all directions.  

**Phenomena as Illusory and Transient**  
All experiences are perceived through the mind’s clarity as illusory and transient.  

**Three Jewels in Daily Recollection**  
Buddha, Dharma, and Sangha are continuously recalled as refuge objects in practice.  

**Liberation's Resulting Respect**  
Attainment of liberation yields respect from all three realms of existence.  

**Precepts for Mental Stability**  
Diligent observance of precepts purifies and stabilizes the mind nature.  

**Inherent Ignorance Dispelling**  
The mind nature’s pure essence naturally eliminates ignorance.  

**Purity Influencing Sentient States**  
The mind nature’s purity or impurity directly determines whether sentient beings experience peace or suffering.  

**Clarity Dissipates All Darkness**  
The mind’s clarity eradicates darkness throughout all realms.  

**Pure Actions Upheld for Purity**  
Cultivating wholesome deeds sustains and refines the mind nature’s purity.  

**Universal Illumination Scope**  
The mind nature’s illumination extends to all ten directions of existence.
