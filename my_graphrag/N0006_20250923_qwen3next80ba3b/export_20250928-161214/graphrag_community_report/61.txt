**Meditation Techniques for Scattered Minds: Boiling Water Metaphor and Breath Counting**  

A scattered mental state during meditation, symbolized by boiling water, disrupts concentration and prevents clear reflection. Meditation practices, specifically breath counting, address this disturbance to stabilize focus.  

**Boiling Water Symbolizes Scattered State**  
The scattered mental condition is metaphorically depicted as boiling water, illustrating an agitated state incapable of clear reflection and hindering meditation.  

**Breath Counting Restores Focus**  
Counting breaths is a core meditation method to pacify scattered mental states and restore focus.  

**Meditation Aims to Overcome Scattered Mind**  
The purpose of meditation is to overcome the scattered mind through focused practice.
