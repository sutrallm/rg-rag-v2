**Affliction Roots and Treatment Strategies**  

Effective treatment of mental suffering requires identifying the underlying causes of afflictions. Root cause analysis is essential before applying countermeasures, as understanding these origins enables targeted elimination of afflictions. Treatment strategies directly address affliction removal through evidence-based interventions.  

**Afflictions Origin from Underlying Causes**  
The text states the roots of afflictions are the specific underlying causes that directly produce afflictions. This establishes a clear causal relationship where identifying these roots is foundational to addressing mental suffering.  

**Treatment Requires Root Cause Investigation**  
To apply effective treatments, the underlying causes of mental suffering must be investigated. Without this step, treatments cannot be appropriately designed or applied, as the text emphasizes investigation as a prerequisite for successful intervention.  

**Countermeasures Apply After Root Understanding**  
Methods to counteract and eliminate afflictions are only used once their roots are understood. This dependency ensures countermeasures are precisely tailored to the source of afflictions, rather than addressing surface symptoms.  

**Treatment Targets Affliction Elimination**  
Treatment is defined as the application of methods specifically aimed at counteracting and eliminating afflictions. This objective-oriented approach focuses exclusively on resolving the afflictions themselves through structured intervention.
