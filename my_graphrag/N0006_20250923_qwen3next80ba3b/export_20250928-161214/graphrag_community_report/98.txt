**Greed and Anger as Obstacles to Spiritual Progress**  

**Summary**  
Greed and anger are fundamental obstacles classified under the Three Poisons and Five Hindrances, directly contributing to suffering and hindering spiritual development. Removing these afflictions leads to liberation, manifests Good Dharma, and requires specific contemplative practices to address their root causes and manifestations. Both disrupt mental clarity, obstruct progress along the Holy Path, and must be eradicated to advance spiritually.  

**Core Classification of Greed and Anger**  
Greed and anger are both part of the Three Poisons and Five Hindrances, causing distress, disrupting mental stability, and obstructing spiritual progress and mental clarity.  

**Greed Metaphors: Tree Root, Snake, Mirage, and Heat Haze**  
Greed is symbolized by a tree root requiring severing, a snake, a mirage, and heat haze mistaken for water, illustrating its illusory and obstructive nature.  

**Anger as a Hostile Force**  
Anger manifests as hostility and ill will, identified as a snake metaphor and part of the Three Poisons and Five Hindrances, disrupting inner peace and mental stability during meditation practice.  

**Liberation through Greed Elimination**  
Greed obstructs the path to liberation; its removal directly achieves liberation and enables Good Dharma to manifest naturally.  

**Root Causes of Greed**  
Greed arises from attachment to the Five Desires, external phenomena, clinging, misconceptions about reality, and attachment to gain and loss.  

**Techniques to Overcome Greed**  
Mindfulness practice, impure contemplation of the body, and insight into the emptiness of all phenomena eliminate attachment and desire.  

**Consequences of Greed**  
Greed directly causes suffering, fetters, and contributes to all suffering among sentient beings through attachment.  

**Removal of Anger and Manifestation of Good Dharma**  
Removing anger allows Good Dharma to manifest naturally and obstructs the Holy Path when unaddressed.  

**Wisdom Dispelling Ignorance**  
Sunrise dispelling darkness represents removing ignorance through clarity and wisdom, enabling the natural emergence of Good Dharma.  

**Preventing Greed During Sensory Input**  
Six Roots Purity prevents the arising of greed during sensory experiences.
