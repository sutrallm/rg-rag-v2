**Buddhist Threefold Training: Ethical Discipline, Meditation, and Wisdom as Path to Enlightenment**  

**Summary**  
The Buddhist Threefold Training integrates ethical discipline (Precepts), meditation, and wisdom as a systematic path toward enlightenment. Practitioners engage with these components to overcome mental afflictions, cultivate inner peace, transcend ignorance and suffering, and achieve liberation from the cycle of rebirth. The combined practice of these elements also subdues inner demons, as documented in Buddhist scripture.  

**Ethical Discipline as Foundational Element**  
Precepts form the ethical foundation of Buddhist practice as one of the three essential components of the Threefold Training. This moral discipline establishes the basis for spiritual development and guides practitioners in cultivating right conduct.  

**Meditation's Role in Cultivating Inner Peace**  
Meditation serves as one of the three core components of the Threefold Training, enabling mental focus and inner peace. It provides the practice through which practitioners stabilize the mind and develop deeper awareness.  

**Wisdom's Transcendence of Ignorance**  
Wisdom constitutes one of the three fundamental components of the Threefold Training, providing insight to transcend ignorance and suffering. It allows practitioners to see reality clearly and dismantle delusions that perpetuate cyclic existence.  

**Systematic Path to Liberation**  
The Threefold Training is the systematic method through which practitioners achieve liberation from suffering and the cycle of rebirth. This structured approach ensures progress from ethical conduct to mental cultivation and ultimately to profound understanding.  

**Overcoming Mental Afflictions Through Integrated Practice**  
Mental afflictions and negative states must be overcome through ethical discipline, meditation, and wisdom to attain peace. These three elements work synergistically to address the root causes of psychological and existential distress.  

**Subduing Inner Demons Through Unified Components**  
The combination of Precepts, Meditation, and Wisdom subdues inner demons, as stated in the teaching "具足戒定慧者，能如狮子威猛般降伏内心诸魔." This integration empowers practitioners to confront and resolve internal obstacles with unwavering strength.
