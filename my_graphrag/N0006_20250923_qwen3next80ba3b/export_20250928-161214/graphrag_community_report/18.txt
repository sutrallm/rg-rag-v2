**Sunrise Metaphor and Greed/Anger Removal Enabling Good Dharma**  

**Summary**  
The text describes how eliminating greed and anger directly allows Good Dharma to manifest naturally. Sunrise symbolizes the dispelling of darkness (ignorance) through clarity and wisdom. General obstacle removal fosters spiritual advancement by generating positive teachings.  

**Greed and Anger Removal Enables Dharma**  
Removing these emotions facilitates the natural manifestation of Good Dharma.  

**Sunrise Symbolizes Ignorance Dispelling**  
Sunrise dispelling darkness represents the removal of ignorance by clarity and wisdom.  

**Obstacle Removal Promotes Spiritual Advancement**  
Eliminating obstacles generates positive teachings that advance spiritual progress.
