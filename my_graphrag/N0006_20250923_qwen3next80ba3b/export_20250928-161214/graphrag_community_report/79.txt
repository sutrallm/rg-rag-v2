**Ethical Precepts and Saintly Attainment for Liberation and Reverence**  

Moral precepts serve as the indispensable basis for spiritual cultivation, facilitating mental tranquility, elimination of afflictions, and wisdom development. Saintly practitioners embody these principles, achieving complete liberation from suffering and earning universal respect from all beings, including celestial entities. Adherence to precepts averts calamities, fosters inner peace, and is vital for transcendence of rebirth and attainment of enlightenment.  

**Foundational Role of Precepts in Spiritual Practice**  
Precepts form the essential ethical foundation necessary for developing successful meditation practice and establish a stable base for spiritual growth, supporting mental concentration and wisdom cultivation.  

**Prevention of Harm Through Ethical Conduct**  
Observing moral precepts avoids harmful actions, preventing severe misfortune and catastrophic events, and promotes inner tranquility by reducing mental afflictions.  

**Saintly Liberation and Universal Respect**  
Saints have fully attained liberation through the eradication of mental afflictions and transcendence of suffering, embodying virtue and wisdom, which earns reverence from all sentient beings and celestial entities.  

**Interdependence of Precepts and Wisdom**  
PRECEPTS and WISDOM together form essential components of the path to liberation; PRECEPTS provide ethical discipline for cultivating WISDOM, and maintaining these precepts fosters deeper wisdom and mental clarity.  

**Celestial Honor for Spiritual Attainment**  
HEAVENLY BEINGS respect and honor those who uphold moral discipline and cultivate wisdom, recognizing spiritual realization and the transcendence of suffering as reasons for deep reverence.
