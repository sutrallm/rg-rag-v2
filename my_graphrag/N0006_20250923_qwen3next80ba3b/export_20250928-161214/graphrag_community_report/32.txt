Buddhist Content Transformation: PERSON, LOCATION, and STANZA Removal Requirements  

Processing Buddhist content necessitates complete removal of PERSON references, geographical location details, and STANZA sections. Doctrinal content must remain intact, with processing steps structured using numbered titles as per instructions.  

Person References Elimination  
All human individuals, specific simile examples like 'like a person enters the mountain', and explicitly listed persons must be entirely removed during step 1 of processing.  

Geographical Location Omission  
References to cities, countries, regions, and other geographical places must be excluded from processing as specified in the instructions.  

STANZA Section Deletion  
Poetic verse sections positioned at the end of Buddhist content must be fully removed according to the fifth processing step.  

Doctrinal Content Integrity  
Original doctrinal details must be preserved exactly as instructed without alteration.  

Structured Step Numbering  
Processing steps require numbered titles to ensure clear and organized structure during transformation.
