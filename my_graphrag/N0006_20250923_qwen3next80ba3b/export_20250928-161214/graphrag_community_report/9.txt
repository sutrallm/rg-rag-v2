**Threefold Training of Precepts, Meditation, and Wisdom: Path to Liberation and Celestial Honor**  

**Summary**  
The threefold training of precepts, meditation, and wisdom constitutes the essential framework for spiritual development. Mastery of these elements achieves liberation from suffering and eliminates all afflictions. Sages who embody this training are honored by celestial beings due to their enlightened state.  

**Foundational Spiritual Structure**  
Precepts, meditation, and wisdom form the core elements of the spiritual training system, each representing a non-negotiable component required for progress in this practice.  

**Liberation through Integrated Practice**  
Complete integration of precepts, meditation, and wisdom serves as the sole pathway to freedom from suffering, enabling sages to eradicate all afflictions inherent in existence.  

**Celestial Recognition of Enlightenment**  
Heavenly deities express profound respect for sages whose enlightenment stems directly from the full realization of precepts, meditation, and wisdom, affirming the spiritual authority achieved through this process.
