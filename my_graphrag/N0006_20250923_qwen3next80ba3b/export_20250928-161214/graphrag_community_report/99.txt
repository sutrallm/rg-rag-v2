**Three Poisons and Compassion Pathways to Liberation**  

The fundamental causes of suffering are greed, hatred, and delusion, which must be eradicated for liberation. Compassion, cultivated through the Four Immeasurable Hearts, directly addresses hatred and attachment. Sensory purity, precepts, concentration, and patience are key practices for eliminating these root afflictions, while attachment to sensory objects and teachings perpetuates suffering. Understanding the Five Aggregates and transforming delusion through wisdom are critical for ending cyclic distress.  

**Three Poisons Underpin Suffering**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion serve as the core mental defilements driving all suffering. Each manifests uniquely: greed through attachment, hatred through aversion, and delusion through ignorance. Their elimination is essential for liberation, as they are the primary sources of mental afflictions and bondage.  

**Compassion Reduces Hatred and Clinging**  
Compassion, defined as the universal wish to alleviate others' suffering, diminishes hatred and dissolves self-centered attachment. This practice, rooted in the Four Immeasurable Hearts, directly facilitates liberation by replacing harmful intentions with empathetic action.  

**Sensory Purity Prevents Afflictions**  
Maintaining purity of the six sense organs stops greed, hatred, and delusion from arising during sensory interactions. This practice culminates in the emptiness of the Five Aggregates, leading to the cessation of suffering.  

**Precepts and Discipline Advance Liberation**  
Adherence to precepts, cultivating patience to avoid retaliation, and developing concentration all support the removal of the Three Poisons. Persistent diligence in these practices is necessary for achieving mental clarity and liberation.  

**Attachment to Sensory Objects and Teachings Fuels Suffering**  
Clinging to external sensory experiences and written teachings creates bondage and perpetuates suffering. Addressing this attachment directly targets the root causes of cyclic distress in existence.  

**Five Aggregates Structure Existential Suffering**  
The Five Aggregates form the foundation of conditioned existence, generating all suffering. Their emptiness, achieved through sensory purity, is the pathway to ending this cycle.  

**Wisdom Transforms Delusion**  
Delusion arises from misperceiving reality, contributing to suffering through ignorance. Wisdom eradicates this delusion by addressing its underlying causes, transforming confusion into awakening and resolving afflictions.
