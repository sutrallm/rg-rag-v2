**Mind Management Principle: Core Buddhist Text Processing Requirements**  

This report outlines essential requirements for transforming Buddhist scriptures into structured vernacular formats while preserving doctrinal integrity. It emphasizes converting metaphors and dialogues into explicit teachings, removing specific names and poetic verses, structuring content with numbered titles, and highlighting diligence to counter spiritual obstacles.  

**Metaphor Conversion to Direct Doctrine**  
Figurative expressions in Buddhist texts are converted into direct Dharma explanations during processing to illustrate deeper teachings.  

**Dialogue Transformation into Doctrinal Statements**  
Conversational exchanges, such as those between the Buddha and Ananda, are processed into straightforward doctrinal explanations.  

**Numbered Titles for Thematic Organization**  
Numerical ordering structures teachings into clear thematic sections to enhance comprehension and reference.  

**Person and Location Name Removal**  
Identifiable personal names and geographical identifiers are removed from transformed text as part of processing requirements.  

**Poetic Verse Deletion**  
Poetic verses at text endings are designated for deletion during processing.  

**Effort for Spiritual Advancement**  
Diligent and persistent exertion is essential for overcoming hindrances, maintaining meditation progress, and achieving liberation; absence of effort causes stagnation.  

**Death Demon King Symbolism**  
A symbolic representation of death, temptation, and spiritual danger, hindering progress when practitioners lack diligence.  

**Recklessness as Practice Hindrance**  
Negligence and laxity in practice impede spiritual progress and increase vulnerability to spiritual threats.  

**AFICTIONS as Suffering Source**  
AFICTIONS are the primary cause of suffering, creating mental distress and cyclic existence.  

**Dual Meditation Practices**  
Calm abiding and insight meditation collectively develop mental stability and wisdom for practitioners.  

**Monastic Practice Structure**  
Monks receive direct teachings, uphold strict discipline, and engage in meditation and study to attain enlightenment.  

**Doctrinal Content Preservation**  
Core religious principles must be accurately retained during text transformation.
