**Non-Lax Behavioral Practices for Mental Purity in Spiritual Practice**  

Consistent application of vigilant and non-lax behavioral practices sustains mental purity within spiritual practice by upholding pure actions without negligence.  

**Vigilant Commitment**  
A state of vigilant attentiveness and commitment to practice, free from negligence or complacency.  

**Pure Actions for Mind Refinement**  
Cultivating pure actions is a key practice to uphold and refine the mind nature's purity.  

**Non-Lax Consistency**  
Non-laxity ensures consistent and mindful cultivation of pure actions without negligence.
