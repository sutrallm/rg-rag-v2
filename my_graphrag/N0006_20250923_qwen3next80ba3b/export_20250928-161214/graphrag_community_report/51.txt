**Saints' Qualities and Heavenly Beings' Reverence**  

Saints attain liberation through ethical discipline, wisdom, and compassion, embodying moral purity, inner tranquility, and profound understanding. Their spiritual accomplishments earn universal respect, particularly from Heavenly Beings who honor their realized virtues and transcendence of suffering.  

**Moral Precepts Establish Reverence**  
Saints strictly uphold ethical conduct, maintaining purity through disciplined practice. This adherence forms the foundation for universal respect, as moral discipline is recognized as essential for generating admiration from all sentient beings.  

**Wisdom Achieves Liberation**  
Profound wisdom enables Saints to eradicate mental afflictions and transcend suffering by realizing the true nature of reality. This leads to complete freedom from cyclic existence, culminating in Nirvana as the ultimate state of liberation.  

**Tranquility Through Meditative Practice**  
Through focused meditation and Samadhi, Saints cultivate inner calm and stability, free from external distractions. This state of tranquility arises directly from disciplined mindfulness and sustained spiritual practice.  

**Celestial Honor for Saintly Realization**  
Heavenly Beings specifically revere Saints for their virtues, wisdom, and mastery over suffering. This honor stems from recognition of their fully realized spiritual attainment and embodiment of the path to enlightenment.  

**Integrated Threefold Training**  
Saints seamlessly integrate ethical discipline, meditative concentration, and wisdom into unified practice. Each component reinforces the others, resulting in complete mastery of the mind and liberation from all forms of bondage.
