**Elephant Metaphor: Mind Taming and Parable Perception Limitations**  

**Summary**  
The elephant symbolizes the mind, requiring disciplined meditation for control. In the blind men’s parable, individuals touching single parts of the elephant misperceive the whole, demonstrating how limited sensory input creates incomplete understanding of reality.  

**Meditative Discipline for Mind Taming**  
The elephant serves as a metaphor for the mind, emphasizing that meditation is necessary to tame its nature through disciplined practice.  

**Fragmented Sensory Input Yields Incomplete Comprehension**  
In the blind men’s parable, physical interaction with isolated sections of the elephant results in misperception of the entire entity, illustrating how restricted sensory data prevents full comprehension of reality.
