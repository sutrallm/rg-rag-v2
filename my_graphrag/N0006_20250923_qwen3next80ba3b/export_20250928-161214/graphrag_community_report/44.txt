**Dialogue and Metaphor Conversion to Direct Buddhist Doctrine**  

All conversational exchanges and quoted speech, including the Buddha's dialogue with Shariputra, must be rephrased into explicit doctrinal statements. Metaphorical narratives and parables are converted into direct Dharma teachings. Essential doctrinal details remain intact during processing, with all non-direct elements removed for clarity.  

**Dialogue Conversion to Direct Exposition**  
Conversational exchanges and quoted speech require transformation into explicit non-conversational doctrinal explanations. Original dialogue format is entirely eliminated, ensuring teachings are presented as direct statements.  

**Metaphorical Content Transformation**  
Narrative metaphors, analogies, and parables in original materials must be explicitly converted into direct Dharma teachings. This removes allegorical and poetic language to maintain instructional clarity.  

**Doctrinal Detail Preservation**  
All essential doctrinal information must remain unchanged through processing. Transformations from dialogues or metaphors do not alter the substance of core teachings, only the presentation format.
