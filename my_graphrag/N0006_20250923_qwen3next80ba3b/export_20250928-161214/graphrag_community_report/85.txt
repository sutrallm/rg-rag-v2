**Buddhist Meditation and the Path to Liberation from Afflictions**  

Buddhist meditation forms a core practice within the Threefold Training, designed to overcome distractions, mental afflictions, and suffering. Rooted in mindfulness and concentration, it addresses the three poisons—greed, hatred, and delusion—as primary causes of cyclic existence. Through ethical precepts, focused attention, and wisdom, practitioners eliminate afflictions to achieve inner peace, tranquility, and liberation, symbolized by metaphors such as the lotus and pool of still water.  

**Afflictions as Root Source of Suffering**  
Mental defilements termed afflictions, including the three root poisons—greed, hatred, and delusion—along with disturbances like anger, sloth, restlessness, and doubt, cause suffering, bind beings to cyclic existence, obscure reality, and perpetuate distress. Their cessation leads to transcendence of suffering, as indicated by phrases describing the eradication of afflictions to overcome pain.  

**Mindfulness as Foundational to Meditation Practice**  
Meditation cultivates mindfulness as essential for maintaining present-moment awareness and focused attention. The Chinese phrase '心专注且保持正念时，能远离一切杂念' demonstrates that right mindfulness removes distractions. This awareness dissolves afflictions through sustained present-focused observation.  

**Ethical Precepts as Essential Foundation for Meditation**  
Ethical precepts form the necessary foundation for effective meditation. Observing pure precepts enables complete elimination of mental afflictions through disciplined conduct. Adherence to moral guidelines nurtures inner peace and mental serenity by reducing disturbances.  

**Overcoming Distractions Through Focused Attention**  
Scattered mental states, symbolized by boiling water, disrupt concentration. Techniques such as breath counting stabilize the mind, while a tranquil environment free from external stimuli ensures effective practice. Maintaining focused awareness reduces dispersion and cultivates calmness.  

**Liberation Achieved by Eradicating Mental Defilements**  
Complete eradication of afflictions through wisdom, mindfulness, and practice leads to liberation from suffering and cyclic existence. Nirvana results from extinguishing all defilements, as stated in the phrase '断尽烦恼，证得究竟清净'.  

**Symbolic Metaphors for Meditative States and Challenges**  
Symbolism includes the lotus blooming unstained representing purity amid challenges, pool water illustrating a calm and clear mind, boiling water depicting a scattered state, and the rare Udambara flower signifying the difficulty of proper meditation practice. These metaphors clarify targeted mental states and obstacles.  

**Integration of Morality, Concentration, and Wisdom**  
The Threefold Training combines ethical conduct, meditative concentration, and wisdom to eliminate afflictions. This integrated approach ensures mental stability, discernment of reality's nature, and transformation. All components work together to achieve inner peace and liberation.
