**Title**  
Spiritual Regression and Harm from Laziness Counteracted by Mindfulness and Diligence  

**Summary**  
Laziness, defined as mental sloth, complacency, idleness, or lack of effort, impedes spiritual progress and wisdom cultivation while causing harm through careless behavior. Mindfulness eliminates laziness to enable consistent spiritual effort, and diligence sustains steady practice to counteract its effects.  

**Spiritual Progress and Wisdom Impeded by Laziness**  
Laziness, characterized by mental sloth, complacency, idleness, or lack of effort, directly hinders spiritual progress and the cultivation of wisdom.  

**Harm Caused by Laziness Through Careless Behavior**  
Laziness results in harm via careless behavior, which manifests as physical or mental injury.  

**Mindfulness Dispels Laziness for Consistent Spiritual Practice**  
Mindfulness counteracts laziness by fostering consistent spiritual effort, dispelling its obstructive effects.  

**Diligence Sustains Effort to Counteract Laziness**  
Diligence maintains constant effort in practice, directly opposing laziness and supporting ongoing spiritual development.
