**Conversion of Figurative Elements to Doctrinal Explanations**  

The text establishes requirements to transform metaphors, analogies, and dialogue into explicit doctrinal explanations. All figurative elements must be converted to direct representations while maintaining doctrinal details.  

- **Direct Doctrinal Explanation Mandate**: Doctrinal principles must be explained explicitly and directly without figurative language or dialogue.  
- **Metaphor and Analogy Conversion**: Metaphors and analogies must be converted into direct doctrinal explanations.  
- **Dialogue Transformation**: Dialogue is transformed into doctrinal representation.  
- **Doctrinal Detail Preservation**: Doctrinal details remain intact within the doctrinal representation.  
- **Figurative Language Conversion**: Figurative language must be converted into doctrinal representation.
