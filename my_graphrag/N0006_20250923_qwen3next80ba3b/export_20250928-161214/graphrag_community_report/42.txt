**Numbered Titles and Plain Language Requirements for Religious Doctrines**  

Numbered titles structure religious doctrinal content with plain language, retain all details, exclude poetic verses, and ensure clarity and precise communication.  

**Numbered Title Structure**  
Numbered titles organize paragraph topics and sections clearly. Example formats specify their use for structural organization.  

**Plain Language Application**  
All paragraph topics, religious doctrine, and Dharma doctrine must use plain language for precise expression.  

**Doctrinal Detail Retention**  
Religious teachings must retain all details without omission when communicated in plain language.  

**Poetic Verse Exclusion**  
Poetic verses must be removed to maintain plain language style standards.
