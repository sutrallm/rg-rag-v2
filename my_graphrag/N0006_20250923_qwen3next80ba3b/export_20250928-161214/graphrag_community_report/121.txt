**Buddust Meditation Practices: Cultivating Mindfulness, Concentration, and Diligence for Liberation**  

Meditation is a core Buddhist practice forming part of the Threefold Training, essential for cultivating mental clarity, tranquility, and insight. It addresses mental afflictions—rooted in greed, hatred, and delusion—as the primary cause of suffering and cyclic existence. Ethical discipline, mindfulness, and concentration work synergistically to reduce disturbances, while persistent diligence and adherence to precepts enable overcoming heedlessness, transcending attachment, and achieving liberation from rebirth.  

**Mental Afflictions Root Cause of Suffering**  
Mental afflictions, including greed, hatred, and delusion, are the primary root cause of suffering within cyclic existence. Their complete cessation leads directly to liberation from pain and transcendence of suffering, as established in Buddhist teachings.  

**Meditation Cultivates Inner Peace**  
Meditation achieves inner peace through calming the mind, focused attention, mindfulness, and eliminating distractions, disturbances, and afflictions. This practice stabilizes the mind and fosters profound tranquility, as described in the aim to "獲得內心安寧".  

**Diligent Practice Essential for Overcoming Heedlessness**  
Persistent and sustained effort counteracts heedlessness and negligence in spiritual practice. This diligence, symbolized by carrying a heavy load or fleeing a burning house, prevents bondage to the cycle of rebirth and supports spiritual progress toward liberation.  

**Ethical Precepts Support Meditative Success**  
Adherence to moral and ethical precepts establishes a necessary foundation for effective meditation. Observing pure precepts reduces mental afflictions through disciplined conduct, enabling their complete elimination and aiding spiritual growth.  

**Synergy of Mindfulness and Concentration**  
Mindfulness forms the foundation for developing concentration, while concentration enables the arising of wisdom. Together they maintain mental clarity, remove distractions, and foster stability essential for deeper insight and spiritual development.  

**Heedlessness Sustains Rebirth Cycle**  
Negligence in spiritual practice binds practitioners to the cycle of rebirth. Lack of attention and diligence perpetuates suffering, causes stagnation, and increases vulnerability to mental disturbances, necessitating vigilant mindfulness for transcendence.  

**Concentration Develops Wisdom**  
Deep concentration cultivates a focused mental state necessary for profound wisdom. Stable concentration serves as the bridge to insight, directly supporting the development of wisdom required for liberation.  

**Transcending Sensory Attachment**  
Clinging to the five desires fuels greed and perpetuates the cycle of rebirth. Overcoming attachment to transient sensory experiences through disciplined observation and mindfulness is essential for achieving liberation.  

**Threefold Training Integration**  
Practitioners actively engage in ethical discipline, meditation, and wisdom cultivation as part of the Threefold Training. This integrated approach cultivates pure actions, moral conduct, and insight, enabling transcendence of cyclic existence and attainment of inner freedom.
