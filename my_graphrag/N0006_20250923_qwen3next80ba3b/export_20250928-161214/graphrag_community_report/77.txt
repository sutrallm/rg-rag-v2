Transformation of Dialogues and Metaphors into Direct Buddhist Doctrinal Statements  

**Executive Summary**  
The processing mandates converting all dialogues and metaphors into explicit, non-conversational doctrinal explanations while strictly preserving all specific doctrinal details without modification or simplification.  

**Dialogue Transformation into Explicit Doctrinal Statements**  
All conversational exchanges must be restructured into explicit, non-dialogue explanations. Original dialogue format is eliminated during processing, including instances like the Buddha's conversations with Shariputra, to maintain direct doctrinal focus.  

**Preservation of Doctrinal Details**  
Specific factual and nuanced elements of Buddhist teachings must remain unchanged. Processing instructions require absolute retention of core principles without simplification or omission.  

**Metaphor Conversion to Direct Doctrinal Statements**  
Figurative language, including metaphors, parables, and analogies, must be transformed into literal doctrinal explanations. All allegorical content is explicitly converted to ensure clarity without metaphorical language.
