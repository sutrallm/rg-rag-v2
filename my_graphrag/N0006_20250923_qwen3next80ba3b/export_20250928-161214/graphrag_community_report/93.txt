**Liberation Through Emptiness, Dependent Origination, and Non-Self**  

The text describes EMPTINESS, DEPENDENT ORIGINATION, and NON_SELF as interconnected principles essential for liberation. EMPTINESS signifies that all phenomena lack inherent existence and are interdependent. DEPENDENT ORIGINATION explains how phenomena arise from causes and conditions, forming a causal chain that underlies afflictions and the cycle of birth and death. NON_SELF denotes the absence of a permanent self, which dissolves attachment and prevents distractions. The Four Establishments of Mindfulness serve as the practical framework for observing impermanence and non-self, directly addressing suffering's origins. Wisdom encompasses comprehension of these principles, enabling non-attachment and the cessation of suffering.  

**Dependent Origination Explains Afflictions and Rebirth**  
Dependent Origination describes phenomena arising in dependence on causes and conditions, forming a causal chain that specifically addresses the origin of afflictions and perpetuates the cycle of birth and death through desire.  

**Non-Self Realization Dissolves Attachment and Distractions**  
Non-Self realization dissolves clinging to a permanent identity, reducing attachment and preventing mental disturbances by removing the basis for internal defilements.  

**Four Establishments of Mindfulness as Observation Framework**  
The Four Establishments of Mindfulness systematically focus on body, feelings, mind, and phenomena to cultivate recognition of impermanence and absence of self, forming the core practice path for mental cultivation.  

**Suffering Arises from Impermanence and Causal Conditions**  
Suffering is inherent in all phenomena due to impermanence and originates from the causal chain of Dependent Origination, permeating every stage of the cycle of birth and death.  

**Impermanence Perception Cultivates Non-Attachment**  
Perceiving impermanence directly results in non-attachment toward all phenomena, as they are transient and lack fixed essence.  

**Wisdom Comprehends Emptiness, Dependent Origination, and Non-Self**  
Wisdom encompasses deep comprehension of EMPTINESS as the true nature of phenomena, DEPENDENT ORIGINATION as reality's mechanism, and NON_SELF as an inherent characteristic of all existent entities.
