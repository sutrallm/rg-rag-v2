**FETTERS and LIBERATION in Buddhist Philosophy**  

FETTERS, termed "结缚", are mental bonds binding sentient beings to suffering and samsara, maintained through Buddhist practice aimed at severing these bonds. Complete removal of FETTERS results in ultimate LIBERATION. GREED directly causes FETTERS. Understanding emptiness naturally leads to their forsaking. IMPERMANENCE, FIVE SKANDHAS, and FETTERS must be preserved accurately in Buddhist philosophical discourse.
