**Meditative Concentration Countering Distraction and the Five Desires**  

Meditative concentration directly pacifies distraction as stated in '平息散乱' and serves as a foundational path to liberation. It eliminates mental afflictions such as greed and delusion caused by clinging to the five desires, which fuel suffering and obstruct spiritual progress. Adherence to ethical precepts and mindfulness practices support concentration, whereas heedlessness causes mental laxity. Wisdom and concentration mutually reinforce each other to achieve enlightenment.  

**Distraction Eliminated through Concentration Practice**  
Meditative concentration eliminates distraction through stable mental focus, as directly indicated by the Chinese phrase '平息散乱'.  

**Five Desires Drive Greed and Suffering**  
Clinging to the five desires fuels greed through misconception, results in suffering and ignorance, disturbs mental clarity and spiritual progress, and causes spiritual困境.  

**Ethical and Mindfulness Practices Support Concentration**  
Adherence to ethical precepts stabilizes the mind to support meditative concentration, while mindfulness practices and the four establishments of mindfulness foster awareness and deepen concentration.  

**Heedlessness Hinders Concentration Development**  
Heedlessness obstructs meditative concentration development by causing mental laxity and lack of focus.  

**Wisdom and Concentration Mutual Reinforcement**  
Wisdom and meditative concentration mutually reinforce each other in the path to enlightenment.  

**Concentration Enables Liberation from Samsara**  
Meditative concentration is a foundational path leading to ultimate liberation from suffering and aids in escaping the cycle of samsara.
