**Buddhist Wisdom and Impermanence: Path to Liberation**  

**Summary**  
Wisdom acts as luminous clarity that illuminates the true nature of reality—impermanence, non-self, and emptiness—dispelling ignorance and attachment. Through practices like mindfulness and meditation, this wisdom dismantles afflictions, severs attachment to phenomena and spiritual attainments, and directly enables liberation from suffering and the cycle of rebirth. Ethical conduct and concentration form essential foundations for developing wisdom, while sages embody its realization through deep understanding of all phenomena.  

**Wisdom illuminates reality’s true nature**  
Wisdom serves as a lamp that reveals ultimate truth and pierces darkness, as described by "wisdom like light piercing darkness." It directly perceives impermanence, non-self, emptiness, and dependent origination—enabling clear understanding beyond conceptual illusions.  

**Impermanence governs all conditioned phenomena**  
All compounded phenomena are transient and subject to change, characterized by constant arising and cessation. This fundamental truth is observed in the Four Establishments of Mindfulness, particularly when examining mind states and dharmas. Recognizing impermanence undermines attachment and provides the basis for liberation from suffering.  

**Wisdom severs attachment to all phenomena**  
Wisdom dissolves clinging by revealing the empty, impermanent nature of existence. It enables abandonment of attachment to dharmas and scriptures, as illustrated by the bamboo raft metaphor, and prevents constriction by spiritual practice outcomes or desires for results.  

**Ethical conduct supports wisdom development**  
Precepts establish the ethical foundation necessary for cultivating wisdom and maintaining mental clarity. Together with wisdom, they form essential components of the path to liberation, reinforcing each other for progressive spiritual advancement.  

**Meditation cultivates wisdom**  
Meditation develops focused concentration, which serves as the bridge to profound wisdom. Practices like Samatha-Vipassana and the Four Establishments of Mindfulness allow direct observation of impermanence, suffering, and non-self through sustained mindfulness of body, feelings, mind, and phenomena.  

**Wisdom achieves liberation from suffering**  
Realizing the true nature of reality through wisdom is the direct path to Nirvana and the complete cessation of suffering. As stated in "唯有真实智照才能导向解脱," true wisdom alone leads to liberation from samsara, fueled by insight into the Five Aggregates’ impermanent nature and the Four Noble Truths.  

**Sages embody perfected wisdom**  
Those who have attained enlightenment exemplify profound wisdom through deep realization of reality, enlightened conduct, and understanding of all phenomena’s emptiness. This wisdom, cultivated via insight into impermanence, earns respect from all sentient beings.  

**Wisdom dispels ignorance and afflictions**  
Wisdom eradicates fundamental ignorance, delusions, and mental afflictions like greed and hatred by piercing through illusory grasping. It discerns cause-effect relationships in existence and dissolves the three poisons through profound comprehension of reality’s true nature.
