**Four Contemplation Foundations for Affliction Eradication**  

Systematic observation of body, feelings, mind, and phenomena through the four mindfulness practices completely eradicates all afflictions. Feelings—characterized by pleasure, pain, or neutrality—must be observed without clinging, as they form part of the five aggregates. Mind and phenomena serve as critical observation targets, with body as a foundational element of contemplation.  

**Fourfold Mindfulness Practice Eliminates Afflictions**  
Practicing the four mindfulnesses completely eradicates all afflictions.  

**Feelings Must Be Observed Without Clinging**  
Clinging to feelings is instructed to be avoided; feelings comprise sensory and emotional experiences of pleasure, pain, or neutrality and are one of the five aggregates.  

**Mind as Central Observation Focus**  
The mind is examined as a key object in the four mindfulness practices.  

**Phenomena as Fourth Foundation for Contemplation**  
Dharmas (phenomena) are observed continuously as the fourth object of mindfulness.  

**Body as Foundational Contemplation Target**  
The body is one of the four objects designated for mindfulness contemplation.
