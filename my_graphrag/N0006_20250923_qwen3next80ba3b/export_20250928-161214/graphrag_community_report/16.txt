**Anger and Greed as Obstructive Forces in Spiritual Progress**  

The text identifies anger and greed as critical barriers to spiritual development. Anger is specifically part of the Three Poisons and Five Hindrances, disrupting mental stability and clarity. Both emotions are symbolically represented as snakes. Removing anger allows good dharma to manifest naturally, while greed impedes progression along the holy path. Addressing anger during meditation is essential for overcoming these obstacles.  

**Anger within Three Poisons and Five Hindrances frameworks**  
Anger disrupts mental stability and inner peace as a component of both the Three Poisons and Five Hindrances, directly hindering spiritual progress and mental clarity.  

**Metaphorical representation of anger and greed as snakes**  
Both emotions are depicted symbolically as snakes in the source text, illustrating their obstructive nature in spiritual advancement.  

**Removal of anger enabling natural dharma manifestation**  
Eradicating anger permits good dharma to emerge naturally without external intervention.  

**Meditation as critical phase for anger resolution**  
Anger must be overcome specifically during meditation practice to advance spiritually.  

**Greed’s obstruction of holy path progression**  
Greed actively impedes advancement along the holy path as a distinct negative mental state.
