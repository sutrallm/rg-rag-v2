**Mani Jewel, Mindfulness, Sunrise, and Lamp in Dispelling Ignorance**  

**Summary**  
The text describes how various light metaphors combat ignorance. Darkness symbolizes confusion, delusion, and obscured clarity, dispelled by radiant sources like the Mani jewel, mindfulness, inner light, sunrise, and lamps. Observing moral precepts stabilizes the mind to manifest inner light. Mindfulness illuminates mental clarity, while sunrise and lamps represent specific methods of removing ignorance through light.  

**Mani Jewel Signifies Mind's Illuminating Power**  
The Mani jewel, described as a wish-fulfilling jewel, symbolizes the mind’s capacity to dispel ignorance and illuminate all directions.  

**Mindfulness Illuminates Mental Clarity**  
Mindfulness dispels darkness by radiating clarity within the mind, acting as a metaphorical light source.  

**Precepts Stabilize Inner Light Manifestation**  
Observing moral precepts stabilizes the mind, enabling the manifestation of inner light.  

**Inner Light Foundation for Wisdom**  
Inner light serves as the foundation for developing wisdom.  

**Sunrise Symbolizes Ignorance Elimination**  
Sunrise dispels darkness, representing the removal of ignorance.  

**Lamp Provides Clarity Illumination**  
Lamps dispel darkness and illuminate clarity through direct illumination.
