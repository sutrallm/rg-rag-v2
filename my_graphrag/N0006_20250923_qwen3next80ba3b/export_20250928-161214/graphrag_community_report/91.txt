Title: Liberation from Samsara: Three Trainings and Wisdom Path  

Summary  
This report outlines the Buddhist path to liberation from cyclic existence and suffering. Liberation requires eliminating afflictions through the threefold training of ethical discipline, meditation, and wisdom, with wisdom realization directly enabling this freedom. Suffering arises from attachment, greed, and impermanence, and its cessation is achieved through mindfulness, non-attachment, and transcending the cycle of rebirth.  

Afflictions Cause Suffering  
Afflictions are the primary and root cause of suffering within cyclic existence; their cessation leads to liberation from pain and transcendence of suffering, as indicated by phrases stating that increasing afflictions result in mental distress and their elimination allows suffering to be overcome.  

Wisdom Directly Enables Liberation  
Wisdom directly leads to liberation from samsara and suffering by revealing the true nature of reality; only true wisdom guides to liberation, as explicitly stated in "唯有真实智照才能导向解脱".  

Three Trainings Form Essential Path  
The threefold practice of ethical discipline, mental concentration, and wisdom forms the core systematic path to enlightenment; it is required for spiritual development and directly eliminates afflictions.  

Impermanence Underlies Suffering  
Impermanence is a root cause of suffering; conditioned phenomena are inherently subject to suffering due to their transient nature, and observing impermanence directly leads to liberation from suffering.  

Mindfulness Masters Liberation Path  
Mindfulness enables mastery of the liberation path through continuous guarding of mind's attention, as stated in "唯有持续守护心念，才能真正掌握解脱之法".  

Non-Attachment Facilitates Freedom  
Non-attachment, including detachment from the five aggregates and worldly phenomena, results in liberation from suffering; breaking all fetters and clinging leads directly to this state.
