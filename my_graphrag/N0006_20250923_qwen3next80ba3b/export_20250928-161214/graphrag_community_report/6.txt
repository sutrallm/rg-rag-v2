**Causality, Impermanence, and Right View: Existential Principles**  

Correct understanding of reality requires comprehension of causality, impermanence, and existence's fundamental nature. Wisdom discerns causal structures, while Right View includes recognition of impermanence and accurate causality understanding.  

**Action Outcomes Determined by Causality**  
Actions generate specific consequences according to inherent cause-effect principles.  

**Reality Comprehension Integrates Multiple Elements**  
Accurate perception of existence depends on understanding causality, impermanence, and the foundational characteristics of reality.  

**Wisdom Discerns Existential Causality**  
Wisdom functions to identify and interpret causal structures governing all aspects of existence.  

**Right View Encompasses Impermanence and Causality**  
Right View necessitates awareness of impermanence and fully incorporates proper understanding of causal principles.
