**Preservation and Clarification of Religious Doctrine Details**  

The preservation of all doctrinal details remains unchanged during processing. Metaphors and dialogues are converted into direct explanations to ensure clarity and maintain teaching integrity. Doctrinal expositions explicitly present details without modification.  

**Preservation Without Alteration**  
All doctrinal details, including factual and nuanced elements of teachings such as Buddhism, must be preserved exactly without omission or modification during processing.  

**Metaphor Transformation**  
Figurative language must be converted into direct doctrinal explanations to uphold clarity and accuracy while maintaining the integrity of the teachings.  

**Dialogue Rephrasing**  
Conversations expressed allegorically require adjustment into explicit doctrinal statements to preserve clarity and doctrinal accuracy.  

**Exposition-Based Presentation**
