### Concentration, Desire, and Ethical Foundations in Buddhist Practice  

**Summary**  
Concentration, cultivated through meditation and supported by ethical precepts, serves as a foundation for wisdom, inner peace, and liberation. Desire is identified as the root cause of suffering and the cycle of rebirth. Meditation and mindfulness work synergistically to transcend desire, alleviate mental troubles, and overcome obstacles.  

**Mental Stillness Cultivation**  
CONCENTRATION cultivates STILLNESS through mental focus and stability.  

**Overcoming Five Hindrances for Stable Concentration**  
Overcoming the Five Hindrances results in stable concentration.  

**Mindfulness and Concentration Synergy**  
Mindfulness and concentration coexist to maintain mental clarity.  

**Ethical Precepts Underpin Concentration**  
PRECEPTS establish the ethical foundation necessary for cultivating stable mental CONCENTRATION.  

**Afflictions Eliminated Through Concentration**  
CONCENTRATION eliminates AFFLICTIONS through focused mental stability.  

**Inner Peace Achieved via Concentration**  
CONCENTRATION enables INNER PEACE by stabilizing the mind and reducing turbulence.  

**Concentration Overcoming Distractions**  
Strong CONCENTRATION helps overcome DISTRACTIONS by stabilizing attention, as exemplified by "心念专注...远离纷扰".  

**Death Cycle Perpetuated by Desire**  
DESIRE is identified as the primary cause perpetuating the cycle of birth and death in Buddhist doctrine.  

**Mental Troubles Source in Desire**  
Desire is a source of mental troubles.  

**Craving Fuels Suffering**  
DESIRE fuels SUFFERING by perpetuating craving and attachment to transient experiences.  

**Concentration Enables Wisdom Development**  
CONCENTRATION develops the focused state necessary for WISDOM to arise, deep concentration enables the development of profound wisdom and insight, and stable concentration serves as the bridge to developing profound wisdom.  

**Meditation Transcends Desire**  
Meditation helps transcend desire.  

**Observing Truth Transcends Desire**  
Observing TRUTH allows transcending DESIRE.  

**Concentration Overcoming Craving**  
Concentration helps overcome craving and desire.  

**Mindfulness Overcoming Craving**  
Mindfulness helps overcome craving and desire.  

**Mental Troubles Reduced by Mindfulness**  
The practitioner removes mental troubles through mindfulness. Mindfulness eliminates mental troubles.  

**Obstacles Removed by Concentration**  
CONCENTRATION removes OBSTACLES by calming the mind and fostering clarity.  

**Overcoming the Three Poisons**  
Concentration helps overcome the three poisons.  

**Practicing Without Desire**  
A Buddhist monk who practices without desire or attachment. The practitioner avoids desire disturbances. The Buddhist monk practices without desire.
