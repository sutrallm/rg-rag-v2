**Mind Purity and the Four Immeasurable Hearts in Sentient Beings' Well-being**

Sentient beings' experiences of suffering or peace are directly determined by the purity of their minds. Cultivating the Four Immeasurable Hearts—loving-kindness, compassion, sympathetic joy, and equanimity—without limitation toward all beings reduces suffering and promotes well-being. A mind free of defilements enables compassionate actions that benefit sentient beings, while greed and impurity cause suffering. Saints inspire universal respect through embodiment of wisdom and virtue.

- **Mind Purity Determines Peace and Suffering**  
  Sentient beings experience suffering when minds are filled with greed or impurity, and peace when their minds are pure, as mind nature directly influences these states.

- **Universal Application of the Four Immeasurable Hearts**  
  The Four Immeasurable Hearts—loving-kindness, compassion, sympathetic joy, and equanimity—are cultivated toward all sentient beings without discrimination or limitation.

- **Compassion's Focus on Alleviating Suffering**  
  Compassion specifically manifests as the wish to alleviate suffering in all sentient beings, making them direct objects of this practice.

- **Loving-Kindness Wishes Universal Happiness**  
  Loving-kindness involves the heartfelt wish for all sentient beings to experience happiness and well-being as part of the Four Immeasurable Hearts.

- **Sympathetic Joy in Others' Success**  
  Sympathetic joy is cultivated as the genuine rejoicing in the happiness and success of others, one of the Four Immeasurable Hearts.

- **Equanimity Ensures Mental Balance**  
  Equanimity maintains impartiality toward all beings and mental evenness despite circumstances, serving as the fourth quality within the Four Immeasurable Hearts.

- **Clear Mind Enables Beneficial Actions**  
  A mind clear of defilements fosters compassion and wisdom, enabling effective actions that benefit all sentient beings.

- **Saints Inspire Through Wisdom and Virtue**  
  Saints inspire respect from all sentient beings by embodying wisdom and virtue.
