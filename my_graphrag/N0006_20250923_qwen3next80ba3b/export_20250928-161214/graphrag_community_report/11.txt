**Emptiness, Dependent Origination, and Liberation from the Cycle of Birth and Death**  

All phenomena lack inherent existence and are interdependent. Dependent origination describes how phenomena arise from causes and conditions, forming a causal chain that explains afflictions and suffering. The cycle of birth and death is driven by karma and craving and marked by pervasive suffering. Realizing emptiness dissolves false perceptions, leading to liberation. Desire is the primary cause perpetuating samsara, and all phenomena, including the five aggregates, are empty of self-nature.  

**Phenomena Lack Inherent Existence**  
All material and mental phenomena are impermanent and devoid of self-nature, reflecting the concept of emptiness.  

**Dependent Origination Explains Afflictions**  
All phenomena arise from interdependent causes and conditions, forming a causal chain that explains afflictions and demonstrates emptiness.  

**Cycle of Birth and Death Driven by Craving**  
Continuous rebirth and death occur due to karma and craving, with suffering prevalent throughout all stages.  

**Liberation via Emptiness Realization**  
Dissolving false perceptions of inherent existence through realization leads to freedom from bondage.  

**No-Self and Emptiness Are Interdependent**  
These concepts are central to understanding reality, mutually defining and supporting each other.  

**Desire Perpetuates Samsaric Cycle**  
Craving acts as the primary cause sustaining the continuous process of birth, death, and rebirth.  

**Impermanence Understood Through Contemplation**  
Deep observation reveals the transient nature of all phenomena, essential for grasping emptiness.  

**Five Aggregates Are Empty**  
The constituents of existence are devoid of inherent self-nature, consistent with the broader principle of emptiness.  

**Worldly Phenomena Lack Self-Nature**  
All worldly matters have no independent existence, as explained by dependent origination.
