**Meditative States, Non-Attachment, and Mental Liberation**  

**Summary**  
Meditative states are temporary and impermanent mental conditions requiring observation without attachment to prevent suffering. Attachment to these states perpetuates suffering, while mental liberation emerges from overcoming all attachment. Mind freedom remains unbound by transient mental conditions.  

**Transient Nature of Meditative States**  
Meditative states are temporary and impermanent mental conditions, absorptions, or experiences during practice.  

**Non-Attachment in Observation**  
Meditative states must be observed without attachment and not clung to during practice.  

**Attachment Perpetuates Suffering**  
Attachment to states causes suffering through clinging to transient mental conditions, hindering progress.  

**Mental Liberation via Overcoming Attachment**  
Mental liberation is attained by overcoming all forms of attachment and obstacles.  

**Mind Freedom Unbound by Temporary Conditions**  
Mind freedom exists independently of any temporary mental states.
