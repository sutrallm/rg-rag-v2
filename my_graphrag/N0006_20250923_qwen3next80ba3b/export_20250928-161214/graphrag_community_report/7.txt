**TRUTH as the Path to LIBERATION in Buddhist Practice**  

**Summary**  
Buddhist doctrine establishes TRUTH as the ultimate reality (Dharma) that reveals inherent suffering in all sensations and enables liberation through observation. WISDOM and MINDFULNESS are vital for perceiving and understanding TRUTH, which completely eliminates AFFLICTIONS, transcends DESIRE, and requires overcoming ignorance and recognizing non-self. Attachment to desires obstructs TRUTH perception, and supernatural abilities are not the focus of Buddhist cultivation.  

**TRUTH Embodies Ultimate Reality and Suffering**  
TRUTH is the ultimate reality or Dharma in Buddhism, representing the true nature of phenomena. It reveals inherent suffering present in all sensations, and observation of this truth leads to liberation.  

**WISDOM Enables TRUTH Realization**  
WISDOM facilitates understanding of TRUTH and allows direct realization of ultimate truth according to Buddhist principles.  

**MINDFULNESS Perceives TRUTH**  
MINDFULNESS permits perception of TRUTH.  

**TRUTH Eliminates AFFLICTIONS**  
TRUTH leads to the complete elimination of AFFLICTIONS.  

**All Feelings Manifest as Suffering**  
TRUTH manifests as all feelings being suffering.  

**Overcoming Ignorance for Enlightenment**  
Overcoming ignorance is necessary to realize ultimate truth and gain enlightenment.  

**TRUTH Transcends DESIRE**  
Observing TRUTH allows transcending DESIRE.  

**Non-Self Understanding is Critical**  
Understanding non-self is essential for realizing ultimate truth in Buddhist doctrine.  

**Attachment to Desires Obscures TRUTH**  
Attachment to the five desires obscures the perception of ultimate truth.  

**Buddhist Practice Prioritizes Truth Over Supernatural Abilities**  
Buddhist cultivation focuses on truth and liberation rather than supernatural abilities, which are secondary despite potential attainment through practice.
