**User Translation Requests Missing Source Content**  

User-submitted translation requests omit necessary original content, causing the assistant to generate error notifications. The assistant requires original content to perform processing and adheres to user-specified formatting in responses. Providing original content is the user's responsibility for successful task execution.  

**Translation Requests Lack Source Content**  
User submitted translation queries without providing required original content, where the submission does not include the necessary material expected for processing.  

**Assistant Identifies Missing Source Content**  
The assistant processes requests and detects the absence of required original content, triggering responses to notify users of the omission.  

**Source Content Mandatory for Processing**  
Original content is essential for translation tasks; the assistant cannot proceed without it and relies on its presence for accurate rendering and adjustments.  

**Response Format Follows User Specifications**  
Assistant utilizes provided formatting guidelines to structure responses, including errors related to missing materials, ensuring consistency with user requirements.  

**User Responsibility for Content Provision**  
Users are accountable for including original content in requests, and its absence in submissions results in processing failures.
