**Non-Attachment, Realms, Impermanence, and Wisdom in Buddhist Liberation**  

This report details how non-attachment to phenomena, realms, and outcomes enables liberation from suffering. Understanding impermanence and the illusory nature of existence fosters non-attachment, which is cultivated through wisdom. Ethical precepts form the foundation for wisdom development, while wisdom and meditative concentration mutually reinforce each other. Avoiding attachment to results and superficial characteristics prevents bondage, allowing practitioners to transcend samsara.  

**Impermanence Understanding Drives Non-Attachment**  
Understanding transient nature of all phenomena naturally leads to non-attachment, as explicitly linked to Buddhist wisdom throughout the text.  

**Non-Attachment Releases Suffering**  
Refraining from clinging to experiences and outcomes directly results in freedom from suffering, as stated in the source material.  

**Realms Non-Attachment Transcends Samsara**  
Avoiding attachment to any mental or experiential realms allows practitioners to break free from the cycle of birth and death, as emphasized by the phrase "不执着于任何境界".  

**Outcome Non-Attachment Prevents Bondage**  
Attachment to results creates bondage even when pursuing spiritual goals; non-attachment ensures liberation from such constraints, as noted in "不被结果所缚".  

**Wisdom Through Reality Insight**  
Wisdom enables non-attachment by providing insight into the true nature of reality, including impermanence and non-self, forming the basis for enlightenment.  

**Ethical Precepts Build Wisdom Foundation**  
Ethical guidelines establish the necessary groundwork for developing profound wisdom required for enlightenment, as directly referenced in the text.  

**Wisdom and Concentration Interdependence**  
Wisdom and meditative concentration mutually reinforce each other on the path to enlightenment, forming a synergistic relationship.  

**Superficial Characteristics Avoidance**  
Non-attachment prevents clinging to superficial characteristics of phenomena, ensuring progress toward liberation without distraction.
