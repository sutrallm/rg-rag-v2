**NO-SELF and EMPTINESS: Path to Liberation and Disturbance Prevention**  

**Summary**  
The understanding of NO-SELF—the absence of a permanent, independent self or soul—is essential for overcoming attachment and delusion. Wisdom enables the realization of this concept, which is critical for spiritual development. NO-SELF and EMPTINESS are interdependent, forming the core of Buddhist understanding of reality. Realizing NO-SELF is fundamental to achieving ultimate liberation and prevents demons from disturbing the mind and causing distractions.
