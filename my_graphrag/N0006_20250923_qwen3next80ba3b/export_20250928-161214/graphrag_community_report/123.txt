**Three Poisons, Five Hindrances, and Paths to Enlightenment**  

This report details core Buddhist concepts identifying greed, hatred, and delusion as the Three Poisons—fundamental causes of suffering requiring eradication for liberation. The Five Hindrances (greed, hatred, sloth, restlessness, doubt) obstruct meditation progress and mental clarity. Sentient beings’ experiences of peace or suffering depend directly on mental purity. Practices such as compassion cultivation, moral precepts, mindfulness, and specific meditation techniques are essential for overcoming obstacles and achieving liberation.  

**Three Poisons as Root Causes of Suffering**  
The Three Poisons—greed, hatred, and delusion—are the fundamental mental defilements and primary sources of suffering. They obstruct spiritual progress and must be eliminated to attain liberation.  

**Five Hindrances Disrupting Meditation Progress**  
Greed, hatred, sloth, restlessness, and doubt are the five primary obstacles to meditation and spiritual advancement. They cause agitation, distraction, and loss of concentration, directly impeding mental stability and clarity.  

**Compassion as Pathway to Liberation**  
Compassion, nurtured through the Four Immeasurable Hearts, focuses on alleviating the suffering of all sentient beings. It serves as a vital method to transcend self-centered attachment and achieve liberation.  

**Mind’s Inherent Purity Dispelling Ignorance**  
The mind naturally possesses a pure essence, symbolized by a lotus and wish-fulfilling jewel, which illuminates all directions and dispels ignorance. It perceives phenomena as transient and illusory when free from defilements.  

**Sensory Attachment Fuels Greed and Suffering**  
Attachment to external sensory objects and the Five Desires is the foundational cause of greed. This attachment generates fetters and directly contributes to distress, afflictions, and suffering among sentient beings.  

**Moral Precepts Stabilizing Mental Clarity**  
Diligent observance of ethical precepts purifies and stabilizes the mind, manifests inner light, and actively eliminates the Three Poisons. This practice ensures consistency in spiritual development without negligence.  

**Delusion Misrepresenting Reality**  
Delusion arises from misconceptions about sensory objects, creating false distinctions and erroneous perceptions. It is a root poison that breeds attachment, confusion, and suffering.  

**Symbolic Metaphors for Wisdom and Purity**  
The lotus (growing unstained in mud), Mani jewel (dispelling darkness), sunrise, and lamp illustrate the dispelling of ignorance through mindfulness and inner clarity. These symbols represent transcendence of worldly impurities.  

**Purity of Sense Organs Preventing Defilements**  
Maintaining purity in the six sense organs prevents attachment and aversion during sensory experiences. This stops greed, delusion, and hatred from arising, ensuring mental stability.  

**Four Immeasurable Hearts Cultivating Universal Benevolence**  
Boundless loving-kindness, compassion, sympathetic joy, and equanimity toward all sentient beings without limitation form the foundation for mental balance. These practices transcend discrimination and support universal well-being.
