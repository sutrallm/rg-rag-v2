**Celestial Reverence for Saintly Enlightenment Qualities**  

Celestial beings honor saintly beings for their virtue, wisdom, spiritual attainments, and transcendence of suffering. Saintly beings have eradicated afflictions, attained nirvana, uphold pure precepts, perceive phenomena without attachment, and practice specific meditative disciplines.  

**Celestial Reverence for Saints**  
Celestial beings revere and venerate enlightened sages, honor virtuous individuals for virtue, wisdom, and spiritual attainments, support spiritual journeys, and respect enlightened states.  

**Non-Attachment to Signs**  
Characteristics of phenomena should not be clung to; attachment to signs leads to clinging to false perceptions.  

**Perception of Impermanent Aggregates**  
Saintly beings perceive the five aggregates as impermanent illusions, transcending attachment to them.  

**Suffering Transcendence**  
Saintly beings have transcended all suffering through complete spiritual liberation.  

**Pure Precepts Upholding**  
Saintly beings uphold pure precepts as foundational for virtuous discipline.  

**Nirvana Attainment**  
Saintly beings have attained nirvana through complete enlightenment and liberation from cyclic existence.  

**Meditative Stillness**  
Saintly beings abide in stillness as part of meditative practice.  

**Inner Peace Embodiment**  
Saintly beings embody inner peace from overcoming afflictions and attachments.  

**Non-Attachment to States**  
Saintly beings transcend attachment to states by recognizing impermanent and illusory nature.  

**Affliction Eradication**  
Saintly beings have eradicated afflictions through deep meditation and wisdom.  

**Samatha and Vipassana Practice**  
Saintly beings have perfected the practice of samatha and vipassana.
