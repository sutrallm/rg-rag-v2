**Title: Waterfall Discourse Chant: Structure, Themes, and Cross‑Sectional Links in Chapter Nine**

**Summary**  
The chant identified as *攝頌* occupies a central role in Chapter Nine of the Waterfall Discourse. It serves simultaneously as a concise summary of the chapter’s teachings and as a unifying verse that reinforces the message through repetition. The content of the chant centers on the dynamics of craving—its binding power, refined form, and continuous flow—while also inviting listeners to practice right effort. The hymn incorporates specific terms that describe the organization of craving, methods of attachment, and states of rest, illustrating the doctrinal architecture of the passage. Moreover, *攝頌* references a wide array of other sections (classification, excellence comparison, Bussa, Listen, and giving‑benefit) signalling its integrative function across the broader text.

**Chant’s Dual Function**  
*攝頌* appears once at the conclusion of Section Eight and again within the Waterfall Discourse. In both placements it performs two complementary tasks:  
1. It distills the key doctrines of the chapter into two compact poems that echo the narrative.  
2. It acts as a collecting verse that binds listeners to the teaching, reinforcing the message through collective chanting.  

**Craving‑Centric Theme**  
The hymn repeatedly cites craving in several guises:  
- *上 `下` 分* denotes the division between upper and lower craving.  
- *妙欲* highlights a refined desire, suggesting a subtle form of attachment.  
- *瀑流* portrays craving as a waterfall, emphasizing its continuous, unbroken flow.  
- *軛取* and *繫* refer to binding or attachment mechanisms, underscoring how craving restrains.  

**Term Functions and Symbolism**  
The verses of *攝頌* employ terms that convey both physical and metaphysical states:  
- *隨眠* (“following sleep”) connects craving to restlessness after sleep.  
- *蓋* (“cover” or “encompass”) speaks to the encompassing nature of craving.  
- *蘊* represents an underlying reservoir of desire.  

These terms collectively paint a picture of craving as a powerful, perpetually flowing force that enshrouds the mind.

**Cross‑Referential Scope**  
Beyond summarizing its immediate chapter, *攝頌* references a network of other sections, including:  
- The classification chapter and the excellence comparison section.  
- The four Bussa and four Listen sections, which detail acts of piety.  
- The four giving‑benefit sections that elaborate on generosity.  

This extensive list indicates that the chant is positioned as a bridge linking disparate thematic strands within the larger canon.

**Poetic Summaries**  
The hymn contains two distinct summary poems, each employing numerical and thematic repetition. The first poem uses the pattern “鲇罠三无常 三诶三无我 只至啊谓此三 學儿子诶三,” while the second repeats “注向东併六 注向海併六 二六一十二 以之说此品.” These recurrent structures reinforce doctrinal points—impermanence, non‑self, and the circular nature of craving—through rhythmic mnemonic devices.

**Textual Placement**  
*攝頌* is situated both as part of Chapter Nine and as a concluding element in Section Eight, showing that it occupies a pivotal junction within the Waterfall Discourse. This dual placement suggests an intentional design to create cohesion across the council of teachings and to ensure that core concepts are reiterated at both sectional and chapter levels.
