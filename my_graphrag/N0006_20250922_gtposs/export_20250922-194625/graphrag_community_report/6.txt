**Title**  
Sravasti's Teaching Landscape and Its Key Monastic Actors  

**Executive Summary**  
The information indicates that Sravasti (舍衛城) is the central hub where the Buddha (世尊) lives and delivers sermons. Within the city, distinct sub‑areas—such as the East Garden (東園) and the Qishu Forest (祇樹林)—serve as venues for various teaching sessions, notably those addressing the Four Mindfulnesss and the Five Covers. The hall of 鹿子母堂, situated in the East Garden, is where the Buddha receives reverence from senior disciples, including 具壽賓頭盧, 具壽阿難, and 具壽舍利弗. Outside Sravasti, the city of 曹寺城 hosts the比德 community for instruction, while visitors like 罗睺罗 travel to Sravasti to meet the Buddha. These spatial and relational patterns reveal the organized flow of teaching activities and the prominence of specific locations and individuals in the monastic ecosystem.  

**Key Observations**  

*Centrality of Sravasti*  
Sravasti is repeatedly identified as the Buddha’s residence and the primary venue for sermons, including the Shavasa teaching. The city is portrayed as the main hub for pastoral activities, providing a stable backdrop for all discussed teachings.  

*鹿子母堂 as the Receiving Hall*  
鹿子母堂, located within the East Garden, is where the Buddha delivers discourse and is approached by disciples for reverence. Multiple senior monks—具壽賓頭盧, 具壽阿難, 具壽舍利弗—enter this hall to pay respect, underscoring its role as a focal point for listener‑teacher interaction.  

*East Garden’s Contextual Function*  
The East Garden is the broader area encompassing 鹿子母堂. Text references consistently place teaching events and residence of key figures within this zone, indicating its importance as the immediate setting for the Buddha’s activities.  

*祇樹林 and its Secluded Garden for Contemplation*  
祇樹林, a forest within Sravasti, contains the secluded sub‑area known as the give‑loneliness garden (給孤獨園). It is repeatedly cited as a meeting point for contemplative teachings, implying a deliberate distinction between public sermons at 鹿子母堂 and more private, reflective instruction within the forest.  

*Disciples’ Engagement at 鹿子母堂*  
Mentions of 具壽賓頭盧, 具壽阿難, and 具壽舍利弗 converging at the hall to greet and honor the Buddha illustrate a pattern of senior monk participation. This reinforces the hall’s status as a gathering place for influential monastics.  

*曹寺城 and the 比德 Community*  
曹寺城 serves as the assembly point for 比德 monks, who attend teachings, practice, and live within the city during instructional periods. The city’s role contrasts with Sravasti by hosting a distinct monastic group yet sharing a similar pedagogical focus on the Buddha’s sermons.  

*Visitor Dynamics of 罗睺罗*  
罗睺罗 is noted to travel to Sravasti in order to meet and receive instruction from the Buddha. This pattern highlights Sravasti’s appeal to monks beyond its immediate vicinity and underscores its function as a pilgrimage hub for learning.  

*Emphasis on Key Teachings*  
References to discussions of the Four Mindfulnesss and the Five Covers, especially within church contexts such as 鹿子母堂 and 祇樹林, pinpoint central doctrinal topics that gather attention from both the Buddha and senior disciples alike.  

These patterns collectively chart how location, audience, and subject matter intertwine within the monastic world centered on Sravasti.
