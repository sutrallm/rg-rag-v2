**Title**  
Monastic Leadership and Geographical Reach of 婆耆沙 in Ancient Buddhism  

**Executive Summary**  
The text foregrounds 婆耆沙 as a prominent Buddhist monk who performed essential monastic duties—receiving precepts, constructing dwellings, preaching, and authoring texts—while sustaining the order’s discipline. His life spanned multiple key centers in ancient India, notably 阿臘毘, 王舍城, and the temple of 阿伽羅婆奢提耶, where he collaborated with 豨俱律陀劫波. 婆耆沙’s devotion is evident through repeated praise of the Buddha, 大目犍連, and other respected disciples, including the Sharīra followers and 阿若憍陳如. His activities illustrate a network of spiritual interactions that reinforced monastic authority and doctrinal transmission across regions.  

**Central Monastic Leadership**  
婆耆沙 actively undertook foundational monastic functions: he received ordination, built monastic dwellings, delivered sermons, compiled scripture on Maitreya, and educated monks to preserve discipline. These actions establish him as a key facilitator of cultural and spiritual continuity within the Buddhist community.  

**Wide‑Spanning Residence**  
Through multiple mentions of his stays in 阿臘毘, 王舍城, and specifically the temple/monastery 阿伽羅婆奢提耶, the monk’s itinerant pattern indicates a deliberate strategy to influence several important urban and monastic centers. The repeated residency at 阿臘毘’s temple points to a focal point for his teaching and monastic governance.  

**Collaborative Practice with Peer Monk**  
The text records a close association between 婆耆沙 and 尼俱律陀劫波, especially at 阿伽羅婆奢提耶 where they shared a monastic hall and practiced the Bodhisattva path together. This partnership exemplifies cooperative monasticism and mutual support among senior disciples.  

**Devotion to the Buddha and Other Teachers**  
Repeated references to praising the Buddha’s actions, enjoining him, and highlighting his virtues showcase the monk’s deep reverence and acknowledgment of the Buddha’s central role. The way 婆耆沙 addresses the Buddha’s deeds indicates both personal devotion and a broader teaching strategy that models humility and love for the Sangha.  

**Admiration for Mahākusalaprajñā**  
Sermons that extol the profound meditation and compassionate instruction of 大目犍連 reveal the monk’s respect for a celebrated ascetic. By presenting 大目犍連 as a model of spiritual attainment, 婆耆沙 both honors the tradition and underscores the virtues his own community should emulate.  

**Praise of Sharīra and Other Disciples**  
The monk’s reverence toward the Sharīra teacher and the respectful tribute to 阿若憍陳如 demonstrate a culture that values the experiential teaching of respected senior monks. These endorsements illustrate the spread of Dharma through peer recognition across various sub‑lineages.  

**Role as an Upholder of Monastic Discipline**  
His engagement in preaching, writing scriptures, and recognizing the spiritual achievements of prominent monks underscores a broader responsibility: to uphold monastic norms, disseminate teachings, and ensure the ethical standards of the community remained intact.
