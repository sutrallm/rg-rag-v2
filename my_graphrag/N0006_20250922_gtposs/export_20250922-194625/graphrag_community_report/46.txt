**Title**  
Key Components of the Sheng Fa Pi Teaching  

**Summary**  
The Sheng Fa Pi is a doctrinal discourse delivered to a monk within the lecture hall of Sumeru City. It presents a catalogue of ten fundamental “aspects” of reality, with the aspect of birth (生之法) explicitly highlighted among them. The discourse also references aspects of anxiety (忧之法), death (死之法), defilement (污穢之法), annihilation (消亡之法), old age (老之法), sickness (病之法), and destruction (破壞之法). The closing of the teaching is marked by 摄顶 (Shādíng) noting it as the summary paragraph of the Sheng Fa Pi.  

**Key Components**  

1. **Audience and Setting**  
   The scripture specifies that the teaching was given to a monk (比丘) inside the lecture hall of Sumeru City (舍衛城). This situates the discourse in a formal didactic context where monastic listeners were the intended recipients.

2. **Core Content: Ten Aspects of Reality**  
   The Sheng Fa Pi enumerates ten “之法” (aspects), among them explicitly *生之法* (the law of birth). The text lists several other aspects, illustrating a comprehensive view of worldly phenomena:  
   - *忧之法* – the law of anxiety  
   - *死之法* – the law of death  
   - *污穢之法* – the law of defilement  
   - *消亡之法* – the law of annihilation  
   - *老之法* – the law of old age  
   - *病之法* – the law of sickness  
   - *破壞之法* – the law of destruction  

3. **Conclusion Section**  
   A concluding portion of the discourse is identified by the phrase “攝頂曰”, indicating that the section is the final summary of the Sheng Fa Pi. This highlights the structured design of the teaching, culminating in a concise recapitulation.

4. **Interrelationship of Aspects**  
   By juxtaposing *生之法* with the other seven aspects, the narrative underscores the cyclical nature of existence: birth is intertwined with anxiety, death, defilement, annihilation, old age, sickness, and destruction, suggesting an overarching continuity in the experience of reality.

These elements collectively provide a concise framework for decision‑makers to understand the doctrinal focus, contextual delivery, and structural closure of the Sheng Fa Pi.
