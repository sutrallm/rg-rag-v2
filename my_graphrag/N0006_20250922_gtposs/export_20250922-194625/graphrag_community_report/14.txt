**Title**  
Spatial Dynamics of the Wang She City Forest Encounter  

**Summary**  
The brief passage highlights a pivotal meeting between a noble seeker and the Buddha within the forested area of Wang She City. The setting functions as a dedicated, natural space inside the city where devotion is expressed and reciprocated. The Buddha’s immediate invitation to the seeker underscores active recognition and acceptance within this environment.  

**Key Spatial Dynamics**  
- The Wang She City forest is explicitly identified as a forested area inside Wang She City.  
- All encounters described—arrival, presentation of devotion, and approach—occur specifically within this forest.  
- The forest serves as the distinct backdrop for the encounter, separating it from the broader urban setting.  

**Buddha’s Direct Invitation**  
- Upon the seeker’s arrival, the Buddha addresses him with the invitation “來！須達多.”  
- This direct address indicates the Buddha’s immediate recognition of the seeker’s devotion and willingness to engage.  

**Seeker’s Active Pursuit**  
- The seeker, referred to as “須達多,” approaches “世尊” directly within the forest.  
- The narrative emphasizes the seeker’s proactive movement toward the Buddha, illustrating the impetus for the meeting.
