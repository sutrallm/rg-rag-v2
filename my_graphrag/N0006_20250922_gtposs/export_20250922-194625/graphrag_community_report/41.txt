**毘舍城大林重閣堂教學概覽**

**Executive Summary**  
The Buddha resided within the Chong Ge Hall, a dedicated classroom situated inside the large forest of Visha City. All instruction was delivered from this hall. The hall’s placement within the city’s forest and its exclusive use for teaching highlight its central role in the Buddha’s activities.

- **Residence and Instruction Center**  
  The Buddha lived and conducted teaching activities directly within the Chong Ge Hall.

- **Strategic Location**  
  The hall is located inside the large forest of Visha City, indicating a deliberate setting within a natural, secluded environment.

- **Purpose‑Fixed Facility**  
  The description identifies the structure explicitly as a “講堂” (classroom), underscoring its purpose as a dedicated space for instruction.
