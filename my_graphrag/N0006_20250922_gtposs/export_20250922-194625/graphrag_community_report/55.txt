**Title**  
毒（Poison) and 魔 (Demon) – A Multi‑Aggregate and Concrete Model for Buddhist Teaching  

**Executive Summary**  
- The evil force *毒* is defined through all five aggregates: form (色), feeling (受), perception (想), mental formations (行), and consciousness (識).  
- 各 aggregate represents a negative aspect, together illustrating how attachments bind the mind to the demon.  
- Concrete manifestations of the demon—刺 (stab), 死者 (death), 殺者 (murderer), 病 (sickness), 痛 (pain), 痛種 (type of pain), 癰 (boil)—are listed as explicit teaching examples.  
- Instructors use the *毒* model as a conceptual tool to identify and confront different levels of evil within the instructional framework.  

---

### Five‑Aggregate Construction of the Evil Force  
*毒* is framed as a demon whose negative influence manifests in the five aggregates.  
- **色** – form is burdened with the demon’s negative quality.  
- **受** – feeling is tainted by the demon’s harmful aspect.  
- **想** – perception is distorted by the demon’s influence.  
- **行** – mental formations become the vehicle for the demon.  
- **識** – consciousness is continuously marked by the demon.  

This structure shows how every dimension of experience can be corrupted by attachment, creating a holistic barrier to liberation.  

### Concrete Physical Manifestations Listed in the Teaching  
The dialogue enumerates seven tangible expressions of the demon:  
1. 刺 – a stab or wound  
2. 死者 – a death or deceased person  
3. 殺者 – a murderer or killer  
4. 病 – sickness or illness  
5. 痛 – pain  
6. 痛種 – a type or kind of pain  
7. 癰 – a boil or swelling  

These examples illustrate both internal and external forms of suffering that practitioners are encouraged to recognize and detach from.  

### Attachment as the Binding Path to the Demon  
**世尊** warns that *attachment binds one to the demon (魔)*.  
- The binding occurs precisely through sustained practices of attachment.  
- Once attachment continues, the demon’s negative effects entrench themselves, preventing liberation.  

This highlights the central role of detachment in overcoming the demon’s influence.  

### Role of the Demon Concept in Instructional Framework  
Within teaching, *毒* is identified as a specific level of evil force.  
- It functions as a conceptual tool for instructors and practitioners.  
- By categorizing evil at this level, teachers can guide students toward recognizing and releasing attachment across all aggregates and manifestations.  

### Dialogue Demonstrates Demon Appearing Across Senses and Forms  
During the exchange with **具壽羅陀**:  
- The Buddha explains *魔* as manifesting in each aggregate’s negative aspect (受, 想, 色, 行, 識).  
- Additionally, *魔* is shown to take the explicit forms listed (刺, 死者, 殺者, 病, 痛, 痛種, 癰).  

This dual presentation—aggregate‑based and concrete—provides a clear, multi‑layered teaching method for confronting all facets of evil.
