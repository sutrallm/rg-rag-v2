**Title**  
Binding Forces and Liberation: Buddha’s Teachings on Desire, Rebirth, and Absolute Knowledge  

**Summary**  
The passage outlines how sensory cravings (欲), greed (貪), joy (喜), and affection (愛) bind living beings (眾生) to the cycle of birth, death, and rebirth (生死). The Buddha directs monks to sever desire and greed within the skandhas, highlighting the transformative power of absolute knowledge (所徧知), which eradicates desire, greed, ignorance (愚癡), and anger (瞋恚). Color (色) is identified as the origin of desire, greed, and joy. The dialogue between the Buddha and the seeker 具壽羅陀 illustrates the practical application of these doctrines.  

**Binding Forces of Conditional Beings**  
- Living beings are defined by a set of conditioning factors: desire, greed, joy, and affection.  
- These factors keep them entangled in the regenerative cycle expounded as 生死.  

**Monastic Instruction for Cessation**  
- The Buddha commands monks to cut the desire and greed that arise within the skandhas.  
- The instruction focuses specifically on internalizing the cutting of both 欲 and 貪, underscoring their role as primary impediments to liberation.  

**Form as the Root of Emotional Activation**  
- Color (色) is cited as the source that engenders desire, greed, and joy.  
- This causal link establishes form as the launching point for the emotional states that sustain attachment.  

**Absolute Knowledge as a Comprehensive Resolution**  
- 所徧知 is presented as an all‑encompassing awareness that nullifies desire, greed, ignorance, and anger.  
- It functions as the path by which conditioned beings can transcend their binding factors.  

**Dialogue Between Teacher and Seeker**  
- The Buddha explains the nature of 眾生 and 所徧知 to 具壽羅陀.  
- 具壽羅陀’s questions about 眾生 and 所徧知 demonstrate the instructional method used to clarify the doctrines.  

**Cycle of Birth, Death, and Rebirth**  
- 生死 is described as encompassing all conditioned beings, highlighting the enduring loop that desire and other emotions perpetuate.  
- Understanding this cycle is the foundation for grasping the necessity of eradicating the binding factors.  

**Roles of Affection and Joy**  
- Both affection and joy are listed among the binding factors that tether beings to the cycle.  
- Their inclusion signals that even positive emotions can hinder liberation if they become an attachment.
