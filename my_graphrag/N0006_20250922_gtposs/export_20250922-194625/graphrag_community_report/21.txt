**Eastward Flow: Symbolic Path of Monastic Practice**

### Executive Summary  
The text portrays the direction East as the spiritual goal of practitioners, using the Ganges River and sea as metaphors for the journey toward Nirvana. Monks are depicted as moving along a natural flow toward the East, with the river’s eventual merging into the sea representing the attainment of ultimate liberation. The doctrine of the four right efforts is illustrated through this fluid motion, underscoring the disciplined progression required to reach enlightenment.

---

## East as Spiritual Target  
The customary direction East (東) is consistently identified as the desired destination for a spiritual aspirant. It represents the intended path of the river’s flow and symbolically points to the attainment of Nirvana.

## River as Practice Metaphor  
The Ganges River (恆河) is employed figuratively to illustrate the natural flow of practice. Its movement toward the East and its entry into the sea serve as a vehicle for demonstrating how monks’ practice proceeds toward liberation.

## Sea as Ultimate Liberation  
The sea (海) is referenced as the ultimate destination within the chant. In Buddhist symbolism it embodies the final liberation that monks seek through their disciplined practice.

## Four Right Efforts Mirrored in Flow  
The doctrine of the four right efforts (四正勤) is analogously presented through the river’s motion. Correct effort is visualized as the steady progression of the river toward the East and its eventual emptiness into the sea, reinforcing the path toward Nirvana.

## Monks as Flowing River  
Monks are metaphorically compared to the Ganges, emphasizing their natural and continual movement in the direction of the spiritual East. Their progress is visualized as a continuous stream, aligning individual effort with the broader, collective flow toward enlightenment.

## Culmination in the Sea  
The river’s emptiness into the sea (海) symbolizes the culmination of practice. This act marks the transition from disciplined effort to the ultimate attainment of Nirvana, completing the cycle of spiritual progression.
