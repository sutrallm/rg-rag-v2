**Title:**  
Monastic Life and Instruction at 祇樹林給孤獨園 in Sravasti

**Summary:**  
A network of senior monks and disciples gathered in Sravasti, primarily within the garden at 祇樹林給孤獨園. They engaged in regular dialogue with the Buddha, addressed personal doctrinal errors, taught novice monks, and maintained close teacher‑student bonds. A recurring theme is the dual role of monks as both contemplative practitioners and educators. Occasional interactions with lay merchants in Narada and trading towns illustrate the monks’ outreach beyond monastic settings. The text records both residential patterns and instructional activities, revealing a structured environment of learning and practice.

**Findings**

- **Residential Focus in Sravasti**  
  The majority of monks—including 具壽焰摩迦, 具壽低舍, 具壽跋迦梨, 阿難, and Gu Shou Sharlif—are mentioned as residing or practicing at 祇樹林給孤獨園. This indicates a central communal hub where monastic life and study were concentrated.

- **Addressing Erroneous Views and Personal Struggles**  
  具壽焰摩迦 actively seeks instruction to eliminate erroneous views and engages in dialogues about the self, craving, and the nature of suffering. 具壽低舍 discusses fatigue, lack of discernment, and the laws of the mind, while 具壽跋迦梨 contemplates impermanence and suffering during illness. These exchanges point to individualized spiritual challenges confronted through the Buddha’s guidance.

- **Teacher‑Student Relations and Service Dynamics**  
  Gu Shou Sharlif maintains a direct relationship with the Buddha and example of master–servant interaction is seen with Pūndara, who serves as his novice. The role of the novice includes attending the Buddha’s residence, demonstrating a structured hierarchy and mentorship within the community.

- **Extension of Monastic Interaction to Lay Communities**  
  The Buddha’s stay in Narada, where he dialogues with local merchants, and the narrative of a town involving an exchange of a shirt and monetary settlement highlight the monk’s engagement with lay economic activities. This shows a pattern of monks extending their teachings beyond purely monastic bounds.

- **Teaching of Novice Monks (比丘)**  
  All three senior monks—具壽焰摩迦, 具壽低舍, 具壽跋迦梨—are recorded as instructing and engaging in discourse with novice monks. This consistent involvement signals an organized educational program for new monks within the garden.

- **Multiplicity of Gardens and Sites**  
  Aside from 祇樹林給孤獨園, there are references to gardens in 舍衛城, the town of 鈐鎝城 (Sravasti), and a location called 佬 where 具偕道利旭 and 具偕康洛颜 interacted. These multiple sites demonstrate a distributed yet connected network of monastic activity.
