**Title**  
Five‑Root Doctrine Taught by the Buddha in Anghanna Deer Park, Saʰar City  

**Executive Summary**  
The Buddha, while residing in Saʰar City, delivered the doctrine of the five roots to monks. He chose Anghanna Deer Park—a designated area within the city—as the teaching venue, underscoring the significance of both the audience and the location in the dissemination of these teachings.  

**Details**

- **Doctrine and Audience**  
  The Buddha instructed monks in the doctrine of the five roots, indicating a focused spiritual lesson aimed at monastic followers.

- **Teaching Venue**  
  Anghanna Deer Park, located within Saʰar City, served as the physical setting for the sermon, highlighting the use of a notable public space for doctrinal instruction.

- **Residency Context**  
  The event occurred during the Buddha’s residence in Saʰar City, marking this period as noteworthy for its contribution to the spread of Five‑Root teachings.
