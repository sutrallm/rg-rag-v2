**Title**  
Chapter 15 Overview – Structure, 10‑Verse Composition, and Core Motifs (不放逸品, 生法品)

**Summary**  
Chapter 15 contains the subsection “不放逸品”, which is a concise unit of ten verses (1–10). A distinct concluding marker closes the chapter. The prose highlights ten short lines that together form the group known as 十句. The passage foregrounds the theme of “調伏欲貪” (taming greed) and introduces a method for dealing with desire through the phrases “以覺支相應之不放逸品” and “用以廣說欲貪”.  

The chapter cites verses 133–142 for contextual background and states the principle “一切是…之法” seven times, covering laws of sorrow, death, impurity, disappearance, ageing, sickness, and destruction. Symbolic imagery appears repeatedly: footstep (足跡) precedes roof (屋頂), followed by root kernel (根核). Key motifs within the ten verses—王月, 夏生花, 日衣, 足跡—are presented either together or consecutively, highlighting natural cycles and celestial symbolism.

---

### Chapter Fifteenth’s Section Layout  
The subsection “不放逸品” exists only within Chapter 15. It is bounded by a start in verse 1 and an end in verse 10, while the entire chapter references verses 133–142. A final marker denotes the conclusion of the chapter.

### Composition of the Ten Verses  
Exactly ten lines constitute this group, collectively called 十句. Individual titles found in the verses include 根核, 王月, 夏生花, 日衣, 足跡, and the associated footer images.

### Central Theme – Taming Greed  
Narrative focus is on 調伏欲貪, as expressed by the expressions “以覺支相應之不放逸品” and “用以廣說欲貪”. These introduce a teaching method aimed at addressing and subduing desire.

### Imagery and Symbolic Portrayals  
Footstep (足跡) imagery appears in the introductory clause. It is followed by roof (屋頂) imagery, and then root kernel (根核) imagery, creating a visual pair that reinforces the overall message of the verses.

### Seven Laws of All Things  
Repetition of “一切是…之法” enumerates seven laws:  
1. 忧之法 (law of sorrow)  
2. 死之法 (law of death)  
3. 污穢之法 (law of impurity)  
4. 消亡之法 (law of disappearance)  
5. 老之法 (law of ageing)  
6. 病之法 (law of sickness)  
7. 破壞之法 (law of destruction)  

These underlie the doctrinal statements within the chapter.

### Sequential Grouping of Key Motifs  
– 王月 and 夏生花 are listed together, implying a thematic link between celestial and seasonal cycles.  
– 王月 and 日衣 appear consecutively, indicating a sequential narrative within the ten‑verse structure.

### Reference Points  
Verses 133–142 supply the broader contextual framework of Chapter 15. The range marker 1–10 isolates the ten verses of “不放逸品”, and the concluding marker signals the chapter’s closure. This systematic structure guides readers through thematic and symbolic progression.
