**Title**  
因陀迦夜叉 – Guardian, Mountain, and City Context  

**Executive Summary**  
The text repeatedly identifies the supernatural guardian 因陀迦夜叉 as dwelling on the mountain 因陀峰山, which lies close to the ancient city 王舍城. This geographic positioning is consistently emphasized as the backdrop for the guardian’s meetings with the Buddha, 世尊, who is visited while the guardian recites verses that probe the nature of existence. The narrative’s focus on location underscores its importance in shaping the encounter and the thematic content of the verses.  

---

### Geographic Anchor
- The guardian’s residence is always stated as “on 因陀峰山 near 王舍城.”  
- The mountain’s proximity to the city is a defining element in every mention, suggesting that this physical setting is essential to understanding the guardian’s role and the context of the meetings.  

### Interaction with the Buddha
- The guardian visits 世尊 and engages in recitation of verses during the meeting.  
- The verses question the nature of existence, indicating a philosophical exchange that relies on the guardian’s presence.  

### Recurring Location Emphasis
- All four excerpts repeat the same location details, illustrating that the place where the guardian dwells is central to the narrative’s structure.  
- The repeated emphasis implies that the location is not incidental but a focal point for the events described.
