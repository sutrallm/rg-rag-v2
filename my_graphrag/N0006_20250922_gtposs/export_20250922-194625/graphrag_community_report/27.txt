**Title:**  
Root Principles and Faith Basis in Chinese Professional Practice  

**Executive Summary**  
- “根” is treated as a core principle that defines the foundation of any task, reflecting faith and moral determination in the working method.  
- It embodies the genuine significance of work, system, and rules, and it is regarded as trustworthy and meaningful.  
- “信根” is identified as one of the five components that form the five roots.  
- The cultivation of 信根 is guided by four concepts: ① 離貪 ― abandoning greed; ② 遠離 ― distance from negative influences; ③ 滅盡 ― complete extinguishment of obstacles; ④ 迴向 ― returning focus toward the practice.  
- Together, these elements emphasize the importance of moral clarity, systematic integrity, and focused intent in professional conduct.  

---

### Foundational Role of 根 in Work  
- 根 represents the bedrock of a task, indicating that the method undertaken is driven by faith.  
- It connects the action to moral determination, ensuring that work is not merely procedural but infused with trustworthy intentions.  
- By rooting actions in this principle, practitioners align everyday operations with a deeper commitment to genuine significance and system integrity.  

### Composition of 信根 in the Five Roots  
- 信根 is one of the five distinct constituents that together make up the set of roots.  
- While 根 serves as a general foundation, 信根 specifically denotes a faith‑based element within that broader framework.  

### Guiding Concepts for Cultivating 信根  
- **離貪**: Remov­ing greed from the practitioner’s focus, enabling clear judgment.  
- **遠離**: Maintaining distance from disordered or distracting forces that could undermine faith.  
- **滅盡**: Extinguishing residual doubts or negative thoughts that hinder determination.  
- **迴向**: Redirecting attention and energy back toward the practice, reinforcing commitment.  

### Practical Implications for Professional Practice  
- Grounding work in 根 encourages consistent reliance on a moral compass, leading to trustworthy outcomes.  
- Embedding 信根 as part of the five roots illustrates the necessity of faith alongside other virtues in achieving holistic professional excellence.  
- The four guiding concepts provide a systematic approach to cultivating this faith base, ensuring continual self‑refinement and focused intention.  

*These observations collectively illuminate how foundational principles, faith components, and cultivation practices intertwine to shape disciplined, trustworthy professional behavior.*
