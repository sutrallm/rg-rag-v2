**Executive Summary**  
The text reveals how female Buddhist monastics (比丘尼) are deeply integrated into the doctrinal instruction of the Four Mindfulnesss delivered by the Buddha (世尊) and his attendant, 阿難. It shows that 比丘尼 not only receive the same teachings as male monks but also participate actively in the study and practice of the Four Mindfulnesss. The practice occurs within the communal setting of 舍衛城 and under the broader guidance of the “world.”

4. **Integration of Female Monastics in Monastic Community** – 比丘尼 are a subgroup within the broader monastic community of 比丘, showing a clear hierarchical integration.  
5. **Practical Setting for Study** – 比丘尼 reside in 舍衛城 while the discourse on the Four Mindfulnesss takes place, providing a concrete location for their practice.  
6. **Guidance by the World** – Female monastics commit to studying the Four Mindfulnesss under the guidance of the world, indicating an external, communal accountability or support system.
