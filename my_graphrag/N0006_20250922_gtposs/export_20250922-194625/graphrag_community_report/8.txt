**Title:**  
Geographic and Narrative Anchors of Early Buddhist Discourses in 法岳羢国, 毕舍離國, and 法真梁国  

**Summary:**  
The text places the Buddha’s teaching activities in several distinct gardens and forested settings across three kingdoms. The primary venues are 佩遵堊 in 法岳羢国, 福译坛 in 法真梁国, and 菴羅園 plus 竹林叢 in 毕舍離國. Key disciples such as 具壽阿难 and 俵腹佛 appear repeatedly, moving between these locations to receive instruction, practice, and to engage in healing scenes. Monastic figures like 胡司何坂 and the 比尔 community are also situated within this spatial framework.  

---

### Multi‑Location Presence of the Buddha  
The Buddha is depicted teaching both at the garden of 佩遵堊 and at the garden of 福译坛. 佩遵堊 is specified as a setting within 法岳羢国 where early chapters unfold, while 福译坛 is identified as a garden in 法真梁国 where the Buddha often sits in instruction. The same site is also mentioned in the Narada section as the place where a merchant sells clothes, indicating its recurrent use in the narrative.  

### Forest and Bamboo Grove Context  
Discourses occur in a forest area described as 菴羅園 and in a bamboo grove cluster called 竹林叢. Both locations are situated within 毕舍離國. The bamboo grove hosts a healing scene involving 具壽阿难, emphasizing the therapeutic dimension of the Buddha’s interactions. These places form natural settings that contextualize the spiritual teachings within the text.  

### Associations of 具壽阿难 Across Sites  
具壽阿难’s presence is mapped to several places:  
- He resides in 舍衛城 where he activities with the teachings.  
- He travels to 菴羅園 and 竹林叢 to listen to and practice the Buddha’s instructions.  
- He recuperates from illness in 竹林叢 under the Buddha’s care.  
- He visits 鹿子母堂 to sit in respect and to pay homage to the Buddha.  
These movements illustrate a pattern where the devotee seeks guidance, practice, healing, and devotion across multiple locations.  

### Monastic Figures Within Communities  
胡司何坂 is identified as a monk belonging to the monastic community termed 比尔. He participates in communal practices and engages in teaching and discourse with novice monks in that community. Additionally, a number of 比丘 are stated to reside in 菴羅園, highlighting the concentration of monastic practice at this site.  

### Role of 俵腹佛 in the Narrative  
俵腹佛 is portrayed as a direct disciple who regularly receives guidance from the Buddha. Aside from the Buddha, the disciple is described as a major figure who listens to worldly teachings and implements them. He also partakes in the Buddha’s sermons, exhibiting behavior that mirrors the Buddha and previously achieved harmony with the world through conversation. This underscores the disciple’s dual role as both student and transmitter of the teachings.  


These spatial and relational details reveal a structured environment in which teaching, practice, healing, and community interaction intertwine across multiple kingdoms and settings.
