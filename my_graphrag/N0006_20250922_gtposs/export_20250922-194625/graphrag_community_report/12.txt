**Title**  
Buddha‑Arhasi Sessions in the Bamboo Forest of Wang She City  

**Summary**  
Arhasi travelled to Wang She City and engaged in a dialogue with the Buddha. During this visit, the Buddha taught while seated in a bamboo forest, a designated meditation locus within the city, where he also received disciples. Arhasi asked extensive questions concerning the origin of suffering and was provided with instruction. These interactions highlight the central role of the bamboo forest as a setting for teaching, dialogue, and personal study.

**Key Observations**  

**Arhasi’s Journey to Wang She City**  
- The monk, identified as 阿支羅, travelled specifically to the city of 王舍城 to meet the Buddha.  

**Dialogue on the Origin of Suffering**  
- Upon meeting the Buddha, 阿支羅 presented numerous questions about the origin of suffering and received comprehensive teachings.  

**Buddha’s Meditation in the Bamboo Forest**  
- The Buddha sat in meditation, taught, and welcomed disciples in the竹林, a bamboo forest area within 王舍城.  

**Arhasi’s Occupation of the Meditation Locus**  
- While staying in the city, 阿支羅 spent part of his time in the竹林, recognizing it as the primary meditation spot used by the Buddha and his followers.
