**Title**  
Geographic & Historical Overview of Buddha’s Key Residences in Bārā and Adjacent Regions  

**Summary**  
- The city inside Bārā is recognized as a pivotal life‑historic location for Buddha’s teachings.  
- A distinct country, which houses the city of Bhārā, served as a temporary residence for Buddha.  
- Buddha also resided in the city named 支羅, and in the country named 跋祇國.  
- Within Bārā, a support city provides an official administrative function, indicating a structured governance framework.  

**Key Contexts**

_1. Life‑Historic Setting inside Bārā_  
The city situated within Bārā is highlighted as a significant site in the history of Buddha’s teachings, underscoring its cultural and spiritual importance.

_2. Country Containing Bhārā as Buddha’s Residence_  
The nation that contains the city of Bhārā is noted for being a period of Buddha’s residence, linking the place directly to his itinerant life.

_3. Buddha’s Stay in the City of 支羅_  
Records state that Buddha lived in the city identified by the characters 支羅, indicating another landmark in his journey.

_4. Buddha’s Residence in 跋祇國_  
Documentation confirms Buddha’s stay in the country known as 跋祇國, adding to the roster of major sites connected to his legacy.

_5. Administrative Support City within Bārā_  
A city operating under Bārā serves a supportive administrative role, reflecting an organized local governance structure tied to the larger nation.

These points collectively provide a concise map of Buddha’s movements across key geographical locations and the socio‑administrative backdrop of each site.
