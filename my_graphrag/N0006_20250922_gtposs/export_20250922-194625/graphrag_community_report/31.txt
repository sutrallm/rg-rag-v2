**Title:** Waterfall Discourse – Five Roots, Five Obstacles, and Right Effort  

**Summary**  

The Waterfall Discourse is a compact chapter that arranges its teachings around the five upper obstacles, the five roots, and the four right efforts. A key explanatory tool, 根相應, is employed repeatedly to illuminate how the roots correspond with the obstacles. The discourse culminates in a brief section that draws a direct link between sincere diligence and the overcoming of hindrances. A concise verse, 摄頌, encapsulates the entire teaching, while the final subsection, 正勤相應, offers a detailed look at the nature of correct effort.  

**Core Elements**  

---

**Waterfall Discourse Focus**  
- Concentrates on the interplay between the five upper obstacles (五上分结/五上障) and the five roots (五根).  
- Emphasizes the four right efforts (四正勤) as a means to address both the obstacles and the roots.  

---

**Use of 根相應**  
- Functions as a core explanatory framework that connects each root to its corresponding obstacle.  
- Reappears as a repeated explanation of the Waterfall Discourse throughout the chapter.  

---

**Summative Verse – 摄頌**  
- Offers a concise recapitulation of the entire Waterfall Discourse.  
- Serves as a mnemonic aid for the combined teachings of roots, obstacles, and right effort.  

---

**Linking Diligence and Hindrance Rescue**  
- The ending subsection explicitly indicates the correlation between correct diligence and the overcoming of hindrances.  
- This relationship is underscored as a principal outcome of the four right efforts.  

---

**Concluding Subsection – 正勤相應**  
- Expands upon the nature of right effort (正勤) and its application to the broader Waterfall Discourse.  
- Highlights the role of targeted practice in resolving the five obstacles.
