**Title**  
Dialogue on Dwelling: Shakyamuni Lineage in 迦毗羅衛城  

**Summary**  
The passage records a series of meetings held in the garden of the trees (尼拘律樹園) within the capital city of 釋氏國, 迦毗羅衛城. A senior monk of the Shakyamuni lineage engages in discussions with 盧夷強耆, the senior monk 釋氏摩訶男, and the historical figure 世尊. The central topics are the nature of the Buddha’s dwelling (如來住) versus ordinary dwelling (有學之住) and the practice of breath meditation. The dialogues demonstrate a close intellectual network centered on the city of 釋氏國 and its capital.

**Central Venue for Discourse**  
- All meetings described occur in the garden or grove located within 迦毗羅衛城, specifically the area known as 尼拘律樹園.  
- This garden serves as the common physical setting for exchanges among the senior monk, 盧夷強耆, 釋氏摩訶男, and 世尊, indicating its importance as a gathering place for contemplative discussion in the Shakyamuni tradition.

**Focus on the Nature of Dwelling**  
- The recurring theme in each dialogue is a comparison between the Buddha’s dwelling (如來住) and ordinary dwelling (有學之住).  
- Participants repeatedly interrogate the distinctions and implications of these two types of dwelling, highlighting an ongoing concern with how the nature of dwelling reflects broader teachings.

**Participants and Their Interactions**  
- The senior monk of the Shakyamuni lineage serves as the principal interlocutor, engaging with three key figures: 盧夷強耆, 釋氏摩訶男, and 世尊.  
- 釋氏摩訶男 appears in two separate conversations—first with 盧夷強耆, then with 世尊—demonstrating his role as a mediator between the Shakyamuni lineage and the Buddha.  
- Each figure contributes to a layered discussion that incorporates both doctrinal analysis and practical meditation techniques.

**Administrative and Geographic Setting**  
- 盧夷強耆’s residence is identified as the garden within 迦毗羅衛城, which itself is the capital city of the Shakyamuni people’s country, 釋氏國.  
- The city provides the administrative backdrop for the dialogues, underscoring its role as a central hub for spiritual leadership and intellectual exchange within the lineage.  

These observations together illuminate the structure, focus, and context of the contemplative dialogues occurring in 迦毗羅衛城.
