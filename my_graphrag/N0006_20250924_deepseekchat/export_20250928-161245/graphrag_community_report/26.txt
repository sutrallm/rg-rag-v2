**Title: Path to Liberation: Understanding the Goal of Spiritual Practitioners**  

**Summary:** Spiritual practitioners aim to transcend the cycle of birth and death through dedicated practice. By cultivating meditation and wisdom, they seek to eliminate afflictions and achieve liberation, ultimately attaining peace and freedom from the perpetual cycle of existence.  

**The Goal of Transcending Reincarnation**  
Practitioners strive to overcome the cycle of birth and death, viewing it as a source of bondage. Their primary objective is to break free from this cycle through continuous effort and spiritual discipline.  

**The Role of Meditation and Wisdom**  
Meditation and wisdom are essential tools for practitioners in their journey toward liberation. These practices help them develop a deeper understanding of existence and cultivate the mental clarity needed to transcend reincarnation.  

**Liberation Through the Elimination of Afflictions**  
By removing afflictions, practitioners aim to achieve a state of peace and tranquility. This process allows them to exit the cycle of life and death and attain ultimate freedom from suffering.  

**Attainment of Peace and Freedom**  
The ultimate outcome of their efforts is the realization of liberation, characterized by profound peace and the cessation of the perpetual cycle of existence. This state represents the fulfillment of their spiritual journey.
