**Title: Pathways to Liberation Through Selflessness and Purity**  

**Summary:**  
The text highlights two key factors that enable practitioners to attain liberation: selflessness (无我) and inner purity (内心纯净). These elements are interdependent, with purity facilitating the state of selflessness, which is described as essential for achieving ultimate freedom.  

**Key Findings:**  

**1. Selflessness as the Foundation of Liberation**  
The state of selflessness (无我境界) is identified as the critical condition for attaining liberation. It represents a transcendent state where the practitioner moves beyond individual identity, enabling spiritual freedom.  

**2. Inner Purity as a Catalyst for Selflessness**  
A purified mind (内心纯净) actively supports the practitioner’s journey into selflessness. This suggests that moral and mental clarity are prerequisites for dissolving the ego and accessing liberation.  

**3. Interdependence of Purity and Selflessness**  
The text implies a cyclical relationship: inner purity fosters selflessness, while the pursuit of selflessness likely reinforces purity. Together, they form a transformative path toward spiritual emancipation.
