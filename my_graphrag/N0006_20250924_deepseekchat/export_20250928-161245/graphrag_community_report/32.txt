**Title: The Attributes and Achievements of a 觉悟者**

**Summary:**  
The text highlights the characteristics and accomplishments of a 觉悟者, an individual who has attained the highest state of spiritual enlightenment through rigorous practice. Key attributes include wisdom and the eradication of attachment, aversion, and ignorance. A 觉悟者 achieves ultimate liberation, transcending the cycle of birth and death, and serves as a model for others on the path of spiritual practice.

**Wisdom as a Defining Attribute**  
A 觉悟者 embodies wisdom, which is central to their elevated state. This wisdom signifies a profound understanding and clarity that emerges from deep spiritual practice.

**Achievement of Ultimate Liberation**  
The 觉悟者 reaches the state of 涅槃, the ultimate liberation. This state represents complete freedom from the cycle of birth and death, marking the culmination of their spiritual journey.

**Eradication of Attachments and Defilements**  
Through their practice, a 觉悟者 has eliminated all forms of attachment, aversion, and ignorance. This purification allows them to transcend the suffering and limitations imposed by these defilements.

**Transcendence of Causal Bonds**  
A 觉悟者 has surpassed the constraints of karma and causality. This liberation from the cause-and-effect cycle reinforces their freedom from all forms of bondage, including the cycle of rebirth.

**Role as a Spiritual Exemplar**  
The 觉悟者 serves as a guiding example for others on the path of spiritual practice. Their attainment of complete liberation and freedom from suffering demonstrates the potential of dedicated and disciplined practice.
