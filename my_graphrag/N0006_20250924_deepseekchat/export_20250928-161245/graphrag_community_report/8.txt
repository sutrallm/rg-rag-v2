### Title: The Role of Moral Discipline in Spiritual Practice  

**Summary:**  
The text emphasizes the fundamental importance of moral discipline (持戒) in spiritual practice, highlighting its role as the first step toward liberation. Through adherence to moral precepts, practitioners purify their body, speech, and mind, laying a foundation for deeper meditation and ultimate liberation. Wisdom and non-attachment to external forms are central to the practice of moral discipline, which is essential for spiritual progress.  

**Moral Discipline as the Foundation of Spiritual Practice:**  
Moral discipline is identified as the initial and essential step in spiritual practice. It serves to purify the practitioner's body, speech, and mind, creating a solid foundation for further spiritual development.  

**Wisdom and Non-Attachment in Moral Discipline:**  
The true essence of moral discipline lies in wisdom, which guides practitioners to avoid attachment to external forms or rituals. This approach ensures that the practice remains rooted in inner purity rather than superficial adherence.  

**Moral Discipline and Liberation:**  
By adhering to moral precepts, practitioners clear obstacles on their path to liberation. Moral discipline is described as a critical element that paves the way for achieving ultimate freedom and is indispensable for spiritual progress.  

**Moral Discipline as a Core Practice for Saints:**  
For saints or advanced practitioners, moral discipline is a fundamental and non-negotiable aspect of their spiritual journey. It serves as both a core principle and a prerequisite for advancing to higher spiritual states.  

**Fasting as a Form of Moral Discipline:**  
Fasting (斋戒) is presented as a specific expression of moral discipline. Both practices share a foundation in cultivating a pure mind, emphasizing inner transformation over external observance.  

**Progression from Moral Discipline to Meditation:**  
The text outlines a sequential progression in spiritual practice, with moral discipline serving as the first step, followed by meditation (禅定). This structure underscores the interdependence of moral clarity and meditative focus in the spiritual journey.
