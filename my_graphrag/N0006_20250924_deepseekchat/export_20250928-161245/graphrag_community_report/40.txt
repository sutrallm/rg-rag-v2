**Title: The Foundations of Buddhist Practice: Discipline and Diligence**

**Summary:**  
The text emphasizes the fundamental role of discipline (戒律) and diligent practice (精進修行) in Buddhist teachings. Discipline is the cornerstone for eliminating all afflictions and attaining ultimate liberation (無上解脫). Diligent practice, guided by the Buddha’s teachings, is essential for mastering meditation (禪定之法) and achieving the state of no birth and no death (無生無滅).

---

**1. Discipline as the Core of Spiritual Practice**  
Discipline is described as the foundation for spiritual cultivation. Strict adherence to discipline is crucial for cutting off all afflictions and laying the groundwork for attaining ultimate liberation.

**2. Diligent Practice Guided by the Buddha’s Teachings**  
Diligent practice involves consistent effort in following the Buddha’s instructions. It is necessary for mastering meditation techniques, which are integral to spiritual progress.

**3. The Essential Role of Diligence in Achieving Liberation**  
Diligent practice is highlighted as a prerequisite for attaining the ultimate state of liberation. Sustained effort is required to eliminate afflictions and reach the state of no birth and no death, which represents the highest spiritual achievement.
