### Qualities and Attainments of the Enlightened Being

#### Executive Summary
The Enlightened Being emerges as an awakened practitioner who masters the three trainings of precepts, meditation, and wisdom through non-indulgent practice. This mastery leads to profound comprehension of impermanence, emptiness, and no-self, fostering detachment from realms, temptations, and dualities. As a result, the Enlightened Being surpasses suffering, pleasure, birth, death, aging, illness, and rebirth, securing eternal peace, liberation from afflictions, and a model role for others, with a pure mind free from dust.

#### Mastery of the Three Trainings
The Enlightened Being fully embodies precepts, meditation, and wisdom as the core trainings, achieved through non-indulgent practice. These trainings enable profound wisdom, meditative stability, and concentration, allowing the individual to penetrate impermanence, emptiness, and no-self while remaining unattached to external influences.

#### Comprehension of Core Doctrines
Through the three trainings, the Enlightened Being thoroughly discerns impermanence in all phenomena, emptiness, and no-self. This understanding arises from inner freedom and stability, leading to complete realization that severs ties to birth, aging, sickness, and death.

#### Transcendence of Suffering and Dualities
The Enlightened Being overcomes suffering, pleasure, and the cycle of rebirth via insight and non-attachment derived from the trainings. This state extends to equanimity beyond dualities, free from the dominance of impermanence, death, and external temptations, including the death demon king.

#### Attainment of Eternal Peace and Liberation
Eternal peace is realized by departing from afflictions through the trainings and insights, surpassing cycles of birth, death, and impermanence. The noble path supports this by eradicating poisons, resulting in liberation from all afflictions and a role as a model for others.

#### Cultivation of a Pure Mind
A pure mind, untainted by dust and temptations, marks the Enlightened Being's attainment. This purity stems from concentration, stability, and the three trainings, ensuring inner freedom and detachment from realms.
