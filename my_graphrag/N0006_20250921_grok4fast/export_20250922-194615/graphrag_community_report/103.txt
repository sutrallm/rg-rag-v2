### Path to Jie Tuo: Cultivation Practices of Xing Xiu Zhe

#### Executive Summary
Xing Xiu Zhe pursue Jie Tuo through integrated practices of meditation, wisdom, and mindfulness, emphasizing diligence via Jing Jin Xing Xiu to master Zen Ding, eradicate afflictions, and avoid Wu Ming and Shuai Bai. Continuous adherence to Zheng Fa purifies the mind, fosters inner peace, and ensures freedom from worldly bonds without clinging to meditative states, ultimately transforming practitioners into Sheng Zhe by contemplating impermanence, suffering, emptiness, and no-self.

#### Foundations of Practice for Xing Xiu Zhe
Xing Xiu Zhe build their path on meditation and diligent efforts to cultivate mindfulness, wisdom, precepts, and concentration, directly targeting the elimination of afflictions for complete mental freedom.

#### Attainment of Ultimate Liberation
Jie Tuo emerges from combining Zen Ding with Zhi Hui that examines Wu Chang, Ku, Kong, and Wu Wo, allowing Xing Xiu Zhe to purify the mind, transcend samsara, attachments, and realms through sustained meditation and contemplation.

#### Role of Continuous Diligence in Mastery
Jing Jin Xing Xiu enables Xing Xiu Zhe to maintain vigilance and follow Zheng Fa consistently, securing true advantages in meditation and preventing the dominance of Wu Ming, which otherwise causes decline in practice.

#### Safeguards Against Mental Deterioration
Through Jing Jin Xing Xiu, practitioners steer clear of Shuai Bai by avoiding indulgence and staying alert, preserving the integrity of their meditative progress and concentration.

#### Non-Attachment in Meditative States
Even during pursuit of liberation, Xing Xiu Zhe who achieve freedom via mindfulness refrain from grasping meditative absorptions or their outcomes, ensuring the mind remains unbound and liberated.

#### Development of Wisdom and Inner Peace
Focused practice in Zen Ding grants Xing Xiu Zhe escape from distractions and afflictions, cultivating inner peace while mindfulness generates wisdom that reveals impermanence, non-self, and impurity, foundational to mind purification.

#### Transformation into Enlightened Beings
Diligent Xing Xiu Zhe perfect precepts, wisdom, concentration, and affliction removal through foundational mindfulness, evolving into Sheng Zhe and attaining full Jie Tuo by freeing the mind from all bonds.
