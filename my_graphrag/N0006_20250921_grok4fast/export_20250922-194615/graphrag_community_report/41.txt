### Saints' Qualities and Transcendence through Precepts and Wisdom

#### Summary
Saints possess complete moral precepts and insightful wisdom, which allow them to eradicate afflictions, including the three poisons, and to transcend the sufferings of birth, old age, sickness, and death. These qualities support diligent cultivation, enable dwelling in stillness, and facilitate surpassing worldly existence.

#### Qualities Defining Saints
Saints are characterized by full endowment with moral precepts and insightful wisdom. These qualities form the core traits that distinguish saints and support their accomplished practice.

#### Eradication of Afflictions
Possession of precepts and wisdom enables the complete cutting and eradication of afflictions, including the three poisons and numerous others. This process allows saints to achieve thorough severance of such hindrances.

#### Transcendence of Primary Sufferings
Saints fully and thoroughly transcend the four primary sufferings of existence: birth, old age, sickness, and death. This transcendence occurs through their endowed precepts and wisdom, marking a complete surpassing of worldly existence.

#### Benefits of Diligent Cultivation
Endowment with precepts and wisdom supports diligent cultivation, leading to dwelling in stillness. These qualities empower practitioners to overcome afflictions and achieve a state beyond ordinary worldly conditions.
