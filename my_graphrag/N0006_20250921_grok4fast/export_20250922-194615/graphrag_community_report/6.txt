### Path to Nirvana: Attainment through Threefold Training and Eradication of Afflictions

#### Executive Summary
Nirvana stands as the ultimate state of peace, bliss, and liberation, achieved by eradicating afflictions, defilements, and the three poisons—greed, hatred, and delusion—via the threefold training of precepts, concentration, meditation, insight, and wisdom. This process frees practitioners irreversibly from samsara's cycle of birth, aging, sickness, death, and suffering, transcending even heavenly joys. Diligence, non-negligence, and mindfulness, combined with non-attachment to the pursuit itself, enable progression to this unconditioned state, where the mind abides in tranquility, fearlessness, purity, and compassion. Awakened beings and saints realize Nirvana as the highest goal, serving as guides free from all obstructions and worldly bonds.

#### Nature of Nirvana as Supreme Liberation
Nirvana represents an eternal, unconditioned state of ultimate peace, bliss, and freedom, attained by awakened beings through vigilant practice that extinguishes all afflictions, defilements, greed, hatred, delusion, attachments, craving, ignorance, and the roots of suffering. It culminates in parinirvana, providing complete transcendence beyond the three realms, rebirth, karma, illusions, conditioned phenomena, and even heavenly joys, freeing one from all pain, disturbances, and residual obstructions without remainder. As the highest spiritual goal taught by the Awakened One, it features a cooled, liberated mind in profound tranquility, fearlessness, purity, compassion, supernatural powers, and unshakeable enlightenment, where saints reside eternally beyond worldly toils and sorrows.

#### Threefold Training as Foundation for Attainment
The threefold training of precepts, meditation, and wisdom forms the core path to Nirvana, enabling the complete eradication of afflictions and the realization of impermanence, no-self, and emptiness. Precepts provide moral discipline, meditation cultivates concentration and insight into no-self, and wisdom illuminates true reality, severing the roots of suffering and guiding to eternal stillness and irreversible liberation. This integrated practice, when perfected, achieves Nirvana as supreme, unobstructed ease beyond all afflictions, with compassion enhancing its realization for unobstructed freedom and non-attachment to desires or outcomes.

#### Diligence and Non-Negligence as Direct Path
Diligence serves as the essential effort in Buddhist practice, overcoming laxity, indolence, and negligence to break the cycle of birth and death, leading directly to Nirvana's peace and bliss. Combined with mindfulness, it enables entry into jhanas for extinguishing defilements and attaining remainderless Nirvana, while non-indulgence, vigilance, and right effort prevent evil karma, resist distractions, and foster sustained practice without delusion. Non-laxity maintains purity, avoids the three poisons, and progresses toward the ultimate fruit, ensuring persistent, non-indolent pursuit that transforms practitioners into supreme teachers respected by devas.

#### Eradication of Afflictions and Three Poisons
Attaining Nirvana requires breaking free from afflictions, defilements, and the three poisons through diligent cultivation, insight, and wisdom, which illuminate impermanence and sever greed, hatred, delusion, craving, attachment, and ignorance. Practices like mindfulness, meditative contemplation, and right effort eliminate clinging, understand no-self, and extinguish the causes of suffering, resulting in freedom from samsara, rebirth, and all dependencies. Overcoming ignorance via wisdom and shattering the poisons grants immediate fruition of Nirvana's inner peace, eternal clarity, and liberation, with concentration supporting this process to achieve stillness without remainder.

#### Transcendence of Samsara and Four Sufferings
Nirvana provides eternal escape from samsara's wheel of birth, aging, sickness, death, and pervasive suffering, driven by karma, greed, and craving, which diligent practice cuts off to realize complete transcendence. The four sufferings are escaped through non-laxity and the Noble Path, which eradicates afflictions and their causes, leading to pure, undefiled Nirvana free from contamination. Realizing dependent origination and no-self in all things breaks this cycle, surpassing worldly bonds and illusions for immortal bliss and independence from rebirth across realms.

#### Non-Attachment in Pursuit and Realization
Pursuit of Nirvana occurs without attachment or bondage to it or its fruits, enabled by insight into impermanence and mental liberation that preserves true freedom and provides necessary wisdom. This non-clinging, even to the state itself, ensures unobstructed transcendence, allowing entry into Nirvana via elimination of attachments and realization of no-self. Liberated practitioners abide in this state free from fears, desires, and outcomes, with dispassion redirecting from worldly pleasures and one-pointedness penetrating reality for unwavering focus.

#### Qualities and Role of Realized Beings
Enlightened practitioners who realize Nirvana become awakened ones, saints, and supreme guides, illuminating truth with wisdom, free from all suffering, afflictions, and remaining obstructions. They earn respect from devas and holy beings, possessing qualities like compassion fused with wisdom, the four immeasurables, and supernatural powers, while abiding in quietude, profound joy, and eternal tranquility. As non-indolent teachers, they serve as ultimate exemplars of liberation, having severed all habits, defilements, and evil paths through the four noble fruits and right view of the Four Noble Truths.

#### Superiority Over Heavenly Joy and Conditioned States
Nirvana surpasses heavenly joy and celestial pleasures, offering eternal, unshakeable bliss beyond conditioned phenomena and the three realms' influences. While meditative practice attains profound bliss resembling heavenly states, Nirvana provides far greater, remainderless peace through transcendence of all bonds, attachments, and karmic cycles, independent of worldly threats or temptations. This state aligns with supreme Bodhi, granting eternal enjoyment free from confusion, pain, and the binding forces of existence.
