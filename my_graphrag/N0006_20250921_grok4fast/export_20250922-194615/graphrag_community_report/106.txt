### Core Elements of Mental Liberation, Wisdom, and Non-Attachment in Meditation

#### Executive Summary
Mental liberation emerges as the central achievement through meditation and mindfulness, freeing practitioners from delusions, greed, hatred, and attachment to enable profound realization of impermanence, emptiness, and no-self. This liberation interconnects with wisdom to foster transcendence of worldly desires, realms, and practice outcomes, allowing enlightened beings to pursue spiritual attainments without bondage while abiding in nirvana. Key patterns highlight non-attachment as essential for unhindered freedom, with diligent practice supporting mastery over afflictions and realizations of profound truths.

#### Achievement of Mental Liberation Through Meditation Practices
Meditation practice, combined with wisdom and mindfulness observation, directly leads to mental liberation by severing delusions, greed, hatred, and other afflictions. This process enables practitioners, including wise meditators and those on the holy path, to transcend attachments to states, outcomes, realms, and results, resulting in a pure mind free from bindings and allowing free movement with wisdom. Consistent diligent practice ensures mastery, fostering non-attachment during pursuit of spiritual goals.

#### Realization of Impermanence and Its Role in Fostering Freedom
Perception of the impermanence of all phenomena, the world, and worldly things serves as a foundational realization gained through mental liberation and meditation. This understanding explains the transience without clinging, enabling transcendence of desires and attachments, including sensual cravings, while maintaining freedom in practice. Enlightened individuals naturally comprehend impermanence, which supports abiding in nirvana and avoiding bondage to pursued results or realms.

#### Profound Understanding of Emptiness as True Nature of Existence
Emptiness represents the lack of inherent existence in all phenomena and dharmas, comprehended by enlightened beings as ultimate wisdom through mental liberation and meditation. This realization detaches practitioners from boundaries, realms, and outcomes, providing unhindered freedom and non-attachment. It forms a core part of severing delusions, with insightful wisdom reinforcing this comprehension to ensure transcendence without clinging to any achievements or states.

#### Non-Attachment to Realms, States, and Spiritual Pursuits
Practitioners achieving mental liberation, such as liberated beings and Zen meditators, pursue various realms, states, levels, and existential boundaries without clinging or becoming bound, even when seeking them through practice. This avoidance of attachment to realms or any phenomenon ensures ongoing freedom, preventing binding by pursuits and allowing transcendence of attachments. Mentally liberated individuals engage in these without attachment, maintaining an unbound state.

#### Interconnection Between Wisdom and Mental Liberation
Wisdom and mental liberation mutually reinforce each other, with mental liberation through meditation leading to penetrating understanding of impermanence and emptiness, which in turn facilitates further liberation by severing delusions. This perceptive understanding enables clear perception of worldly transience, recognition of no-self and suffering, and transcendence of desires without attachment. The foundation provided by liberation grants wisdom for non-clinging to realms, attainments, or results.

#### Characteristics and Realizations of Enlightened Beings
Enlightened beings, realized through the holy path and possessing pure minds free from greed, attachment, and delusion, comprehend impermanence, suffering, emptiness, and no-self while abiding naturally in nirvana. They achieve detachment from all realms, phenomena, and liberation's results, pursuing meditative attainments and outcomes without bondage. This state ensures unhindered freedom, with realizations of profound truths like the emptiness of dharmas central to their existence.

#### Transcendence of Sensual Desires and Attachments in Practice
Liberated minds overcome worldly cravings and sensual desires, even during pursuit of meditative results and attainments, by maintaining non-attachment and avoiding bondage. Insightful wisdom supports this transcendence, allowing practitioners to engage in spiritual pursuits unbound. Mental liberation ensures freedom from such attachments, reinforcing the path to nirvana and preventing clinging that could hinder practice.
