### Core Elements of Wisdom, Impermanence, and Liberation in Buddhist Practice

#### Executive Summary
The text outlines interconnected Buddhist principles centered on wisdom as a transformative force cultivated through meditation, precepts, and diligence, which reveals the impermanent and empty nature of all phenomena, dispels afflictions like greed, hatred, and delusion, and enables non-attachment to realms, states, and outcomes. This leads to mental, mind, and heart liberation, transcending suffering, samsara, and the cycle of birth, aging, sickness, and death, ultimately achieving nirvana as unhindered freedom and eternal peace. Key patterns emphasize how realization of impermanence motivates non-negligence, severs attachments, and fosters detachment even from spiritual results, allowing practitioners—such as saints, awakened beings, and arahants—to abide in tranquility without bondage.

#### Wisdom as the Illuminating Force in Spiritual Cultivation
Wisdom emerges as a profound discernment of reality, developed by saints, noble ones, awakened beings, arahants, and holy practitioners via the three core trainings of precepts, meditation, and diligence, including mindfulness, right view, and non-indolence. It acts as a supreme lamp exposing impermanence, suffering, emptiness, impurity, interdependence, no-self, and dependent origination in all phenomena and dharmas, while eradicating ignorance, delusions, the three poisons (greed, hatred, delusion), craving, ego, defilements, worldly deceptions, dualistic views, and sensory disturbances. This discernment clarifies the Four Noble Truths, cause and effect, and the transient nature of mental and physical phenomena like bubbles or dreams, as well as the five aggregates, three realms, fame, profit, pleasures, pains, meditative states, and nirvana itself, free from clinging. Paired with precepts and mind cultivation, it severs affliction roots, breaks samsara's cycle, extinguishes suffering's seeds, and yields non-clinging freedom, complete liberation, eternal stability, supreme bliss, ultimate peace, enlightenment, arahantship, parinirvana, and fearless navigation among dharmas. It enables abiding in tranquility, silence, quietude, stillness, and a vast void-like mind, subduing the mind, avoiding evil paths, generating dispassion, fostering vigilance, protecting body and mind, adhering to true Dharma, transcending merits unstained, and inspiring respect from heavenly beings, deities, and devas. Obstructed by arrogance and mental scattering but supported by humility, spiritual friends, and compassion, wisdom (智慧, zhì huì) allows viewing the world unbound by sensory attachments, pursuing nirvana over worldly joys, detaching from the five aggregates, escaping suffering's sea, and realizing liberation from ignorance, karma, fears, and pain.

#### Impermanence as the Transient Essence of Existence
Impermanence (无常) defines the inherent, profound truth of all existence as transient, changing, fleeting, illusory, insubstantial, and uncontrollable, encompassing worldly phenomena, dharmas, conditioned things, realms, body, feelings, mind, sensory experiences, and cycles of birth, aging, sickness, death, rebirth, and change that bind beings under death's control. Discerned by awakened beings and liberated practitioners through wisdom, meditative contemplation, mindful practices like Zen meditation, and mental liberation, it motivates non-negligence, sustained diligence, and renunciation. Realization cuts greed, hatred, delusion, and the three poisons, generates disenchantment with sensual pleasures, and prevents attachment to states, outcomes, realms, merits, spiritual results, or nirvana. It overcomes habits, defilements, attachments, afflictions, bonds, and worldly traps, leading to non-attachment, unbound liberation, transcendent wisdom, unhindered freedom, inner peace, eternal tranquility, and transcendence of suffering. Recollection of impermanence, alongside death, sustains diligence, prevents slackness, and counters its control through non-indulgence and true Dharma adherence, while mindfulness of its arising and ceasing illuminates defilements and guides toward the right path.

#### Attachment and Its Transcendence through Discernment
Attachment (執著, 貪愛, 貪求) involves clinging to phenomena, sense objects, desires, sensory pleasures, worldly things, conditioned items, realms, states, spiritual attainments, pursuits, outcomes, birth, death, or liberation results like nirvana, arising from negligence and sensory indulgence, binding to suffering, rebirth, and mental bondage, hindering unhindered progress by creating dependency on goals. Awakened beings, wise practitioners, and liberated individuals avoid, sever, eradicate, or transcend it via insight into impermanence, suffering, no-self; wisdom from meditation, mindfulness, and non-negligent practice; and contemplative observation, pursuing spiritual outcomes unbound. Advanced practitioners, through meditative wisdom and insight into impermanence, remain non-attached to states, realms, phenomena, results, nirvana's fruits, or birth-death cycles during pursuits, achieving mental, mind, or heart liberation ensuring non-clinging and unhindered freedom. Wisdom overcomes attachment by breaking delusions, preventing bondage to sensory temptations, meditative states, karma, samsara, realms, mental conditions, outcomes, boundaries, impermanent things, and spiritual goals like nirvana, fostering unobstructed existence even amid temptations.

#### Mental, Mind, and Heart Liberation as States of Unbound Freedom
Mental liberation, mind liberation, heart liberation, and liberation of mind represent profound freedom from attachments, clinging, bonds, defilements, afflictions, greed, hatred, delusion, and the five hindrances (desires, ill-will, sloth, restlessness, doubt), attained through diligent meditative practice including Zen meditation, jhanas stages (initial detachment with rapture and joy, sustained joy, equanimous joy and lightness, pure equanimity and mindfulness), precepts, awareness, concentration, and wisdom completion. It enables insight into impermanence, suffering, emptiness, no-self, impure and selfless nature of phenomena, worldly existence as dream-like illusion, and transience of conditioned items, freeing from sensory bonds, worldly constraints, samsara, realms, outcomes, meditative experiences, and nirvana pursuit without attachment. Results include unhindered ease, ultimate peace, clear untroubled inner state like pure water, joyful equanimous abiding, release from birth-aging-sickness-death cycle, transcendence of impermanence itself, non-binding to states or results, and free movement with wisdom. Achieved by non-indulgent practitioners, it severs afflictions, purifies the mind, and allows abiding beyond bonds, with diligence leading to mastery and transcendence.

#### Emptiness as the Inherent Lack in All Phenomena
Emptiness (空性 or 空), one of the four marks of existence and a worldly truth alongside suffering, denotes the profound lack of inherent, independent, substantial, solid, permanent self, or essence in all phenomena, dharmas, and worldly things, arising from dependent origination and observed through impermanence in body and mind. Realized by awakened beings and practitioners via meditation, wisdom, insight, three trainings, Zen practice, right mindfulness, and meditative discernment, it dispels delusions, false views, illusions, greed, anger, and temptations. This fosters detachment from realms, sensual pleasures, achievements, fears, and nirvana, breaking attachments and afflictions, eliminating self-illusion, purifying the heart, preventing defilement, attaining untainted wisdom, and achieving pure liberation and samsara transcendence.

#### Diligence and Non-Negligence as Catalysts for Progress
Diligence, through consistent non-negligent effort in spiritual practice, develops, cultivates, masters, and attains wisdom alongside meditative achievements, countering impermanence and death's control, evading its traps via true Dharma adherence, and motivating sustained practice to prevent slackness. Non-indolence and non-negligence enable true wisdom mastery, mental liberation, guarding wisdom from obscuration by greed, hatred, and delusion, while penetrating impermanence, suffering, and no-self. Combined with awakening alertness, it prevents laxity, endows right mindfulness and wisdom, purifies the mind from three poisons, allows free dharma navigation without delusion, rapidly realizes the holy path, eternally severs birth-death cycle, leads to supreme bliss, enlightenment, and nirvana abiding. Diligent non-lax persons achieve awakening and holy path swiftly, outpacing others.

#### Interplay of Precepts, Meditation, and Mindfulness in Eradication
Precepts, as ethical foundation and support with wisdom and meditation in threefold training, enable saints, holy beings, practitioners, sages, and noble ones to eradicate afflictions, troubles, pains, suffering, desires; cultivate mind; abide in stillness, tranquility, quietude; gain insight into impermanence and suffering; maintain purity; realize liberation, enlightenment, and spiritual achievement by breaking bonds. Meditation, with right mindfulness, serves as primary means for wisdom cultivation, purifying and stabilizing mind, removing obscurations, eradicating delusions, bonds, three poisons, generating wisdom light dispelling ignorance, providing insight into impermanence, suffering, impurity, emptiness, no-self, true nature, equality, fostering liberations, severing troubles, transcending attachments without binding pursuit, leading to ultimate liberation and non-attachment. Mindfulness generates wisdom escaping samsara, uproots greed, anger, delusion roots, illuminates dharmas' nature via body-mind observation, achieves purification, severs attachment, breaks greed, and achieves liberation transcending rebirth.

#### Awakening, Vigilance, and Saints' Qualities in Transcendence
Awakening involves alertness and sustained effort for meditative mastery, insight into impermanence, suffering, dependent origination, detachment from sensory pleasures and five aggregates in desire realm, purifying mind from three poisons via non-laxity, mindfulness, right knowledge, allowing free dharma entry-exit without delusion, rapid holy path realization surpassing lesser states, eternal birth-death severance, supreme bliss, enlightenment, nirvana abiding. Vigilance generates wisdom as essence and outcome, leading to noble path and nirvana, while wisdom illuminates and protects vigilance path, discerning right way, avoiding deviations. Saints possess wisdom with precepts, discipline, diligence, three trainings, enabling mind cultivation, meditation practice, unshakable peace, stillness, quietude, tranquility, silence, unhindered freedom; vast void-like mind illuminating darkness; severing all afflictions, defilements, troubles, bonds, greed, anger, delusion; transcending attachments, suffering, rebirth cycle to nirvana; profound dharma insight; transcendent peace abiding; devas, gods, heavenly respect; temptation resistance; supreme enlightenment; serene minds. The Awakened One embodies wisdom eradicating afflictions, extinguishing three poisons, revealing reality, illuminating ignorance like lamp or sunlight, breaking delusion, transcending samsara.

#### Suffering, Samsara, and Nirvana as Ultimate Goals
Suffering, inherent to transient phenomena tied to impermanence control attempts, is transcended by wisdom eliminating causes, severing attachments, cutting afflictions, eradicating greed, love, bonds; encompassing birth, aging, illness, death, pains, dharmas' marked suffering. Through contemplation, observation, illumination, understanding, it reveals as fundamental characteristic with impermanence, emptiness, no-self, motivating detachment, breaking illusory discriminations, liberating from sensual bonds, leading to tranquility, ultimate liberation; combined with precepts, severs all suffering by affliction severance. Samsara, as suffering cycle of birth-death, is escaped by wisdom eradicating afflictions, attaining nirvana, permanently leaving cycle. Nirvana, pursued without attachment via wisdom in threefold training, eradicates afflictions like greed, hatred, delusion, craving, ignorance by illuminating reality, impermanence of three realms; severs suffering roots, transcends samsara, breaks birth-aging-sickness-death-rebirth; state of peace, bliss, eternal stillness, irreversible liberation; fused with compassion, supreme unobstructed ease beyond afflictions; insight enables non-attachment even to it, supporting progression without clinging, enhancing liberated freedom, transcending all phenomena.

#### Body Contemplation and Realms in Non-Attachment Practice
Body contemplation observes body as impermanent, impure, composed of four elements without self or permanence, via diligent mindfulness and clear comprehension regardless of posture or focus, generating mindfulness, abandoning body attachment. Realms, as mental, existential, achievement-based states, levels, boundaries, worldly circumstances, or spiritual conditions, are not clung to by liberated beings, enlightened individuals, wise meditators achieving mental liberation through Zen meditation, even when pursued, maintaining freedom and non-attachment during practice; understood via meditative insight, encompassing worldly or spiritual realms where unattached practitioners remain unbound to ensure mental liberation.
