### Path to Awakening: Eradicating Greed, Hatred, and Delusion for Nirvana

#### Executive Summary
The provided descriptions outline the qualities of an Awakened Being, who achieves enlightenment by fully perceiving no-self, emptiness, impermanence, and suffering in all phenomena, while completely eliminating the three poisons—greed, hatred, and delusion—that drive suffering and rebirth in samsara. Through diligent meditation, mindfulness, guarding the six senses, and cultivating wisdom, precepts, and non-laziness, practitioners sever attachments, transcend the three realms, and attain inner peace, ultimate freedom, and the bliss of Nirvana. This process reflects a structured path where eradicating root afflictions leads to liberation from cyclic existence, sensory temptations, and all forms of disturbance, culminating in a state of unconditioned purity and respect from heavenly beings.

#### Core Qualities of an Awakened Being
An Awakened Being represents full enlightenment, achieved by perceiving the no-self, emptiness, impermanence, and suffering nature of all dharmas, including the selflessness of body, mind, and the five aggregates. This state involves eradicating the three poisons, uprooting all afflictions and their seeds, extinguishing defiled actions, and severing attachments and desires, which permanently ends samsara, rebirth, and disturbances like birth, aging, sickness, and death. Such beings remain unaffected by sensory objects, guard the senses with mindfulness, embody right view, right conduct, precepts, inner purity, and profound wisdom, dwell in quiet mind, inner peace, ultimate freedom, and Nirvana's bliss, free from afflictions, worries, or obstructions—likened to space, earning respect from heavenly beings, and serving as exemplars who escape suffering and transcend worldly bounds.

#### Role of the Three Poisons in Binding to Samsara
The three poisons—greed, hatred, and delusion—act as fundamental mental defilements that stain the mind, fuel suffering, and bind beings to rebirth in samsara and the three realms. Triggered by sense contacts, they ensnare individuals in entrapment and temptations through laxity, forming the root of all afflictions, perpetuating various sufferings such as birth and death, and fueling attachments to sensory pleasures. Equivalent to anger and ignorance, these core afflictions must be thoroughly eradicated by practitioners, saints, holy ones, and Awakened Beings via diligent practice, meditation, mindfulness, insight into impermanence, the three learnings, precepts, non-indulgent efforts, and wisdom, resulting in liberation, inner peace, mental clarity, transcendence of suffering, purification, reflection of true reality, and escape from defilement seeds and cyclic existence.

#### Importance of Diligence and Non-Laziness in Practice
Diligence and non-laziness drive continuous progress toward awakening without retrogression, involving persistent effort in practice that distances practitioners from the bonds of greed, hatred, and delusion, preventing falling under their control. This foundational quality maintains vigilance, avoids laxity that leads to sinking into the poisons and degeneration, fosters detachment, enables mind control, and culminates in liberation, inner peace, and mastery of meditation methods. Supported by non-indulgent refinement and mindfulness, it prevents the abyss of suffering and directly enables distancing from afflictions through focused efforts.

#### Guarding the Six Senses to Prevent Afflictions
The six senses—eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, and mind—must be guarded through mindfulness and vigilance to block the arousal of greed, hatred, delusion, or aversion from sensory contacts, thereby preventing affliction growth and sustaining inner purity and focus. Unguarded senses allow external objects to trigger these poisons, generating subsequent defilements, while proper guarding, as practiced by Awakened Beings, maintains mental liberation and avoids sensory temptations that fuel samsara's cycle.

#### Perception of Empty Self-Nature and No-Self in Phenomena
Empty self-nature reveals the lack of inherent existence in the three realms, breaking craving bonds and enabling dual liberation of mind and wisdom. Awakened Beings directly see the no-self essence of all dharmas, including body, mind, and five aggregates, through meditative insight, perceiving selflessness, emptiness, impermanence, and suffering in worldly dharmas and phenomena, which destroys attachments, ends rebirth, breaks affliction roots, severs suffering, and uproots fundamental causes. This realization, combined with wisdom, allows perceiving all dharmas as empty and selfless, transcending worldly bounds while remaining unaffected by the five aggregates.

#### Transcending the Three Realms Through Wisdom
The three realms—desire (sensual), form, and formless—constitute cosmic divisions binding beings in cyclic existence via unsevered afflictions and craving, perpetuating rebirth until viewed as empty and selfless through wisdom. The three poisons bind beings to these realms, requiring their severance for transcendence; insight into their empty self-nature via meditation leads to wisdom liberation, freeing one from craving bonds and influences. Awakened Beings, by eradicating these poisons, escape the realms' grasp, achieving freedom beyond their bindings and realizing Nirvana.

#### Attainment of Nirvana by Eradicating Afflictions
Nirvana emerges as the state for an Awakened Being, reached by permanently ending the affliction cycle, free from the three poisons, samsara's temptations, remaining afflictions, and rebirth, abiding in its bliss and ultimate peace without remainder. Eradicating the three poisons through spiritual practices like meditation, insight, and wisdom directly leads to this attainment, extinguishing defilements, severing all afflictions via precepts, and resulting in quietude, complete freedom, and transcendence. Saints and holy ones share this by cutting off poisons and defilements through diligent cultivation, reflecting true reality unadulterated, like a purified mind mirroring phenomena without distortion.

#### Integration of Meditation, Mindfulness, and Wisdom in Eradication
Meditation, combined with insight and wisdom, eradicates and severs the three poisons, enabling non-negligent practitioners to attain mental liberation; mindful contemplation identifies and uproots their sources, while Zen meditation purifies the mind by dispelling them. Awakened Beings use wisdom to perceive selflessness, emptiness, and impermanence, destroying attachments; saints employ it to cut off the poisons completely. The three learnings lead to their full eradication, with precepts and wisdom providing foundation for severing numerous afflictions. Mindfulness guards sense doors and perceives dharmas' true nature, while persistent non-laziness refines practice to master these methods, fostering detachment and escaping poison bonds.

#### Shared Path of Saints, Holy Ones, and Awakened Beings
Saints, holy ones, and Awakened Beings share enlightenment qualities, eradicating the three poisons—greed, hatred, delusion (or ignorance)—along with other defilements through diligent cultivation, precepts, and wisdom, completely cutting them off for transcendent peace. They overcome suffering forms rooted in these poisons, transcend worldly bounds, and represent the ultimate noble path free from all afflictions. Enlightened beings embody freedom from greed, attachment, and delusion, with mind liberated from obstructions; their transcendence earns devas' respect and allows navigating the world without disturbance.
