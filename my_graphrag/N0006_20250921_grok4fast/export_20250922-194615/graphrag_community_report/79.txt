### Qualities and Attainments of Noble Practitioners and Saints

#### Executive Summary
Noble practitioners and saints embody discipline, wisdom, and diligence to cultivate mindfulness and stillness, enabling the complete eradication of afflictions, transcendence of suffering, and achievement of liberation. These qualities, upheld through ethical conduct and moral integrity, support mental tranquility and inspire respect from gods, with Zen meditation facilitating full realization of these benefits.

#### Discipline as Ethical Foundation
Discipline encompasses precepts and ethical conduct that noble practitioners and saints fully possess and uphold via adherence and moral integrity. This quality combines with wisdom to foster diligent mind cultivation and tranquility, directly supporting the elimination of troubles, suffering, and afflictions, while defining key attributes that lead to their attainments.

#### Wisdom's Role in Complete Eradication
Noble practitioners integrate complete wisdom with discipline to cultivate stillness and eliminate all afflictions entirely. This pairing empowers saints to reach liberation, allowing noble practitioners to perfect their qualities and fully transcend suffering through abiding in mental stillness.

#### Diligence in Mind Cultivation
Diligence manifests in noble practitioners' persistent efforts to cultivate the mind and dwell in stillness, resulting in the cutting off of all afflictions and freedom from suffering. This practice underpins their ability to surpass suffering and achieve perfected states.

#### Attainment of Liberation and Respect
Through discipline, wisdom, and diligent practice, noble practitioners and saints achieve liberation by eradicating all afflictions and transcending suffering. Their accomplishments earn admiration and respect from gods, highlighting the profound impact of these qualities.

#### Benefits of Zen Meditation
Noble practitioners participate in Zen meditation to realize its full advantages, including the eradication of afflictions and attainment of liberation, further reinforced by their endowment with discipline that aids in transcending suffering.
