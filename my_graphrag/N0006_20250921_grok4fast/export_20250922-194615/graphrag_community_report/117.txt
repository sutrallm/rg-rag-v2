# Attainment of Supernatural Powers through Precepts, Meditation, and Wisdom

## Executive Summary
Diligent practitioners achieve supernatural powers, including the divine eye and past life knowledge, by perfecting precepts, meditation, and wisdom. These abilities enable discernment of karma, rebirths, and spiritual roots, guiding beings toward liberation while transcending the cycle of birth and death. Karma binds sentient beings to suffering through actions and their fruits, but non-indolent practice eradicates afflictions, leading to supreme awakening and compassionately supporting others. Supernatural powers serve as tools subordinate to wisdom for ultimate freedom beyond delusions.

## Role of Diligent Practice in Developing Abilities
Extraordinary capabilities arise from non-indolent practitioners who dwell in mindfulness, avoid negligence, and master precepts, meditation, and wisdom. Stability in the Fourth Jhana forms the foundation for these abilities, including flying, manifesting forms, divine eye, divine ear, and past life knowledge. Such practice cultivates a stable mind, eradicates afflictions, and positions practitioners as exemplars respected by devas, enabling compassionate guidance of others based on discerned capacities.

## Functions of the Divine Eye in Guiding Beings
Pure vision, known as the divine eye, allows perfected practitioners to observe karma, rebirths, and the spiritual roots of sentient beings. This power penetrates the causal effects of actions, identifying how evil deeds lead to suffering and good deeds to happiness. By revealing unconscious karmic obstacles from sensory indulgences, it supports leading beings toward liberation, serving as a subordinate tool to wisdom for transcending the cycle of birth and death.

## Impact of Karma on Sentient Beings
Actions and their resultant fruits create entrapment in the cycle of birth and death, where evil leads to abysses of pain and decline, while good fosters happiness. Indulgent immersion in sensory pleasures accumulates unseen karmic obstacles, perpetuating suffering without awareness. This force binds beings to samsara but can be overcome by abandoning evil, cultivating good, developing non-attachment, and applying wisdom, preventing descent into further suffering.

## Utility of Past Life Knowledge for Transcendence
The ability to recollect previous existences and cycles of rebirth provides insight into karma and its fruits across lives, clarifying the wheel of samsara. Cultivated through diligent practice, it helps transcend confusion and delusions, supporting the path to supreme awakening by understanding rebirth patterns and karmic influences.

## Path to Supreme Awakening and Liberation
Non-indolent cultivation of precepts, meditation, and wisdom eradicates afflictions and worldly suffering, surpassing supernatural powers through awakened wisdom. Zen meditation breaks attachments, freeing practitioners from karmic draws into rebirth cycles. This complete path achieves ultimate realization beyond ordinary confusions, with abilities like the divine eye and past life knowledge aiding compassionate discernment and guidance toward liberation.

## Limitations of Supernatural Powers Relative to Wisdom
While concentration in meditation yields supernatural powers for observing beings and cycles, true freedom requires wisdom to eradicate ignorance and surpass these abilities. They function as supportive tools, not ends in themselves, emphasizing that diligence in the full triad of precepts, meditation, and wisdom leads to complete spiritual perfection and respect from devas.
