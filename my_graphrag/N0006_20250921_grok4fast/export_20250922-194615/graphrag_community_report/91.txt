### Stages of Sainthood and Fetters in Attaining Nirvana

### Summary
The Noble Fruits outline four progressive stages of sainthood that systematically eradicate specific fetters, leading from initial breakthroughs in self-perception and doubt to complete liberation from existence and entry into nirvana. The first stage eliminates three foundational fetters, the second severs five lower fetters tied to sensual bonds, the third removes five higher fetters beyond sensual realms, and the fourth achieves eternal freedom from all attachments. This process, supported by three trainings, results in supreme liberation from suffering and samsara.

### Progressive Eradication Through Noble Fruits
The four stages form a sequential path to liberation, with each building on the prior by targeting increasingly subtle bonds. The first stage addresses core misconceptions like self-view, attachment to rites, and doubt. The second extends this by breaking sensual desire and ill-will alongside the initial three. The third focuses on higher attachments, while the fourth culminates in total detachment from existence, enabling nirvana without remainder. This structure underscores a methodical dismantling of samsara's hold, progressing from psychological barriers to existential ones.

### Composition and Role of the Three Fetters
The three fetters—self-identity view, attachment to precepts and practices, and doubt—represent the initial obstacles to spiritual progress, directly broken in the first stage of sainthood. Their elimination establishes a foundation by resolving identity confusion, ritual dependence, and uncertainty about the path, allowing practitioners to advance without these persistent doubts undermining further development.

### Breakdown of the Five Lower Fetters and Their Elimination
Comprising self-identity view, doubt, attachment to rites, sensual desire, and ill-will, the five lower fetters bind individuals to samsara through sensory and emotional attachments. These are fully eradicated in the second stage via diligent practice, severing the cycle of desire and aversion that perpetuates rebirth. This achievement marks a pivotal shift toward liberation, as it removes the most immediate ties to worldly suffering.

### Nature and Removal of the Five Higher Fetters
The five higher fetters involve attachments extending beyond sensual experiences into subtler realms, eradicated solely in the third stage. Their targeted removal in this phase addresses residual bonds that persist after lower fetters are gone, highlighting the need for advanced practice to overcome non-sensory clinging and approach final freedom.

### Attainment of Supreme Liberation
Eradicating the five lower fetters through the three trainings yields supreme liberation, freeing individuals from all bonds and suffering. This state represents a comprehensive release from samsara, achieved once sensual and emotional fetters are overcome, even before higher stages, but fully realized only with the complete sequence leading to the fourth stage's eternal nirvana.

### Culmination in Eternal Nirvana
The fourth stage eternally ends attachment to existence, granting entry into nirvana without remainder and completing the path from all fetters. This final breakthrough signifies the ultimate significance of the prior stages, as it transforms partial freedoms into irreversible cessation of rebirth and suffering.
