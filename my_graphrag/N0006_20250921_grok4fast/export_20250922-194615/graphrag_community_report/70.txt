### Foundations of Shen Tong in Zen Practice

#### Executive Summary
The provided material outlines the interdependent roles of diligent effort and perfected meditative states in generating supernatural powers within Zen practice. Perfected Zen Ding serves as the core foundation for Shen Tong, while Qin Fen ensures sustained progress and application. Sheng Zhe exemplify this through their refinement of practices and overcoming of obstacles.

#### Supernatural Powers Stem from Perfected Meditative Foundation
Shen Tong emerge exclusively from perfected Zen Ding, which acts as the sole basis for these powers. They represent outcomes of consistent meditative cultivation, ensuring that such abilities arise naturally from a refined state of concentration.

#### Diligence as Essential for Meditative Progress
Qin Fen, defined as persistent effort, prevents slackness in practice and drives advancement through Zen Ding levels. This sustained diligence supports uninterrupted cultivation toward liberation, making it a critical element for practitioners.

#### Perfected Zen Ding Enables Supernatural Manifestation
Perfected Zen Ding provides the necessary groundwork for producing Shen Tong, positioning it as an irreplaceable precursor. Without this perfected state, the development of these powers remains unattainable.

#### Exemplars of Diligence in Refinement and Obstacle Removal
Sheng Zhe illustrate Qin Fen by applying it to deepen Zen Ding and eliminate Fan Nao. Their approach highlights how focused effort leads to both enhanced meditative purity and the resolution of internal hindrances.

#### Diligence Requirement for Practical Applications of Powers
Access to the beneficial uses of Shen Tong depends on Qin Fen within Zen Ding practice. This effort ensures that practitioners can fully engage with and utilize these powers in their spiritual development.
