### The Three Poisons: Greed, Hatred, and Delusion in Buddhist Afflictions and Liberation

### Executive Summary
The provided material outlines greed, hatred, and delusion as fundamental afflictions that bind sentient beings to suffering and samsara by obscuring reality and fostering attachment and aversion. These poisons infiltrate the mind through unguarded senses, amplify via heedlessness and indolence, and generate evil karma leading to rebirth in painful realms. Countermeasures include diligent mindfulness, mind contemplation, meditation, wisdom, patience, compassion, and non-indolence, which observe and eradicate these afflictions, enabling concentration, tranquility, and ultimate liberation in Nirvana. Awakened beings and saints fully extinguish the poisons, achieving unconditioned peace, freedom from attachments, and a vast, undisturbed mind.

### Core Nature and Effects of Delusion
Delusion functions as ignorance that obscures true understanding of reality, promoting illusory thinking, attachment, and fear while confusing perceptions and leading to evil karma and superficial sensory enjoyment. It invades through unguarded senses, grows with heedlessness, and dominates the indolent, perpetuating cycles of birth, death, suffering, and rebirth in samsara or evil realms. As part of Mara's army, it controls the mind with false perceptions, hinders enlightenment and spiritual progress, and obstructs liberation unless addressed. Manifesting as an obscuring mental activity that arises and passes, it binds individuals with greed and hatred, causing deviation from the right path. It underlies afflictions by creating illusory bonds and serves as a core defilement eradicated by vigilant practitioners through precepts, mindfulness, quietude, meditation, and wisdom into impermanence, emptiness of sense objects, and true reality.

### Characteristics and Consequences of Hatred
Hatred appears as aversion and anger that disturbs the mind, intrudes via unguarded senses, and amplifies through indulgence, causing mental and physical torment comparable to self-harm or poisoned wine. It generates evil karma, perpetuates rebirth in painful states and evil paths, and blocks spiritual progress, inner peace, meditation, and liberation, leading to downfall if unchecked. Observed as present or absent in mind contemplation, it must be contemplated without reaction to achieve tranquility. As a root defilement and part of Mara's army, it inflicts suffering and fetters the mind to samsara. Indolence permits its domination, exacerbating turmoil and obstructing enlightenment, while laxity binds individuals to it alongside other poisons, resulting in suffering.

### Interconnection of the Three Poisons
Greed, hatred, and delusion collectively bind sentient beings in suffering, preventing awakening, liberation, and escape from samsara, while fostering joy and wisdom through diligent practice and guarded mindfulness. They invade via unguarded senses, trap in evil realms through indulgent actions, and overwhelm even those with good deeds via negligence, leading to ruin and perpetual rebirth pain. Greed binds with hatred and delusion, causing indulgent downfall into samsara. Hatred contributes to their binding, resulting in descent to evil realms. Delusion, with greed and hatred, forms the root of evil karma destroyed through effort, and together they obstruct liberation, foster ignorance and attachment, and poison the mind.

### Role of Mind Contemplation in Overcoming Afflictions
Mind contemplation observes the mind for presence or absence of greed, hatred, or delusion; distraction or concentration; expansiveness or liberation, internally or externally, with diligence, clear comprehension, and mindfulness free from worldly greed and grief. This practice realizes the mind as illusory and impermanent, without clinging to mental states, arising pure wisdom. It involves watching the arising and passing of these poisons without following them, remaining undisturbed, and recognizing their variability to avoid clinging. As one of the four foundations of mindfulness, it calms distraction, brings tranquility, and enables non-reactivity to achieve calm.

### Diligence and Non-Indolence as Protective Forces
Diligence overcomes delusion's control by rooting out confusion through sustained effort, supports wisdom to dispel it, and enables mastery of meditative methods for Nirvana. It counters hatred by preventing its domination, removes it energetically from the mind, and frees from its bonds for supreme liberation. Non-indolence protects practitioners and others from falling into afflictions like greed, hatred, and delusion through alert guardianship like a treasure, fostering clarity, wisdom, and vigilant protection against their intrusion. It counters delusion via concentrated effort for clear insight, overcomes it through non-negligence for clarity, and facilitates hatred's removal by cultivating good dharmas and maintaining mental focus. Laxity and heedlessness, conversely, strengthen delusion and amplify hatred, leading to painful consequences and inattention to the holy path.

### Wisdom's Dispelling of Poisons
Wisdom dispels delusion, ignorance, and delusions, revealing the true nature of all dharmas, illuminating like light in darkness, enabling discernment of right from wrong, and escaping cyclical existence. It fully removes hatred for complete mental liberation and transcends it as one of the three poisons poisoning the mind. Gained from insight into impermanence, emptiness of sense objects, and true reality, it eradicates delusion alongside greed and hatred in enlightened states, allowing transcendence of suffering and abiding in silence. It counters hatred through meditative practice and supports its severing via compassion and quietude.

### Mindfulness and Guarding Sense Gates
Mindfulness guards sense gates to prevent influx of greed, hatred, and delusion, distances from the three poisons, and enables awareness of mental fluctuations to avoid interference from delusions and scattered thoughts. Sustained mindfulness of the mind calms distraction, brings tranquility, observes states like greed, hatred, and delusion in the foundations of mindfulness, and frees practitioners from their bonds. It observes the mind's activities, including arising and passing of poisons, laxity, scattering, and attachments, without reaction.

### Meditation's Path to Concentration and Stillness
Meditation destroys elements of Mara's army like delusion and hatred through guarding, cultivates a focused and concentrated mind for mindfulness, leading to stillness and liberation. It eradicates delusion through mindfulness, masters to avoid binding by the three poisons, and achieves stillness, peace, and freedom from scattering and attachments in Zen practice. Diligent cultivation via meditation abides in quietude, severs afflictions, and allows the mind to gain concentration, calm, tranquility, and insight into impermanence for freedom from distraction, afflictions, and attachments.

### Patience and Compassion Against Hatred
Patience counters hatred, extinguishes anger, makes the mind firm like a diamond, and cultivates inner peace undisturbed by adversity or external conditions. It acts as a sturdy fortress against conflicts and fosters compassion. Compassion counters hatred, calms inner storms, leads to peace, and eradicates it alongside greed and delusion through quietude, completely severing it for transcendence of suffering.

### Attainment of Liberation and Nirvana by Awakened Beings
Awakened beings and saints eradicate delusion, greed, hatred, and other afflictions through wisdom, compassion, and quietude, achieving unconditioned liberation, eternal calm beyond worldly constraints, and a spacious, free mind like the void. They uproot delusion thoroughly for quietude and unconditioned freedom, eliminate hatred entirely to transcend emotional suffering and lack it altogether, enabling a vast mind as space, and abide in quietude, supreme enlightenment, and Nirvana's peace. Liberation severs bonds of hatred and delusion, provides unattached freedom, releases the mind from hatred for quiet joy, and eliminates suffering through their absence, allowing abiding in the realm of quietude.
