### Progression Through Jhanas and Transcendence of Realms

#### Executive Summary
Jhanas represent a sequence of meditative absorptions from the first to the fourth, attained through diligent practice, enabling practitioners to surpass the form realm and the higher formless realm. Advanced meditative states facilitate transcendence of these existential levels, with mastery of higher jhanas allowing progression beyond both.

#### Structure and Stages of Jhanas
Jhanas consist of progressive meditative absorptions numbered from the first to the fourth, forming a structured sequence that practitioners advance through systematically.

#### Role of Diligent Practice in Attainment
Diligent practice serves as the essential method for achieving and progressing through the jhanas, enabling the mastery required to move from initial stages to higher ones and ultimately surpass associated realms of existence.

#### Transcendence of the Form Realm
The form realm, as a foundational level of existence, is transcended by practitioners who attain advanced meditative states, specifically through the achievement of jhanas via sustained diligent effort.

#### Surpassing the Formless Realm
The formless realm represents a higher existential level beyond the form realm, which is surpassed by those who master the higher jhanas, highlighting the elevated capacity of advanced meditative progression to exceed even superior planes of being.
