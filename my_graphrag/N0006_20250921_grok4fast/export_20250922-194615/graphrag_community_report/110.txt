### Vigilant Ones and Heavenly Realms: Rewards of Non-Indulgence

### Executive Summary
Vigilant ones who practice non-indulgence earn widespread respect from both the world and heavenly realms due to their profound merits, leading to a life of joy and avoidance of evil realms. Heavenly realms actively recognize and honor holy beings for transcending worldly suffering through virtuous qualities, aligning with worldly admiration for these practitioners and enabling transcendence of birth and death.

### Vigilance and Non-Indulgence as Foundations of Respect
Vigilant ones cultivate non-indulgence, which directly results in respect from both worldly and heavenly sources, as this practice generates profound merits that are difficult to perceive but serve as the underlying reason for their honored status.

### Joy and Protection from Evil Realms
Through vigilant non-indulgence, practitioners dwell in joy and steer clear of evil realms, highlighting the significant protective role of their discipline, which safeguards their inner treasures much like the wise preserve valuables.

### Heavenly Realms' Alignment with Worldly Recognition
Heavenly realms, as higher planes, extend respect and recognition to holy beings who have overcome worldly suffering via virtuous qualities, joining the world in honoring non-indulgent vigilant ones and underscoring a unified appreciation across realms.

### Transcendence Beyond Birth and Death
The profound merits from non-indulgent vigilance enable vigilant ones to transcend the cycle of birth and death, emphasizing the ultimate significance of their practices in achieving liberation from samsaric entanglements.

### Consistent Honor for Profound Merits
Vigilant ones receive ongoing respect from the world for their merits rooted in non-indulgence, with heavenly realms reinforcing this honor, revealing a pattern of sustained admiration that stems from the depth and subtlety of their virtuous achievements.
