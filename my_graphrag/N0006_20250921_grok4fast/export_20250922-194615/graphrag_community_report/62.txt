### Buddhist Teachings on Greed, Anger, and Liberation

#### Executive Summary
The text outlines greed and anger as central afflictions that drive suffering, evil actions, and the cycle of samsara, binding individuals to sensory desires and worldly attachments. These poisons, alongside delusion, fuel mental torment, separation from loved ones, and barriers to spiritual progress. Liberation from these afflictions is achieved through diligent practice, meditation, wisdom, mindfulness, and compassion, leading to pure mental states, freedom from rebirth, and attainment of Nirvana. Non-indolence and avoidance of temptations enable practitioners to abandon evil deeds, eradicate defilements, and realize impermanence, emptiness, and no-self, resulting in supreme joy and unobstructed peace.

#### Greed as a Core Affliction Binding the Mind
Greed functions as one of three root poisons, equivalent to hatred and delusion, that entangles the mind with desires for sensory pleasures like sights, sounds, smells, tastes, and touches, as well as attachments to worldly phenomena and realms. It appears as consuming vines, a devouring flame, a poison arrow, hunger, thirst, or intoxicating wine, dominating the indolent, invading through unguarded senses, and growing with heedlessness. This affliction distracts from spiritual paths, pulls toward evil actions and enjoyments, and even extends to attachments to liberation itself, perpetuating cyclic suffering unless uprooted through practice.

#### Consequences of Greed in Perpetuating Suffering and Samsara
Greed serves as the source of all suffering, causing sorrow, separation from loved ones, mental unrest, torment like arrow piercings, physical and mental ruin, and eternal bondage in samsara through fueled attachments and evil karma. It binds individuals with hatred and delusion, leading to indulgent downfall into lower realms, endless grief from familial dispersion, and perpetual rebirth if not severed. As part of Mara's army, it hinders pure meditation and Nirvana, staining the mind like defilements and fostering negligence that sustains the cycle of afflictions and worldly fears.

#### Anger as a Parallel Affliction Fueling Torment
Anger, another primary poison alongside greed and delusion, controls and poisons the mind and body like a toxin, manifesting as aversion that perpetuates suffering, restricts progress, and drives afflictions if unchecked. It contributes to cyclic suffering and self-inflicted harm, binding practitioners to unrest and evil realms. Like greed, it arises and fades as a mental state observed in contemplation, but its eradication brings quietude and liberation from dukkha.

#### Role of Diligence and Non-Indolence in Countering Afflictions
Diligence acts as an antidote to greed, enabling escape from its traps, rooting out attachments, abandoning evil actions, and mastering meditation to avoid temptations and domination by poisons. Non-indolence focuses on precepts, continuous effort, and vigilance to distance from greed and anger, preventing their growth through heedlessness or unchecked senses, and fostering a focused mind that abandons unwholesome deeds for purity and progress toward Nirvana.

#### Meditation and Mindfulness as Tools for Eradicating Defilements
Meditation gradually breaks greed and anger by eliminating the three poisons, overcoming Mara's army, and freeing the mind from bondage to the five aggregates. Mindfulness in the present moment observes the arising and passing of greed without attachment, acting as a barrier against invasions by afflictions, revealing their emptiness, and leading to clear, undisturbed states like unpolluted water. Through contemplation in mind observation, it severs sensory cravings and attachments, preventing descent into evil paths.

#### Wisdom and Insights into Core Realities for Transcendence
Wisdom, arising from meditation and diligent practice, eradicates greed, anger, and delusion by cutting off attachments to realms, liberation, and worldly influences, viewing indulgences as dangerous pits. Insights into impermanence extinguish clinging and fears; understanding dukkha cuts off poisons; realization of emptiness and no-self severs bonds through non-attachment and contemplation. This leads to complete liberation, roaming heavenly realms, supreme joy, and a vast, unobstructed mind free from rebirth and afflictions.

#### Compassion and Ethical Practices in Overcoming Anger and Evil Actions
Compassion counters anger, preventing self-harm and enabling progress to Nirvana by choosing non-harm over aversion-fueled deeds. Adherence to precepts and giving purifies greed without attachment, accumulating merit while abandoning evil actions driven by poisons, which result in torment, separation, and ruin. Holy practitioners, through quietude and non-negligent cultivation, fully extinguish these defilements, attaining mental freedom and permanent peace.

#### Attainment of Liberation and Nirvana by Awakened Beings
Awakened beings, saints, and holy ones completely eradicate greed, anger, delusion, and attachments through wisdom, dwelling in quietude, and ending afflictions, transcending samsara for unobstructed freedom as vast as space. This state involves total severance of sensory loves, evil karma, and cyclic suffering, resulting in pure joy, access to heavenly realms, and supreme liberation unbound by worldly pulls or the five aggregates.
