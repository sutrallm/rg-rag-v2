### Doctrinal Processing Protocols

#### Executive Summary
The protocols emphasize preserving core teachings and law meanings without alteration during translation and rewriting into vernacular Chinese. Key elements such as proper names of people and locations must be removed, while metaphors, dialogues, and other components are reorganized into direct doctrinal expressions. An example format guides the structure, with adjustments like numerical sequencing for titles and deletion of the ending verse. However, missing content halts full execution of these steps, ensuring doctrinal integrity remains paramount throughout.

#### Preservation of Core Teachings
Specific aspects of religious teachings and law meanings, referred to as doctrinal details, require intact retention during all stages of processing and revision. This ensures that underlying elements of doctrine are not modified, maintaining their original significance in the transformed output. Key teachings must be upheld directly, forming the foundation of any reorganization.

#### Removal of Proper Names
Names of individuals and locations, identified as characters and places, are designated for complete deletion during rewriting. This step aligns with instructions to eliminate these components, streamlining the content to focus solely on doctrinal essence without personal or geographical references.

#### Adherence to Structural Template
A provided sample structure, known as the example format, serves as the mandatory guide for completing translation and rewriting tasks. It has been reviewed and consulted in queries, dictating the prescribed organization to ensure consistency in output.

#### Reorganization of Expressive Elements
Metaphors and dialogues undergo conversion into direct doctrinal meanings or law expressions as part of the processing. This transformation reorganizes these parts to emphasize underlying teachings, integrating them seamlessly into the core doctrinal framework.

#### Translation into Vernacular Chinese
Conversion to everyday Chinese language stands as a central requirement in the rewriting process. It applies to the entire doctrinal content, with all preservation and deletion rules observed to produce accessible yet unaltered teachings.

#### Adjustments to Titles and Endings
Titles within the content are to be revised into numerical sequences during processing. Additionally, the concluding verse is specified for removal, contributing to a focused doctrinal presentation without extraneous elements.

#### Limitations Due to Missing Content
Absence of the original material prevents advancement of translation, rewriting, and related actions. This gap underscores the need for complete source availability to apply protocols effectively while safeguarding doctrinal details.
