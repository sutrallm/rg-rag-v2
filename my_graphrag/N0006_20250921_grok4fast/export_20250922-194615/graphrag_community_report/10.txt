### Spiritual Foundations: Ding Li, Tan Chen Chi, and Sheng Zhe

### Executive Summary
The text outlines core spiritual concepts where Ding Li represents achieved mental stability that fosters quietude and eliminates fundamental afflictions. Tan Chen Chi identifies greed, hatred, and delusion as root causes of suffering, which are fully eradicated by Sheng Zhe through integrated practices of precepts, wisdom, and concentration. This integration directly links Ding Li with broader methods to achieve complete liberation from these poisons.

### Ding Li as Mental Concentration
Ding Li refers to a state of mental concentration or stability attained via diligent practice. This state enables individuals to dwell in quietude and thoroughly eradicate greed, hatred, and delusion, forming a foundational element in spiritual progression.

### Tan Chen Chi as Root Poisons
Tan Chen Chi encompasses the three primary afflictions—greed, hatred, and delusion—that underlie all suffering. These are entirely extinguished by Sheng Zhe employing precepts, wisdom, and concentration, resulting in the cessation of suffering.

### Sheng Zhe and Their Attainment of Ding Li
Sheng Zhe are those who possess Ding Li, utilizing it to maintain quietude and achieve full elimination of greed, hatred, and delusion, demonstrating their advanced spiritual capability.

### Integration of Ding Li with Precepts and Wisdom
When Ding Li is combined with precepts and wisdom, it facilitates the complete extinguishing of Tan Chen Chi, providing a comprehensive pathway for saints to overcome the root poisons and end suffering.
