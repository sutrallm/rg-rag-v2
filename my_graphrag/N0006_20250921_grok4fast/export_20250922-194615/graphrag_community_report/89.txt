### Path of Non-Lax Practitioners Beyond the Five Fetters and Death Lord

#### Summary
Non-lax practitioners, through vigilant and diligent meditation, eradicate the five fetters of greed, hatred, sloth and torpor, restlessness and worry, and doubt, achieving joy, peace, extended lifespan, and unshakable liberation that transcends the control of the Death Lord, a symbol of mortality over lax beings.

#### Vigilant Nature of Non-Lax Practitioners
Non-lax practitioners maintain constant vigilance to avoid indulgence, engaging in diligent meditation that directly counters laxity and leads to profound internal freedom.

#### Eradication of Binding Forces Through Practice
The five fetters—greed, hatred, sloth and torpor, restlessness and worry, and doubt—act as specific mental afflictions that bind individuals, but diligent practice in meditation severs them completely, resulting in attainable joy and peace.

#### Transcendence Over Mortality's Influence
The Death Lord represents a personified force of mortality that exerts control only over lax beings, yet non-lax practitioners escape this dominion through their diligence, securing an extended lifespan and liberation immune to death.

#### Attainment of Enduring Stability and Freedom
By cutting off the five fetters via Zen meditation and diligent effort, non-lax practitioners reach a state of unshakable stability, likened to an unshakeable mountain, and achieve immortal liberation that extends beyond the reach of death.

#### Outcomes of Diligent Meditation Discipline
Diligent meditation by non-lax persons not only severs the five fetters but also fosters joy, peace, and an extended lifespan, culminating in a liberation that remains firm and beyond mortality's grasp.
