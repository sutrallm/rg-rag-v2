### Guidelines for Editing Doctrinal Materials

### Executive Summary
The provided guidelines emphasize targeted modifications to original content, focusing on eliminating narrative components such as references to individuals and locations while safeguarding all aspects of teachings and doctrines. These requirements, outlined in the processing query, ensure that core principles remain unaltered, affecting only supplementary elements to maintain the integrity of essential messages.

### Elimination of Individual and Location References
Processing instructions require the complete removal of any mentions of people and places within the material. This applies specifically to narrative aspects of the original content, distinguishing them from substantive elements. By targeting these, the modifications avoid impacting foundational principles, as the directive explicitly limits deletions to non-essential descriptive features.

### Safeguarding of Teaching and Doctrine Specifics
All detailed elements related to teachings or doctrines must remain fully intact throughout any changes. The original content's core instructional components are protected, with no alterations permitted to these areas. This preservation is a mandatory aspect of the query's processing rules, ensuring that the essential doctrinal framework is not compromised.

### Integration of Modifications into Processing Workflow
The query establishes these removal and retention rules as integral to the overall text handling process. Modifications are confined to narrative features like references to people and places, while doctrinal details are explicitly exempted. This structured approach in the instructions prevents any unintended disruption to the primary content's integrity.

### Protective Role of Retention Practices
Maintaining doctrinal elements unchanged guarantees that core teachings are insulated from deletions. The guidelines highlight that only narrative components, such as those involving individuals or locations, undergo removal. This selective process upholds the significance of the teachings, allowing modifications to enhance anonymity without diluting the underlying doctrines.
