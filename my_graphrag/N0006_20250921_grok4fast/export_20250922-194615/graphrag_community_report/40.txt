### Eradication of Afflictions and Transcendence of Suffering by Saints, Holy Beings, and Practitioners

#### Executive Summary
Afflictions, rooted in greed, hatred, delusion, ignorance, and negligence, generate suffering across physical, mental, and existential realms, perpetuating the cycle of birth, aging, illness, death, and rebirth in samsara. These disturbances invade lax or scattered minds, causing distraction, agitation, and bondage, while hindering tranquility and liberation. Practitioners overcome them through diligent, non-indolent practice involving precepts for ethical discipline, concentration via meditation and samadhi, and wisdom for insightful discernment of impermanence, emptiness, and no-self. Saints, holy beings, sages, and noble practitioners fully eradicate afflictions via the threefold training, achieving complete transcendence of suffering, inner peace, nirvana, and eternal freedom from rebirth. Such accomplished individuals earn universal reverence, including from devas and celestial beings, and embody compassion to alleviate suffering for all sentient beings.

#### Afflictions Defined and Their Manifestations
Afflictions include mental defilements such as the three poisons of greed, hatred (anger and ill-will), and delusion (ignorance), along with desire, craving, laxity, sloth, restlessness, doubt, recklessness, sensory attachments, ego-clinging, unwholesome states, habitual patterns, scattering, idleness, torpor, and worldly pains. They arise from ignorance, negligence, and unwholesome states, invading lax or scattered minds to produce distraction, agitation, exhaustion, suffering, and bondage, thereby trapping individuals in samsara's cycle of birth, old age, sickness, death, and ongoing existence, while obstructing spiritual progress, inner tranquility, and liberation.

#### Suffering as Inherent to Conditioned Existence
Suffering encompasses all physical and mental pain, distress, dissatisfaction, and torment, including the universal cycle of birth, aging, illness, death, and rebirth in samsara. It stems from afflictions like the three poisons, attachments, ignorance, wrong views, negligence, indolence, indulgence in desires, impermanence, unawareness, evil actions, sensual delusions, the five aggregates, fetters, and evil karma. As one of the four marks of existence and the first of the Four Noble Truths, alongside impermanence, emptiness, and no-self, suffering manifests in endless pains, bindings of worldly life, distractions, illusions, scattered mind, and existential dissatisfaction in all actions and dharmas, perpetuating entanglement like a fish in a net for those who indulge.

#### Interconnection Between Afflictions and Suffering
Afflictions serve as primary causes, roots, and generators of suffering, binding the mind, controlling individuals, and leading to bondage in samsara and all forms of worldly suffering, including birth, aging, sickness, and death. Greed, hatred, and delusion as core afflictions disturb indolent individuals, preventing stable meditation and causing suffering and rebirth. Negligence, indolence, indulgence, laxity, and idleness catalyze afflictions' emergence, growth, and dominance, resulting in mental unrest, torment, downfall into evil realms, and perpetuation of suffering cycles. Eradicating afflictions severs suffering's roots, enabling transcendence and full extinguishment.

#### Practices for Overcoming Afflictions and Suffering
Diligent, non-indolent, and non-negligent practice, including meditation (zen meditation, samadhi, Zen Ding), mindfulness, vigilance, guardianship of senses, and avoidance of idleness, allows practitioners to escape, abandon, overcome, or gradually eliminate afflictions. Precepts provide ethical discipline and non-indulgent living to prevent indulgence and affliction arising, forming a stable foundation for meditation. Concentration through meditative practice and mind cultivation in quietude or tranquil abiding tames the mind, guards senses, and severs affliction roots. Wisdom, developed via insight, right view, meditative contemplation, and discernment of impermanence, emptiness, no-self, and dependent origination, dispels afflictions' darkness, uproots their causes, and breaks attachments. Combined as the threefold training or three learnings, these eradicate afflictions entirely, fostering inner peace, tranquility, purity, disenchantment, and liberation.

#### Role of Mindfulness, Vigilance, and Non-Indolence
Mindfulness guards body and mind against afflictions' intrusion, particularly during meditation, acting as a shield and sword to cut their roots, counteract greed, anger, hatred, and delusion, and sustain distance from worries. Vigilance prevents afflictions like greed and malice from invading via unguarded senses. Non-indolence, non-negligence, and non-laxity promote alertness, continuous effort, and adherence to teachings, avoiding control by afflictions, death, regret, and rebirth cycles. These practices accumulate merits, break affliction bonds, exempt from their dominance, and enable gradual eradication, leading to mental liberation and avoidance of evil paths.

#### Characteristics and Achievements of Saints and Holy Beings
Saints, holy beings, sages, noble practitioners, and awakened ones are enlightened practitioners endowed with precepts, wisdom, diligence, concentration, meditation, compassion, and quietude. They diligently cultivate the mind to abide in tranquility, stillness, silence, mental quietude, and a quiet mind free from obstacles, guarding against distractions with mindfulness. Through these, they completely eradicate, sever, cut off, burn away, or extinguish all afflictions and their seeds, including the three poisons, transcending suffering, worldly pains, samsara, rebirth, and evil realms. This yields ultimate liberation, supreme peace, bliss, purity, enlightenment, nirvana, heart-liberation, and eternal freedom, with practitioners remaining undefiled and compassionately aiming to eliminate suffering for all sentient beings.

#### Tranquility as a State of Mental Abiding
Tranquility is the quiet, peaceful, serene, calm, silent, still, and stable state of the mind, achieved through diligent cultivation, meditation, mind training, precepts, wisdom, and mindfulness. Protected from external interferences, it surpasses worldly pleasures and serves as a gateway to severing afflictions, overcoming indulgences, transcending suffering, and attaining inner peace, ultimate liberation, and complete affliction elimination. Saints and holy beings dwell in tranquility during practices, enabling thorough eradication of troubles, abiding without mental hindrances, and fostering vast, pure hearts as expansive as the void.

#### Reverence from Celestial Beings
Saints, holy beings, sages, and noble practitioners earn reverence, respect, inspiration, and awe from devas, deities, gods, celestial beings, and higher entities due to their eradication of afflictions, transcendence of suffering, fulfillment of precepts, attainment of wisdom, diligent mind cultivation, tranquil abiding, enlightened state, and spiritual accomplishments. These qualities command universal respect in worldly and heavenly realms, with divine beings honoring their mastery, merits, undefiled purity, and exemplary transcendence, inspiring even devas to revere them without disturbance.

#### Path to Liberation and Nirvana
The path to liberation involves eradicating afflictions through non-indolence, diligence, insight, the Noble Path or Holy Path, and the Four Noble Truths, leading to freedom from suffering, samsara, rebirth, and evil paths. Contemplation of suffering, impermanence, emptiness, no-self, and dependent origination evokes disenchantment, breaks attachments, and motivates liberation. Full realization via precepts, concentration, wisdom, and the three trainings achieves nirvana as ultimate peace, complete liberation, eternal freedom from worldly rebirth and suffering, supreme bliss, and permanent end of affliction cycles, with enlightened beings remaining eternally free through non-attachment.
