### Xin Jie Tuo in Zen Meditation Practice

#### Executive Summary
Xin Jie Tuo represents mental liberation through Zen meditation, fostering discernment of impermanence without attachments, which enlightened practitioners like the Xiu Xing Zhe achieve via Zen Ding to attain unhindered freedom, while avoiding Zhi Chi to realms or outcomes.

#### Xin Jie Tuo as Mental Liberation
Xin Jie Tuo emerges from Zen meditation, liberating the mind to recognize the impermanent nature of phenomena, free from attachments to meditative states or liberation results, thereby granting unhindered freedom for practitioners.

#### Avoidance of Zhi Chi by Enlightened Practitioners
Enlightened individuals steer clear of Zhi Chi, defined as clinging to realms or liberation fruits, maintaining unbound freedom even while pursuing meditative discernment, ensuring no hindrance in their practice.

#### Role of Zen Ding in Achieving Xin Jie Tuo
Zen Ding, attained through dedicated practice, guides the Xiu Xing Zhe toward Xin Jie Tuo, facilitating deep discernment into Wu Chang Ben Zhi while preventing Zhi Chi, thus supporting overall liberation.

#### Discernment Enabled by Xin Jie Tuo
Through Xin Jie Tuo, the Xiu Xing Zhe gains clear discernment of Wu Chang, the fundamental impermanence, reinforcing the practice's focus on non-attachment.

#### Freedom from Attachments via Xin Jie Tuo
Xin Jie Tuo specifically liberates practitioners from Zhi Chi to any states or liberation outcomes, securing complete unhindered liberty as the core outcome of Zen meditative progress.
