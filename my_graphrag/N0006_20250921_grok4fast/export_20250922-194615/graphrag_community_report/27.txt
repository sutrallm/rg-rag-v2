### Guidelines for Reformatting Dialogues and Metaphors in Buddhist Scriptures

#### Executive Summary
The provided guidelines outline a systematic approach to processing elements in Buddhist scriptures and related materials, focusing on transforming conversational and figurative components into clear, doctrinal expositions. Dialogues are restructured into direct statements of core principles, while metaphors are simplified into straightforward explanations of dharma truths. This preserves essential Buddhist doctrines through reorganization and conversion, ensuring clarity and fidelity to underlying meanings in both scriptures and associated documents.

#### Reformatting Dialogues into Doctrinal Statements
Conversational elements, such as spoken parts in Buddhist scriptures and related materials, are reorganized into direct statements that capture their essential meaning. This involves rephrasing them as straightforward explanations of core ideas or converting them into doctrinal statements. The process ensures that dialogues contribute to elucidations of dharma principles, excluding non-essential components to maintain doctrinal purity.

#### Conversion of Metaphors into Direct Explanations
Figurative expressions in Buddhist scriptures and related materials are simplified by reorganizing and converting them into direct, non-figurative statements. This transformation turns illustrative language into clear expositions of doctrinal truths, emphasizing the underlying dharma meanings without retaining metaphorical forms.

#### Preservation of Core Dharma Principles
Buddhist doctrines within scriptures and related materials are maintained through direct restatements, focusing solely on core principles like truths and teachings. By deriving these from restructured dialogues and converted metaphors, the guidelines highlight a process that organizes content for clarity, ensuring that dharma meanings are elaborated and preserved as foundational elements.

#### Integration of Dialogues and Metaphors with Dharma Elucidations
The handling of dialogues and metaphors directly supports expositions of dharma by restructuring conversational parts into doctrinal explanations and converting figurative language into elaborations of core truths. This interconnected approach in Buddhist scriptures and related materials reorganizes original content to produce straightforward, organized presentations of ethical and legal principles inherent in Buddhist teachings.
