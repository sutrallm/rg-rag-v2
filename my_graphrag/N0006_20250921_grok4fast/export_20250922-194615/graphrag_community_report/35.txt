### Spiritual Attainment and Eradication of Afflictions among Noble Practitioners

#### Executive Summary
The text outlines the path of advanced spiritual figures such as noble ones, holy practitioners, and saints, who cultivate stillness via precepts, wisdom, diligence, and mindfulness. This practice enables the total elimination of afflictions and suffering, breaks the cycle of rebirth, and secures reverence from celestial beings, resulting in liberation and supreme bliss.

#### Cultivation of Stillness as a Path to Inner Peace
Stillness emerges as a serene mental state of stability and tranquility, attained by noble ones, holy practitioners, and saints through ongoing training of the mind, meditation, and subduing mental disturbances. This abiding in quietude represents a foundational practice that fosters peacefulness by redirecting focus inward, allowing practitioners to maintain undisturbed composure amid life's challenges.

#### Role of Precepts in Supporting Mental Discipline
Precepts serve as an essential foundation for noble ones and holy practitioners, integrating with wisdom to enable the development of stillness and the removal of afflictions. Full adherence to these ethical guidelines provides the moral structure necessary for mind cultivation, ensuring purity and focus that prevent the resurgence of troubles and facilitate transcendence beyond ordinary suffering.

#### Integration of Wisdom and Diligence in Eradicating Afflictions
Wisdom, combined with diligent effort, equips holy practitioners and saints to sever all forms of mental pains and cycles of birth, aging, and death. This duo drives the profound quietude of the mind, leading to complete extinction of afflictions and the attainment of liberation, as it uncovers the root causes of distress and empowers consistent practice toward enduring freedom.

#### Mindfulness as a Tool for Maintaining Stillness
Mindfulness enables practitioners to sustain inner purity and concentration, allowing noble ones and holy figures to dwell steadily in stillness. By training the mind to remain present and undistracted, it counters the pull of afflictions, supports the transcendence of samsara, and contributes to the overall cessation of suffering through heightened awareness.

#### Outcomes of Stillness Leading to Transcendence and Respect
Abiding in stillness through meditation and mind training results in the total cutting off of afflictions for saints and noble practitioners, granting them freedom from rebirth cycles and supreme bliss. This achievement not only elevates their spiritual status but also evokes admiration from devas and deities, underscoring the transformative power of these practices in inspiring even heavenly reverence.

#### Interconnection of Spiritual Qualities in Ultimate Realization
Noble ones and awakened beings embody a harmonious blend of precepts, wisdom, diligence, and mindfulness, which collectively propel the mind toward profound stillness and the eradication of all troubles. This unified approach ensures the complete transcendence of suffering, positioning these practitioners as exemplars of liberation and highlighting how integrated qualities amplify the effectiveness of spiritual cultivation.
