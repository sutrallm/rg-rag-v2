### Avoiding Disturbances in Meditation Through Focus and Right Mindfulness

#### Executive Summary
The text describes disturbances as turmoils, distractions, or interferences that practitioners counter in meditation by focusing the mind and maintaining right mindfulness. This approach enables distancing from such elements, achieving the essence of meditation, and realizing inner peace. Repeated emphasis is placed on the interconnected nature of meditation and disturbances, where concentrated practice with mindfulness serves as the key method to escape distractions.

#### Defining Disturbances in Meditation
Disturbances consist of turmoils, distractions, or interferences that disrupt meditation. A focused and mindful mind inherently avoids these, as evasion forms part of meditation's core purpose. This avoidance highlights how such elements contrast with the desired state of mental clarity during practice.

#### Role of Right Mindfulness in Concentrated Practice
Right mindfulness is actively upheld by practitioners during periods of focused mind concentration. This maintenance directly supports escaping distractions and interferences, allowing the mind to remain undistracted and aligned with meditation's goals.

#### Process of Distancing from Disturbances
Practitioners achieve separation from disturbances through intentional focus of the mind combined with sustained right mindfulness. This dual effort during meditation practice facilitates a deliberate withdrawal, preventing engagement with turmoils or distractions that could hinder progress.

#### Interconnection Between Meditation and Disturbances
Meditation inherently involves navigating disturbances, with its essence defined by staying away from them via focus and mindfulness to attain inner peace. This linkage underscores that true meditative realization requires overcoming such interferences as a foundational step.

#### Enabling Effects of Right Mindfulness
By sustaining right mindfulness alongside a focused mind, practitioners effectively remain detached from disturbances throughout concentrated sessions. This capability ensures that the practice remains undisturbed, promoting the full realization of meditation's peaceful outcomes.
