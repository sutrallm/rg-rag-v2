### Qualities and Attainments of Sages

#### Executive Summary
Sages represent advanced spiritual practitioners distinguished by their possession of precepts, wisdom, discipline, and insight. Through diligent cultivation and subduing of the mind, they achieve tranquility, completely sever afflictions, and transcend the cycle of birth-death suffering. This transcendence earns them respect from devas and heavenly beings, highlighting their perfected state of existence free from pain.

#### Core Qualities of Sages
Sages embody perfected attributes including precepts for spiritual discipline, profound wisdom to thoroughly address afflictions, and insight as an integral part of their advanced practice. These qualities form the foundation of their holy status, enabling them to eradicate suffering and gain reverence from higher beings such as devas and heavenly entities.

#### Practices for Mind Cultivation
Sages engage in diligent efforts to subdue and cultivate the mind, fostering a state of abiding tranquility. This disciplined approach directly counters afflictions, allowing practitioners to break free from the painful cycle of birth and death by completely severing these root causes of suffering.

#### Transcendence of Suffering
By applying wisdom and diligence, sages fully eliminate afflictions, resulting in the transcendence of birth-death suffering. This achievement positions them beyond ordinary existence, where they dwell in tranquility and command respect even from deities and heavenly beings, underscoring the profound impact of their spiritual discipline.
