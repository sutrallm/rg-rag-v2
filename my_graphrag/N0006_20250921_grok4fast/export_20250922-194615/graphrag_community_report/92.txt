### Path to Supreme Bodhi via Non-Indulgent Practice, Threefold Training, and Holy Path

#### Executive Summary
Non-indulgent practice forms the foundation for spiritual cultivation by exerting effort to avoid greed, anger, and delusion, sustaining focus and mindfulness to achieve inner peace and liberation. The Threefold Training in morality, concentration, and wisdom purifies practitioners, eliminates afflictions, severs attachments to the Five Aggregates, and leads to nirvana beyond samsara. Dhyana provides meditative absorption for mental liberation, while the Holy Path culminates these efforts, enabling transcendence of rebirth, escape from bad realms, and attainment of Supreme Bodhi's eternal joy and security. Persistent diligence and avoidance of heedlessness are essential to master these elements and realize complete freedom from suffering.

#### Non-Indulgent Practice as Foundation for Affliction Elimination
Non-indulgent practice requires continuous exertion and avoidance of indulgence to distance practitioners from greed, anger, and delusion, preventing the mind from wandering and enabling escape from worldly distractions. This core element sustains focus through mindfulness, blocks external disturbances like a fortress, and directly leads to inner peace, liberation, and entry into the path toward enlightenment. Persistent cultivation of this practice results in eternal peace, mastery of dhyana, and transcendence of rebirth cycles by eliminating the roots of afflictions.

#### Threefold Training's Role in Complete Purification
The Threefold Training integrates morality, concentration, and wisdom as a complete path perfected by Noble Ones to purify individuals, eliminate all suffering and afflictions, sever attachments, shatter ignorance, and eradicate affliction roots. Morality provides precepts as one pillar, concentration fosters meditative focus for mental purification, and wisdom uproots defilements through contemplation. Diligently completing this training destroys ignorance, achieves nirvana's supreme bliss, enables permanent departure from samsara's suffering, and grants ultimate peace beyond birth, aging, illness, and death.

#### Holy Path as Culmination of Diligence and Good Dharma
The Holy Path emerges from persistent avoidance of indulgence and heedlessness, combined with non-indolent effort in cultivating good dharma, leading only those who follow it to mastery of the Threefold Training. It severs attachments, eradicates afflictions, liberates from bonds, transcends rebirth, escapes bad realms, and realizes Supreme Bodhi. Vigilance and diligence enable access to this sacred way, unlike heedlessness which fosters afflictions, ultimately resulting in freedom from cycles of suffering and attainment of eternal joy and security.

#### Dhyana's Function in Achieving Mental Liberation
Dhyana involves focused mindfulness and absorption, progressing from initial states to higher levels through non-heedless diligence, allowing escape from distractions, dissipation, worries, and defilements to attain inner peace. As a key aspect of concentration in the Threefold Training, it serves as a stepping stone to mind and wisdom liberation, fosters non-attachment, and aids in transcending worldly bonds. Only unwavering diligence masters dhyana, enabling practitioners to remain unstained and free from mental wandering.

#### Five Aggregates' Binding Influence and Path to Freedom
The Five Aggregates—form, feeling, perception, formations, and consciousness—compose existence and bind individuals to suffering via attachments, desires, and greed, perpetuating cycles in realms like the desire realm. Liberation requires mindful observation of their impermanence, emptiness, and no-self nature through dharma contemplation and wisdom, severing attachments to achieve complete freedom. Awakened beings engage with the aggregates without being bound or deluded, transcending their influence while navigating worldly life, thus breaking the cycle of birth and death.

#### Supreme Bodhi's Attainment and Lasting Benefits
Supreme Bodhi represents ultimate awakening via the Holy Path, freeing practitioners from sufferings of birth, aging, illness, and death, and granting eternal enjoyment of nirvana's bliss. It results from completing the Threefold Training, following the Holy Path, and persistent non-indulgent practice, leading to transcendence of attachments to the Five Aggregates and escape from bad rebirths. This state provides everlasting joy, security protected from disturbances, and permanent liberation beyond samsara's unfavorable cycles.
