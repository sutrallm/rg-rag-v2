### Foundations of Mindfulness for Purification and Liberation

#### Executive Summary
The four foundations of mindfulness—body, feelings, mind, and dharmas—form the basis of a practice centered on continuous observation of internal and external phenomena to cultivate awareness. This approach purifies the mind by eliminating greed, hatred, and delusion, while recognizing impermanence and impurity in the body and all dharmas. Through diligent application, practitioners distance themselves from worldly disturbances and laxity, achieving inner peace, steady progress, and ultimate liberation.

#### Continuous Observation Across Four Foundations
Practice involves sustained awareness of body, feelings, mind, and dharmas to maintain mindfulness and foster mental purification. This comprehensive observation addresses internal and external aspects, leading to the elimination of attachments and delusions. The practitioner engages in this across various postures—walking, standing, sitting, and lying down—to ensure unbroken awareness, ultimately resulting in mind liberation from afflictions.

#### Body Contemplation and Recognition of Impermanence
Observing the body in postures and activities reveals its impermanent nature, both internally and externally. This contemplation dispels attachments by highlighting the body's transient qualities, allowing practitioners to maintain mindfulness without clinging. Such recognition directly counters delusions tied to physical form, promoting detachment and clearer awareness.

#### Body Contemplation and Understanding of Impurity
The body's impure nature becomes evident through focused contemplation, which eliminates attachments and associated delusions. By examining its components and processes, practitioners overcome misguided views of permanence or purity, using this understanding to purify the mind and avoid entanglement with bodily concerns.

#### Feelings Contemplation and Observation of Sensations
Examining the arising and passing of sensations forms a key element of the practice, tracking feelings in their temporary states. This observation builds awareness of how feelings contribute to mental states, aiding in the overall purification process by preventing unchecked reactions and fostering equanimity.

#### Mind Contemplation and Monitoring Mental States
Tracking the mind's varying states ensures integral awareness within the foundations. This involves noting shifts in mental conditions to identify and address afflictions, supporting the broader goal of liberation by clarifying the mind's operations and reducing its volatility.

#### Dharmas Contemplation and Comprehensive Coverage
Observing aggregates, hindrances, and senses rounds out the foundations, providing a complete framework for awareness. This contemplation addresses phenomena beyond the personal, revealing the impurity inherent in all dharmas and generating wisdom that penetrates their essential nature.

#### Elimination of Greed, Hatred, and Delusion
Mindfulness across the four foundations purifies the mind specifically from greed, hatred, and delusion. Greed fades through detached observation that distances from desires; hatred dissipates via sustained awareness of internal and external triggers; delusion clears by recognizing impermanence and impurity, leading to profound mental clarity and freedom.

#### Distancing from Worldly Disturbances and Laxity
Diligent engagement in these practices enables practitioners to remain unaffected by external distractions and internal laxity. This creates an unobstructed path for steady advancement, akin to moving forward in a clear space, resulting in inner peace and sustained focus away from disruptive influences.

#### Path to Liberation Through Mental Purification
The foundations collectively guide toward liberation by systematically purifying the mind from afflictions. Continuous practice builds wisdom into the impurity of dharmas, reinforcing detachment and awareness, while ensuring the practitioner stays committed to observation for ultimate release from cyclic suffering.
