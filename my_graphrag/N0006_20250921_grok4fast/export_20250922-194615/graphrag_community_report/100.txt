### Enlightened Beings and Their Path to Liberation from Afflictions

#### Executive Summary
Enlightened beings known as Sheng Zhe achieve complete freedom from mental afflictions, worldly suffering, and the cycle of birth and death through the cultivation of moral precepts, wisdom, concentration, and diligence. These practitioners eradicate greed, hatred, delusion, and related disturbances via focused meditation and disciplined practice, attaining inner stillness and earning reverence from celestial and heavenly beings. The interconnected qualities of discipline, mental stability, and insightful contemplation enable the severing of root poisons and transcendence of birth, aging, sickness, and death, highlighting a structured progression from diligent cultivation to ultimate liberation.

#### Eradication of Mental Afflictions Through Core Practices
Sheng Zhe fully sever Fan Nao, encompassing worries, disturbances, troubles, greed, hatred, delusion, and other afflictions, by embodying perfect precepts, wisdom, concentration, and diligence. This process, supported by Zen Ding and focused meditation, allows practitioners to dwell in quietude, break the cycle of suffering, and achieve inner calm, demonstrating how disciplined mental cultivation directly counters worldly distractions and leads to complete freedom from birth and death.

#### Achievement of Mental Stability and Stillness
Ding Li, as mental concentration developed through diligent practice, enables Sheng Zhe to abide in Ji Jing, a state of stillness that facilitates the complete elimination of greed, hatred, and delusion. Combined with Jie Lv and Zhi Hui, this stability extinguishes Tan Chen Chi, the three root poisons, underscoring the foundational role of sustained focus in refining the mind and escaping disturbances for profound inner peace.

#### Transcendence of Fundamental Sufferings
Sheng Zhe overcome Ku, the sufferings of birth, old age, sickness, and death, by eradicating all afflictions through wisdom and meditation, which provide insightful views to disrupt the chain of mental troubles leading to liberation. This transcendence marks the significance of contemplative practices in breaking free from worldly attachments and pains, allowing noble practitioners to navigate meditative states and worldly existence without bondage.

#### Cultivation of Mindful Mental States
Sheng Zhe diligently cultivate Xin Nian, a focused and mindful state of the mind during meditation, alongside Qin Fen to refine Zen Ding and distance from Fan Nao such as distractions and worries. Paired with Zheng Nian, these states support separation from disturbances, illustrating how intentional mental refinement empowers practitioners to progress toward enlightenment and embody complete qualities of diligence and focus.

#### Interconnection of Moral Discipline and Enlightenment
Jie Lv, as moral precepts upheld by noble practitioners, forms a core trait of Sheng Zhe, fully supporting the development of concentration, wisdom, and mental cultivation to eliminate afflictions. When perfectly fulfilled, these precepts aid in severing Fan Nao and achieving transcendence, revealing their essential function in the path to enlightenment as a supportive foundation for all liberating practices.

#### Reverence from Celestial Beings for Accomplished Practitioners
Zhu Tian, heavenly beings, and Tian Jie Zhong Sheng develop respect toward Sheng Zhe for their embodiment of discipline, wisdom, concentration, diligence, and liberation from afflictions. Perfectly accomplished noble ones inspire this veneration through their eradication of root poisons and transcendence of suffering, emphasizing the universal recognition of these qualities as markers of supreme spiritual attainment among celestial and all beings.

#### Integration of Three Trainings for Complete Liberation
Noble ones integrate the three trainings of morality, concentration, and wisdom to fully eradicate Fan Nao, enabling transcendence of Sheng Si De Tong Ku and worldly pains toward Nirvana. Zhi Hui, as wisdom possessed by Sheng Zhe, contemplates Ku to break worldly traction, while Ding Li and Jie Lv extinguish Tan Chen Chi, highlighting how this unified approach allows diligent Xiu Xing Zhe to become Sheng Zhe and freely dwell in Jing Qui Zhuang Tai.
