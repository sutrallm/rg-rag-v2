### Path of the Awakened One to Liberation from Samsara

#### Executive Summary
The Awakened One achieves liberation from the cycle of birth, death, and rebirth—known as samsara—through the integration of precepts, meditation, wisdom, and mindfulness. This path severs attachments and afflictions, enables transcendence of sensual pleasures, and leads to supreme bliss and eternal quietude free from suffering. Awakened practitioners earn respect from celestial beings due to their virtuous qualities, illuminating the truth of the world and embodying the perfected holy path.

#### Transcendence of Samsara by the Awakened One
The Awakened One escapes the endless wheel of samsara, which involves repeated birth, aging, illness, and death driven by afflictions, by perfecting the holy path and extinguishing these afflictions. This liberation allows abiding in tranquility and the joy of Nirvana, free from suffering, as indolence leads to entrapment in this cycle like a dark abyss, while vigilant practice provides escape.

#### Role of Wisdom in Illuminating Truth
Wisdom enables the Awakened One to reveal the truth of the world, uncovering realities beyond sensual pleasures and samsara. Through this illumination, attachments are severed, afflictions cut off, and the mind is cultivated to experience supreme bliss, transcending the cycle of rebirth and earning respect from celestial beings for embodying such clarity.

#### Integration of the Three Learnings for Eradication of Afflictions
Precepts, meditation, and wisdom—the three learnings—equip practitioners to thoroughly eradicate the roots of afflictions, leading to complete liberation and eternal freedom from suffering. Founded on precepts, this comprehensive approach cuts off desires and indolence, allowing the Awakened One to abide in quietude and joy after observing the body and mind through mindfulness.

#### Unmoved Stance Toward Sensual Pleasures
The Awakened One remains unaffected by sensual pleasures, which are desires that bind others to samsara, by transcending them via wisdom and the holy path. This detachment results in profound joy and liberation, positioning the Awakened One beyond worldly cycles and illuminating a path to supreme bliss untainted by such attachments.

#### Respect Earned from Celestial Beings
Celestial beings develop admiration for the Awakened One due to virtuous conduct rooted in precepts and wisdom, as well as the transcendence of samsara and afflictions. This respect highlights the significance of the holy path, where perfected practitioners inspire heavenly entities through their embodiment of liberation and illumination of worldly truths.

#### Achievement of Supreme Bliss and Nirvana
Supreme bliss arises for the Awakened One upon fully severing attachments and attaining liberation, providing eternal freedom outside the cycle of rebirth and suffering. Nirvana represents this state of joy and quietude, achieved through the three learnings and vigilant practice, marking the culmination of the holy path beyond birth, death, and sensual entanglements.
