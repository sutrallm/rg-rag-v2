### Qualities and Transcendence of Holy Beings

#### Executive Summary
Holy beings achieve transcendence over worldly suffering through strict adherence to precepts, cultivation of wisdom, diligent mind training in tranquility and meditation, and complete eradication of afflictions. These practices enable them to surpass pains of existence and earn respect from devas and celestial beings in heavenly realms.

#### Defining Features of Holy Beings
Holy beings represent advanced practitioners who fully embody precepts and wisdom while diligently cultivating the mind in tranquility through meditation. This integration allows them to eradicate all afflictions, sever troubles, and transcend suffering, distinguishing them from ordinary existence.

#### Role of Precepts and Wisdom in Attainment
Precepts combined with wisdom form the foundational qualities of holy beings, supporting their ability to abide in tranquility, cut off afflictions, and reach a liberated state. These elements enable the exhaustion of troubles and the surpassing of worldly pains.

#### Practices in Mind Cultivation and Meditation
Diligence in mind cultivation leads holy beings to abide in tranquility, facilitating the severing of afflictions and the transcendence of suffering. Meditation practice reinforces this effort, allowing advanced practitioners to evolve into holy beings by fully integrating these methods.

#### Eradication of Afflictions and Transcendence
Holy beings thoroughly exhaust all afflictions and troubles through precepts, wisdom, tranquility, and diligent cultivation, thereby transcending worldly suffering. This process ensures they surpass the pains inherent in existence, achieving a state beyond ordinary troubles.

#### Respect from Devas and Celestial Beings
Devas and celestial beings in heavenly realms honor holy beings for their precepts, wisdom, liberation from afflictions, and transcendence of suffering. This recognition stems from the inspirational qualities and diligent practices that holy beings demonstrate.
