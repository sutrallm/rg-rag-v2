### Mindfulness Foundations Targeting Hatred and Greed

#### Executive Summary
Mindfulness practice, through its four foundations—body, feelings, mind, and dharmas—purifies mental states by observing impermanence in sensations and emotions, directly countering hatred, anger, greed, and desire. Contemplation of feelings focuses on arising and passing of painful, pleasant, and neutral sensations to prevent attachment and aversion, while mind contemplation examines states like greed and anger to avoid delusion. Supported by the three trainings, these practices fully eliminate afflictions, fostering mental purity and liberation through continuous internal and external awareness.

#### Contemplation of Feelings Prevents Attachment to Pleasure and Aversion to Pain
By observing the arising and passing of painful, pleasant, and neutral sensations both internally and externally, mindfulness avoids being led by feelings, distancing practitioners from greed through detachment from pleasant sensations and from hatred by countering aversion to pain. This process highlights sensations' impermanent nature, enabling mental purification as a core element of the foundations.

#### Contemplation of Mind Reveals Impermanence of Emotional States
Examining the mind's conditions—such as with or without greed, with or without anger, distracted or concentrated—through internal and external mindfulness uncovers the transient quality of these states, preventing delusion by emotions and directly addressing anger to maintain clarity. This observation purifies the mind from afflictions like hatred and greed.

#### Foundations of Mindfulness Purify Through Comprehensive Observation
Systematic awareness of body, feelings, mind, and dharmas distances from greed and hatred by fostering continuous observation, with feelings contemplation emphasizing impermanence in sensations and mind contemplation targeting specific mental afflictions. This integrated approach achieves mental purity by avoiding emotional entanglements.

#### Three Trainings Eradicate Afflictions Completely
The three trainings fully eliminate hatred, anger, greed, and desire, building on mindfulness foundations to provide complete liberation from these mental impurities. They support the observances in feelings and mind contemplations, ensuring thorough purification beyond mere distancing.

#### Mindfulness Targets Afflictions for Mental Liberation
Hatred, anger, and greed represent key mental afflictions that mindfulness practice addresses through awareness and observation, leading to purity and liberation. Continuous internal and external focus on phenomena like sensations and mental states prevents being overwhelmed by these forces, emphasizing impermanence as the underlying principle for overcoming them.
