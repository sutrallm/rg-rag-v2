### The Three Trainings: Morality, Concentration, and Wisdom in Eradicating Afflictions

#### Executive Summary
The provided material outlines the central role of the three trainings—morality, concentration, and wisdom—in achieving ultimate liberation by noble ones through the complete elimination of core afflictions such as greed, hatred, and delusion. Mindfulness serves as a key practice for purifying the mind by overcoming delusion via sustained awareness, supporting the broader fulfillment of these trainings.

#### Fulfillment of the Three Trainings by Noble Ones
Noble ones achieve the complete fulfillment of morality, concentration, and wisdom, enabling the total eradication of afflictions and the attainment of ultimate liberation. This embodied practice directly targets the root causes of mental impurities, ensuring a path to freedom from suffering.

#### Role of Mindfulness in Overcoming Delusion
Delusion, identified as a primary affliction akin to ignorance, is observed and overcome through mindfulness cultivation, leading to mind purification. Sustained awareness in this practice directly addresses and removes this obstacle, fostering clarity and mental purity.

#### Eradication of Greed via the Three Trainings
The three trainings of morality, concentration, and wisdom serve to completely eradicate greed alongside other afflictions. This comprehensive approach ensures that greed, as a fundamental hindrance, is fully eliminated, contributing to the overall purification process.

#### Elimination of Hatred through the Three Trainings
Hatred is fully eliminated by the three trainings of morality, concentration, and wisdom, in conjunction with other afflictions. This targeted eradication removes hatred as a source of mental disturbance, aligning with the broader goal of affliction-free liberation.

#### Complete Removal of Delusion, Greed, and Hatred
Delusion, along with greed and hatred, is completely eliminated through the three trainings of morality, concentration, and wisdom. This holistic elimination of the primary afflictions underscores their interconnected nature and the trainings' efficacy in achieving total mental freedom.
