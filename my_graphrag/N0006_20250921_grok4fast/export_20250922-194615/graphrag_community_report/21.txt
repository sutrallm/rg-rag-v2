### Body Contemplation Practices and Realizations

### Executive Summary
Body Contemplation serves as a foundational practice within the Four Foundations of Mindfulness, emphasizing observation of the body's impermanence and impurity to cultivate mindfulness, recognize its composition from four great elements, and achieve non-attachment by realizing the absence of self and permanence.

### Core Components of Body Contemplation
Body Contemplation entails observing the body as impermanent and impure, practiced diligently with clear comprehension and mindfulness, irrespective of posture or whether the focus is internal or external. This process generates mindfulness and leads to understanding the body as composed of four elements, devoid of self or permanence, ultimately resulting in the abandonment of attachment to the body.

### Composition and Realization of the Body
The four great elements form the entirety of the body, a realization attained through Body Contemplation that highlights the body's lack of self and impermanence.

### Purpose of Observing Impermanence
Body Contemplation centers on examining the body's impermanence to foster non-attachment.

### Role in Abandoning Attachment
Practitioners engage in Body Contemplation by scrutinizing the body's impermanence and impurity, which directly contributes to relinquishing attachment.

### Integration Within Broader Practices
Body Contemplation constitutes one of the four core practices that make up the Four Foundations of Mindfulness.

### Combined Observation of Key Qualities
Body Contemplation involves contemplating both the impurity and impermanence of the body.

### Elemental Structure Recognition
Through Body Contemplation, the body is understood as consisting exclusively of the four great elements.
