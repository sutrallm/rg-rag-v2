<heading>Principles of Zen Meditation and Spiritual Attainment<\heading>
- **Essence of Zen Meditation**: A mindful, focused mind distances one from worldly disturbances, fostering inner peace by escaping dissipation and afflictions.

- **Importance of Diligent Practice**: Persistent effort in following teachings prevents subjugation by death or Mara; constant vigilance against dissipation ensures mastery of Zen methods.

- **Wisdom Beyond Attachment**: Meditation yields heart liberation and insight into impermanence, freeing practitioners from attachment to any state or pursuit, even in seeking results.

- **Qualities of Saints**: Endowed with precepts and wisdom, saints cultivate the mind, abide in stillness, eradicate afflictions, and transcend suffering, earning the gods' respect.
