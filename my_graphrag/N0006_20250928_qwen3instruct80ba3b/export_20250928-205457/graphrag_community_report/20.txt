**Title: Zezhuo, Wusuode, and the Nature of True Liberation in Spiritual Practice**

**Summary**  
True liberation arises not through striving or acquisition, but through the complete cessation of clinging to experiences, outcomes, or states. Attachment to spiritual goals, including the notion of being liberated, perpetuates the illusion of self. Liberation is realized only when all seeking ceases—when the mind recognizes its inherent completeness and relinquishes all conceptual grasping. Those who have transcended striving, known as Shengzhe, embody this state, having gone beyond all desires to attain or avoid any condition.

**Attachment to Spiritual Goals Reinforces the Illusion of Self**  
Clinging to experiences, states, or outcomes—termed zezhuo—sustains the false sense of a permanent, separate self. This attachment arises even in spiritual practice, where individuals may seek liberation as if it were a reward or destination. The more one strives to attain a spiritual state, the more the mind reinforces its own boundaries and desires, preventing true freedom.

**Liberation Is the Cessation of Seeking, Not the Acquisition of a State**  
Wusuode, or non-attainment, reveals that Nirvana or Niepan is not something to be gained, achieved, or constructed through doctrine, ritual, or supernatural means. It is the recognition that no self exists to be liberated and no external condition needs to be fulfilled. True freedom is found in the cessation of all desire to become something different—when the mind stops seeking and realizes it has never been incomplete.

**Meditative Clarity Dissolves Attachment Through Non-Interference**  
Chanding, or direct, non-attached observation, produces a stable and clear awareness that naturally unwinds clinging. This is not an effort to change or improve the mind, but to observe without grasping. Through this practice, attachment loses its hold not by force, but by the mind’s quiet recognition of its own unaltered nature.

**The Shengzhe Have Transcended All Concepts of Attainment, Including Liberation Itself**  
Those known as Shengzhe no longer identify with the idea of being liberated, enlightened, or free. Their realization is beyond all conceptual frameworks—they do not desire to be free, nor do they desire to remain bound. The absence of any agenda to be or not to be marks their condition as fully beyond all dualities, including the spiritual duality of liberation versus delusion.

**Wusuode Is the Complete Resolution of Zezhuo**  
The only pathway to liberation is the total release of the desire to attain. Zezhuo cannot be overcome through greater effort, better techniques, or deeper understanding—all of which remain forms of attachment. Only when the impulse to reach, fix, or acquire something—however spiritual—ceases entirely does true freedom arise. Wusuode is not a destination; it is the stillness that follows the end of all striving.
