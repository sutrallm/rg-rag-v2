**Title: The Threefold Training of Jiedinghui as the Path to Liberation for Zhongsheng**

**Summary**  
The unified threefold practice of ethical discipline, meditative concentration, and wisdom forms the indispensable path through which all sentient beings can overcome greed, hatred, and delusion, thereby realizing their innate Buddha-nature and attaining liberation. Each component is mutually reinforcing: ethical conduct enables stable concentration, which in turn generates liberating wisdom. Absence of any one element results in instability or delusion. Only when all three are fully developed do they culminate in the perfected state of a saintly being, directly leading to the extinction of the three root afflictions and the realization of nirvana.

**Ethical Discipline Enables Meditative Stability**  
Ethical discipline creates the foundational conditions necessary for the mind to settle into deep concentration. Without moral grounding, the mind remains agitated by the consequences of unskillful actions, preventing the Stillness required for meditative depth. This sequential relationship establishes discipline not as an end in itself, but as the essential precursor to the mental clarity needed for higher practice.

**Meditative Concentration Generates Liberating Wisdom**  
The focused and undistracted state cultivated through meditative practice directly gives rise to wisdom. This wisdom is not conceptual but transformative—it arises from direct insight into the nature of reality, enabling the dissolution of attachments and misperceptions. Concentration, thus, functions as the active bridge between outward ethical behavior and inward liberation.

**The Triad Is Indivisible for True Liberation**  
No single element of the threefold training can produce full awakening on its own. When ethical discipline is neglected, concentration lacks moral grounding; without concentration, wisdom remains theoretical; without wisdom, discipline becomes rigid and wisdomless. Their interdependence means that abandoning even one undermines the entire process, leading to incomplete or deluded progress.

**All Sentient Beings Possess the Capacity for Awakening**  
Every being inherently contains the potential for enlightenment, obscured only by habitual delusions and clinging. This potential is not conditional upon status, prior attainment, or external circumstance. The universal accessibility of the path removes any hierarchy among practitioners—liberation is available to all through the same threefold method.

**The Perfected State Is Defined by Harmonious Unity of the Three**  
A saintly being is not distinguished by extraordinary powers or esoteric knowledge, but by the seamless integration of unblemished conduct, unwavering mental stability, and liberating insight. This unity is not an accumulation of qualities but their dynamic resonance—each sustaining the others in continuous, self-reinforcing harmony.

**The Threefold Training Extinguishes the Root Afflictions**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion are not overcome through suppression or willpower alone, but through the systematic unfolding of the triad: ethical discipline curbs greed’s expression, concentration stills hatred’s turbulence, and wisdom uproots delusion at its source. Together, they constitute the precise mechanism for ending suffering, making the threefold training the singular pathway to nirvana.
