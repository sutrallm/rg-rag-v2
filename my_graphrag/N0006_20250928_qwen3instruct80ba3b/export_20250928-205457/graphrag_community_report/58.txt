**Title: Fannao, Wuai, and Jiedinghui: The Roots of Suffering and the Path to Liberation**

**Summary**  
Suffering in cyclic existence arises from afflictions rooted in craving and mistaken identity, particularly the belief in a permanent self and ownership. These afflictions—manifesting as greed, hatred, and delusion—are sustained by attachment to sensory experiences and the five aggregates. Their eradication is achieved through wisdom that perceives impermanence and non-self, supported by the unified practice of jiedinghui, which directly dismantles the three root mental poisons. Liberation is contingent upon the complete cessation of these afflictions.

**Afflictions Stem from Craving and Misidentification**  
The core source of suffering is craving, which drives attachment to sensory pleasures and the mistaken belief that the five aggregates constitute a lasting, desirable self. This misperception fuels the three root mental poisons—greed, hatred, and delusion—binding beings to continuous cyclic existence.

**Wisdom Dissolves the Root Afflictions**  
Understanding impermanence and non-self directly counteracts craving and ignorance. When reality is seen clearly, greed and delusion lose their foundation and naturally dissolve, removing the emotional and cognitive distortions that sustain suffering.

**Liberation Requires the Unified Practice of Jiedinghui**  
The eradication of greed, hatred, and delusion is accomplished not by isolated efforts but through the integrated development of jiedinghui. This triadic practice is the operational means by which the afflictions are uprooted, leading directly to liberation.
