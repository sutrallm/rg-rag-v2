**Title: The Sage: Liberation Through the Eradication of Greed, Hatred, and Delusion**

**Summary:**  
The Sage has achieved complete freedom through the total removal of greed, hatred, and delusion, resulting in a mind that is unshakably calm, morally pure, and free from all attachments. This state is sustained by balanced compassion and direct insight into reality, particularly the absence of a permanent self and the empty nature of phenomena. The Sage’s inner state is likened to empty space—boundless, unstained, and free from disturbance—enabling continual mindfulness and transcendence of rebirth cycles.

---

**Eradication of Greed, Hatred, and Delusion Defines Liberation**  
The Sage has wholly uprooted greed, hatred, and delusion—not as partial improvements, but as complete annihilations that mark the threshold of true freedom. These three are not merely subdued; they are extinguished as the foundational defilements binding beings to suffering and rebirth. Their removal is not symbolic but transformative, serving as the sole criterion for the Sage’s realized state.

**Wisdom and Compassion Are Interdependent Foundations of the Path**  
Liberation is not wrought by insight alone nor by compassion alone, but through their seamless union. Insight reveals the suffering inherent in all conditioned existence, while compassion motivates action without expectation or attachment to outcomes. This balance ensures that wisdom does not descend into detachment devoid of care, and that compassion does not devolve into emotional entanglement.

**The Mind Is Like Empty Space: Unblemished, Limitless, and Free**  
The inner condition of the Sage is not described as heightened brilliance or occupied stillness, but as void-like—devoid of clinging, bias, or conceptual boundaries. This metaphor conveys a mind untouched by attachment, not even to spiritual attainments, reflecting total non-attachment and freedom from all forms of bondage, internal or external.

**Pure Moral Conduct Serves as the Ground for Insight**  
Ethical living is not an end in itself but the essential foundation upon which profound insight arises. The Sage’s moral discipline is not rigid adherence but a natural expression of inner clarity, cultivated not for reward but as the necessary stabilizer for the mind’s ability to see reality without distortion.

**Transcendence of Rebirth Arises from Realizing Emptiness and the Illusion of Self**  
Delusion, particularly the belief in a permanent, separate self, is dispelled through direct experiential insight into the nature of phenomena. This realization dismantles the root of samsara—not through belief or effort alone, but through unmediated perception of emptiness. With this, the cycle of rebirth is not escaped through force, but dissolved through understanding.

**Non-Attachment Is the Expression of Realized Wisdom**  
The Sage’s freedom from clinging is not passive indifference but an active, luminous release—even from spiritual experiences. This extends beyond material desires to include attachment to states of peace, insight, or spiritual identity. True freedom is demonstrated by the absence of any fixation, however subtle, ensuring that no form of bondage, even sacred ones, remains.
