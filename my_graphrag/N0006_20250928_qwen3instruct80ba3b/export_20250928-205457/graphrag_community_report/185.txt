**Title: The Path to Liberation Through the Realization of Non-Self**

**Summary:**  
Liberation from greed, hatred, and delusion, as well as transcendence of birth and death, is achieved only through the full realization that there is no inherent self. This understanding is attained by observing the five aggregates as impermanent and not constituting a permanent identity. The direct experience of constant arising and passing away in body and mind dismantles the illusion of a lasting self.

**Observing the Five Aggregates Reveals the Absence of a Permanent Identity**  
The five aggregates—form, feeling, perception, mental formations, and consciousness—are the focal point for contemplation. By examining them closely, one discovers that none can be claimed as “I” or “mine.” This direct observation is the method through which clinging to a fixed self is uprooted.

**Impermanence is the Key to Dissolving the Illusion of Self**  
When one sees how all mental and physical phenomena arise and vanish moment to moment, the belief in a stable, enduring self naturally collapses. This constant flux exposes the fallacy of permanence, making it impossible to sustain attachment to a separate, unchanging identity.
