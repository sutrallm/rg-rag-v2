**Title: Dependent Origination, the Three Poisons, and the Liberation of Saintly Beings**

**Summary**  
All phenomena arise through interdependent causes and conditions, with no independent self or permanent essence. This understanding dismantles the illusion of autonomy, leading to the realization of impermanence, non-self, and emptiness. The root of suffering lies in ignorance—the mistaken belief in permanence, satisfaction, and a self—which gives rise to greed, hatred, and delusion. These three afflictions fuel craving, aversion, and karma, perpetuating the cycle of birth, death, and rebirth. Liberation is achieved only through the complete eradication of these poisons, sustained by moral purity, continuous mindfulness, and direct insight into dependent origination. Those who attain this state transcend emotional reactivity, worldly attachments, and the cycle of suffering, embodying inner stillness and equanimity, and are revered even by celestial beings.

**Dependent Origination as the Foundation of Liberation**  
All phenomena emerge not independently but through interdependent conditions. This principle reveals that nothing possesses an inherent or permanent essence, directly undermining the illusion of self. Because conditions are transient, all things are inherently unstable and fleeting. Recognizing this causal structure exposes the falsity of permanence and self-existence, serving as the philosophical basis for non-self and emptiness. Ignorance of this truth initiates the chain of suffering and rebirth; conversely, its recognition is the key to breaking the cycle.

**The Three Poisons Sustain the Cycle of Rebirth**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion are the originating causes of suffering and continued existence. Greed arises from the false belief that external phenomena can provide lasting fulfillment; hatred stems from misunderstanding suffering and reacting with aversion; delusion is the core misunderstanding of reality—mistaking impermanent, conditioned phenomena as stable, satisfying, and self-owned. Together, these afflictions generate karma and deepen attachment, forcing beings to remain trapped in endless cycles of birth and death. Their complete removal is the defining mark of spiritual freedom.

**Ignorance Is the Root of All Afflictions**  
Ignorance is not mere lack of knowledge but the active misperception of reality: seeing the transient as permanent, the unsatisfactory as pleasurable, and the conditioned as possessing an independent self. This foundational delusion generates the three poisons—greed, hatred, and delusion itself—and is the sole reason samsara persists. No amount of suppression of craving or anger can uproot suffering without addressing this fundamental error. Overcoming ignorance demands the direct realization that no self exists independently of causes and conditions.

**Saintly Beings Transcend Through Moral Purity and Mindfulness**  
Those who have attained liberation are characterized by purified conduct, the complete absence of the three poisons, and unwavering mindfulness. They observe all thoughts and sensations as they arise and pass, without clinging or rejecting. This practice prevents sensory input from igniting craving or aversion. Their inner stillness is not passive but the result of rigorous mental discipline. They remain untouched by external conditions, having severed all ties to birth, aging, sickness, and death. Their state is not self-proclaimed but recognized by others, including celestial beings, due to its profound purity and equanimity.

**Liberation Requires the Direct Realization of Non-Self**  
Freedom from suffering is not achieved through ritual or belief but through the experiential understanding that no self exists apart from interdependent processes. Recognizing that all things are conditioned reveals that no permanent “I” can be found—this is non-self. The dissolution of the self-concept directly eradicates the illusion that fuels greed, hatred, and delusion. This realization is not intellectual but a transformative seeing that dismantles attachment at its source. It is achieved only through diligent practice, meditation, and sustained attention to how reality truly functions.
