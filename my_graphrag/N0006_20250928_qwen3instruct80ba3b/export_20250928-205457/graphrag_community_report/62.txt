**Title: Nirvana and the Three Poisons in the Path to Liberation**

**Summary**  
Liberation, known as Nirvana, is the complete cessation of suffering achieved through the eradication of greed, hatred, and ignorance—the Three Poisons. This state is marked by perfect equanimity, the absence of clinging or rejection, and freedom from the cycle of birth and death. It is not attained through external means or suppression, but through direct realization of truth in the present moment. The Four Noble Truths frame this path, and those who have fully extinguished the Three Poisons are described as enlightened beings who have transcended cyclic existence.

**The Extinguishing of the Three Poisons Leads to Liberation**  
Greed, hatred, and ignorance are identified as the fundamental sources of all suffering and cyclic existence. Their removal is not a matter of forceful suppression but of recognizing their empty nature. When these mental defilements are fully extinguished, the mind becomes unbound, resulting in Nirvana—the state of ultimate peace and freedom from rebirth.

**Nirvana Is Equivalent to Wu Zhan Xin**  
Nirvana is explicitly described as the experiential condition of Wu Zhan Xin—non-clinging, non-rejecting, and perfect equanimity. This state arises not through effort to control experience, but through the direct realization of non-self, which naturally dissolves attachment and aversion. The mind in this state neither grasps at pleasure nor resists pain.

**Enlightened Beings Have Eradicated the Root Causes of Suffering**  
Those described as enlightened beings are defined by their complete absence of greed, hatred, and delusion. Their liberation is not theoretical but experiential—they have transcended the cycle of birth and death because they have fully uprooted the Three Poisons, which are the sole origin of affliction and samsara.

**The Four Noble Truths Provide the Framework for Attaining Liberation**  
The path to Nirvana is structured by the Four Noble Truths, which articulate the nature of suffering, its origin in the Three Poisons, its cessation, and the way leading to that cessation. This framework directly guides the realization of truth that results in liberation.
