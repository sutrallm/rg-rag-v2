**Title: Wuwo Shishi and Yin Yuan He He: Realization of No-Self Through Conditioned Arising**

**Summary**  
The realization of no-self emerges from direct observation of all phenomena as conditioned, impermanent, and without an inherent owner. This understanding dissolves attachment to the ego, naturally cultivating a mind that does not cling. The principle of conditioned arising serves as the foundation for this realization, explaining how all experiences arise dependently and lack a fixed core.

**Observation of Conditioned Phenomena Leads to Dissolution of Ego-Clinging**  
When all physical and mental phenomena are seen as arising from causes and conditions, the illusion of a permanent self collapses. This recognition removes the basis for ego-clinging, as nothing is experienced as belonging to or constituting a fixed “I.”

**Conditioned Arising Forms the Basis for Understanding Impermanence and No-Self**  
The principle that all phenomena depend on interrelated causes and conditions reveals their transient nature. This dependency exposes the absence of an independent, unchanging essence, making the concepts of impermanence and no-self observable rather than abstract.

**Realization of No-Self Naturally Cultivates Non-Abiding Mind**  
Once the notion of a self is no longer held as real, there is no anchor for attachment or aversion. The mind becomes unbound from clinging, not through effort or suppression, but as a direct consequence of seeing reality as it is—without a center to hold onto.
