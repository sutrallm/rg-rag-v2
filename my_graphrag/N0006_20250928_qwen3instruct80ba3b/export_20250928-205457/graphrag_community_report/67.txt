**Title: Transcending Root Afflictions: Chenhui, Tanyu, and Hunchen in Duan Chu Fan Nao Practice**

**Summary:**  
Chenhui (hatred), Tanyu (craving), and Hunchen (dullness) are identified as core mental afflictions that sustain suffering. These states arise not from external conditions but from Wuming (ignorance), which wrongly perceives a fixed self and clings to experiences as inherently desirable or threatening. Duan chu fan nao, the practice of ending afflictions, does not suppress these states but dissolves them through direct observation of their conditioned, impermanent, and selfless nature. Those who have transcended Chenhui no longer experience aversion, having uprooted the delusion that fuels it. The method centers on wisdom-based awareness, not force, to reveal the false construction of these mental patterns and allow their natural collapse.

**Chenhui arises from the illusion of a separate self**  
Aversion and resistance manifest when experiences are perceived as threats to an imagined, fixed identity. This misperception, rooted in ignorance, constructs hatred as justified, though it only causes self-harm and suffering. Observing this pattern as fabricated allows it to unravel without resistance.

**Tanyu sustains suffering through attachment to impermanence**  
Craving for pleasure is driven by the mistaken belief that enjoyable experiences offer lasting satisfaction. When observed as transient and devoid of inherent self, attachment loses its grip, and craving dissolves naturally without suppression.

**Hunchen obstructs clarity by masking awareness**  
Mental dullness reduces vigilance in practice, creating a fog that prevents clear seeing. It is not an independent state but an affliction treated through the same wisdom-based observation used for hatred and craving—cultivating mindfulness to restore luminosity and presence.

**Duan chu fan nao targets Wuming as the foundational source**  
All afflictions—hatred, craving, and dullness—are traced back to ignorance. The method does not address symptoms directly but uses wisdom to dismantle the root delusion of self, thereby eroding the conditions that give rise to each affliction.

**Tranquility in Shengzhe arises from complete transcendence of aversion**  
Those who have fully released Chenhui no longer react with resistance, signaling a profound shift: they no longer perceive any experience as inherently threatening to a self that no longer exists. Their freedom is not suppression but seeing through the illusion that created aversion in the first place.
