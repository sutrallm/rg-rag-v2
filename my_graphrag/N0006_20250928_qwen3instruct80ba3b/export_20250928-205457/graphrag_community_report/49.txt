**Title: Zhengjian, Zhengxing, and Chanding in the Path to Liberation**

**Summary**  
Liberation is achieved only through the unified practice of right view, ethical conduct, and meditation. Right view, rooted in dependent origination, impermanence, suffering, and non-self, is the essential guide that directs all spiritual practice. Without it, meditation becomes a distraction and ethical conduct lacks transformative power. Ethical behavior stabilizes the mind, enabling clear perception, while meditation deepens the experiential realization of right view. Together, these elements dismantle delusion and lead to the cessation of suffering.

---

**Right View as the Foundation of Liberation**  
Right view is the initial and guiding element of the path, rooted in the understanding of dependent origination, impermanence, suffering, and non-self. It is not theoretical knowledge but direct realization that suffering originates from clinging, not external circumstances. This understanding prevents attachment to meditative states or psychic powers and ensures all practice is aligned with the true nature of reality. It is the direct cause of liberation, as only through seeing the absence of a permanent self in all phenomena can one attain freedom from delusion.

**Ethical Conduct as the Condition for Mental Clarity**  
Ethical behavior, synonymous with jie, prevents mental disturbance and creates the stable foundation upon which clear perception arises. It is not an isolated practice but one that reinforces and is reinforced by right view. Without ethical conduct, the mind remains agitated, making sustained meditation and deep understanding impossible. The alignment of action with Dharma ensures that the path remains grounded and undistorted.

**Meditation as the Tool for Realizing Wisdom**  
Meditation alone does not lead to liberation. It must be united with right view to penetrate reality and dissolve ignorance. While meditation allows for undistorted perception, it is right view that gives it direction and purpose. This union transforms concentration from a state of mere stillness into a vehicle for experiential wisdom. Through this synergy, theoretical understanding becomes lived realization.

**Interdependence of Right View and Ethical Conduct**  
Right view and ethical conduct are mutually sustaining. Correct understanding informs morally grounded action, while consistent ethical behavior thickens the soil in which wisdom takes root. This reciprocity ensures that intellectual clarity does not become detached from lived experience, and moral conduct does not become superficial ritual. Their integration is necessary to avoid spiritual delusion.

**Dependent Origination and Emptiness as the Basis for Non-Self**  
The understanding that all phenomena arise from causes and conditions and lack inherent existence is the philosophical core validating the realization of non-self. This conceptual framework makes possible the direct seeing of impermanence and the dissolution of attachment. Without this foundation, the insight into non-self remains shallow or mistaken, and the path risks falling into metaphysical errors.

**Wisdom as the Transformation of Right View**  
Wisdom is not separate from right view but its deepened, experiential form. Theoretical comprehension of the Four Noble Truths and dependent origination becomes actualized when meditative clarity reveals them as immediate truth. This transformation is what distinguishes the noble one—those who have fully realized and embodied this understanding—from those who merely study it.
