**Title: Non-Clinging and the Path to Inner Liberation**

**Summary:**  
Liberation from suffering and cyclic existence is achieved solely through inner transformation, not external practices. This transformation arises from non-clinging — the steady refusal to grasp or reject experiences — which cultivates inner stillness and dissolves delusion. True silence is not the absence of sound but the absence of mental attachment, and it is identical to liberation itself.

**Non-Clinging Dissolves Delusion**  
By refusing to attach to or resist phenomena, the mind no longer falsely perceives experiences as permanent or tied to a fixed self. This breaks the cycle of misperception that sustains suffering.

**Inner Stillness Equals Liberation**  
Freedom from mental agitation, attachment, and aversion constitutes true silence. This state is not dependent on physical environment but on the quality of mental engagement, and it is the very definition of liberation.

**Wisdom Manifests as Non-Clinging**  
The practical embodiment of inner wisdom is the consistent posture of non-clinging. It is not a passive state but an active, continuous discipline that directly leads to release from suffering, surpassing all ritual or ascetic effort.
