**Title: The Holy Being: Moral Conduct, Wisdom, and Transcendence**

**Summary:**  
A holy being is defined by the complete eradication of greed, hatred, and delusion—the root afflictions that bind ordinary existence. This state is sustained by an unshakable foundation of pure moral conduct and true wisdom, which together enable a mind as open and undisturbed as empty space. Such a being dwell in the world without being moved by its conditions, having severed all attachment and inner turmoil.

**Moral Conduct as the Ethical Foundation**  
Pure moral conduct is not a superficial practice but the essential ethical basis upon which spiritual realization is built. Without this foundation, the cultivation of wisdom and the transcendence of afflictions cannot occur. It is the disciplined and ethical framework that supports all higher states of being.

**Wisdom as the Source of Transcendence**  
True insight wisdom, when fully developed, works in tandem with moral conduct to dismantle the roots of suffering. This wisdom is not theoretical but experiential, allowing the practitioner to see through illusion and no longer be governed by desire or aversion.

**Eradication of Root Afflictions**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion are not merely subdued but utterly severed. The cessation of greed removes clinging; the elimination of hatred brings inner calm; and the removal of delusion dissolves confusion. Together, these three are the pillars of bondage, and their complete eradication defines the holy being.

**Mind Like Empty Space**  
The mind of the holy being is characterized by purity, stillness, and freedom from disturbance. It is not passive emptiness but an active state of non-attachment—unpolluted by pleasure or pain, unstuck by preference or aversion, and undisturbed by external circumstances. This condition is the natural result of moral discipline and wisdom in harmony.
