**The Path of Jiedao: Precepts, Concentration, and Liberating Wisdom**

**Summary**  
The path of Jiedao is a structured, interdependent sequence beginning with moral discipline, progressing through mental focus, and culminating in liberating wisdom. Moral conduct is the necessary foundation that calms the mind, enabling sustained meditative concentration. Without this stability, penetrating wisdom cannot arise. Wisdom is the ultimate destination, emerging only when both ethical behavior and mental clarity are fully developed.

**Foundational Role of Moral Discipline**  
Ethical conduct serves as the essential first step in the path. Without adherence to precepts, the mind remains unsettled by remorse and unrest, making it impossible to attain the stillness required for deeper practice. Moral discipline is not optional—it is the bedrock upon which mental training is built.

**Meditative Concentration as the Enabling Medium**  
Sustained, undistracted focus is the central mechanism through which mental agitation is reduced and inner stability is achieved. This state of meditative concentration is not an end in itself but the indispensable condition that prepares the mind for the emergence of liberating understanding.

**Wisdom as the Culmination**  
The progressive development of moral discipline and meditative concentration leads directly to wisdom—the final and highest achievement. This wisdom is not intellectual but transformative, capable of dissolving delusion. It arises only when the preceding stages are fully realized; no substitute exists for their prior cultivation.
