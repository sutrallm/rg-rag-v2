**Title: Chaoguo Zhuzhe, Huijian Wuchang, and Wuwo Guanzhao: Pathways to Nirvana through Non-Attachment and Impermanence**

**Summary:**  
The core path to liberation involves three interlinked practices: recognizing impermanence, observing the absence of a permanent self, and cultivating transcendent non-attachment. These practices are not isolated but progressively reinforce one another—sustained awareness of transient phenomena dismantles clinging and self-centeredness, which in turn enables the realization of non-self. This culminates in the manifestation of transcendent wisdom that relinquishes even meditative attachments, leading directly to complete freedom from suffering.

**Recognizing Impermanence Dissolves Clinging**  
Contemplating all experiences—pleasure, pain, physical forms, and emotions—as fleeting, like dew or lightning, gradually erodes attachment and resistance. This sustained observation, practiced daily through Zhengnian, weakens the grip of desire and aversion, creating the conditions necessary for freedom.

**Observing Non-Self Unmakes the Illusion of Identity**  
When all phenomena are seen as arising from conditions, impermanent, and lacking inherent existence, the belief in a fixed “I” or “mine” dissolves. This specific meditative mode of awareness directly undermines the foundation of suffering by revealing the constructed nature of selfhood.

**Transcendent Non-Attachment Realizes Nirvana**  
The culmination of recognizing impermanence and observing non-self is the emergence of transcendent non-attachment wisdom. This state is not merely the absence of craving but the complete release from clinging—even to the experience of meditation or enlightenment. It is the active realization of nirvana, where suffering ceases because attachment no longer exists.
