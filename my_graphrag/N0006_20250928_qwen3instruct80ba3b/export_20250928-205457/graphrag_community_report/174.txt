Title: 五蕴与法如幻的虚妄本质

Summary:  
The five aggregates serve as the immediate object for observing the absence of self and the nature of emptiness. When these aggregates are seen as illusory, the realization of emptiness arises. The characterization of all phenomena as illusory functions as a concrete metaphor to illustrate their false, non-intrinsic nature.

Observing the Five Aggregates as Illusory  
The five aggregates are not abstract concepts but direct points of observation through which the absence of self and the nature of emptiness are realized. Recognizing them as illusory—like a mirage or a dream—is the pathway to understanding their lack of inherent existence.

All Phenomena Are Like Illusions  
The description of all phenomena as illusory is not poetic embellishment but a precise explanatory framework. It conveys that everything experienced is devoid of intrinsic reality, appearing momentarily without foundation, thus reinforcing the understanding of absence of self and emptiness by anchoring the abstraction in a tangible analogy.
