**Title: Jiexie, Chenlao Ji Daochang, and Zhongjie Shixiang: The Path and State of Liberation**

**Summary**  
Liberation (jiexie) is achieved through the complete cessation of suffering by eliminating attachment, aversion, and ignorance via wisdom (zhihui). It is not attained by escaping worldly experiences but by transforming them through mindful awareness, as taught in chenlao ji daochang. Those who have attained liberation are free from the cycle of birth and death and no longer bound by greed, hatred, or delusion. The ultimate state of liberation, zhongjie shixiang, is characterized by a mind as empty as space—beyond duality, conceptual grasping, and cause-and-effect logic.

**Liberation Through Wisdom**  
True liberation arises not through external avoidance but through the dissolution of ignorance and attachment by wisdom. This wisdom directly ends suffering, as it removes the roots of cyclic existence. It is the essential and immediate cause of freedom, acting not as a distant goal but as the active force that dismantles the conditions sustaining suffering.

**Troubles as the Path to Freedom**  
Worldly afflictions—desire, anger, delusion—are not obstacles to be escaped but opportunities for transformation. Meeting them with clear awareness turns their energy into the very means of awakening. This perspective redefines the relationship between suffering and liberation, positioning everyday experiences as the ground for deepening wisdom and realizing freedom.

**The Nature of Ultimate Liberation**  
The highest state of liberation is described as a mind like empty space—unshaped by attachment, aversion, or conceptual distinctions. It transcends the boundaries between samsara and nirvana, cause and effect, and all dualities. This state is not an abstract ideal but the direct expression of perfected wisdom, experienced as complete non-dual freedom and the final end of all conditioned existence.
