**Title: Wisdom, the Three Poisons, and the Path to Liberation from Cyclic Existence**

**Summary:**  
Liberation from the cycle of repeated birth and death is achieved solely through the eradication of ignorance and attachment, which are rooted in the three afflictions—greed, hatred, and delusion. This liberation, known as Nirvana, is the complete cessation of these root causes, resulting in freedom from all conditioning forces. Only through diligent practice can one overcome laziness, dispel ignorance, dismantle the illusion of self, and break the chain of conditioned existence. Those who have attained this state are no longer bound by desire, aversion, or misunderstanding of reality.

**Wisdom is the sole means to end ignorance and dissolve illusion**  
Ignorance, the fundamental cause of cyclic existence, is dispelled only by wisdom. This wisdom directly perceives the true nature of reality, dissolving the illusions that sustain clinging and mistaken views of self and phenomena. Without this illumination, all delusions persist.

**The three afflictions—greed, hatred, and delusion—drive the cycle of rebirth**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion function as the internal mechanism perpetuating birth and death. Greed propels attachment to transient phenomena; hatred intensifies resistance and suffering; delusion, synonymous with ignorance, sustains the false perception of inherent existence. Together, they fuel karmic continuity and bind beings to the cycle.

**Nirvana is the complete cessation of the causes of suffering**  
Nirvana is not merely an escape but the total extinguishing of ignorance, craving, and the illusion of self. It is the final state where no karmic force remains to propel rebirth. It stands in direct opposition to cyclic existence, which arises exclusively from delusion and clinging.

**The sense of self is the core mechanism of bondage**  
The conviction in a permanent, independent self generates all forms of attachment and aversion. This clinging to “I” and “mine” gives rise to actions that perpetuate karmic traction. Liberation occurs when this illusion is fully seen through and dismantled.

**Attachment sustains the cycle of existence**  
Attachment to transient conditions, whether through desire or aversion, binds beings to repeated birth. Fixed clinging to appearances, even as favorable or unpleasant, ensures continued karmic production. Release requires the complete release of all forms of clinging.

**Diligent practice is the only pathway to freedom**  
Systematic, unwavering effort is indispensable. Without consistent discipline, the mind remains clouded by negligence, allowing ignorance and craving to dominate. Only through persistent cultivation can one overcome lethargy, cultivate clarity, and uproot the conditions that sustain rebirth.

**Negligence deepens ignorance and blocks liberation**  
Lack of mindful awareness allows delusion to strengthen. When the mind is distracted or complacent, it fails to see the true nature of reality, reinforcing mistaken perceptions and perpetuating the cycle. Vigilance is the necessary counter to this self-perpetuating blindness.

**Greedy desire and hatred are rooted in misunderstanding reality**  
Desire and aversion arise not from external causes but from a fundamental misrecognition of how things exist. Forgetting the emptiness and interdependence of phenomena, beings falsely grasp at or resist what appears, creating endless disturbance.

**The cessation of ignorance is the end of the cycle**  
The entire structure of repetitive existence hinges on ignorance. When this root is severed—through understanding, not effort alone—the train of dependent conditions halts. No new causes arise to generate future birth, decay, or death.
