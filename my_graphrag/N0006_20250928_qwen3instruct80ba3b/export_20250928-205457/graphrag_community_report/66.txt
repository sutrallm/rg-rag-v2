**Title: Wuming, Tan’ai, and the Three Poisons as the Roots of Samsara and the Path to Liberation for Shengzhe**

**Summary:**  
The cycle of birth and death is sustained by ignorance (Wuming), which gives rise to craving (Tan’ai) and the three root afflictions—greed, hatred, and delusion. These mental afflictions stem from a fundamental misperception of reality: the belief in a permanent self and attachment to impermanent phenomena. Liberation is achieved only when these afflictions are fully uprooted through wisdom and direct observation of non-self and impermanence. Those who have attained liberation (Shengzhe) have completely extinguished ignorance, craving, and greed, thereby ending the cycle of rebirth and achieving nirvana.

---

**Ignorance (Wuming) is the foundational cause of all suffering and cyclic existence**  
Wuming distorts perception by causing beings to misrecognize phenomena as permanent, self-contained, and satisfactory. This fundamental error leads to the illusion of a fixed self and drives the entire chain of suffering. Without overcoming this misapprehension, no liberation is possible, as it is the sole root from which all other afflictions emerge.

**Craving (Tan’ai) activates and perpetuates the cycle of rebirth**  
Once ignorance is in place, craving arises as the force that binds beings to samsara. It manifests as attachment to pleasant experiences and aversion to unpleasant ones, reinforcing the false sense of self. Craving does not initiate the cycle but sustains it, acting as the active mechanism that propels karmic continuity through clinging to the five aggregates as “I” and “mine.”

**The three poisons—greed, hatred, and delusion—emerge exclusively from ignorance**  
Greed arises from mistaking impermanent phenomena as belonging to the self; hatred emerges from aversion toward threats to the illusory self; delusion is the very ignorance that obscures truth. All three are not separate origins but manifestations of the same underlying failure to see reality as it is. Their presence confirms ignorance is active; their eradication marks its dissolution.

**Liberation (Shengzhe state) requires complete uprooting of ignorance, craving, and greed**  
Shengzhe are defined not by ritual or belief but by the total absence of the three poisons and the cessation of craving. Their attainment is not a suppression of symptoms but a direct dismantling of the root—ignorance. This state corresponds with nirvana, which is the extinguishing of the three afflictions and the end of rebirth.

**Wisdom and non-self observation are the direct antidotes to ignorance**  
Diligent effort (Jingjin) and insight (Hui) serve as the means to dismantle ignorance. The core practice is the direct observation of non-self and impermanence, which alone can dissolve the delusion of a permanent identity. This is not a theoretical understanding but a transformative realization that uproots the belief in “I” and “mine,” thereby breaking the chain of clinging.

**Ending afflictions targets ignorance, not its manifestations**  
The process of liberation does not seek to manage craving or hatred as isolated issues. Rather, it employs wisdom to sever the source: ignorance. Without addressing the root, all attempts to alleviate suffering are temporary. True cessation occurs only when the misunderstanding of reality is replaced with clear perception.
