**Title: Essential Pathways to Liberation through Wisdom, Meditation, and Discipline**  

**Summary:**  
The text emphasizes three interconnected elements crucial for achieving liberation: wisdom, meditation, and discipline. True wisdom, referred to as “如实智慧,” is identified as the key to liberation. Developing this wisdom is facilitated through deep meditation, which in turn is grounded in maintaining purity of discipline. These elements form a foundational pathway toward spiritual freedom.  

**1. True Wisdom as the Path to Liberation:**  
The text highlights “如实智慧” (true wisdom) as the critical factor for achieving liberation. This wisdom is not superficial but deeply rooted in understanding reality as it is, leading to spiritual freedom.  

**2. Deep Meditation as a Catalyst for Wisdom:**  
Engaging in profound meditation is essential for cultivating true wisdom. The process of deep meditation allows individuals to develop a clear and accurate perception of reality, which is necessary for attaining liberation.  

**3. Purity of Discipline as the Foundation for Meditation:**  
The text underscores the importance of maintaining purity of discipline, or “持戒清净,” as the basis for effective meditation. A disciplined and moral life creates the necessary conditions for achieving deep meditation and, consequently, developing true wisdom.
