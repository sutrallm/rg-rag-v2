**Title: The Path to Liberation Through Wisdom and Practice**  

**Summary:**  
The text emphasizes the role of wisdom and diligent practice in achieving liberation, the ultimate goal of transcending the cycle of birth and death. It highlights the necessity of cultivating wisdom to eliminate afflictions and describes the state of liberation as eternal tranquility and peace.  

**The Role of Wisdom in Liberation**  
Wisdom is essential for achieving liberation. It enables practitioners to completely eradicate all afflictions, paving the way for the ultimate goal of freedom from the cycle of existence.  

**Necessity of Diligent Practice**  
Diligent practice is a prerequisite for reaching liberation. Without persistent effort in spiritual cultivation, the ultimate goal cannot be attained.  

**The Highest State of Practice**  
The pinnacle of spiritual practice is the realization of liberation, marked by the transcendence of the cycle of birth and death and the attainment of everlasting peace and stillness.  

**The State of Liberation**  
Liberation represents the final state achieved after reaching the ultimate goal. It is characterized by eternal serenity and freedom from all suffering.
