**Title: Purification and Liberation through Meditation**

**Summary:**  
The text emphasizes the transformative power of meditation in achieving inner purification, peace, and the highest form of spiritual liberation, known as 無餘涅槃. This process signifies the end of suffering and represents the ultimate goal of spiritual practice.

**Purification of the Inner State**  
Meditation serves as a tool for purifying the inner state, fostering a sense of inner peace and clarity. This purification is essential for progressing toward spiritual liberation.

**Attainment of Complete Liberation**  
The culmination of meditation practice is the achievement of 無餘涅槃, the highest form of liberation. This state represents the end of suffering and the ultimate goal of spiritual endeavors.

**Role of 禪定 in Spiritual Practice**  
禪定, or meditative concentration, is a key practice that facilitates the purification of the 內心 (inner heart/mind). This process is crucial for reaching a state of peace and liberation, which are central to spiritual progress.
