**Title:** The Role of Diligent Practice in Overcoming the Death Demon King and Achieving Liberation  

**Summary:**  
Diligent practice, or 精进修行, is essential in Buddhism for overcoming the metaphorical 死亡魔王 (Death Demon King), which represents the cycle of death, rebirth, and spiritual decline. Through sustained effort, practitioners master meditation (禅定), cultivate wisdom, and eliminate afflictions, ultimately attaining liberation (涅槃). This practice is rooted in the teachings of Buddha, emphasizing discipline, vigilance, and continuous progress to avoid spiritual negligence and achieve enlightenment.  

**1. Overcoming the Death Demon King**  
The 死亡魔王 symbolizes the forces of death, delusion, and spiritual negligence that bind individuals to the cycle of suffering. Diligent practice enables practitioners to resist its control, transcend the cycle of death and rebirth, and pursue liberation.  

**2. Mastery of Meditation**  
Meditation (禅定) is both a core method and a result of diligent practice. Through continuous effort, practitioners achieve mental focus and mindfulness, which are essential for spiritual progress and liberation.  

**3. Cultivation of Wisdom**  
Diligent practice fosters the attainment of wisdom, enabling practitioners to understand the truths of Buddhist teachings. Wisdom and diligent practice are interdependent, forming a critical path toward enlightenment.  

**4. Adherence to Discipline**  
Discipline (戒律) is a fundamental requirement for diligent practice. It helps practitioners eliminate afflictions and maintain progress on the spiritual path, ensuring they remain free from the control of the 死亡魔王.  

**5. The Path to Liberation (涅槃)**  
Diligent practice is a necessary condition for achieving liberation. It allows practitioners to break free from the cycle of death and rebirth, attain enlightenment, and achieve ultimate freedom.  

**6. Foundation in Buddha’s Teachings**  
Diligent practice is guided by the teachings of Buddha. By following these teachings, practitioners establish a solid foundation for their spiritual journey and remain focused on the path to liberation.  

**7. Vigilance and Continuous Effort**  
Persistence and vigilance are critical in diligent practice. Practitioners must remain diligent to avoid spiritual negligence, prevent regression, and steadily progress toward liberation.  

**8. Elimination of Afflictions**  
Through diligent practice, practitioners gradually eliminate afflictions, which are obstacles to spiritual growth. This process is essential for attaining liberation and understanding the truths of Buddhism.
