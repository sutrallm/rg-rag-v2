**Title: The Role of Discipline (持戒) in Buddhist Practice**

**Summary:**  
Discipline (持戒) is the foundation of Buddhist practice, essential for purifying the mind and body, eliminating desires, and maintaining moral conduct. It supports the development of wisdom (智慧) and meditation (禅定), enabling practitioners to achieve inner peace, focus, and eventual liberation. Discipline is interwoven with wisdom and meditation, forming the core framework for overcoming afflictions and attaining enlightenment.

**The Foundation of Purification**  
Discipline is central to purifying the mind and body, helping practitioners eliminate greed and indulgence. By upholding moral conduct, practitioners stabilize their practice, reduce negative emotions like anger, and achieve mental and physical clarity.

**Discipline as the Root of Wisdom**  
Discipline serves as the cornerstone for developing wisdom. A purified moral foundation naturally leads to mental stability, which fosters the growth of wisdom. Discipline and wisdom are interdependent, with each reinforcing the other.

**Discipline and Meditation**  
Discipline provides the groundwork for meditation. A disciplined and purified mind creates the conditions necessary for deep meditative states, enabling practitioners to attain inner peace and focus. Discipline ensures the mental clarity required for meditation.

**Path to Liberation**  
Discipline is crucial for cutting off afflictions and achieving liberation (涅槃). It protects practitioners from harmful actions, guiding them toward the path of liberation and enlightenment.

**The Path of the Sage**  
Sages embody perfect discipline, which is essential for transcending suffering. By cultivating discipline, meditation, and wisdom, sages overcome all afflictions and attain ultimate freedom.

**Overcoming the Three Poisons**  
Practitioners use discipline, meditation, and wisdom to gradually overcome greed, anger, and delusion, the three primary obstacles to enlightenment.

**Essential for Complete Practice**  
Without discipline, complete practice and enlightenment are unattainable. Discipline is an indispensable part of the cultivation process, ensuring the purification and progress of the mind.

**Interconnection of Discipline, Meditation, and Wisdom**  
Discipline, meditation, and wisdom are interconnected components of Buddhist practice. Together, they enable practitioners to purify the mind, deepen understanding, and achieve spiritual fulfillment.

**The Importance of Diligence**  
Diligence is a critical factor in the successful attainment of spiritual goals. Combined with discipline, it supports the practitioner’s journey toward complete enlightenment.
