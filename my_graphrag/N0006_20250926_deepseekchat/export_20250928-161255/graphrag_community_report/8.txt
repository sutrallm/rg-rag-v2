**Title: The Path to Nirvana: Overcoming Greed, Hatred, and Delusion**

**Summary:**
The text emphasizes the essential practices and goals of spiritual cultivation. It highlights the need to overcome greed, hatred, and delusion as the foundation for achieving purity of mind and ultimately attaining nirvana. Wisdom and discipline are identified as crucial tools for this transformative process, while the integration of wisdom and compassion is underscored as a key pathway to enlightenment.

---

**Overcoming Greed, Hatred, and Delusion**
The eradication of greed, hatred, and delusion is a central focus of spiritual practice. These three poisons are viewed as obstacles to mental purification and must be eliminated to achieve liberation.

**The Ultimate Goal: Attaining Nirvana**
The primary objective of spiritual cultivation is to attain nirvana. This state of liberation is reached through the complete elimination of greed, hatred, and delusion, leading to a transcendent existence free from attachment.

**The Role of Wisdom**
Wisdom plays a pivotal role in the spiritual journey. It enables practitioners to overcome the three poisons, purify the mind, and elevate their spiritual state. Wisdom is not only instrumental in liberation but also in advancing to higher levels of practice.

**Discipline as a Foundational Practice**
Discipline serves as a critical support for spiritual practitioners. It helps individuals distance themselves from greed, hatred, and delusion, fostering mental clarity and purity.

**The Union of Wisdom and Compassion**
The integration of wisdom and compassion is a vital method for achieving enlightenment. This harmonious combination provides a balanced and effective approach to realizing the ultimate truth.
