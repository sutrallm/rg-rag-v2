**Title: The Role of Stopping and Contemplation in Cultivating Wisdom and Liberation**

**Summary:**  
Stopping and Contemplation are two methods that facilitate the cultivation of wisdom, enabling practitioners to perceive the true nature of all phenomena. A pure mind is essential for this perception, which is the sole path to liberation and the cessation of the cycle of birth and death. These practices are fundamental in guiding practitioners toward ultimate understanding and freedom.

**The Importance of Stopping and Contemplation**  
Stopping and Contemplation are critical methods for developing wisdom. They assist practitioners in perceiving the true nature of all phenomena, which is essential for spiritual progress.

**The Path to Liberation Through True Perception**  
Perceiving the true nature of all phenomena is the only path to liberation and the cessation of the cycle of birth and death. This understanding is central to achieving ultimate freedom.

**The Role of a Pure Mind**  
A pure mind is instrumental in enabling practitioners to perceive the true nature of all phenomena. This clarity of mind is a prerequisite for attaining liberation and the state of nirvana.
