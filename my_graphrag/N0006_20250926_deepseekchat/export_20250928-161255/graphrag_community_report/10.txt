**Title:** Key Teachings of Buddha on Cultivating a Pure Mind and Achieving Liberation  

**Summary:** The teachings of Buddha emphasize the importance of maintaining a pure mind to overcome afflictions and attain liberation. Practitioners are advised to cultivate forbearance, which stabilizes the mind and aids in approaching ultimate freedom. Regardless of external circumstances, a steadfast and pure mind is essential to avoid being swayed by any situation.  

**1. The Role of a Pure Mind in Liberation**  
A pure mind is fundamental for practitioners to distance themselves from afflictions and ultimately achieve Nirvana. This state of mental clarity is cultivated through disciplined practice and self-awareness.  

**2. Forbearance as a Tool for Inner Stability**  
Forbearance helps practitioners subdue afflictions and fosters inner peace, creating a conducive environment for spiritual progress. It enables individuals to remain undisturbed by external challenges.  

**3. Maintaining Equanimity Amidst External Conditions**  
Practitioners are encouraged to remain steadfast and maintain a pure mind regardless of favorable or adverse external circumstances. This equanimity ensures that they are not influenced or pulled by any external forces.
