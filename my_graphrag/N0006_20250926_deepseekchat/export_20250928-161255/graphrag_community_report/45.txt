**Title:** Leadership and Guidance in the Journey to Buddha’s Teachings  

**Summary:**  
This report highlights the roles of Sariputra and Maha Moggallana in leading groups of monks to meet Buddha, emphasizing their leadership responsibilities and the teachings they received. It underscores the collaborative and individual efforts of these disciples in guiding the monastic community.  

**Sariputra’s Leadership in Teaching and Guidance**  
Sariputra led a group of five hundred monks to meet Buddha, where he also responded to questions regarding the effective leadership of the monastic community. This demonstrates his active role in both guiding and educating the monks under his care.  

**Maha Moggallana’s Role in Monastic Guidance**  
Maha Moggallana, alongside Sariputra, played a significant role in leading five hundred monks to Buddha. His involvement highlights his importance in the leadership structure of the monastic community and his collaboration with Sariputra.  

**Individual Leadership Contributions**  
Both Sariputra and Maha Moggallana are depicted as individual leaders, each taking responsibility for guiding groups of monks to Buddha. This underscores their distinct yet complementary roles in the spiritual and organizational guidance of the monastic community.  

**Recurring Commitment to Teachings**  
The repeated instances of monks being led to meet Buddha by Sariputra and Maha Moggallana emphasize their dedication to ensuring the monks receive Buddha’s teachings, reflecting their commitment to spiritual education and community development.
