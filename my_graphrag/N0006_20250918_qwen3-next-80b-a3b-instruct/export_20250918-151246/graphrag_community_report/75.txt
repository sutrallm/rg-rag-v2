**Title: The Interconnection of Ignorance, Craving, and Spiritual Practice in the Path to Liberation**

**Summary**  
The cycle of suffering and rebirth is sustained by fundamental misunderstanding of reality—specifically, the misperception of the five aggregates as a permanent self. This delusion, known as Wu Ming, gives rise to craving, aversion, and mental defilements, which bind beings to continuous existence. Liberation is achieved only through a disciplined method of spiritual practice that cultivates present-moment awareness, meditative concentration, and diligent effort to dismantle ignorance. The complete cessation of ignorance eliminates all afflictions and ends the cycle of rebirth, marking the state of the saintly person.

---

**Ignorance Drives the Cycle of Suffering**  
Wu Ming, the failure to perceive reality as impermanent, non-self, and inherently unsatisfactory, is the foundational distortion that initiates all suffering. It causes beings to mistake the five aggregates for a lasting self, leading to attachment and aversion. This misperception is not merely a cognitive error but the active force that perpetuates the cycle of birth and death, making it the primary obstacle to freedom.

**Craving Emerges from Misapprehension of Reality**  
Desire and aversion—Ta Nian—are not independent urges but consequences of Wu Ming. When reality is seen through the lens of permanence and selfhood, pleasant experiences are clung to and unpleasant ones rejected. This craving is the energetic force that sustains rebirth, actively maintaining the cycle of existence by reinforcing identification with the five aggregates.

**Spiritual Practice Directly Counters Delusion**  
The correct method of spiritual practice is defined by its rejection of ritual, memory, and anticipation in favor of unwavering awareness of the present moment. By cultivating chanding and adhering to Jing Jin and Zhizhi, the mind is stabilized and clarified, allowing for the direct observation of impermanence and non-self. This process actively dismantles the deluded view that underlies craving and suffering.

**Afflictions Are Manifestations of Ignorance**  
Greed, hatred, and agitation—Fan Yao—are not random emotional responses but internal expressions of Wu Ming. They arise automatically when the mind fails to see the true nature of phenomena, interpreting transient experiences as worthy of clinging or rejection. These mental impurities are sustained by ignorance and cannot be resolved through suppression but only through the clear seeing enabled by disciplined practice.

**Liberation Is Marked by the Complete Cessation of Delusion**  
The saintly person is distinguished not by external achievements but by the total absence of Wu Ming and its downstream effects. With ignorance eradicated, craving ceases, afflictions no longer arise, and the five aggregates are no longer mistaken for self. This state is the natural result of sustained practice and represents the end of the cycle of rebirth, not as a passive state but as the complete unraveling of the root cause.
