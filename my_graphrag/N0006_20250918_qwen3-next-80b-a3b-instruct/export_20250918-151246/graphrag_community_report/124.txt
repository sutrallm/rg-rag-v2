**Title: Progressive Practice and Laziness in the Path to Liberation**

**Summary:**  
Sustained, disciplined application of Dharma teachings is essential to overcome spiritual inertia and achieve stability in chanding. Without this continuous effort, laziness takes hold, causing regression and perpetuating entrapment in samsara. The only effective counter to this inertia is persistent diligent practice, which actively dismantles ignorance and enables forward movement on the spiritual path.

**Chanding Requires Continuous Effort**  
Chanding cannot be maintained or developed without consistent, disciplined action. Its stability is directly tied to the ongoing application of Dharma teachings. Any lapse in effort undermines its presence, making sustained practice not optional but necessary for its cultivation.

**Laziness Promotes Spiritual Regression**  
Laziness manifests as spiritual inertia, complacency, or absence of effort. This state actively impedes the growth of chanding and wisdom, leading not to stagnation but to regression. The absence of diligence reinforces deluded thinking and deepens entrapment in samsara.

**Progressive Practice Is the Antidote to Spiritual Inertia**  
The deliberate, continuous application of Dharma teachings directly opposes laziness. By instilling mindfulness and insight, it disrupts patterns of delusion and compels movement forward. This method is not merely helpful—it is the singular means to break free from the cycle of spiritual stagnation.
