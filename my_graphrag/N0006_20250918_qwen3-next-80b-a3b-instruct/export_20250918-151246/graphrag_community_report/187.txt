**The Cycle of Samsara and the Four Hindrances to Liberation**

**Summary**  
The cycle of birth, death, and rebirth (samsara) is sustained by four interrelated forces: ignorance, hatred, delusion, and greed. These forces perpetuate suffering by distorting perception, generating negative emotional and karmic consequences, and binding beings to repeated existence. Liberation arises only through their cessation, as each one independently reinforces the cycle and prevents clear understanding of reality.

**Ignorance Sustains the Cycle of Rebirth**  
Without clear perception of reality, beings remain unaware of the impermanent and interdependent nature of existence. This fundamental lack of awareness is identified as the root cause of samsara, ensuring continued cycles of birth and death by preventing liberation.

**Hatred Generates Inner Turbulence and Negative Consequences**  
Rejection and anger toward unpleasant experiences produce inner disturbance and accumulate consequences that reinforce suffering. These reactions do not resolve discomfort but instead deepen entanglement in the cycle of rebirth.

**Delusion Mistakenly Establishes a False Sense of Self and Permanence**  
Confusion about the true nature of reality—specifically the belief in a fixed, independent self and inherent existence of all things—acts as the foundational error underlying samsara. This misperception distorts all experience and prevents awakening.

**Greed Fuels Attachment and Perpetuates Suffering**  
Clinging to sensory pleasures, objects, or transient states creates an enduring bond to the material world. This attachment does not bring lasting satisfaction but instead ensures continued participation in the cycle of birth and death.
