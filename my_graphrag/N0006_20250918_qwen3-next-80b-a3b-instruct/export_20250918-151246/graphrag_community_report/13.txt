Title: The Nature of Inner Liberation through Non-Attachment

Summary:  
Inner liberation arises not from altering external circumstances but from a fundamental shift in how the mind relates to thoughts and experiences. This state is characterized by an absence of clinging and aversion, resulting in a mind that is open, unobstructed, and free from emotional reactivity. It is experienced as an emptiness—similar to space—where phenomena arise and pass without resistance.

- The mind achieves liberation when it no longer clings to or rejects experiences.  
- This state is not produced by outward change but by a direct recognition of reality’s true nature.  
- Liberation manifests as an empty, space-like quality of the mind, free from grasping and aversion.  
- Non-attachment is the immediate and sufficient condition for freedom from internal bondage.  
- Emotional reactivity dissolves when the mind no longer resists or pursues perceived experiences.
