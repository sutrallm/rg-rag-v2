**Title: Non-Attaching Wisdom and Anatta Observation as Paths to Liberation**

**Summary:**  
A state of complete mental freedom arises when one recognizes all experiences as impermanent and without inherent self. This understanding, cultivated through direct observation of phenomena as not-self, eliminates clinging to pleasure, pain, or even spiritual states like purity. The cessation of grasping—rooted in the absence of identification with experience—leads naturally to liberation. A sage embodies this condition, remaining undisturbed by transient phenomena and perceiving reality without distortion.

**Non-Attaching Wisdom Is the Direct Path to Freedom**  
This state emerges from seeing all phenomena as impermanent and without a fixed owner. By recognizing that nothing can be permanently held, attachment to experiences, emotions, or meditative states dissolves. This recognition uproots the foundation of suffering, resulting in mental freedom unconditioned by desire or aversion.

**Anatta Observation Reveals the Illusion of Self**  
Through observing all sensory and mental experiences as conditional and transient, one arrives at the understanding that “this is not mine, not I.” This direct seeing dismantles the illusion of personal ownership over phenomena, which in turn ceases the impulse to grasp or reject. It is the process that gives rise to non-attaching wisdom.

**No Extraction Completes Mental Liberation**  
True freedom is reached when even the pursuit of purity or spiritual attainment is relinquished. The mind no longer clings to any experience—including those considered desirable or refined—resulting in unrestricted ease. This is not passive indifference but the absence of any urge to extract or hold onto anything.

**The Sage Embodies Unshakable Equanimity**  
One who has fully realized non-attaching wisdom remains unaffected by pleasure or pain. They perceive reality without overlaying assumptions of self or permanence, allowing experience to arise and pass without interference or reaction. Their presence reflects the natural outcome of sustained non-clinging.
