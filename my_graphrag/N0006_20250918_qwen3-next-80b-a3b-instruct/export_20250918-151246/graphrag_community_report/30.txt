Title: Benxin and Huiguangfanzhao: The Path to Immediate Liberation

Summary:  
Liberation arises directly from the realization of one’s original mind through the practice of turning awareness inward. This realization is not gradual or external but immediate and self-revealing, occurring when external seeking ceases and inner observation takes place. The practice and the realized state are intrinsically linked as cause and effect.

Turning the light inward reveals the original mind  
The practice of self-observation and inner reflection naturally uncovers the innate, pure, and unconditioned nature of the mind. This is not an achievement through effort but a recognition that emerges when attention is no longer directed outward.

Realization of the original mind is liberation  
When the mind perceives itself without distortion or external reference, freedom is instantaneous. There is no separation between understanding and liberation — seeing one’s true nature is itself the end of bondage.

The practice and the realized state are inseparable  
Turning inward is not a means to an end but the very action that brings forth the original mind. The two are not distinct stages but mutually defining: one cannot exist without the other in the process of realization.
