Title: The Path of the Sage: Liberation from Emotional Afflictions

Summary:  
The sage achieves complete freedom by eliminating the root causes of emotional suffering through wisdom and meditation. True liberation is defined not by external conditions but by the cessation of忧悲苦恼. This state is not merely the absence of distress but the result of severing its underlying cause, demonstrating a direct causal relationship between the origin of affliction and its manifestation.

The Root Cause Must Be Eliminated  
Emotional suffering arises from deeper, underlying causes. These causes are not mere symptoms but the source from which distress repeatedly emerges. Eliminating them leads not to temporary relief but to permanent cessation of suffering. This shows that surface-level suppression is insufficient—only targeted removal of the root can bring lasting resolution.

Freedom Is Defined by Absence, Not Presence  
Liberation is not an achievement measured by新增 states or elevated feelings. Rather, it is confirmed solely by the disappearance of specific emotional burdens. The presence of peace or joy is incidental; what matters is the irreversible absence of忧悲苦恼. This reframes success in spiritual practice as a subtraction, not an addition.

Causal Unity Between Origin and Manifestation  
The affliction and its source are inseparably linked in a chain of cause and effect. One cannot be eradicated without the other. This means any effort focused exclusively on managing the expression of suffering—such as calming emotions or avoiding triggers—will fail unless the foundational cause is addressed. The structure of this relationship demands a precise, targeted approach.
