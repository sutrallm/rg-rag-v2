Title: Ethical Conduct, Meditation, and the Ascension of the Wise Sage

Summary:  
Ethical behavior is the essential foundation for mental clarity and meditative stability. Through sustained meditation and cultivated wisdom, mental disturbances are calmed, leading to the dissolution of craving, aversion, and delusion. The wise sage, having fully realized this path, embodies unshakable inner peace and transcends worldly conditions, having eradicated all sources of suffering.

Ethical Conduct as the Basis for Mental Clarity  
Pure ethical behavior creates the necessary conditions for mental stability and clarity. Without it, deeper meditative states remain inaccessible. This conduct is not an isolated practice but the grounding force upon which all further spiritual progress depends.

Meditation Leading to Inner Peace  
The practice of meditation, guided by wisdom, directly reduces agitation, attachment, and aversion. These mental disturbances are not merely suppressed but steadily dismantled, allowing a state of tranquility to emerge naturally and sustainably.

Transcendence Through the Eradication of Defilements  
The wise sage has fully removed greed, hatred, and delusion—the core sources of suffering. This elimination is not partial or temporary but complete and irreversible, resulting in a state that cannot be shaken by external praise or circumstance.

Embodiment of Liberation  
The wise sage does not merely understand liberation but lives it. Their ethical conduct, meditative stability, and realization of ultimate truth are inseparable. This unity manifests as unshakable inner peace, independent of worldly conditions, and defines their entire being.
