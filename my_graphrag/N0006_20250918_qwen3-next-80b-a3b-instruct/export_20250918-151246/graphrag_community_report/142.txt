**Title: Transcending Attachment Through Inner Observation and Concentration**

**Summary**  
Liberation from delusion and attachment is achieved through sustained inner observation and mental stillness. These practices cultivate a direct, non-conceptual awareness of impermanence and non-self, dissolving the illusion of ownership and resulting in natural non-clinging. Enlightened beings maintain this awareness continuously to prevent the return of false notions. Concentration supports this process by stabilizing the mind, enabling clear perception of reality’s true nature.

**Inner Observation Sustains Liberation**  
Continuous inner observation is the essential practice for maintaining awareness and preventing the re-emergence of delusion. It enables direct perception of experience as impermanent and devoid of self, keeping the mind free from reattachment to spiritual states or personal notions of ownership.

**Mental Stillness Enables Clear Perception**  
The cultivation of mental stillness through concentration creates the conditions necessary to see through the illusions of permanence and self. Without this stability, the direct recognition of non-self and impermanence cannot arise with clarity or consistency.

**Non-Attachment Emerges from Realizing Non-Self**  
When the mind fully perceives that nothing possesses inherent existence or belongs to a fixed self, clinging naturally ceases. This understanding is not forced but arises as a direct consequence of seeing reality as it is, including the emptiness of meditative experiences and spiritual achievements.

**Inner Observation Is the Path to Liberation**  
The practice of inner observation is the decisive method on the correct path to realizing the true nature of reality. It is through this direct awareness—uncoupled from conceptual thinking—that the fabric of false identity and ownership is unraveled, leading to freedom.
