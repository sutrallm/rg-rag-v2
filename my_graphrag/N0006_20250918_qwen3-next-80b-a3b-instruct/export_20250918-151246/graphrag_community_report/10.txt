**Title: The Noble Being and the Present-Moment Realization of Nirvana**

**Summary:**  
A noble being, having fully extinguished greed, hatred, and delusion, resides in unwavering mental stillness and direct awareness of reality. This state, characterized by non-reactivity and non-discrimination toward all experiences, is not an endpoint to be reached but the immediate expression of liberation. Equanimity arises naturally from the clear perception of emptiness and impermanence, rendering worldly conditions powerless to disturb the mind. Liberation is not distant; it is fully present in this mode of awareness.

**Equal Mind as the Immediate Expression of Liberation**  
The state of equal mind — non-reactive, non-discriminating awareness — is not conceived as an attainable future goal. Rather, it is the direct, current manifestation of Nirvana. When one perceives all phenomena as empty and impermanent, there is no basis for attachment or aversion. This awareness is not cultivated through effort alone but emerges as the natural condition of a mind freed from conditioning.

**Liberation Through the Eradication of Defilements**  
A noble being is defined by the complete removal of greed, hatred, and delusion. This purification is not symbolic or partial; it is total and irreversible. With these distortions gone, wisdom stabilizes, and the mind no longer responds to external conditions. The absence of these defilements is not a passive state but the active foundation of unwavering stillness.

**Worldly Conditions Lose Power in the Presence of Impermanence**  
Because a noble being sees all experiences — pleasure and pain, gain and loss — as transient and insubstantial, none can disturb the inner equilibrium. There is no need to resist or embrace, to cling or reject. This perception dissolves the mechanism of reaction, allowing the mind to dwell in undisturbed balance regardless of external circumstances.
