Title: The Threefold Training: Precepts, Chanding, and Wisdom in Spiritual Liberation

Summary:  
Ethical conduct through precepts is the necessary starting point for spiritual development, enabling mental stability required for chanding. Chanding acts as the central bridge connecting ethical behavior to the emergence of wisdom, which is the ultimate goal. Progress along this path is sequential and interdependent — without ethical grounding, chanding cannot arise; without chanding, wisdom cannot manifest. The entire path collapses without the foundation of precepts.

Precepts Enable Mental Stability  
Moral guidelines embedded in precepts purify actions and speech, calming the mind and removing distractions that hinder inner stillness. This purification establishes the mental clarity and steadiness required for chanding to develop. The absence of ethical conduct results in agitation, making it impossible for deeper states of focus to take root.

Chanding Serves as the Central Bridge  
Chanding functions as the pivotal link between ethical behavior and wisdom. It is not an end in itself but the essential medium through which the stability provided by precepts transforms into the clarity of wisdom. Without this bridging function, the progression from conduct to understanding cannot occur.

Wisdom Is Dependent on Prior Development  
Liberating wisdom emerges only after the sequential cultivation of precepts and chanding. It is the culmination of the path, not an independent achievement. Full realization of wisdom requires the prior establishment of mental purity and focused concentration; neither can be bypassed or assumed.

The Path Cannot Progress Without Precepts  
Precepts are not one optional component among three — they are the indispensable foundation. All progress in chanding and the eventual arising of wisdom rely entirely on the integrity of ethical conduct. Without this base, the structure of the threefold training cannot be sustained.
