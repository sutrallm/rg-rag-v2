**Title: Nonself, Impermanence, and the Path to Releasing**

**Summary**  
Freedom from mental bondage arises when the mind fully recognizes that no permanent, independent self exists within any experience, and that all phenomena are transient. This understanding naturally dissolves attachment to pleasant states, resistance to pain, and grasping for attainments. True inner liberation—releasing—is not forced but emerges as a natural consequence of seeing reality as it is: fleeting and without a fixed core.

---

**Nonself Dissolves Identification**  
When it is understood that no permanent, independent self exists within any experience, the mind no longer identifies with thoughts, emotions, or identities. This realization removes the foundation upon which attachment and aversion are built, allowing the mind to rest without clinging to or rejecting any arising condition.

**Impermanence Undoes Clinging**  
Recognizing that all phenomena are transient enables the mind to let go of attachment to meditative states, physical sensations, or outcomes. Because nothing endures, holding on becomes unnecessary. This recognition is not theoretical but experiential, directly undermining the impulse to grasp or fear loss.

**Releasing Arises Naturally from Seeing Reality**  
Releasing is not an action of willpower or suppression, but the natural outcome of seeing all experiences as impermanent and without a self to cling to. When the illusion of a fixed center is seen through, resistance to pain and craving for pleasure cease, resulting in equanimity and inner freedom.

**Releasing Requires No Grasping or Avoidance**  
True releasing occurs only when there is complete non-clinging—not merely tolerating unpleasant states, but seeing them as they are: without a subject to suffer, without an object to possess. This includes letting go of the desire for attainments as well as the fear of failure, for both are rooted in the mistaken belief in a self that must succeed or fail.
