**Title: The Sage: Embodiment of Pure Morality and Unshakable Inner Liberation**

**Summary:**  
The Sage represents the culmination of ethical and meditative discipline, having completely eradicated greed, hatred, and delusion. This state is not achieved through external recognition but through unwavering inward cultivation. Moral conduct serves as the essential foundation for deep concentration and clarity, while the Sage remains unaffected by reputation, pleasure, pain, or emotional turbulence. Their mind is stable, unattached, and fully aligned with reality.

**Pure Morality as the Un动摇 Foundation**  
Ethical conduct, free from defilements, is not optional but the indispensable basis for spiritual progress. It enables the mind to settle into deep meditative concentration, creating the conditions necessary for wisdom to arise. Without this moral stability, higher states of awareness cannot be sustained.

**Eradication of the Three Root Afflictions**  
The Sage has fully uprooted greed, hatred, and delusion—these afflictions no longer arise within the mind. This complete liberation removes the sources of suffering and distortion, allowing for a mind that perceives reality without filter or distortion.

**Non-Attachment to Reputation and External Validation**  
The Sage does not pursue, value, or rely on social recognition. External fame is seen as irrelevant to inner realization. Their grounding comes from within, not from how they are perceived by others.

**Untouched Mind as the Mark of Liberation**  
The Sage’s awareness remains unstained by worldly concerns, emotional disturbances, or sensory temptations. This state is not passive detachment but an active, clear perception of existence as it is, unaltered by craving or aversion.

**Moral Discipline as Intrinsic Temperament**  
Discipline is not a practice the Sage performs偶尔—it defines their very being. By guarding the senses and maintaining inner stillness, they naturally inhabit a state of equilibrium, where external stimuli no longer disrupt their peace.

**Transcendence Through Direct Insight**  
Freedom from delusion is not theoretical but the result of direct, unmediated understanding of reality. This clarity allows the Sage to live in serene awareness, beyond the dualities of pleasure and pain, having left behind the limitations of ordinary human consciousness.
