**Title: The Three Poisons and Ignorance as Drivers of Cyclic Suffering**

**Summary:**  
The cycle of birth and death is sustained by ignorance and the three mental afflictions—greed, hatred, and delusion—which together generate craving and aversion. These afflictions are the direct mechanisms through which suffering persists across lifetimes. Liberation is marked by their complete removal, indicating that their eradication is both necessary and sufficient for breaking free from this cycle.

**The Three Poisons Sustain the Cycle of Birth and Death**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion act as active forces that perpetuate rebirth and suffering. They manifest through craving and aversion, which bind beings to continuous existence. Their presence ensures the cycle remains unbroken, as they arise from and reinforce the fundamental misunderstanding of reality.

**Ignorance Is the Foundational Cause of the Cycle**  
Ignorance—the mistaken perception of impermanence as permanence, suffering as happiness, and non-self as self—underlies all forms of craving and clinging. It is not merely one of several factors but the root from which the three poisons emerge, making it the primary condition enabling the cycle of birth and death to continue.

**Eradication of the Three Poisons Defines Liberation**  
The complete removal of greed, hatred, and delusion is the definitive marker of a holy being’s attainment. This absence is not incidental but essential; without these afflictions, the mechanisms that fuel rebirth cease to operate, demonstrating that liberation is contingent upon their total dissolution.
