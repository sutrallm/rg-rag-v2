**Title: Qing Jing and Yuan Huan: The Path to Liberation from Samsara**

**Summary:**  
Liberation from the cycle of birth, death, and suffering is achieved through the attainment of inner purity and tranquility, which arises only after the complete elimination of greed, hatred, delusion, and ignorance. These mental defilements are the sole obstacles to breaking free from perpetual rebirth. The practices of Chan Ding, Jing Jin, and Zhi Neng together enable this transcendence.

**The Nature of Inner Purity as Liberation**  
Inner purity and tranquility, known as Qing Jing, is not a temporary state but the inevitable outcome of eradicating greed, hatred, and delusion. This condition is synonymous with liberation from the cycle of samsara; once achieved, the individual is no longer bound by repeated existence or suffering. The Holy One embodies this state as a direct result of complete spiritual purification.

**The Root of Cyclic Suffering**  
The fundamental cause of entrapment within the endless cycle of birth and death—Yuan Huan—is ignorance, referred to as Wu Ming. This ignorance sustains attachment and mental defilements, which in turn perpetuate suffering. Liberation is only possible through its removal, making it the critical focus of any meaningful spiritual path.

**The Combined Practices Leading to Freedom**  
Breakthrough from samsara requires the synchronized practice of Chan Ding, Jing Jin, and Zhi Neng. These disciplines are not optional or partial measures but are presented as the indispensable means through which ignorance is dissolved and inner purity is realized. Without their combined application, the cycle cannot be transcended.
