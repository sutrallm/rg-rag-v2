**Title: Jiexiao and the Path to Liberation: The Interplay of Faith, Effort, and the Eradication of Afflictions**

**Summary**  
Liberation from suffering is achieved through the complete elimination of greed, hatred, and delusion, which are fueled by ignorance. True faith, born of direct experience rather than blind belief, ignites and sustains the sustained effort required to cultivate meditative stillness and continual mindfulness. These practices lead to the realization of impermanence and non-self, which dismantle clinging—the root of suffering—and culminate in a state where all afflictions are extinguished and worldly conditions are transcended.

**True Faith is the Foundation of Diligent Practice**  
Confidence in the possibility of ending suffering, purifying the mind, and realizing the emptiness of self does not arise from doctrine alone, but from personal verification of these truths. This grounded trust becomes the inner驱动 that fuels consistent spiritual effort, without which progress remains impossible.

**Effort and Diligence Are Non-Negotiable for Liberation**  
Passive belief or intermittent practice cannot disrupt the cycle of suffering. Only sustained, purposeful effort breaks the patterns of delusion, making diligence an essential and irreplaceable component of the path.

**Meditative Stillness Enables the Arising of Wisdom**  
Quieting the mind through meditative stilledness is not an end in itself, but a necessary condition for deep understanding to emerge. This stillness creates the internal space where the nature of reality becomes directly perceivable.

**Afflictions Stem from Ignorance and Must Be Rooted Out**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion are not isolated emotions but manifestations of a deeper failure to see things as they are. Ignorance is their source; thus, eliminating these mental poisons requires confronting and dissolving that root misunderstanding.

**Transcending Attachment Is the Manifestation of Wisdom**  
Letting go of clinging is not merely a moral choice but the natural outcome of seeing impermanence and non-self. When wisdom matures, attachment naturally drops away—making it the clearest sign of transformation.

**Continual Mindfulness Is the Daily Framework for Liberation**  
Spiritual progress is not confined to meditation cushions. Liberation is cultivated moment by moment through sustained awareness in ordinary life, making mindfulness the essential practice underlying all other efforts.

**The Enlightened State Is Defined by the Absence of Afflictions**  
One who has reached liberation is not distinguished by supernatural powers or outward signs, but by the total extinction of greed, hatred, and delusion. This state represents the complete transcendence of suffering and attachment.
