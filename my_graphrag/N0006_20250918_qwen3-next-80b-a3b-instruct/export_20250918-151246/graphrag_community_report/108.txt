**Title: The Saint’s Mastery of Moral Discipline, Wisdom, and Inner Stillness**

**Summary**  
A saint has achieved complete liberation through the thorough cultivation of moral discipline and wisdom, resulting in the total eradication of greed, hatred, and delusion. This state of inner stillness, marked by unshakable mindfulness and freedom from worldly attachment, is recognized and revered by celestial beings. The saint embodies the realization of nirvana, where mental purity and transcendence are fully manifested.

**Moral Discipline as the Foundation of Mental Clarity**  
Ethical conduct and disciplined behavior serve as the essential groundwork for stabilizing the mind. Through consistent moral discipline, the mind becomes free from distraction and impurity, enabling the development of deep concentration and the capacity for profound awareness necessary to uproot the three poisons.

**Wisdom as the Agent of Liberation**  
Wisdom enables the complete dissolution of greed, hatred, and delusion—not through suppression, but through direct understanding. It is this cultivated discernment that grants the saint the ability to see through illusion, transcending the conditions that bind ordinary beings to suffering.

**Inner Stillness as the Expression of Transcendence**  
Stillness is not passive inertia but an active state of mindful observation. It allows the saint to perceive the arising and passing of mental defilements without attachment, leading to their permanent dissolution. This stillness reflects a mind no longer swayed by worldly distinctions or emotional disturbances.

**Celestial Reverence as Recognition of Ultimate Attainment**  
Heavenly beings, though existing in elevated realms, hold the saint in the highest regard. Their reverence stems from the saint’s total absence of worldly clinging and suffering, marking the saint as the highest embodiment of spiritual fulfillment beyond even celestial conditions.

**Nirvana as the Culmination of Eradication**  
The saint’s state is synonymous with nirvana—the full and irreversible cessation of greed, hatred, and delusion. This is not an abstract goal but a lived reality, characterized by perfect mental purity, unwavering mindfulness, and the absence of any trace of affliction.
