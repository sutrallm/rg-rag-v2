**Title: Non-Abiding and the Nature of True Mental Freedom**

**Summary**  
True mental freedom arises not from attaining special states or avoiding suffering, but from relinquishing all attachment—whether to pleasant meditative experiences, painful sensations, or even the idea of liberation itself. This state, known as non-abiding, is sustained by mental stability and cultivated through deep contemplation of the impermanent and selfless nature of all phenomena. It is characterized by the absence of seeking, where the mind remains impartial and unattached to any condition or concept.

**Mental Stability Enables Non-Abiding**  
Chanding provides the necessary mental stability for non-abiding to occur. Without this steadiness, the mind remains prone to clinging or rejecting experiences, preventing the sustained state of non-attachment. Stability is not an end in itself but the foundation upon which the mind can remain unfixed, even amid changing internal conditions.

**Non-Attachment Arises from Contemplation of Non-Self**  
The dissolution of attachment is not achieved through force or suppression but through sustained observation that all phenomena lack inherent self-nature. Recognizing this unreliability of fixed identity removes the grounds for craving, aversion, and fixation, allowing the mind to let go naturally.

**True Freedom Is Defined by the Absence of Seeking**  
Freedom is not measured by the presence of elevated states or spiritual achievements, but by the absence of desire to attain or avoid any experience. When the mind ceases to pursue liberation or resist suffering, it becomes free—not because it has reached a destination, but because it no longer moves toward or away from anything.

**Non-Abiding Is the Expression of Unconditional Acceptance**  
The mind in non-abiding does not hold onto bliss, peace, or even enlightenment as final achievements. It remains open to whatever arises—pleasant or painful, familiar or foreign—without preference or resistance. This impartial acceptance is not passive resignation but active non-clinging, revealing the deepest level of unconditioned awareness.

**Transcending Attachment Leads to Non-Abiding, Even Beyond Liberation**  
The process of letting go does not stop at overcoming ordinary desires. It extends to releasing attachment to the experience of being liberated. Non-abiding includes freedom from attachment to spiritual milestones, ensuring the mind remains unshackled even from its own most sublime accomplishments.
