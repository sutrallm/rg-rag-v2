**Title: The Sheng Zhe: Liberation Through the Eradication of Greed, Hatred, and Delusion**

**Summary:**  
The Sheng Zhe, or enlightened being, is defined by the total elimination of greed, hatred, and delusion—the root causes of suffering and cyclic existence. Liberation is achieved not through isolated practices but through the integrated perfection of moral discipline, meditative concentration, and transcendent wisdom. This state is marked by unwavering mindfulness, inner tranquility, and freedom from attachment, even to spiritual attainments. Even celestial beings revere the Sheng Zhe, as their realization transcends all forms of samsaric existence, including heavenly realms.

---

**Foundation of Liberation: Moral Discipline, Meditative Concentration, and Wisdom**  
The Sheng Zhe embodies three interdependent qualities: pure moral conduct, stable meditative concentration, and transcendent wisdom. Moral discipline clears the mind of remorse and distraction, enabling deeper focus. Meditative concentration provides the inner stillness necessary for insight, but it is incomplete without wisdom. Transcendent wisdom reveals the impermanent and non-self nature of all phenomena, preventing attachment to meditative experiences and securing true freedom.

**Eradication of the Three Poisons Defines Enlightenment**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion are the foundational poisons binding beings to suffering and rebirth. The Sheng Zhe has completely uprooted these three, and this eradication is the sole marker of awakening. No state of peace, joy, or even meditative absorption is considered complete unless these root causes are abolished. Their absence is not merely a reduction of negative states but the full emergence of inner purity and stillness.

**Transcendent Wisdom Reveals Non-Attachment**  
Transcendent wisdom is the direct realization that all phenomena—包括 meditative joy—are impermanent and devoid of self. This insight prevents clinging to any experience, even the most blissful or spiritually elevated. Because the Sheng Zhe does not cling to the idea of attainment, they transcend even the notion of liberation, embodying true freedom unconditioned by any state or achievement.

**Continuous Diligent Practice Sustains the Enlightened State**  
The Sheng Zhe is not simply a passive recipient of insight but engages in unwavering, daily vigilance. Diligent practice is not a preliminary step but an ongoing expression of their being, guarding the six sense faculties and preventing backsliding into ignorance. This vigilance maintains the stability of meditative concentration and ensures moral discipline remains uncorrupted by subtle attachments.

**Inner Purity Is the Natural Result of Liberation**  
Qing Jing—the state of inner cleanliness, peace, and stillness—is not cultivated as a goal but arises spontaneously when greed, hatred, and delusion are fully abandoned. It is the natural condition of a mind no longer entangled by sensory stimuli or conceptual clinging. This purity is inseparable from liberation from the cycle of birth and death, and it is this condition that even celestial beings recognize as supreme.

**The Sheng Zhe Transcends All Samsaric Realms**  
Despite their power and longevity, celestial beings remain within the cycle of rebirth and suffering. The Sheng Zhe, having severed the three poisons and realized non-self, has escaped this cycle entirely. Their state is not an enhanced form of worldly existence but a complete transcendence—honored not by rank or power, but by the absence of all conditioning and attachment.
