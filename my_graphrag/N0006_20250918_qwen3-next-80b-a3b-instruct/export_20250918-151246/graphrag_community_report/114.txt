**Title: Chanding, Virtue, and the Path to Final Liberation**

**Summary**  
Final liberation is achieved through the complete relinquishment of attachment, including attachment to spiritual attainment, leading to boundless peace and non-abiding awareness. This state requires the eradication of clinging, ignorance, and delusion, and can only be realized through mastery of mind, which is cultivated by chanding. Chanding depends on virtue as its foundation—without ethical discipline, the mind remains agitated and incapable of deep concentration. Ethical behavior calms internal conflict, enabling mental clarity and the stability necessary to transcend mental turbulence and attain ultimate freedom.

---

**Ethical Discipline Establishes the Basis for Mental Mastery**  
Virtue, or ethical discipline, is not merely a moral guideline but a necessary condition for the mind to become suitable for deep practice. Without it, the mind is clouded by agitation and internal conflict, making sustained concentration impossible. Purification of the mind through ethical behavior reduces disturbances and creates the stable inner environment where chanding can take root.

**Chanding Is the Direct Means to Cultivate Mastery of Mind**  
Chanding is the specific practice through which one develops the ability to remain undisturbed by thoughts, emotions, and external conditions. It is the active process that transforms the mind from a state of reactivity to one of unwavering stability. This mastery is not an abstract ideal but a tangible result of consistent chanding practice, which quietens the fluctuations that obscure clarity.

**Final Liberation Requires Total Non-Attachment, Including to Liberation Itself**  
The ultimate state is not merely freedom from suffering, but the complete dissolution of all clinging—even to the idea of attainment or enlightenment. True liberation is defined by the absence of any residual attachment, making it a condition of boundless peace beyond any goal-oriented mindset. This state emerges only when ignorance and delusion are entirely uprooted, and the mind no longer clings to identity, outcome, or spiritual progress.

**Mastery of Mind Enables the Realization of Liberation**  
Without the stability provided by mastery of mind, the deeper layers of clinging and delusion remain hidden beneath mental turbulence. Only when the mind is no longer pulled by distractions can its true nature be seen clearly. This stability, forged through chanding and grounded in virtue, allows for the full unraveling of samsaric bonds and the emergence of non-abiding awareness.
