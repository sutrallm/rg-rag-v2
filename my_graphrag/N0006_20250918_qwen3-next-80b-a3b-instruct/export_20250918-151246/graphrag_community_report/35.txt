Title: The Nature of Non-Attachment in True Selflessness

Summary:  
True selflessness is characterized by the absence of attachment to spiritual experiences, including supernatural abilities and meditative bliss. It is not driven by pursuit or desire, but by a state of clarity that remains unmoved by transient phenomena. This state is likened to a mirror—pure, uncluttered, and unaffected by what passes before it.

—

No pursuit of supernatural abilities  
The practice of true selflessness does not seek or value supernatural powers, treating them as meaningless objects of attachment. There is no striving to attain or preserve such phenomena; their irrelevance to the core state is fully recognized.

No clinging to meditative joy  
Meditative bliss is not desired or held onto, as it is seen as an impermanent and illusory experience. The absence of attachment means even the most pleasant states of mind are not grasped, recognized for their transient nature.

Calm clarity like a mirror  
The mind free from self and attachment is compared to a mirror—clear, unobstructed, and unbound by movement. It reflects without retention, neither welcoming nor rejecting, remaining still despite all that arises or passes.
