**Title: Chao Yue Zhu Zhou and Li Jie: The Path of Non-Attachment and Liberation**

**Summary:**  
Liberation is achieved through the complete eradication of afflictions by cultivating mindfulness, concentration, diligence, moral discipline, and wisdom. True freedom requires transcending all forms of attachment, even to spiritual attainments like liberation itself. This transcendence arises from directly perceiving the impermanent and selfless nature of all phenomena. Mindfulness serves as the constant, practical foundation that sustains mental clarity and manifests liberation in every moment of daily life, not merely in formal practice. The sage embodies this state by not clinging to any result, including the notion of having attained freedom.

**Transcending Attachment is Grounded in Seeing Impermanence and Selflessness**  
The depth of freedom comes from recognizing that all phenomena lack inherent essence and are constantly changing. This clear perception dissolves the tendency to grasp, even at the level of spiritual achievements such as liberation. Without this recognition, attachment persists, and true freedom remains unattainable.

**Liberation Requires the Eradication of All Attachments, Including Spiritual Ones**  
Freedom from suffering and the cycle of rebirth is not complete unless even the attachment to having attained liberation is relinquished. Holding onto spiritual milestones as achievements creates a subtle form of bondage. True liberation is marked by the absence of clinging to any state, outcome, or identity, even the identity of being liberated.

**Mindfulness Sustains Liberation in Every Moment of Daily Life**  
Mindfulness is not a temporary practice limited to meditation sessions but a continuous presence in walking, standing, sitting, and lying down. It functions as the anchor that keeps awareness steady and unhooked from past or future, making the clarity of liberation accessible in ordinary activities. It is the only stable ground upon which freedom is consistently realized.

**Wisdom Directly Leads to Liberation by Removing Ignorance**  
The capacity to see through illusion—understanding impermanence and selflessness—is the essential force that severs the roots of suffering. This wisdom is not theoretical but arises from direct insight, and it is the one path that removes the conditions of affliction and attachment, opening the way to freedom.

**Chan Ding Enables Wisdom by Providing Mental Clarity**  
Focused mental stillness creates the internal conditions necessary for wisdom to arise. Without this clarity, perception remains clouded by distraction and delusion. Chan Ding is the preparatory foundation that allows the mind to directly perceive the true nature of reality, making the cultivation of transcendent wisdom possible.

**The Sage Embodies Liberation Through Integrated Ethical Conduct and Wisdom**  
Freedom is not a solitary attainment of insight or discipline alone. It is the result of harmonizing moral behavior with deep understanding. The sage’s state is not defined by external signs or spiritual titles, but by an unbroken alignment of action and awareness, free from any lingering attachment—even to the very concept of enlightenment.
