**Title: Xinxiu and Shengzhe: The Path of Daily Life Liberation**

**Summary**  
True freedom is achieved not through extraordinary states or retreats, but through unwavering mindful awareness in ordinary daily actions. This practice, called Xinxiu, dissolves craving, aversion, and delusion by cultivating non-attachment to self and experience. Those who embody this fully—Shengzhe—live unshaken by pleasure or pain, their every moment reflecting purified awareness. Liberation is not gained by acquiring spiritual states but by releasing all clinging, especially to meditative bliss. Wisdom arises from seeing impermanence and non-self, making everyday life the sole ground for transformation.

---

**Mindful Awareness in Routine Activity is the Core Practice**  
Every action—walking, sitting, interacting—is treated as an opportunity for cultivation. There is no separation between sacred and mundane; spiritual progress occurs through continuous, nonjudgmental attention in ordinary moments. This persistent engagement, called Xinxiu, transforms daily life into the only viable path for inner transformation.

**Non-Attachment to Experience is Essential for Freedom**  
Even blissful meditative states must be relinquished. Clinging to spiritual attainments, including moments of concentration, reinforces the illusion of self. True liberation requires letting go of desire for permanence and fear of impermanence, ensuring freedom is not tied to any experiential condition.

**Craving and Aversion Are the Root Obstacles to Liberation**  
Suffering arises from emotional distortions—desire for pleasant experiences and resistance to unpleasant ones. These drive attachment and sustain cyclic existence. Their eradication is not an outcome but a necessary condition of liberation, addressed through sustained awareness and wisdom.

**Liberation is the Complete Eradication of Clinging, Not the Attainment of States**  
Freedom is defined by the absence of attachment to “self,” “what is mine,” and all phenomena. It is irreversible, unshaken by external conditions, and resembles the earth that holds all things without clinging. This state is not sought through exceptional experiences but through the dissolution of delusion.

**Daily Life is the Only Context Where Genuine Transformation Occurs**  
Enlightenment does not require isolation. Practices like Xinxiu and Chanding unfold in the midst of ordinary existence. Retreats are not paths to liberation; only the attentive engagement with shenghuo—daily life—leads to the unraveling of attachments.

**Wisdom Arises from Direct Perception of Impermanence and Non-Self**  
Understanding that all phenomena are transient and devoid of inherent self allows one to release attachment. This wisdom is not theoretical but experiential, cultivated through constant mindfulness. It is the direct means to liberation and the defining trait of those who have attained it.

**Those Who Are Fully Liberated Live as Continuous Expressions of Mindful Awareness**  
Shengzhe embody liberation in every action. Their minds reflect without clinging, unaffected by pleasure or pain. They do not seek states but embody the result of complete non-attachment, demonstrating that Xinxiu, when perfected, is indistinguishable from daily conduct.

**Meditative Concentration is Integrated into Daily Life, Not Separate from It**  
Chanding—the steady focus of mind—is not confined to seated meditation. It emerges naturally through mindful engagement in routine activities. This integration ensures that concentration and awareness operate continuously, weakening the influence of craving and aversion throughout the day.

**Persistent, Non-Lazy Cultivation is Non-Negotiable**  
Liberation demands Jingjin—uninterrupted, diligent practice across all experiences. There is no shortcut, no pause for complacency. Every moment is a chance to deepen realization, and negligence sustains delusion.

**Liberation and Cyclic Existence Are Inversely Defined**  
Freedom from samsara is not a destination but the cessation of its causes. Where craving, aversion, and delusion cease, cyclic existence ends. Liberation cannot exist without their complete removal, and their presence ensures the persistence of suffering.
