Title: The Nature of Sanctified and Enlightened Beings

Summary:  
Sanctified and enlightened beings have fully transcended the root causes of suffering—greed, aversion, and delusion—through the cultivation of moral purity, wisdom, and mindfulness. Their minds are permanently free from craving, hostility, and ignorance, resulting in an unshakable inner calm and spontaneous compassion that extends universally. This state is not achieved through external acts but through deep internal transformation, manifesting naturally as quietude and benevolence.

—

Sanctified Beings Are Free from the Three Root Afflictions  
These beings have eradicated greed, aversion, and delusion entirely. Their liberation is not partial; each affliction has been completely uprooted. Greed is extinguished, removing attachment and desire. Aversion is destroyed, ending reactive hostility and resistance to unpleasant experiences. Delusion is overcome through direct insight into reality’s true nature—impermanence, suffering, and the absence of self.

—

Quietude Arises Naturally from Mental Liberation  
Inner stillness is not forced or temporary; it emerges as a consequence of being free from internal and external disturbances. The mind of a sanctified being remains undisturbed regardless of circumstances, indicating a profound and lasting transformation rather than a fleeting meditative state.

—

Compassion Is an Inevitable Expression of Liberation  
When self-centeredness is absent, benevolence flows unconditionally toward all sentient beings, regardless of form or species. This compassion is not an act of will or moral duty but a natural byproduct of a mind unbound by craving, aversion, or ignorance.

—

Penetrating Wisdom Dissolves Delusion  
Delusion stems from misunderstanding reality as permanent, self-contained, and free from suffering. Those who have attained purity see reality as it is—impermanent, inherently unsatisfactory, and devoid of fixed identity. This direct perception replaces ignorance with unshakable clarity, rendering delusion impossible to sustain.
