Title: Richang Xinxiang and Yinguan as Pathways to Liberation

Summary:  
The continuous practice of mindful awareness in daily life—richang xinxiang—and the direct observation of phenomena as impermanent, unsatisfactory, and selfless—yinguan—are interdependent practices that together form the foundation of liberation. Yinguan cultivates the release of attachment by revealing the true nature of experience, while richang xinxiang ensures this understanding is lived, not merely contemplated. Together, they transform ordinary actions into sustained opportunities for freedom from delusion.

—  

Richang Xinxiang Embodies Daily Liberation  
Mindful awareness in everyday activities—such as walking, breathing, and thinking—is not a supplementary practice but the direct, practical expression of deeper meditative realization. It makes liberation accessible beyond the confines of formal sitting, ensuring continuity of awareness throughout the day.

Yinguan Reveals the Nature of Experience  
By observing all phenomena as impermanent, unsatisfactory, and without self, this practice dismantles the illusions that sustain attachment. It is the core method through which detachment arises, serving as the engine of wisdom and the basis for freedom from delusion.

Richang Xinxiang Is the Living Expression of Yinguan  
The daily practice of mindful presence is not separate from observing the true nature of reality—it is its embodiment. Mundane actions become vessels of awakening because they are informed by the clarity cultivated through yinguan.

Sages Sustain Liberation Through Continuous Observation  
Those who consistently apply yinguan do so as the enduring foundation of their wisdom. Their path is not occasional or situational but unwavering, rooted in the unbroken observation of reality as it is, enabling persistent release from false constructs.
