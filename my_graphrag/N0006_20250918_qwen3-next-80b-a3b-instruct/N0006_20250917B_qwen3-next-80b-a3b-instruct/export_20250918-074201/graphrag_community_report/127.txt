**Title: Saintly Beings and Their Superiority Over Heavenly Beings**

**Summary:**  
Saintly beings have completely transcended all fundamental emotional and cognitive disturbances—greed, hatred, and delusion—through wisdom, meditation, and moral discipline. They dwell in unshakable inner peace, free from attachment and reactivity, and are no longer subject to rebirth. Their profound clarity and moral perfection cause celestial beings to hold them in deep reverence, surpassing even the status of heavenly entities.

**Mastery Over Root Afflictions**  
Saintly beings have fully extinguished greed, hatred, and delusion, the three root disturbances that fuel suffering and cycle rebirth. This eradication is not partial but complete, achieved through sustained wisdom and meditative practice, resulting in total freedom from attachment and aversion. Their unshakable state arises not from suppression but from deep transformation.

**Inner Stillness and Mental Clarity**  
Through the practice of Chan Ding, saintly beings stabilize the mind, reduce reactivity, and cultivate continuous mental clarity. This allows them to perceive all phenomena as impermanent and devoid of self, eliminating the basis for attachment and aversion. Their peace is not environmental but intrinsic, unaffected by external circumstances.

**Supreme Dignity Beyond Celestial Realms**  
Heavenly beings, though elevated, revere saintly beings due to their unmatched purity and wisdom. The saintly are not merely morally superior but spiritually transcendent—liberated from the cycle of rebirth entirely. This liberation elevates them beyond the dignity of even the most exalted celestial entities.

**Delusion as the Origin of Emotional Disturbance**  
Delusion distorts the understanding of self and other, directly giving rise to anger and aversion. By uprooting delusion through wisdom and meditation, saintly beings remove the source of all emotional turmoil, rendering anger irrelevant and unarising in their consciousness.
