Title: The Path of Spiritual Cultivation and the Obstacle of Indulgence

Summary:  
Sustained effort is essential to remain on the correct spiritual path, as indulgence—characterized by mental laxity and distraction—directly undermines progress and perpetuates suffering. Without active vigilance, one falls into negligence, straying from the path that leads to liberation.

Effort is the necessary practice to maintain alignment with the righteous path.  
Indulgence is the primary barrier to spiritual advancement, causing distraction and reinforcing cyclic suffering.  
The path requires constant vigilance because identity with ignorance and laziness leads inevitably to deviation.  
Spiritual progress is not passive; it demands continuous, deliberate action to counteract the natural tendency toward mental laxity.
