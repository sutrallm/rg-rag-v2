Title: Qing Jing Jie Lv and Wu Lou Zhi Hui in the Path of the Sheng Zhe

Summary:  
The cultivation of pure moral discipline is the essential foundation for achieving unblemished wisdom, which defines the enlightened state of the Sheng Zhe. Moral clarity enables mental stability and concentration, creating the conditions under which transcendent wisdom emerges without contamination from delusion or attachment. The Sheng Zhe exemplifies the perfect integration of both discipline and wisdom as inseparable elements of enlightenment.

Pure Moral Discipline Enables Mental Clarity and Stability  
Ethical conduct, termed pure moral discipline, establishes the mental and ethical purity necessary to clear the mind of disturbance. This clarity supports sustained meditative concentration, which is not attainable without the stability derived from consistent moral behavior. It is not merely a set of rules but an active condition that prepares the mind for higher states of understanding.

Unblemished Wisdom Defines the Enlightened State  
Transcendent wisdom, described as unblemished and free from attachment and delusion, is the defining trait of the Sheng Zhe. This wisdom does not arise independently; it emerges only when the mind is purified by moral discipline. Its presence is the ultimate indicator of enlightenment, distinguishing the Sheng Zhe from those still bound by ignorance.

Moral Discipline is the Condition for the Arising of Unblemished Wisdom  
The existence of unblemished wisdom is contingent upon the prior establishment of pure moral discipline. Without the ethical foundation, the mind remains clouded, making the emergence of such wisdom impossible. The Sheng Zhe, who fully embodies this discipline, naturally manifests unblemished wisdom as its inevitable outcome.
