**Title: The Awakened Being: Liberation Through Wisdom and Compassion**

**Summary**  
An awakened being has fully realized liberation by eradicating greed, hatred, and delusion, and lives in a state of non-attachment, free from conceptual constructs and emotional reactivity. This state is not passive detachment but an active, compassionate engagement with all sentient beings, sustained by profound wisdom that perceives the true nature of reality—non-self, impermanence, and emptiness. Compassion ensures this freedom remains open-hearted and ethically grounded.

**Non-Attachment as Embodied Liberation**  
Liberation is not an abstract ideal but a lived condition marked by freedom from clinging—to external objects, internal states, meditative experiences, or spiritual milestones. The awakened being does not seek to possess or preserve any condition, even those deemed sacred, recognizing all forms as transient and empty of independent existence.

**Wisdom and Compassion as Interdependent Forces**  
Wisdom reveals the nature of reality: the absence of permanent self, the impermanence of all phenomena, and the futility of attachment. This understanding is balanced by compassion, which prevents wisdom from decaying into indifference. Together, they ensure that clarity of perception is matched by responsive care for all beings, sustaining ethical action without intrusion or aversion.

**The Empty-Mind State as Manifestation of Freedom**  
The awakened state is experienced as an empty-mind condition: uncluttered by conceptual proliferation, free from attachment and aversion. This is not a void but a clarity—spontaneous, unconditioned awareness that arises naturally when mental constructs and emotional disturbances have dissolved. It defines daily conduct, not occasional meditative states.

**Eradication of the Three Poisons as Foundation**  
The complete removal of greed, hatred, and delusion forms the bedrock of awakening. These roots of suffering no longer arise within the being, eliminating the internal drivers of conflict, craving, and misperception. Without them, the person moves through life without internal resistance or distortion, aligned with reality as it is.

**Ethical Engagement Without Closeness or Aversion**  
Awakened beings actively care for the well-being of all sentient life, yet remain unaffected by personal attachment or aversion. Their actions are not motivated by emotional investment or desire for outcome, but by the spontaneous expression of wisdom and compassion. This results in consistent, beneficial conduct that is neither cold nor entangled.
