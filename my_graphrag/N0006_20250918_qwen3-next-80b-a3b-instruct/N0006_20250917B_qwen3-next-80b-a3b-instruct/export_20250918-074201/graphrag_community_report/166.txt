**Title: Wu Ming, Fan Nao, and Jing Jin: The Path to Liberation from Cyclic Existence**

**Summary**  
Liberation from suffering arises solely through the eradication of ignorance (Wu Ming), which fuels afflictions (Fan Nao) and karmic force (Ye Li), perpetuating the cycle of rebirth (Lun Hui). This transformation is achieved not by external rituals or temporal efforts, but through relentless mindfulness (Jing Jin) and truthful observation of the present moment. Those who fully dismantle ignorance eliminate all mental defilements and attain freedom, characterized by non-attachment and inner transformation.

---

**Ignorance Fuels the Cycle of Suffering**  
Wu Ming, or ignorance of reality’s true nature—impermanence, non-self, and unsatisfactoriness—is the foundational cause of Lun Hui. It distorts perception, leading beings to believe in permanence, satisfaction, and inherent selfhood. This misunderstanding directly generates craving, attachment, and aversion, which sustain the cycle of rebirth and suffering.

**Afflictions Are the Active Drivers of Cyclic Existence**  
Fan Nao—greed, hatred, and delusion—are the immediate manifestations of ignorance. These mental defilements are not mere symptoms but causal forces that bind beings to Lun Hui. Only when they are entirely abandoned does liberation occur, as seen in the state of the enlightened being who is free from all mental suffering.

**Persistent Mindfulness Breaks the Chain of Delusion**  
Jing Jin—the continuous, unwavering practice of mindfulness and correct conduct—is the necessary antidote to Wu Ming. Without it, the mind slips into sloth and delusion, reinforcing ignorance and karmic binding. Its relentless application prevents spiritual stagnation and directly undermines the roots of craving and false perception.

**Liberation Is an Inner Transformation, Not an External Acquisition**  
Freedom from Lun Hui cannot be attained through rituals, time-bound practices, or external conditions. It emerges solely from direct, truthful observation of the present moment and the dismantling of false notions of self. The sages exemplify this timeless emancipation, unbound by place or ritual, having transcended both karmic force and the afflictions that stem from ignorance.

**Karmic Force Is Sustained Until Transcended by Insight**  
Ye Li, the moral consequences of intentional actions, perpetuates rebirth as long as ignorance remains. Only through awakening—rooted in understanding impermanence and non-attachment—can this force be purified. Until then, beings remain subject to its binding power, regardless of outward conduct or ceremonial observances.
