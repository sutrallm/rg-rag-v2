**Title: The Sage’s Liberation: Freedom from Greed, Aversion, and Delusion**

**Summary:**  
A sage has achieved complete liberation by eradicating the three fundamental sources of suffering: greed, aversion, and delusion. This state is marked by undisturbed peace, the absence of reactive suffering, and the full realization of true nature. Aversion arises from misperception caused by delusion, and its removal, along with greed and delusion, defines the sage’s perfected state of wisdom, mindfulness, and meditative concentration.

**Eradication of the Three Roots of Suffering**  
The sage has entirely uprooted greed, aversion, and delusion—identified as the core afflictions obstructing liberation. Each of these is not merely suppressed but fully extinguished, resulting in a mind no longer driven by craving, resistance, or ignorance.

**Aversion as a Consequence of Misperception**  
Aversion emerges from delusion, which distorts the perception of reality. This misalignment causes resistance to what is unpleasant or impermanent. When delusion is removed, the basis for aversion collapses, and the mind no longer reacts with hatred or rejection.

**Integration of Meditative Concentration into Liberation**  
The sage embodies perfected concentration, not as a separate practice but as an inseparable component of liberation. Deep meditative stability is fully woven into the awakened state, supporting undisturbed peace and clarity.

**Freedom from Reactive Suffering**  
Having overcome aversion, the sage’s mind is free from suffering triggered by external conditions. There is no lingering resistance to experience, allowing the individual to dwell in unwavering peace, unshaken by impermanence or discomfort.

**Realization of True Nature**  
The sage’s liberation is rooted in the direct realization of true nature. This understanding dissolves ignorance entirely, eliminating the foundation upon which delusion and its downstream effects—such as aversion and greed—were sustained.
