**Title: The Nature and Reverence of the Saintly Being in Buddhist Realization**

**Summary:**  
A saintly being is one who has achieved total liberation through the complete eradication of greed, hatred, and delusion, resulting in unwavering inner stillness, moral purity, and profound wisdom. This state is not merely a temporary condition but the outcome of sustained exertion and refined meditative discipline, enabling complete freedom from suffering, rebirth, and all external or internal disturbances. Even celestial beings, despite their blissful and powerful existence, hold reverence for the saintly being, recognizing their transcendence beyond even the highest realms of conditioned existence.

**Perfect Moral Discipline as the Foundation**  
Ethical conduct in action, speech, and thought forms the essential basis for all higher spiritual attainments. This discipline is not incidental but a deliberate and perfected condition that directly enables the emergence of deep meditative states and the cultivation of wisdom, sustaining the saintly being’s purity without regression.

**Mastery of Chanding Leads to Unshakable Stillness**  
Through the perfected practice of chanding, the saintly being achieves a state of calm, inner stability, and non-reactivity. This meditative discipline is not passive but an active mastery that eliminates afflictions, anchors awareness, and ensures constant presence regardless of external conditions or internal disturbances.

**Total Eradication of the Three Root Defilements**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion are not merely subdued but fully uprooted. The cessation of greed eliminates clinging; the removal of hatred dissolves aversion and anger; the dispelling of delusion restores clear perception of reality. Together, this triad’s extinction constitutes the core of complete mental purity and liberation.

**Transcendence of Samsara Through Nirvana**  
The saintly being has severed all ties to the cycle of birth and death, not through force but through the harmonious culmination of moral discipline, meditative mastery, and wisdom. This final release is synonymous with nirvana — the absolute end of suffering and rebirth.

**Non-Attachment as the Expression of Liberation**  
Having fully let go of all forms of clinging — to transient states, achievements, or experiences — the saintly being exists beyond desire and aversion. This absence of attachment is not emptiness but the natural condition of one who sees all phenomena as impermanent and ungraspable.

**Wisdom as the Direct Perception of Reality**  
True wisdom is not intellectual knowledge but the direct, unmediated cognition of things as they are. It is this seeing that dissolves delusion, neutralizes emotional reactivity, and renders external influences powerless, establishing the saintly being as a living embodiment of truth.

**Sustained Exertion as the Long-Term Practice**  
The perfection of the saintly being is not accidental or sudden. It results from continuous, unwavering effort over time, maintaining every quality — discipline, meditation, wisdom — until they become inseparable aspects of being.

**Reverence from Celestial Beings as Proof of Superior Attainment**  
Even beings of immense power and bliss within higher realms acknowledge the saintly being’s superiority. This reverence is not symbolic but evidence that enlightenment surpasses all conditioned states, including divine existence, by virtue of its finality and freedom.

**Embodiment of the Fully Cultivated Path**  
The saintly being is not a partial realization but the living completion of every stage of spiritual practice. Each element — moral discipline, chanding, wisdom, exertion — has been perfected, integrated, and enacted without residue or remainder.
