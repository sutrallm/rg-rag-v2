**Title: Jing Jin and Chi Xu Xiu Xi as the Core of Spiritual Liberation**

**Summary**  
Sustained, disciplined effort—Jing Jin—is the indispensable foundation for achieving meditative stability, transcending attachment, and escaping the cycle of rebirth. It is practiced daily through Chi Xu Xiu Xi, the unceasing cultivation of mindfulness and awareness. Without this persistent discipline, spiritual decline, laziness, and craving take hold, rendering deep insight and moral clarity unattainable. The two are inseparable: one is the intention, the other its lived expression.

---

**Effort Prevents Spiritual Decline**  
Jing Jin directly counteracts Ji E—the state of spiritual complacency and heedlessness. Without persistent, mindful action, the mind lapses into laziness, allowing old habits and delusions to resurface. This decline undermines progress and rebinds the individual to suffering, making continuous effort not optional but essential to avoid regression.

**Diligence Enables Meditative Stability**  
Chan Ding, the deep state of meditative focus, cannot be established or maintained without Jing Jin. The sustained energy, discipline, and vigilance provided by diligent effort stabilize the mind, suppress distractions, and prevent meditation from deteriorating into aimless wandering. Without this foundation, concentration collapses.

**Effort Breaks Attachment and Craving**  
Continuous practice weakens emotional and mental grasping toward pleasure by reinforcing awareness and discipline. By maintaining unwavering presence, Jing Jin reduces the conditions in which craving arises, thereby loosening the mind’s entanglement in desires that perpetuate suffering.

**Insight Requires Persistent Cultivation**  
Wisdom that sees impermanence clearly and transcends ignorance cannot emerge sporadically. It is forged only through relentless mindfulness and correct practice. Without Jing Jin, the development of profound understanding is impossible; insight is not a passive realization but the product of sustained effort.

**Daily Practice Embodies Diligence**  
Chi Xu Xiu Xi is not separate from Jing Jin—it is its daily manifestation. Polishing the mind like a mirror or irrigating a field, this unbroken routine sustains moral discipline and mental clarity. One cannot persist without the other; continuous practice is the living form of diligent effort, binding intention to action in every moment.

**Liberation Depends on Uninterrupted Effort**  
Escape from the cycle of birth and death is only possible through unbroken Jing Jin. Without it, the individual remains subject to rebirth and suffering. The sage sustains enlightenment not through fleeting moments of clarity, but through the constant, unwavering application of disciplined practice.
