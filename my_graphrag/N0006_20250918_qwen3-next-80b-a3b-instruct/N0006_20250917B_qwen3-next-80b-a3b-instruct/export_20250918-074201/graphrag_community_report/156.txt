**Title: The Path of the Saintly Being: Eradication of Greed, Hatred, and Delusion Leading to Liberation**

**Summary:**  
Liberation from the cycle of rebirth is achieved only through the complete eradication of greed, hatred, and delusion—three root defilements that sustain suffering and cyclic existence. Saintly beings are defined by their total freedom from these afflictions, attained through moral discipline, unwavering mindfulness, and direct insight into impermanence, non-self, and unsatisfactoriness. They dwell in unshakable peace, transcending all emotional reactivity and external conditions, and are revered even by celestial beings for surpassing even their blissful existence. The means to this liberation are sustained effort, meditative stability, and wisdom that dismantles illusion at its core.

---

**Moral Discipline Serves as the Foundation for Mental Purification**  
Ethical conduct in action, speech, and thought forms the essential base upon which deeper meditative states and wisdom are built. Without this purity, the mind remains too disturbed to cultivate clarity or insight. It supports the stability required to observe the mind and weaken the grip of attachment, aversion, and delusion.

**Greed Arises from the Illusion of Permanence and Self**  
Craving for pleasurable experiences, possessions, or spiritual attainments stems from the mistaken belief that these can provide lasting satisfaction or constitute a fixed identity. This attachment binds beings to rebirth by generating karma and reinforcing the delusion that transient phenomena are enduring or worthy of possession. Its cessation requires dismantling the false sense of self and the craving that feeds from it.

**Hatred Emerges from Misapprehension of Reality as Threatening**  
Aversion, anger, and hostility arise when one perceives experiences or beings as inherently unpleasant or dangerous due to a false sense of separation and self. This misperception is rooted in delusion—the failure to see all phenomena as impermanent and non-self. Hatred produces inner turmoil, negative karma, and further entanglement in rebirth, and is overcome only through equanimous observation and insight into the impermanent nature of all experience.

**Delusion Is the Foundational Defilement Underlying All Suffering**  
Ignorance of reality—the belief in a permanent self, inherent existence, and lasting happiness—is the root from which greed and hatred grow. It obscures the true nature of all phenomena as transient, unsatisfactory, and devoid of intrinsic identity. This ignorance perpetuates clinging and resistance, trapping beings in samsara. Its eradication is not gradual but total, achieved only when wisdom directly reveals non-self and impermanence.

**Meditative Stability (Chan Ding) Neutralizes Afflictions by Calming Reactivity**  
A steady, undistracted mind, cultivated through disciplined meditation, creates space between stimulus and response. This stability allows one to observe greed without acting on it, see aversion without being consumed by it, and recognize delusion without identifying with it. Chan Ding does not suppress defilements but weakens their power by exposing their transient and empty nature, enabling their gradual dissolution.

**Wisdom Directly Uproots the Three Defilements Through Insight**  
True understanding—born of mindfulness and meditative realization—sees through the false constructs of self, permanence, and satisfaction. This wisdom dismantles ignorance at its source, causing greed to lose its object, hatred to lose its target, and delusion to lose its hold. It is not intellectual knowledge but direct perception that transforms the mind, replacing delusion with clarity and attachment with non-attachment.

**Saintly Beings Have Transcended Samsara and Embodied Supreme Peace**  
Those who have fully extinguished greed, hatred, and delusion no longer generate karma that binds them to rebirth. They dwell in unshakable stillness, unmoved by internal or external conditions, and are no longer subject to suffering or pleasure. Their purity, wisdom, and moral perfection are so complete that even celestial beings honor them, recognizing them as having attained a state beyond all conditioned existence.

**Effortful Practice Is Necessary to Sustain Progress and Prevent Regression**  
Liberation is not passive. It demands continuous vigilance, diligent cultivation, and resistance against sloth, distraction, and complacency. Without sustained exertion, the mind lapses back into ignorance, attachment, and aversion. The path requires constant mindfulness, ethical adherence, and meditative focus to prevent the defilements from re-establishing themselves.

**Celestial Beings Reverence Those Who Have Attained Complete Liberation**  
Though celestial beings enjoy heightened bliss and longevity, they remain within samsara, bound by subtle forms of attachment. Saintly beings, having severed all roots of rebirth, embody a state of freedom that surpasses even divine conditions. Their reverence is not born of awe at power, but recognition of unparalleled wisdom and total freedom from suffering.

**Liberation Is Defined by the Absolute Absence of the Three Defilements**  
There is no partial liberation. One is either fully free from greed, hatred, and delusion—or still bound. The cessation of any one of these three is insufficient; their simultaneous eradication is the hallmark of enlightenment. Only when all are extinguished does the mind reveal its natural clarity, and only then does rebirth cease entirely.
