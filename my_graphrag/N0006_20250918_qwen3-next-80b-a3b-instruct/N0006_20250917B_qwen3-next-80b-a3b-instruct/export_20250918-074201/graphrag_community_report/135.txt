**Title: Shengsi Lunhuai and the Path to Liberation Through Chanxiu, Xingxi, and Jingjin**

**Summary:**  
The cycle of birth and death is rooted in ignorance, attachment, and craving, sustained by karma and delusion. Liberation is not achieved through escape or supernatural means, but through disciplined daily practice that transforms mental habits. Meditation (chanxiu), cultivated through mindful repetition (xingxi) and sustained effort (jingjin), directly confronts the mind’s patterns, revealing their impermanent and unsatisfactory nature. This process extinguishes craving and aversion, dismantles clinging, and realizes liberation in the present moment. The enlightened being, characterized by pure moral discipline and wisdom, has uprooted all afflictions and remains unshaken by worldly conditions.

**Chanxiu Directly Confronts Mental Patterns to Extinguish Ignorance**  
Meditation is not a retreat from reality but a sustained practice of observing thoughts, emotions, and habits as transient, unsatisfactory, and devoid of self. This observation is not for temporary calm or psychic abilities, but to uncover the mind’s mechanisms, thereby removing the root cause of suffering: ignorance. Through this clear seeing, attachment loses its power, and liberation becomes possible.

**Xingxi Cultivates the Mind Gradually Through Daily Mindfulness**  
The transformation of habitual mental patterns occurs not through sudden revelation but through consistent, everyday practice: observing the breath, the mind, and impermanence. Over time, this steady training reduces distraction, stabilizes focus, and naturally gives rise to inner peace and wisdom. It is the essential foundation upon which deeper meditative realization is built.

**Jingjin Is the Necessary Active Effort to Break the Cycle**  
Without disciplined, persistent effort, even accurate teachings remain inert. Liberation cannot be passively received; it requires constant, intentional action to disrupt the cycles of craving and delusion. Jingjin fuels the entire path, making the other practices effective and ensuring progress beyond mere intellectual understanding.

**Attachment Is the Root of Bondage; Letting Go Brings Freedom**  
Clinging to experiences, outcomes, or transient states of mind is the fundamental source of entrapment. The repeated recognition of attachment’s instability through meditation reveals its futility. When attachment dissolves, the mind is no longer bound by the causes of rebirth, and freedom emerges naturally.

**Liberation Is Realized in the Present Moment, Not Future States**  
Enlightenment is not found in distant heavens or future attainments. It arises when one meets arising greed, anger, or clinging with clear awareness—without reacting or identifying with them. This immediate, non-reactive recognition is the essence of being free, now, in this very moment.

**Craving and Aversion Form an Entangling Net That Sustains the Cycle**  
The continuous loop of birth and death is maintained by the interplay of desire and aversion. These forces act like a slow-closing web, binding beings to suffering. Their presence ensures karmic continuity; their cessation is the key to breaking free.

**The Enlightened Being Has Eradicated All Afflictions and Transcended Samsara**  
One who has fully realized liberation possesses unmoving moral discipline and direct wisdom. All craving, aversion, and ignorance are uprooted. Such a being is no longer subject to karmic outcomes or worldly conditions, abides in stillness, and remains untouched by the cycle of birth and death.
