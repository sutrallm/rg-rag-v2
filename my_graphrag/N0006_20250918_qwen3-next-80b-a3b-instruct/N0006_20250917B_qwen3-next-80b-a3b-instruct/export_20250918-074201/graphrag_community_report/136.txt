Title: Shengtao, Fangyi, and Chanding in the Path Beyond Suffering

Summary:  
The cycle of birth and death (Shengtao) is perpetuated by Fangyi—sloth, indulgence, and mental negligence—which obstructs awareness and halts spiritual advancement. Chanding is the prescribed practice to disrupt this cycle, enabling liberation from suffering by overcoming the very conditions that bind beings to it.

Fangyi Directly Sustains the Cycle of Birth and Death  
Fangyi—manifesting as sloth, indulgence, and mental negligence—is explicitly named as the immediate force that traps beings within Shengtao. Its presence clouds awareness, diminishes clarity, and creates conditions where spiritual growth stalls, ensuring continued entanglement in suffering.

Chanding Serves as the Means to Break Free from Shengtao  
Chanding is not merely a practice but the tasked method for escaping the cycle of birth and death. It functions as the active counterbalance to Fangyi, enabling practitioners to dissolve the mental and moral obstacles that sustain suffering and restore the path toward liberation.

Mindfulness, Wisdom, and Moral Discipline Are the Framework for Liberation  
Though not detailed as distinct actions, these three qualities are presented as the essential tools through which practitioners sever their connection to Shengtao. They operate as the broader structure enabling Chanding to be effective, reinforcing the defeat of Fangyi and the realization of freedom from suffering.
