**Title: Liberation Through Non-Abiding Mind and the Realization of No-Self**

**Summary:**  
True liberation is achieved when the mind does not cling to any experience or state, recognizing that all phenomena — form, sensation, perception, mental formations, and consciousness — are transient and without a fixed essence. This understanding dissolves the illusion of a permanent self, removing the basis for attachment and aversion. Freedom arises not through mastering experiences, but through seeing them as they are: impermanent, interdependent, and selfless.

**Non-Abiding Mind Emerges from the Absence of a Central Self**  
When the mind fully realizes that no intrinsic self exists, it naturally ceases to anchor itself in any condition — pleasure, pain, identity, or thought. There is no fixed point to hold onto, so the mind rests without preference or resistance, like open space.

**Impermanence of the Five Aggregates Reveals No-Self**  
The continuous flux of form, sensation, perception, mental formations, and consciousness shows that nothing within them remains constant. This constant transformation makes it impossible to locate a stable, unchanging core — the very notion of a permanent “I” dissolves under direct observation.

**No-Self Recognized as the Ground for Non-Attachment**  
Because no enduring self is found within experience, there is no entity to defend, desire, or fear. Without an “I” to be affected by changing conditions, clinging naturally falls away, allowing experience to flow without resistance or craving.
