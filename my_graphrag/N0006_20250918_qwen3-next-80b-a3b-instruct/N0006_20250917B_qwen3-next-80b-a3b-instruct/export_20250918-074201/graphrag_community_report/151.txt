**Title: The Three Poisons and the Cycle of Birth and Death in Buddhist Liberation**

**Summary**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion fuel the endless cycle of rebirth and suffering. Craving, rooted in ignorance, binds individuals to this cycle by driving attachment to impermanent things. Liberation is achieved only when these forces are fully uprooted through wisdom, effort, and the realization of no-self. Those who have eradicated the three poisons are no longer subject to rebirth.

**The Three Poisons Sustain the Cycle of Rebirth**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion are identified as the fundamental sources of suffering and the driving forces behind the continuous cycle of birth and death. They are not peripheral issues but the core causes that keep beings trapped in repetitive existence, making their removal essential to any path of release.

**Craving Arises from Ignorance and Reinforces Suffering**  
Craving is not an isolated desire but a direct consequence of misunderstanding reality. Ignorance leads beings to cling to transient phenomena, which in turn binds them to suffering and ensures the continuation of rebirth. Without addressing the root of this misunderstanding, craving persists and deepens entanglement.

**Liberation Requires the Eradication of the Three Poisons and Realization of No-Self**  
Freedom from the cycle of birth and death is attainable only through complete removal of greed, hatred, and delusion. This is accomplished not through temporary fixes but through sustained effort and the direct realization that there is no enduring self. This understanding severs the chain of clinging that sustains the cycle.

**Persistent Effort Is Necessary for Breakthrough**  
Breaking free from the cycle of rebirth cannot be achieved passively. It demands consistent and dedicated practice. Without persistent discipline, even an understanding of the causes of suffering will not lead to liberation.
