Title: The Nature of Liberation as Inherent Purity and Completion

Summary:  
Liberation is characterized by the natural revelation of an inherent state that is both wholly pure and completely whole. These two qualities—purity and completion—are not separate conditions but mutually reinforcing aspects of the same fundamental nature of the mind.

The Nature of Liberation as Inherent Purity and Completion  
Liberation is not an attained condition but the spontaneous emergence of what has always been present: a state of inherent purity. This purity is not cultivated or imposed; it arises naturally when conditions allow its unobstructed presence. Similarly, the full manifestation of inherent completion accompanies this purity, revealing the mind’s nature as already flawless and self-sufficient.

Mutual Reinforcement of Purity and Completion  
The qualities of inherent purity and inherent completion are inseparable in describing the essence of liberation. Each depends on the other for full expression—purity removes obscurations, allowing completion to be recognized, while completion affirms the absence of lack, confirming the authenticity of purity. Together, they form a unified description of the liberated state.
