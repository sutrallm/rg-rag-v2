**Title: Zhicheng and Shishi: Cultivating Non-Attachment and Equanimity**

**Summary:**  
Zhicheng and Shishi are mutually reinforcing practices rooted in the understanding of impermanence and non-self. Zhicheng enables observation of all experiences—positive or negative—without attachment, while Shishi fosters equanimity in the present moment by neither clinging to pleasure nor resisting pain. Together, they dissolve distortion and aversion, leading to inner freedom through the direct experience of reality as it is.

**Zhicheng Emerges from Realization of Non-Self**  
The understanding that all phenomena lack inherent self allows Zhicheng to arise naturally. This wisdom permits observation of meditative joys and psychic powers without becoming entangled in them, preventing identification and fostering detachment.

**Shishi Deepens Clarity by Eliminating Distortion**  
By meeting each moment with equanimity—accepting pleasure without craving and pain without recoil—Shishi removes the filters of desire and aversion. This clears perceptual distortion, allowing reality to be seen without added emotional or cognitive noise.

**Shishi Nurtures Zhicheng Through Direct Experience**  
The consistent practice of observing experience exactly as it unfolds, without interference, strengthens the capacity to witness all phenomena without attachment. This repeated exposure without reaction cultivates the very wisdom Zhicheng embodies.

**Zhicheng and Shishi Are Interdependent Pathways to Freedom**  
One cannot fully develop Zhicheng without the disciplined presence of Shishi, nor can Shishi reach its full potential without the foundational understanding of non-self that Zhicheng represents. Their synergy dismantles clinging at its root, leading to enduring inner freedom.
