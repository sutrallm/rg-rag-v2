**Title: Aversion, Greed, and Ignorance as Barriers to Liberation in the Rebirth Cycle**

**Summary**  
Aversion, greed, and ignorance are three interlinked mental forces that bind sentient beings to the continuous cycle of birth, death, and rebirth. These forces generate negative karma, fuel attachment and resistance, and obstruct the clarity needed for liberation. Liberation is attained only through the complete abandonment of these forces, achieved via chanting, exertion, and wisdom. Those who have transcended the cycle no longer react with hatred or craving and are free from the suffering inherent in rebirth.

**Chanting Neutralizes Aversion**  
Chanting stabilizes the mind, preventing reactive responses to unpleasant stimuli. This practice directly counteracts aversion by calming resistance and hatred, allowing the mind to remain unshaken by external conditions.

**Aversion Perpetuates Suffering and Rebirth**  
Aversion produces negative karma through hatred and resentment, which in turn binds beings to suffering and ensures continued existence within the rebirth cycle. Its presence sustains dukkha, making its complete relinquishment necessary for freedom.

**Greed Fuels Attachment and Karma**  
Greed drives desire and clinging, creating the conditions for ongoing karma that anchors beings in the cycle of rebirth. Like aversion, it is a primary force sustaining samsara through attachment to transient experiences.

**Ignorance Is the Foundational Cause of Bondage**  
Ignorance of true reality underlies all other defilements. It prevents the development of wisdom and leads to unwholesome actions, which in turn reinforce the cycle of birth and death. Without overcoming ignorance, liberation is unattainable.

**Liberty Requires Mastery of Chanting, Exertion, and Wisdom**  
Freedom from rebirth is achieved only through the combined discipline of chanting (to quiet aversion), exertion (to cultivate effort and discipline), and wisdom (to dissolve ignorance). Those who have mastered these practices no longer generate aversion or greed and have ended the cycle of suffering.
