**Title: Shengren and the Eradication of Yuanshi as the Path to Liberation**

**Summary:**  
The Shengren is defined as a being who has fully eliminated greed, hatred, and delusion—the yuanshi—and by doing so, has attained liberation from the cycle of rebirth. This state is characterized by moral purity, direct wisdom, stillness, and the realization of impermanence and non-attachment. The removal of the yuanshi is not merely a feature but the essential condition for such transcendence.

**Shengren Achieves Liberation Through the Complete Elimination of the Three Poisons**  
The Shengren is not simply someone who reduces greed, hatred, or delusion, but one who has entirely uprooted them. This eradication is the singular defining trait that distinguishes the Shengren from others. Without this complete removal, transcendence is unattainable, indicating that partial progress is insufficient for liberation.

**Stillness and Direct Wisdom Are Manifestations of a Mind Free from the Three Poisons**  
The Shengren’s inner stillness and direct wisdom arise not as separate qualities but as natural expressions of a mind rid of the three poisons. These attributes are not cultivated independently; they emerge only after greed, hatred, and delusion are fully extinguished, revealing their dependence on the absence of the yuanshi.

**Impermanence and Non-Attachment Are Realized Only After Eradicating the Roots of Suffering**  
The Shengren’s perception of all phenomena as impermanent and non-attached—referred to as chaoshi—is not an intellectual understanding but a lived state. This realization is made possible solely after the yuanshi are eradicated, demonstrating that true insight into reality is contingent upon the cleansing of mental defilements.
