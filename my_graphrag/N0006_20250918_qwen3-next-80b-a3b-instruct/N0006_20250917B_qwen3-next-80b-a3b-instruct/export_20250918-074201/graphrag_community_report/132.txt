**Title: The Saintly Being: Mastery Over Desire, Aversion, and Delusion**

**Summary**  
The saintly being has achieved complete liberation by uprooting greed, hatred, and delusion. This state is marked by unwavering inner stability, moral perfection, and unerring wisdom that perceives reality without distortion. External circumstances—whether pleasant or unpleasant—have no power to disturb this state, as it arises from the total absence of attachment and aversion.

**Perfect Moral Discipline as a Foundation**  
The saintly being consistently upholds perfected moral precepts, which are not merely behavioral rules but an expression of inner purity. These precepts are inseparable from their spiritual discipline and reflect the absence of harmful impulses.

**Unshakable Inner Stability**  
This being remains unmoved by external conditions, not through suppression but through profound internal equilibrium. Neither favorable nor adverse events generate emotional reactivity, because the roots of craving and aversion have been entirely severed.

**Liberation Through Wisdom**  
True freedom is not a state of passive detachment but an active realization of reality as it is. The saintly being’s wisdom allows direct perception unclouded by delusion or attachment, enabling the complete transcendence of the cycle of birth and death.

**Absence of the Three Poisons as the Core Condition**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion are not merely reduced but wholly eradicated. Their complete removal is the defining condition of liberation, making all other qualities—moral discipline, wisdom, and inner stability—possible.
