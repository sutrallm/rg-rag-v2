**Title: The Interdependence of Virtue, Calm Mind, and Wisdom in Liberation**

**Summary:**  
Liberation is achieved through the synergistic cultivation of ethical conduct, mental stillness, and deep understanding of impermanence and non-substantiality. Ethical behavior creates the stable foundation for mental calm, which in turn allows for the direct perception of reality as conditionally arisen and selfless. This perception ends attachment and resistance to change, leading to freedom. A perfected being embodies both moral purity and unshakable clarity, where virtue and wisdom continually reinforce each other.

**Ethical Conduct Enables Mental Stillness**  
Purified behavior establishes moral stability, which is necessary for the mind to settle into a state of calm. Without this foundation, the mind remains agitated, preventing the emergence of clear perception.

**Calm Mind Unveils True Nature**  
The stilling of mental activity through meditation allows for the direct arising of understanding — specifically, the perception that all things are impermanent and lack inherent self. This clarity is not conceptual but experiential.

**All Phenomena Are Conditionally Arisen**  
Everything that exists comes about due to causes and conditions, and nothing endures as a fixed, independent entity. Recognizing this eliminates the misperception that external circumstances or the self are permanent or solid.

**Non-Attachment Follows from Seeing Impermanence**  
When reality is seen as constantly changing and without inherent substance, the emotional reactions of satisfaction or despair tied to external conditions naturally dissolve. Resistance to change ceases, and freedom arises.

**Wisdom and Virtue Are Reciprocal**  
Ethical behavior supports the growth of deep understanding by creating the conditions for mental clarity. Simultaneously, deep understanding informs ethical action, ensuring it is free from self-interest and delusion.

**Liberation Is the Result of Unified Understanding**  
The direct realization that the self is a transient process — not a fixed entity — dismantles illusion. This understanding, combined with moral purity, constitutes the full expression of a perfected state, and is the essential path to freedom.

**Perfected Nature Is Embodied in the Saint**  
One who has fully realized these principles exhibits unwavering ethical conduct and sees all phenomena as impermanent and without substance. Such a person is free from delusion and no longer bound by the cycle of craving and aversion.
