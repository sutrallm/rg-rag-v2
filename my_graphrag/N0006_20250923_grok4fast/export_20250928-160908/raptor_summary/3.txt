<heading>Meditation: Path to Liberation and Inner Peace<\heading>
- A focused mind with sustained mindfulness avoids disturbances, like a wild animal in a mosquito-free forest; through meditation, practitioners escape indulgence and worries, attaining inner peace.

- Diligent followers of the Buddha's teachings evade the death demon's control; only persistent effort without indolence masters meditation.

- Meditation brings mental liberation and insight into worldly impermanence; liberated practitioners avoid clinging and remain unbound, even when seeking results.

- Those with precepts and wisdom, who diligently cultivate the mind in stillness, eradicate afflictions and transcend suffering; such practitioners earn respect from devas.
