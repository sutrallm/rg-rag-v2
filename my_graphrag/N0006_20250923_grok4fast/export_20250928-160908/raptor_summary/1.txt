<heading>Core Principles of Meditation and Spiritual Liberation<\heading>
- **Essence of Meditation**: A focused mind and sustained mindfulness distance one from distractions, freeing practitioners from laziness and afflictions to achieve inner peace and clarity.

- **Importance of Diligent Practice**: By following teachings and persisting in effort, one evades death and Mara; only undistracted practice masters meditation and advances toward liberation.

- **Wisdom Transcending Attachment**: Meditation fosters mental liberation and insight into impermanence, enabling practitioners to avoid clinging to states or outcomes, thus attaining true freedom.

- **Qualities of Saints**: Endowed with precepts and wisdom, saints cultivate serene mental stillness, eradicate afflictions, and transcend suffering, earning respect even from gods.
