**Report on Practitioner Liberation and Ultimate Peace**

**Summary**
This report outlines the path to liberation as described in the provided text. The central objective for a practitioner is to break through a state of attachment, specifically by severing the roots of greed, hatred, and delusion. Achieving this goal results in a state of ultimate peace and complete mental purity, which are the definitive markers of a perfected practitioner who is free from sorrow and distress.

**The Central Objective: Breaking the Net of Attachment**
The primary aim for the practitioner is to break through what is termed the "net of attachment." This act is the necessary condition for achieving liberation. The text defines this net as being composed of the "three poisons": greed, hatred, and delusion. Therefore, the process of liberation involves actively severing these specific chains.

**The Consequence of Severing the Three Poisons**
The direct outcome of breaking the net of attachment by severing greed, hatred, and delusion is the attainment of ultimate peace. This state is also described as "ultimate安稳" (ultimate safety/stability) and is synonymous with the perfection of practice. The text establishes a clear causal chain: severing the three poisons breaks the net of attachment, which leads directly to this peaceful state.

**The State of a Perfected Practitioner**
A practitioner who has achieved this perfection is characterized by two primary qualities. First, they experience a complete absence of sorrow and distress. Second, their mind is in a state of thorough purity and is entirely free from defilement. This mental purity is identified as the definitive sign that practice has been brought to completion.
