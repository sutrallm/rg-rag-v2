**Report on the Path to Nirvana**

**Summary**
The text outlines a clear causal sequence for achieving complete liberation, or Nirvana. It identifies the ultimate goal, the necessary condition for reaching it, and the specific practice required to cultivate that condition. The state of being a Saint is defined as the successful culmination of this process.

**The Goal of Spiritual Practice**
The ultimate objective described is achieving a state of complete liberation, which is explicitly defined as the attainment of Nirvana. This state represents the final goal of the spiritual path.

**The Prerequisite for Liberation**
Attaining this liberation is dependent on possessing a specific type of understanding termed "liberating wisdom." This wisdom is identified as a critical and necessary condition that directly leads to the state of complete freedom.

**The Method for Cultivating Wisdom**
The text specifies a single, defined practice as the means to acquire this liberating wisdom: the practice of meditative concentration. This discipline is presented as the pathway through which the essential wisdom is developed.

**The Outcome of the Process**
An individual who successfully completes this path—cultivating liberating wisdom through meditative concentration and thereby achieving Nirvana—is designated as a Saint. This term specifically describes one who has reached the state of total liberation.
