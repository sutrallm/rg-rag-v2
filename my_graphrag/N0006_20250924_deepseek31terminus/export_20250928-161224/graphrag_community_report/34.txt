**Report on Wisdom, the Five Aggregates, and Renunciation**

**Summary**
The analysis reveals a defined sequence where a specific form of wisdom, 般若, examines the five aggregates, leading to a recognition of their impermanent and decaying nature. This understanding is the direct cause for the emergence of genuine renunciation or detachment.

**The Process of 般若 (Wisdom)**
般若 is identified as a distinct type of wisdom. Its primary function is to engage in contemplation, specifically focusing on the nature of the five aggregates.

**The Nature of the Five Aggregates**
The object of 般若's contemplation is the fundamental characteristic of the five aggregates: they are impermanent and subject to decay.

**The Consequence of Understanding Impermanence**
The direct outcome of perceiving the impermanent nature of the five aggregates through 般若 is the arising of genuine renunciation or detachment. This detachment is not an independent action but a natural consequence of this specific understanding.
