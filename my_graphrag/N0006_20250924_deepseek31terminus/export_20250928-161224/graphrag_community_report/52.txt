**Report on the Relationship Between Practitioners, Saints, and Truth**

**Summary**
The text establishes a clear progression from practitioner to saint, defined by the complete realization of truth. This realization, characterized by a mind perfectly aligned with truth, is presented as the singular mechanism for achieving the ultimate goals of severing the root of生死 (life and death) and attaining supreme peace.

**The Progression from Practitioner to Saint**
A practitioner is one who clearly recognizes the truth of impermanence. A saint is distinguished as a practitioner who has fully realized this truth. This indicates that sainthood is not a separate category but the culmination of the practitioner's path, achieved through complete insight.

**The Critical Role of Mental Alignment**
The complete alignment of one's mind with truth is identified as the essential condition for action. The state where "心念完全契合真理" (the mind's thoughts are fully in accord with truth) is the precise point at which the fundamental root of生死 (life and death) can be severed.

**The Dual Outcome of Realizing Truth**
Aligning with truth produces two primary, interconnected outcomes. The first is the attainment of "无上安乐" (supreme peace and happiness). The second is the severing of the root of生死 (life and death). The text explicitly states this dual consequence twice, underscoring its fundamental importance.
