**Title: The Path and Outcomes of Zen Meditation Practice**

**Summary:**
Zen meditation practice is defined by the pursuit of a state of Nirvana, which results in freedom from afflictions. Practitioners who embody the principle of heedfulness are granted a form of protection from the dominion of death.

**Insights:**

**The Ultimate Goal of Practice is Nirvana**
The text defines the ultimate objective for a Zen practitioner as achieving the state of Nirvana through disciplined practice.

**Liberation from Affliction through Practice**
A direct outcome of this practice is the practitioner's ability to become free from the disturbances of afflictions and troubles.

**Heedfulness as a Shield Against Death**
For a practitioner who is unwavering and diligent ("not heedless"), the text states that the "lord of death" holds no power, indicating that such disciplined practice confers a transcendent form of security.
