**Report on the Path to Awakening and Mastery of Meditation**

**Summary**

This report details the interconnected concepts central to a specific spiritual practice. The ultimate objective is attaining a state of awakening, known as 觉悟. Achieving this state requires unwavering diligence (精进) and leads to a final state of liberation (解脱). Furthermore, those who reach awakening gain a complete and authentic mastery of the essential principle of meditation (禅定要义).

**The Ultimate Objective of Practice**

The text defines 觉悟 (awakening) as the ultimate goal of practice. It is described as an enlightened or awakened state achieved through persistent vigilance and diligence, representing the realm of a perfected practitioner.

**The Foundational Role of Diligence**

The attainment of 觉悟 is directly dependent on 精进 (diligence). The text explicitly states that diligence is essential for reaching awakening, indicating it is a fundamental, necessary component of the path.

**The Resultant State of Liberation**

A practitioner who has fully achieved 觉悟 enters a state known as 解脱 (liberation). This liberation is presented as the final condition resulting from complete awakening.

**Mastery as a Consequence of Awakening**

True and complete mastery of the essential meaning or principle of meditation (禅定要义) is a direct outcome of attaining 觉悟. The text specifies that awakened practitioners are the ones who can genuinely master this essential principle.
