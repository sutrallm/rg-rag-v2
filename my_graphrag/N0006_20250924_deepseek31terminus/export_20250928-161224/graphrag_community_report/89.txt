**Title: The Path of Non-Attachment and the Perception of Phenomena**

**Summary**

This report outlines a specific path of spiritual development. Through meditation, a practitioner achieves mental liberation, which results in a state of non-attachment and non-bondage. This state enables a truthful perception of the constant flux of worldly phenomena—their arising and ceasing. Crucially, this perspective of non-attachment extends to spiritual achievements themselves, preventing any form of clinging.

**The Mechanism for Achieving Non-Attachment**

Meditation is the direct cause of mental liberation. This liberation is not an end in itself but is the necessary condition for attaining a specific wisdom: the wisdom of non-attachment and non-bondage. The process is sequential, where disciplined practice leads to a fundamental shift in mental state.

**The Consequence of Liberation on Worldly Perception**

A practitioner who has achieved mental liberation gains the capacity to perceive phenomena truthfully. This perception is characterized by a clear observation of the three fundamental characteristics of worldly phenomena: their constant arising, their ceasing, and their overall state of flux. The liberated mind sees these processes without distortion.

**The Inclusivity of Non-Attachment**

The wisdom gained through this path is comprehensive. It is explicitly designed to prevent attachment not only to worldly phenomena but also to spiritual attainments. This ensures that the practitioner's liberation remains complete and is not compromised by clinging to the experiences or states achieved through their own practice.
