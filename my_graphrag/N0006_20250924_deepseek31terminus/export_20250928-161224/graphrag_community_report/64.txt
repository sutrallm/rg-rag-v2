**Report on the Attributes and State of a Liberated Being**

**Summary**
This report details the defining characteristics, mental state, and ultimate attainment of a liberated being, a fully enlightened sage. Such an individual has realized the truth of impermanence, eradicated ignorance, and dwells in the state of Nirvana, which is characterized by peace, fearlessness, and freedom from all bonds. Their mind is marked by equanimity and purity, remaining undisturbed by external circumstances and free from attachment to self. Their way of life reflects this inner liberation through simplicity, humility, and wise conduct.

**Attributes of a Liberated Being**
A liberated being is a fully enlightened sage who has attained complete awakening. This state is defined by having shattered ignorance and severed all afflictions. They have realized the true nature of impermanence, which is fundamental to their liberation. Having transcended the karmic consequences of both good and bad deeds, they have reached the deathless and unborn realm of Nirvana, the ultimate state of peace and security.

**The Mind of Equanimity and Purity**
A liberated being possesses a mind of equanimity, a balanced and calm state that does not cling to favorable or adverse conditions. This mental stability is undisturbed by external phenomena. Furthermore, their mind is a pure mind, free from greed and hatred. This purity is likened to still water that clearly reflects all things. This state is one where all defilements are exhausted, there is no more distress, and attachment to "I" and "mine" is abandoned, allowing transcendence of the cycle of birth and death.

**Independence from External Conditions**
A liberated being is not swayed by external circumstances. While external phenomena, which are objects contacted by the six sense faculties, can influence an ordinary person's mind, the liberated being remains stable and unimpeded, as free as wind in the sky, having been released from all fetters.

**The Way of Life**
A liberated being lives the life of a sage. The traits of this life include not accumulating possessions, not despising others, and having gentle speech and conduct coupled with clear wisdom. One who lives in such a manner resides in the world with a constantly pure mind.

**The Attainment of Nirvana**
Nirvana is the final state reached by a liberated being, constituting a mind that is fully liberated. It is the ultimate peace and the fearless state of freedom attained through liberation.
