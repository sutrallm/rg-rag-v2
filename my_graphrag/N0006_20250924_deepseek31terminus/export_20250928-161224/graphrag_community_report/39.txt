**Report on the Observation of Physical and Mental Phenomena through Correct Knowledge**

**Summary**
The text outlines a process for spiritual practice centered on the observation of physical and mental phenomena (名色). This observation is to be conducted using correct knowledge and view (正知见), with the specific purpose of abandoning deeply ingrained tendencies of greed and aversion (贪嗔惯性).

**Key Findings**

**The Object of Observation: Physical and Mental Phenomena**
The primary focus for a practitioner is the observation of physical and mental phenomena, referred to as 名色. These phenomena encompass all activities and manifestations of the body and mind.

**The Method of Observation: Correct Knowledge and View**
The prescribed method for observing these phenomena is the application of correct knowledge and view (正知见). This correct understanding is the essential tool for gaining a clear and accurate perception of 名色.

**The Purpose of Observation: Abandoning Habitual Tendencies**
The ultimate objective of this observational practice is to abandon deeply rooted habitual tendencies, specifically those of greed and aversion (贪嗔惯性). The application of 正知见 directly facilitates this process of letting go.
