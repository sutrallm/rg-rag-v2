**Report on the Attributes and Attainments of a Sage (聖者)**

**Summary**

This report details the defining characteristics and spiritual state of a Sage (聖者), an individual who has achieved the highest level of spiritual realization. A Sage is distinguished by the complete eradication of mental afflictions, which liberates them from all suffering. Their state is marked by profound tranquility, perfect wisdom, and impeccable moral discipline. This attainment is so significant that it commands reverence from celestial beings. The path to becoming a Sage involves diligent cultivation of mindfulness and concentration to overcome the root causes of distress.

**The Sage Has Achieved Complete Liberation from Suffering**

A Sage has transcended the inherent suffering of the cycle of birth and death (samsara). They have severed the root of all mental afflictions and defilements, having eradicated them completely. This liberation means they are free from all distress, oppression, and psychological bondage. A specific type of Sage, an Arhat, has reached the birthless state of Nirvana, where all afflictions are burned away. The noble one has therefore transcended all suffering and is unwavering, not shaken by praise or slander.

**Tranquility and Joy Are Hallmarks of the Sage's State**

The Sage diligently cultivates mindfulness and abides in a state of inner tranquility and stillness, known as 寂靜. This profound peace is the direct result of meditative concentration (禪定). Their mind is described as equal like the earth, free from dualities. Abiding in this tranquility, the Sage experiences a natural state of joy, referred to as the joy of the Dharma. Their actions and speech naturally accord with the truth, manifesting effortless wisdom.

**Perfect Wisdom and Moral Discipline Are Foundational Qualities**

A Sage is characterized by possessing perfect wisdom (慧) and correct understanding (正見). This wisdom is used to understand the nature of worldly phenomena and the truth of arising and passing away, allowing the Sage to cease clinging. They are also endowed with pure moral discipline (戒律), which they strictly observe. These two qualities are fundamental to their attainment, enabling them to平息 and ultimately sever mental afflictions.

**The Sage Is a Revered Figure for Celestial Beings and Practitioners**

The spiritual achievement of a Sage is so great that they are revered, admired, and praised by celestial beings (諸天/Devas). These heavenly beings respect and are unable to look down upon such an accomplished practitioner. The Sage is regarded as a field of merit and a source of blessings for the world, serving as the ultimate goal for any spiritual practitioner who seeks to overcome afflictions and achieve liberation.
