**Report on the Buddhist Path to Liberation through the Threefold Training**

**Summary**
This report outlines the essential components and progression of the Buddhist path to liberation, known as the Threefold Training. The path is a sequential and interdependent process beginning with moral discipline, which establishes the foundation for meditative concentration. This concentration, in turn, gives rise to wisdom, which is the ultimate means for severing ignorance and attaining Nirvana. The goal is a state of liberation from desire and attachment, symbolized by the lotus flower, which remains unstained by the world.

**The Sequential and Interdependent Nature of the Threefold Training**
The Threefold Training of moral discipline, concentration, and wisdom constitutes a complete and progressive path. Moral discipline serves as the foundational practice, creating the stability necessary for developing meditative concentration. This concentration is not an end in itself but is the essential condition from which wisdom arises. The relationship is sequential: discipline leads to concentration, and concentration leads to wisdom. This interdependence is symbolized by a tripod cauldron, where all three legs are necessary for stability; the absence of any one compromises the entire structure.

**Moral Discipline as the Foundational Practice**
Moral discipline, or precepts, is the initial and essential component of the path. It functions as a pure foundation and a strong embankment for all subsequent spiritual practice. Without this foundation of ethical conduct, the development of deeper states of meditative concentration is not possible. It is the first step in the sequential training and is a quality possessed by a sage.

**Meditative Concentration as the Catalyst for Wisdom**
Meditative concentration is the second component of the training, arising directly from a foundation of moral discipline. Its primary role is to serve as the basis for the emergence of wisdom. It is the state from which wisdom is born and developed. While it provides stability, its ultimate purpose is to lead to the next stage. This concentration is also a defining attribute of a sage.

**Wisdom as the Culminating Goal for Liberation**
Wisdom is the final and ultimate goal of the Threefold Training. It is a discerning insight into the true nature of reality that dispels ignorance. This wisdom does not arise in isolation but is dependent on the prior cultivation of concentration. It is the key that allows for the complete severance of the root of ignorance, leading directly to the attainment of Nirvana and liberation. The possession of this wisdom is the hallmark of a sage and the final mark of a圆满修行.

**Liberation as Freedom from Worldly Attachment**
The objective of the entire path is liberation, defined as freedom from desire, attachment, and the bondage of the world. This liberated state is one of being in the world but not stained by it, a condition symbolized by the lotus flower that grows from muddy water yet emerges clean and pure. The practitioner who achieves this state is compared to the lotus, having risen unstained by worldly defilements through the complete practice of the Threefold Training.
