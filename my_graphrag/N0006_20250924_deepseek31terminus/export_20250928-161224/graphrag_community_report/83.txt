**Report on the State of 身心轻安 Resulting from 禅定**

**Summary**

This report details the characteristics and prerequisites of the state known as 身心轻安 (lightness and ease of body and mind). The analysis confirms that this state is a direct outcome of the practice of 禅定 (meditation). It is defined by the absence of two specific hindrances: 昏沉 (dullness or lethargy) and 躁动 (agitation or restlessness). The state is described as innate, indicating it is a fundamental quality of mental stillness.

**Characteristics of 身心轻安**

身心轻安 is a condition characterized by a sense of lightness and ease that is experienced in both the physical body and the mind. This state is not merely the absence of negative qualities but represents a positive, balanced condition.

**Prerequisites for Achieving 身心轻安**

The primary method for attaining 身心轻安 is the consistent practice of 禅定. This practice is the causative factor that cultivates the necessary mental environment for this state to arise.

**Elimination of Hindrances**

A defining feature of 身心轻安 is the complete absence of 昏沉. This hindrance, characterized by mental dullness, lethargy, or heaviness, is overcome through the meditative process.

**Freedom from Disturbance**

The state of 身心轻安 is also marked by the absence of 躁动. This hindrance, which encompasses agitation, anxiety, and restlessness, is pacified through 禅定, allowing for undisturbed mental stillness.

**Innate Nature of Mental Stillness**

The state of mental stillness that underlies 身心轻安 is not an artificially created condition. The text describes it as an innate state, suggesting it is a natural and fundamental potential of the mind that is uncovered through practice.
