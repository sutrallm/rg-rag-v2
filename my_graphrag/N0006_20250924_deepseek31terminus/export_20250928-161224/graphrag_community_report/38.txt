**Report on the Practice and Outcomes of Wisdom Observation (智慧观照)**

**Summary**

The practice of Wisdom Observation is a foundational method for achieving liberation. It involves using discerning awareness to observe physical and mental phenomena, leading to the direct realization that all things are impermanent, empty of a permanent self, and dependently originated. This practice, supported by meditative concentration, enables a practitioner to abandon attachments to the concepts of "self" and "mine," ultimately resulting in a mind that is clear, undisturbed, and free from grasping.

**Core Principles of Wisdom Observation**

**The Method of Discerning Awareness**
Wisdom Observation is the practical application of wisdom within the threefold training of morality, concentration, and wisdom. It is defined as the practice of observing physical and mental phenomena with mindfulness to correctly perceive their impermanent and illusory nature. This involves applying discerning intelligence to see the emptiness and dependent origination of all phenomena.

**The Objective of Abandoning Erroneous Attachments**
The primary purpose of this observation is to gain a deep understanding that the five aggregates are not a permanent self. Through this direct insight, a practitioner gradually abandons mistaken attachments to the body and mind. This process specifically targets the relinquishment of the delusion of a permanent, independent self and the extended concept of "mine," which refers to possessions belonging to this self.

**The Role of Meditative Concentration**
Meditative concentration provides the necessary focus that serves as the foundation for conducting Wisdom Observation. The two practices are mutually supportive and work together to facilitate the development of insight.

**Outcomes of Successful Practice**

**Realization of Impermanence and Emptiness**
A practitioner engaged in Wisdom Observation gains the ability to clearly perceive the impermanent nature of the world. This practice leads to the insight that all phenomena are illusory, impermanent, and inherently empty of a permanent self-nature because they arise dependent on conditions.

**Achievement of a Liberated Mind**
A practitioner who attains liberation of the mind possesses the capability for Wisdom Observation. Such an individual, described as a realized or enlightened practitioner, has a mind symbolized by a clear mirror. This mind accurately reflects all phenomena without attachment, stain, or obstruction. This state is characterized by a mind that is unmoved and has transcended duality and grasping.

**The Path to Ultimate Truth**
The practice of Wisdom Observation is a key method for achieving liberation. It allows one to realize the ultimate truth or reality through non-attached wisdom, leading to a state of freedom characterized by non-attachment to any state or condition.
