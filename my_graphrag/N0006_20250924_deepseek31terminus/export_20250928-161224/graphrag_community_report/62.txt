**Report on Cultivation Practices and the Abandonment of Desire**

**Summary**
The text outlines a path to liberation through the systematic abandonment of desire and greed. This process, essential for achieving a tranquil mind and freedom from suffering, involves guarding the senses, applying wisdom, and maintaining mindfulness. The practitioner's progress is contrasted with the folly of those who indulge their desires, which leads to negative consequences and perpetuates cyclic existence.

**The Necessity of Abandoning Desire**
The practitioner must abandon and renounce desire and greed. This abandonment is a necessary condition for achieving a mind free from obstacles and for being far removed from suffering. The complete eradication of desire results in a state of liberation and non-attachment.

**The Path to Nirvana**
Nirvana is a state that must be realized through the thorough cutting off of greed. Distancing oneself from and severing greed is the pathway to achieving the stillness of Nirvana. Letting go of attachment to the five desires is part of this process of liberation.

**The Role of Mindfulness and Wisdom**
A cultivator must constantly guard the sense doors. Maintaining mindfulness prevents afflictions like greed from invading the mind and keeps it from being impacted by the waves of the five desires. One should use wisdom to observe and thereby abandon greed.

**Consequences of Indulgence**
The foolish person is characterized by indulgence and negligence. Such a person indulges desires and allows the sense doors to remain unguarded, leading to entanglement in afflictions. A person filled with greed will inevitably attract suffering as a consequence. This state of being mired in desires is metaphorically described as a dark well.

**The Nature of Greed**
Greed is a specific manifestation of affliction, primarily seen as clinging to the five desires of the world. It is a craving for sensual pleasures and, along with self-attachment, constitutes a fundamental obstacle that must be uprooted on the path to liberation.

**Contrast Between the Wise and the Foolish**
The wise and the foolish are sharply contrasted in their capacity to comprehend truth. The foolish indulge their desires, while the wise serve as a counter-example. The mind that chases after sensual pleasures is as unsteady as a lamp flame in the wind.

**Outcome of Severing Desire**
Abandoning greed leads to the cessation of deluded thoughts. Ultimately, abandoning desire results in cutting off the cycle of birth and death. Greed and attachment are negative states from which the mind must free itself.
