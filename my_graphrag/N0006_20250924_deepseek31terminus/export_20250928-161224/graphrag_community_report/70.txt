**Report on the Severance of the Root of Afflictions**

**Summary**

The text establishes that the root of afflictions is the fundamental source of all suffering. This root cause can be completely severed through the complete practice of the Threefold Training. An individual who accomplishes this, referred to interchangeably as a practitioner or a sage, achieves liberation by crossing the flood of birth and death.

**Severance of the Root of Afflictions as the Path to Liberation**

The root of afflictions is identified as the origin of all suffering. The text states that this root cause can be permanently eliminated. The method for achieving this severance is the complete practice of the Threefold Training.

**The Practitioner and the Sage as Synonymous Figures**

The terms "practitioner" and "sage" are used with identical attributes, indicating they refer to the same individual at different stages or from different perspectives. Both are defined by the key accomplishment of severing the root of afflictions.

**Liberation as Crossing the Flood of Birth and Death**

The ultimate outcome for the practitioner who severs the root of afflictions is described as crossing the flood of birth and death. This signifies a final liberation from the cycle of existence that is driven by afflictions.
