**Report on the Core Principles and Practices of the Path to Liberation**

**Summary**

This report outlines the fundamental structure and operational mechanics of the Path to Liberation (解脱道). The path is a systematic and sequential process beginning with moral discipline, progressing through meditative concentration, and culminating in wisdom. Its primary objective is to guide practitioners beyond the cycle of birth and death to the secure state of Nirvana. The essential method for achieving this goal is the consistent practice of the Noble Eightfold Path (八正道), which functions as the central framework for eradicating core obstructions, specifically craving, aversion, delusion, and the attachment to a self (我执). Success is achieved through direct experiential understanding and continuous mindfulness, not intellectual speculation.

**Foundational Framework of the Path**

The Path to Liberation is defined as a complete and sequential process. It is structured around the Threefold Training of moral discipline (戒), meditative concentration (定), and wisdom (慧). These stages are interdependent and must be cultivated in a progressive manner. The ultimate destination of this path is Nirvana, characterized as a state beyond suffering, safe and secure.

**The Central Role of the Noble Eightfold Path**

The Noble Eightfold Path is identified as the fundamental element and core practical method of the Path to Liberation. It is not a theoretical concept but a practical path to be embodied, described as a raft for crossing a river and the ultimate refuge for the wise. Its practice is directed toward the immediate goal of cutting through internal bondage and experiencing peace and bliss.

**Mechanism for Overcoming Obstacles**

The path's effectiveness lies in its method for removing fundamental obstacles to enlightenment. The primary targets for eradication are craving, aversion, delusion, and the attachment to a self. The specific tool for this removal is wisdom (般若智慧), which enables a practitioner to observe the body and mind, thereby allowing for the abandonment of the mistaken cognition of a permanent self.

**Operational Principle of Direct Realization**

The practice of the path emphasizes direct experiential understanding over intellectual knowledge. The cultivation of wisdom is a process of systematic practice that leads to a direct realization of the true nature of phenomena. The Noble Eightfold Path is the vehicle for this realization, aiding practitioners in directly perceiving the fundamental source of reality. This process is sustained by continuous mindfulness and observation.
