**Report on the Relationship Between Impermanence, Non-Attachment, and Liberation**

**Summary**
This report outlines the core principles of a specific philosophical system. The fundamental truth is the impermanent nature of all phenomena. Direct insight into this impermanence, cultivated through meditation, leads to a state of mental liberation known as '心解脱' (mind liberation). This liberated state is characterized by '无执著' (non-attachment), a wisdom that frees an individual from clinging to any state or attainment.

**The Centrality of Impermanence**
All phenomena and things in the world are characterized by change, flux, and a lack of permanent essence. This impermanent nature is identified as a fundamental truth and basic principle of reality. The very essence of the world is its transient and fleeting quality.

**Wisdom as the Means of Perception**
A deep, clear perception of impermanence is not ordinary understanding but is achieved through a faculty described as wisdom. This wisdom allows for a thorough, accurate, and genuine observation of the true nature of reality. It is the specific instrument for contemplating the principle of impermanence.

**Liberation Through Meditation**
The practitioner, through the discipline of meditation or mental cultivation, can attain a state called '心解脱' (mind liberation). This is the pivotal achievement that enables the practitioner to perceive impermanence with complete clarity and without distortion.

**Non-Attachment as Liberated Wisdom**
Upon attaining mental liberation, the practitioner manifests a quality of '无执著' (non-attachment) or '离执' (freedom from clinging). This is not merely a behavior but a profound wisdom state, characterized by the absence of clinging to any experience, state of being, or spiritual attainment. It is a complete freedom from craving for present pleasures or future rewards.

**The Causal Sequence of Realization**
A direct, causal relationship exists between these concepts. The practitioner's insight into the impermanent nature of the world, gained through meditation, forms the foundation for achieving non-attachment. Observing impermanence leads to a purified mind free from obstructions, which in turn results in the unshakable, non-attached wisdom of the liberated individual. The awakened one observes impermanence, and this observation is integral to non-attached wisdom.
