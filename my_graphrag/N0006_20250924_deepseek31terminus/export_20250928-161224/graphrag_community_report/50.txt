**Report on the Dharma, Its Practice, and the Path to Liberation**

**Summary**

This report outlines the fundamental principles of the Buddha's teachings (the Dharma) and their practical application. The Dharma provides the theoretical foundation and guidance for practitioners seeking liberation. Diligent practice of these teachings enables individuals to overcome spiritual hindrances (魔障) and achieve the ultimate goal of a pure, liberated state (清净解脱). The essential, nectar-like core of these teachings is identified as the 甘露法要.

**The Dharma as the Foundation for Practice**

The Dharma constitutes the teachings, doctrine, and truth preached by the Buddha. It serves as the guiding principle and theoretical basis for all practitioners. Its purpose is not merely academic study but requires implementation and personal realization through dedicated practice.

**The Goal of Pure Liberation**

The ultimate objective of following the Dharma is to achieve 清净解脱, which is described as a state of inner purity, complete freedom, and is equated with the realm of Nirvana.

**Hindrances on the Path and Their Overcoming**

魔障 are defined as obstacles, interferences, and evil influences encountered during spiritual practice. A specific condition leading to their束缚 is when the mind is distracted, likened to a fish thrown ashore. These hindrances can be overcome through a focused, single-pointed mind and, ultimately, freed from by diligent practice in accordance with the Dharma. Those who are diligent are explicitly stated to be freed from 魔障.

**The Role of Diligent Practice**

Practice is the essential mechanism for progress. Diligent practice, which involves and is applied to following the Dharma, enables one to overcome the hindrances on the path. It is through practicing the Dharma that one can脱离魔障束缚 and ultimately achieve清净解脱.

**The Essence of the Teachings**

The 甘露法要 is a specific term for the essential, nectar-like Dharma teachings pointed out by the Buddha. These teachings represent the true path that leads beings to a place of safety and freedom from fear.
