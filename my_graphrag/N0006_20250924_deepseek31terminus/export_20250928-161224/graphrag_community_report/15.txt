**Report on the Eradication of Greed and Aversion for a Pure Mind**

**Summary**
The text establishes Greed and Aversion (Tāntā) as the fundamental causes of suffering, binding beings to a cycle of affliction. It outlines a path for practitioners, where the consistent maintenance of mindfulness cultivates a power of awareness. This awareness is the key method for subduing these defilements, with their complete eradication resulting in a pure, impeccable mind and defining the state of a sage.

**Analysis of Core Concepts**

**Greed and Aversion as Foundational Defilements**
Greed and Aversion are identified as primary defilements and the root cause of all affliction. Their primary effect is to bind individuals to the cycle of suffering, making their management and removal central to spiritual progress.

**The Outcome of Eradicating Defilements**
The complete removal of Greed and Aversion results in a mind that is pure and without impurity. This state is not merely a reduction of negativity but a fundamental transformation. Furthermore, an individual who achieves this complete eradication is defined as a sage.

**The Path to Subduing Defilements through Mindfulness**
A practitioner can subdue Greed and Aversion through dedicated cultivation. The essential method for this cultivation is the consistent maintenance of mindfulness. This sustained practice is identified as the important method for developing the power of awareness.

**The Role of Awareness in Overcoming Defilements**
The power of awareness, cultivated through mindfulness, enables a practitioner to gradually subdue inner Greed and Aversion. The process is incremental, with increasing awareness directly leading to a greater capacity to overcome these fundamental defilements.
