**Report on the Path to Liberation from the Cycle of Birth and Death**

**Summary**
The text describes a spiritual path where a practitioner, through diligent cultivation, aims to achieve liberation from the cycle of birth and death. The central mechanism for achieving this liberation is the abandonment of craving and attachment to external phenomena. A practitioner who has completely severed desire, known as a 离欲者 (Liyuzhe), is freed from worldly bonds and is no longer subject to rebirth.

**The Goal of Spiritual Practice**
The ultimate objective for a practitioner is to attain Nirvana, which is defined as liberation from the cycle of birth and death. The core task of the practitioner is to engage in diligent cultivation to avoid being bound by this cycle.

**The Mechanism of the Cycle**
The cycle of birth and death is sustained by craving and attachment to external phenomena. This attachment is identified as the key factor that perpetuates the cycle. Therefore, severing this attachment is the method to cut off the cycle at its root.

**The Means of Abandonment**
The specific means to abandon craving is mindfulness. Furthermore, wisdom is described as the faculty that provides insight into the true nature of reality, enabling the severance of the fundamental cause of the cycle of birth and death.

**The State of a Liberated Being**
A 离欲者 (Liyuzhe) is a practitioner who has reached the state of having completely severed greed and desire. Such a being is characterized by a mind free from attachment, is detached from worldly bonds, and has transcended the cycle of birth and death, meaning they are no longer subject to rebirth.
