**Report on the Role of Diligence (Vīrya) in Spiritual Practice**

**Summary**

The text defines diligence (Vīrya) as an essential, sustained effort in spiritual practice based on the Buddha's teachings. It is presented as a foundational quality for achieving liberation. Diligence prevents negligence and laziness, enabling practitioners to overcome the threat of death, avoid control by negative forces, and attain a stable state of meditation. This quality is both a prerequisite for and a sustaining factor of meditative abiding and the holy life.

**Diligence as a Foundational Quality for Liberation**

Diligence is described as the bedrock of liberation and a necessary condition for any spiritual practitioner. It is not merely an optional attribute but a fundamental requirement for progress on the spiritual path.

**The Necessity of Diligence for Meditative Stability**

Achieving and maintaining a state of meditation is directly dependent on diligent practice. The text explicitly states that diligence is an indispensable prerequisite for entering into and abiding in meditative concentration.

**Diligence Prevents Negligence and Laziness**

The practice of diligence is characterized by sustained effort and vigilance, which are the direct opposites of negligence and laziness. Diligence is fundamentally based on the principle of non-negligence, and its application ensures that a practitioner does not become slack or fall into idleness.

**Diligence Overcomes the Threat of Death and Negative Forces**

Through diligent practice, an individual can transcend the danger associated with mortality. This effort allows one to avoid falling under the control of negative forces or the "king of death," leading to a state of safety and liberation.

**Diligence Leads to Liberation from Worldly Bonds**

The consistent application of diligence results in freedom from the fetters that bind a being to conditioned existence. This liberation from the bonds of life is a direct outcome of following the teachings with sustained effort.

**Diligence as the Practice of the Holy Life**

Engaging in diligent practice is an integral part of the holy life. It involves the continuous application of the Buddha's teachings through meditation and the cultivation of wisdom, representing the active path of a spiritual follower.
