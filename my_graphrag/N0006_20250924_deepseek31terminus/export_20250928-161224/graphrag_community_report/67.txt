**Report on the Path to Liberation through Wisdom and Practice**

**Summary**
This report details the path to spiritual liberation as a systematic process. The ultimate goal is解脱 (liberation), a state of complete freedom from suffering,生死流转 (the cycle of birth and death), and all forms of执著 (attachment). This is achieved through the cultivation of智慧 (wisdom), which is an insight into the无常 (impermanent),虚幻 (illusory), and空 (empty) nature of all phenomena.智慧 is developed through the foundational practice of the三学 (threefold training):戒律 (moral discipline),禅定 (meditative concentration), and智慧 itself. These elements are interdependent and must be cultivated in sequence by the修行者 (practitioner) to purify the mind, destroy无明 (ignorance)—the root cause of suffering—and ultimately attain涅槃 (Nirvana). The圣者 (sage) is an accomplished practitioner who has fully realized this path.

**The Threefold Training: A Sequential Path to Purification**
The三学 of戒律,禅定, and智慧 constitute a complete and systematic path for spiritual achievement.戒律, the foundation, involves moral discipline and provides the stability necessary for further practice.禅定, developed through meditation, brings mental focus and calm, preparing the mind for the emergence of智慧.智慧 is the culminating insight that arises from a mind stabilized by戒律 and禅定. This sequential practice—first戒律, then禅定, then智慧—progressively purifies the mind, severs the roots of烦恼 (afflictions), and leads to liberation.

**Wisdom as the Key to Liberation**
智慧 is the pivotal faculty that enables解脱. It is a profound, intuitive understanding that allows one to perceive the true nature of reality: that all phenomena, including the五蕴 (aggregates) and the世间 (world), are impermanent, illusory, and empty of a permanent self. This direct insight dismantles无明, the fundamental ignorance that binds beings to suffering. By澈见 (clearly seeing) this truth, the practitioner naturally abandons执著, cutting off the fuel for生死流转. Thus,智慧 is both the means to achieve liberation and a defining characteristic of the liberated state.

**The Interdependence of Meditation and Wisdom**
禅定 and智慧 have a mutually supportive and causal relationship.禅定 is the practice of mental concentration that creates the conditions for智慧 to arise. A mind settled through禅定 can perceive reality with clarity, leading to the insight that constitutes智慧. Conversely, the wisdom gained through this insight further purifies and stabilizes the meditative state. This synergy between a calm mind and clear insight is essential for progressing toward心解脱 (liberation of mind) and ultimately breaking the bonds of suffering.

**Liberation as Freedom from Attachment**
解脱 is the ultimate result of the path, characterized by complete freedom from all forms of attachment and bondage. It is a state of心无挂碍 (a mind without obstruction) and无所执着 (non-attachment). A liberated being, the圣者, no longer clings to any experience, achievement, or even the state of涅槃 itself. This freedom is achieved by using智慧 to overcome执著, which stems from a misunderstanding of reality.解脱 involves the severing of all烦恼 and the destruction of the net of afflictions, resulting in a state of ultimate安稳 (peace) and自在 (autonomy) beyond birth and death.

**The Role of the Practitioner and the Sage**
The修行者 is the individual who undertakes the practices of戒律,禅定, and智慧 with the goal of attaining解脱. The圣者 is the realized practitioner who has successfully completed this path. A圣者 is fundamentally characterized by the possession of perfect智慧, which enables the eradication of defilements and transcendence of生死流转. The圣者 serves as a paradigm of accomplishment, having destroyed无明 and attained the state of涅槃, becoming an object of reverence within the世间.

**Overcoming Ignorance and Sensory Desires**
无明 is identified as the root cause of suffering and cyclic existence. It is a state of spiritual darkness regarding the true nature of reality.智慧 is the specific antidote to无明; its light dispels this darkness. A primary manifestation of无明 is attachment to sensory desires (forms, sounds, etc.), which are seen as虚幻幻影 (illusory phantoms).通过智慧观照 (observing through wisdom), one can see the impermanent nature of these desires and naturally let go of贪爱 (craving), thereby weakening the power of无明.
