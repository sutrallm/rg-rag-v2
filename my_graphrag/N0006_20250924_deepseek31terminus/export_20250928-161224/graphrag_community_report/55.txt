**Report on the Attainment of Inner Stability through Meditative Concentration**

**Summary**

This report details the principles and outcomes of a specific meditative practice. The practice is a core component of a three-part training system and is built upon a foundation of moral discipline. Its primary objective is to cultivate a deeply focused and mindful state, leading to inner tranquility. This state enables an individual to remain undisturbed by internal and external distractions, including eight specific worldly conditions. By sustaining this practice with diligent effort, a practitioner overcomes a state of carelessness and is liberated from afflictions and sorrow. The sustained practice of this concentration subsequently gives rise to wisdom, with both elements working together to achieve ultimate mental freedom and peace.

**The Foundation in Moral Discipline**

Moral purity allows the mind to naturally become stable, creating the necessary conditions for the practice of meditative concentration. A purified state of conduct is a prerequisite for effective engagement in this deeper training.

**The State of Undisturbed Focus**

The goal of this practice is to achieve a state of profound stillness where the mind is unified and focused, accompanied by mindful awareness. This concentrated state is the direct cause for attaining inner silence and stability, allowing the mind to abide without being shaken.

**Overcoming Distractions and Worldly Conditions**

A key function of this meditative state is to render the practitioner immune to various disturbances. These include distractions originating from both internal states and external environments, as well as the eight worldly winds of gain, loss, defamation, praise, fame, obscurity, pain, and pleasure. A mind established in correct mindfulness remains unmoved by these conditions, like an immovable rock.

**Liberation from Affliction and Sorrow**

Through the consistent application of this focused mind, a practitioner can overcome a state of spiritual carelessness and be liberated from afflictions and suffering. Abiding in this concentration leads directly to freedom from sorrow and the attainment of inner peace.

**The Synergy with Wisdom**

The relationship between meditative concentration and wisdom is mutually supportive. The practice of concentration serves as the method for perceiving the illusory nature of phenomena, which in turn gives rise to wisdom. This developed wisdom then guides and deepens the concentration. Together, they function to pacify烦恼 and lead to liberation.

**The Role of Diligent Effort**

Sustained and diligent effort, without negligence, is an essential requirement for being able to abide in this meditative concentration. A practitioner must continuously strive and apply themselves to enter and maintain this deep state of focus.
