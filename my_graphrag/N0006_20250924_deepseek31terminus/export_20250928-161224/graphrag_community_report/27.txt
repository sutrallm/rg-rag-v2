**Report on the Concepts of Ultimate Liberation and Nirvana**

**Summary**

The text defines Ultimate Liberation (究竟解脱) as the final state attained by a sage. This state is equated with Nirvana (涅槃), with Nirvana being the condition of having achieved Ultimate Liberation. A sage who has reached this state has completely eradicated all afflictions and severed all fetters.

**The Equivalence of Ultimate Liberation and Nirvana**

Ultimate Liberation is described as the ultimate state achieved by a sage. The text explicitly states that Nirvana and Ultimate Liberation both refer to the ultimate realm beyond afflictions. It further clarifies that Nirvana is the state of having attained Ultimate Liberation, and that achieving Ultimate Liberation is synonymous with realizing Nirvana. This establishes that the two terms are different names for the same ultimate goal.

**The State of an Liberated Sage**

A sage who has attained Ultimate Liberation is characterized by the complete exhaustion of afflictions. The text specifies that such an individual has "already漏尽烦恼," meaning all defilements and distresses have been fully eradicated.

**The Meaning of Liberation as Severance of Bonds**

Ultimate Liberation is defined by the action of cutting off all bonds. The text explains that this liberation signifies the state of having "already断除一切系缚," or severed every tie that binds an individual to suffering and the cycle of existence. This severance is the direct cause of the sage's liberated state.
