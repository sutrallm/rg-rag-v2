**Report on the Path to a Suffering-Free State through Wise Observation**

**Summary**

The text describes a causal process initiated by a wise person. Through their observation, an individual's mind is liberated from greed and fear. This liberation is the direct cause of achieving a state characterized by peace, stability, and the absence of suffering.

**The Mechanism of Attainment**

The primary cause for reaching a state of suffering-free peace and stability is the liberation from greed and fear. The text explicitly states that this liberation "leads to" this desired state, establishing a direct causal relationship.

**The Role of the Wise Person**

The process of liberation is initiated by the observation conducted by a wise person (智者). This specific type of observation is described as "wise," and its effect is the liberation of the mind from the negative states of greed and fear.

**The Prerequisite for Peace**

Freedom from greed and fear is presented as the necessary condition for achieving peace. The text defines the resulting state not just as peace, but specifically as one free from suffering, implying that greed and fear are the fundamental causes of suffering.
