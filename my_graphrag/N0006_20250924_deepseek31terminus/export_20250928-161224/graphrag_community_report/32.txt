**Report on the Path to Liberation from the Cycle of Birth and Death**

**Summary**

This report outlines the fundamental principles for transcending the cycle of birth and death (samsara). The process is defined by the application of wisdom to observe phenomena, particularly the impermanent nature of the five aggregates and the law of dependent origination. The ultimate objective is to achieve Nirvana, a state of complete liberation characterized by the extinguishing of all defilements and freedom from all bondage. Key practices include diligent mindfulness, vigilance, and the cessation of attachment.

**The Mechanism of Samsara**

Samsara is a state of bondage where sentient beings cycle continuously through birth and death due to ignorance, defilements, and karmic force. It is a dangerous path that requires active effort to avoid and sever. Subtle mental activities perpetuate this cyclic existence.

**The Goal of Ultimate Liberation**

The ultimate goal for a practitioner is to attain ultimate liberation (究竟解脱). This state represents the final, complete freedom, the extinction of all defilements, and the achievement of Nirvana. It is characterized by a mind that is cool, pure, and undisturbed by worldly concepts, having transcended all dualities.

**Wisdom as the Primary Tool for Transcendence**

Wisdom is the essential means for overcoming samsara. Its primary application is through the practice of observing with wisdom (观慧), which involves a clear seeing of the true nature of the five aggregates as empty and unsatisfactory. This wisdom is specifically manifested as the correct knowledge and vision of the law of dependent origination (缘起法).

**The Function of Observing with Wisdom**

The practice of observing with wisdom enables practitioners to avoid falling into samsara, break free from its bondage, and clearly see its nature. Understanding the law of dependent origination allows one to be freed from the cycle of birth and death. This same practice also allows one to overcome the bondage of the tempter, Mara.

**Key Practices for Severing the Cycle**

Specific practices are identified as crucial for cutting off the stream of samsara. These include diligent practice, the maintenance of constant mindfulness, the practice of vigilance (不放逸), and the complete cessation of all defilements, which is a prerequisite for reaching the other shore.

**The Significance of the 'Other Shore'**

Reaching the "other shore" (彼岸) signifies that one is no longer bound by samsara and will not fall back into it. This arrival at the other shore is synonymous with having attained ultimate liberation. An awakened being has reached this state and is no longer subject to the cycle.

**The Role of Seeing the Five Aggregates as Empty**

A specific method for transcending samsara is to thoroughly see the five aggregates as empty. This insight, facilitated by observing with wisdom, is a direct path to liberation.

**The Relationship Between Mental and Ultimate Liberation**

The liberation of the mind (心解脱) is a state that allows one to transcend the cycle of birth and death. Ultimate liberation represents the complete fulfillment and final accomplishment of this mental liberation.

**The Practitioner's Understanding**

A true practitioner understands the beginning and the end of the cycle of birth and death. Awareness and discernment are key to severing the chain of samsara.
