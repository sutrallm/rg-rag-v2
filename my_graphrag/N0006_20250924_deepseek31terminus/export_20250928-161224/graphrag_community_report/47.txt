**Report on the Cultivation of Diligence and Heedfulness through Buddha's Teachings**

**Summary**

The analysis indicates that a practitioner develops the power of diligence and heedfulness by adhering to the Buddha's teachings. This cultivated power is presented as the direct means to avoid being ensnared by the lord of death.

**Key Findings**

**The Role of Buddha's Teachings in Cultivating Diligence**
The text establishes a direct causal link: the power of diligence is developed specifically through the act of following the Buddha's teachings. This teaching is the foundational method for acquiring this power.

**The Protective Function of Cultivated Power**
The combined power of diligence and heedfulness, once developed, serves a critical protective function. It enables the practitioner to avoid a specific negative outcome: being trapped by the lord of death.

**The Interdependence of Diligence and Heedfulness**
The text presents diligence and heedfulness as a unified pair of qualities cultivated together by the practitioner. They are not described as separate or sequential pursuits but as concurrent powers developed through the same practice.
