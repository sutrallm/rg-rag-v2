**Report on the Path to Liberation from the Cycle of Birth and Death**

**Summary**

This report details the process and ultimate goal of spiritual practice as a progression from a state of bondage to one of complete freedom. The fundamental problem is the cycle of birth and death (生死轮回), a state of suffering perpetuated by ignorance, afflictions, and karmic force. The solution is diligent practice, which involves applying wisdom to observe the nature of phenomena, particularly the impermanence of the five aggregates and the law of dependent origination. A practitioner who succeeds becomes an awakened being (觉悟者), characterized by the extinction of all defilements and freedom from karmic results. The final achievement is ultimate liberation (究竟解脱), synonymous with reaching the other shore (彼岸) and attaining Nirvana, a state of perfect peace and safety beyond all bondage.

**The Nature of the Cycle of Birth and Death**

The cycle of birth and death is a continuous process of rebirth fueled by ignorance, afflictions, and karmic actions. It is a state of bondage and danger from which beings seek freedom. This cycle is characterized by suffering and is the fundamental condition from which spiritual practice aims to liberate an individual.

**The Means of Transcendence**

Transcending this cycle requires diligent practice based on the Buddha's teachings. Key methods include extinguishing afflictions, thoroughly perceiving the emptiness of the five aggregates, severing attachments, and maintaining vigilant mindfulness. The core practice is the application of wisdom to observe phenomena, understanding their impermanent and unsatisfactory nature.

**The Role of Wisdom and Understanding**

Wisdom is the critical faculty for liberation. Its primary function is the direct knowledge and vision of the law of dependent origination, the principle governing the arising and passing away of all phenomena. This understanding allows a practitioner to avoid falling into the cycle. This practice of wise observation also enables one to break free from the bondage of negative influences.

**The State of the Awakened Being**

An awakened being is a sage who has attained ultimate wisdom and complete liberation. Such an individual is no longer bound by the cycle of birth and death, the results of good and evil actions, or any worldly concepts. Their mind is purified, free from defilements and ignorance. They dwell in a state beyond conceptual disputes, fully understanding the nature of arising and cessation, and have transcended all dualistic concepts, including good and evil.

**The Destination: The Other Shore and Ultimate Liberation**

The other shore represents the ultimate state of liberation, the antithesis of the suffering-filled "near shore" of cyclic existence. It is a state of Nirvana, characterized by the complete extinction of suffering, the absence of desire and doubt, and the severing of all attachments. Reaching this shore signifies that one no longer falls into the cycle of birth and death. This achievement is the ultimate fulfillment of liberation, where a being is completely free, undisturbed by worldly concerns, and abides in secure peace.
