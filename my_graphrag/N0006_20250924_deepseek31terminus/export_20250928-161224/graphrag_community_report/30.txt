**Title: The Attributes and Liberation of the Awakened Sage**

**Summary**

The text describes the state of an enlightened being, or 觉悟者, who has achieved full awakening and liberation. This individual has attained ultimate wisdom, which allows them to understand the impermanent nature of the world and the law of cause and effect. Consequently, they are no longer bound by the cycle of birth and death, the consequences of good and evil actions, or any afflictions. They dwell in a state of nirvana, free from delusion and attachment.

**The State of Ultimate Wisdom and Liberation**

A 觉悟者 is a sage who has attained ultimate wisdom. This profound understanding allows them to clearly perceive causality and the true nature of reality. Through this insight, they have eradicated all afflictions and destroyed ignorance, achieving liberation and freedom within this lifetime.

**Freedom from Karmic Consequences**

The law of karma dictates that actions have corresponding results. However, a 觉悟者 is not bound by the fruits of good or evil deeds. Having achieved liberation, the individual is no longer stained or constrained by these karmic consequences.

**Understanding of Impermanence and the Nature of Existence**

The awakened one possesses a clear insight into the impermanent nature of the world and the aggregates that constitute existence. They comprehend the nature of arising and cessation, which frees them from attachment to any state or phenomenon.

**Transcendence of Dualistic Concepts**

A 觉悟者 has moved beyond all relative and dualistic concepts, such as good and evil. They abide in a state free from conceptual elaboration, where discursive thought has ceased. This represents a transcendence of ordinary, conditioned perception.
