**Report on the Practice of 正念寤寐 and Its Effects on 煩惱雜染**

**Summary**
The text describes a method for achieving 禅定 (meditative concentration) through the practice of 正念寤寐, which is defined as maintaining constant mindfulness while both awake and asleep. The primary effect of this diligent practice is the gradual weakening of 煩惱雜染 (mental defilements and impurities).

**Key Findings**

**The Path to Meditative Concentration Through Constant Mindfulness**
The method for attaining 禅定 is explicitly stated as the maintenance of 正念寤寐. This practice requires sustaining awareness not only during waking hours but also during sleep, indicating a state of continuous, unbroken mindfulness.

**The Gradual Weakening of Mental Impurities**
A direct causal relationship is established between the practice of 正念寤寐 and the condition of 煩惱雜染. The text specifies that engaging in this practice results in these mental defilements and impurities being gradually weakened over time. This outcome is contingent upon diligent and sustained effort.

**The Integral Nature of Sleep in the Practice**
The practice of 正念寤寐 is distinguished by its inclusion of the sleep state. The term itself combines mindfulness (正念) with being both awake and asleep (寤寐), indicating that the cultivation of awareness is not suspended during sleep but is considered an essential, continuous component of the path.
