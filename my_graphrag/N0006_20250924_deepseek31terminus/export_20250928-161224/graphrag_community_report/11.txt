**Report on the Attributes and Attainments of a Sage (聖者)**

**Summary**

This report details the defining characteristics and spiritual accomplishments of a Sage (聖者). A Sage is an individual who has achieved the highest level of spiritual realization, characterized by profound wisdom and pure moral discipline. Through diligent meditation and mindfulness, they have attained a state of inner tranquility, severed all afflictions, and transcended the suffering inherent in the cycle of birth and death. Their liberation is so complete that they are held in reverence by celestial beings, and they abide in a state of unwavering peace and effortless wisdom.

**Attainment of Liberation and Transcendence of Suffering**

A Sage has achieved full liberation, having severed the root of all mental defilements and afflictions. This complete eradication allows them to transcend all suffering, including the inherent distress of the world and the specific suffering of the cycle of birth and death (samsara). They are described as having transcended all forms of oppression and distress.

**Cultivation of Inner Tranquility through Meditation**

The practice of meditation (禪定) is central to the Sage's path. They diligently cultivate mindfulness and concentration, which leads directly to the achievement of an inner state of tranquility and stillness (寂靜). This tranquility is both the goal of their practice and the resulting state in which they perpetually abide.

**Possession of Pure Moral Discipline and Perfect Wisdom**

A Sage is defined by two fundamental qualities: pure moral discipline (precepts/戒律) and perfect wisdom (right view/正見). They strictly uphold ethical precepts and possess a correct understanding of reality. This wisdom involves a full comprehension of the nature of worldly phenomena and the truth of arising and passing away, without clinging to any state, including liberation itself.

**Recognition and Reverence by Celestial Beings**

The spiritual accomplishments of a Sage are so significant that they are recognized, admired, and praised by celestial beings (諸天/Devas). These heavenly beings respect and hold the Sage in high esteem, unable to look down upon them, highlighting the Sage's exalted status within the spiritual hierarchy.

**Abiding in Unwavering Equanimity and Dharma Joy**

A Sage remains unwavering and unshaken by external conditions such as praise or slander. Their mind is described as "equal like the earth, free from dualities." They abide in the joy of the Dharma (法喜), a state of happiness that arises from their tranquil mind and alignment with truth. Their actions and speech naturally accord with this truth, manifesting effortless wisdom.
