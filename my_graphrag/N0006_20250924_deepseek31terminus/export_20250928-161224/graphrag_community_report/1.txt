**Report on the Path to Mental Liberation through the Insight of Impermanence**

**Summary**
This report details the process and outcome of achieving mental liberation (心解脫) through meditative practice. The core finding is that liberation is attained by a practitioner who, using wisdom, gains a profound insight into the impermanent nature of all phenomena (無常). This insight directly leads to detachment (離欲) and the abandonment of all forms of clinging, resulting in a state of freedom where no mental state or attainment is grasped.

**The Mechanism of Liberation**
Mental liberation is a specific state of freedom achieved through meditative practice, particularly meditative concentration. It is characterized by the mind's release from afflictions and bondage, resulting in a fearless and unshakable state. The defining feature of this liberation is the absence of grasping and attachment to any experience or achievement.

**The Central Role of Insight into Impermanence**
A practitioner who attains mental liberation does so by thoroughly seeing and understanding the principle of impermanence. This involves a clear and direct observation that all worldly phenomena are subject to constant change, arising, and passing away. This fundamental characteristic of existence is the true nature of all conditioned things.

**Wisdom as the Catalyst for Detachment**
Wisdom is the faculty that enables the contemplation and understanding of impermanence. By observing and subduing the mind with this wisdom, a practitioner cultivates detachment. This detachment is the practice of distancing oneself from clinging to objects, states, or results, which is the direct cause leading to mental liberation.

**The Resultant State of Non-Attachment**
The culmination of this process is a state where no clinging remains. The liberated individual does not grasp any mental state, realm, or meditative attainment. They are completely free from执着, meaning they do not hold on to any perception, concept, or sensory experience. This signifies a final severing of greed and desire.
