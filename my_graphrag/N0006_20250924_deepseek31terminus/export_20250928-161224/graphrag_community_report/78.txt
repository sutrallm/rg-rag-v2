**Report on the Nature of the Awakened One and the Ultimately Liberated**

**Summary**

The text defines the state of an "Awakened One" as the ultimate achievement for a practitioner, characterized by complete liberation, the realization of Nirvana, and a permanent end to the cycle of birth and death. This state is described as a higher level than that of a "Sage," marked by a mind free from all bonds and a direct perception of the illusory nature of phenomenal existence.

**The Awakened One as the Culmination of Practice**

An Awakened One is the final state attained by a practitioner who has completed their spiritual path. This represents the highest possible achievement, evolving from the foundational state of a Sage into one of perfect accomplishment.

**Complete Mental Liberation from Bondage**

The defining characteristic of an Awakened One is a mind that has been utterly and completely freed from all fetters and attachments. This state of mental purity and freedom from bondage is the core of their liberation.

**Direct Realization of Phenomenal Illusion**

An Awakened One has achieved a profound and direct insight into the true nature of existence, specifically perceiving the five aggregates as being fundamentally unreal or illusory.

**Attainment of Nirvana and Cessation of Rebirth**

The Awakened One is one who has verified and attained Nirvana. A direct consequence of this attainment is the severing of the very root of the cycle of birth and death, ensuring they will never again enter this cycle. The "Ultimately Liberated One" is a specific term for such a being, emphasizing their permanent state of freedom from rebirth.
