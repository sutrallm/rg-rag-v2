**Title: The Attainments and Status of the Arhat**

**Summary:**
This report details the defining characteristics and spiritual achievements of an Arhat. The Arhat is established as the highest class of saint, having reached the ultimate goal of Nirvana. This state represents complete liberation from all afflictions and is characterized by a profound understanding of the true nature of life.

**Insights:**

**The Arhat as the Pinnacle of Spiritual Practice**
An Arhat is a practitioner who has reached the highest possible level of spiritual accomplishment. This status is not merely advanced but represents the ultimate achievement attainable through cultivation.

**Definitive Liberation through Nirvana**
The fundamental state of an Arhat is Nirvana, specifically the state of final liberation without residue. This condition is defined as complete and ultimate freedom from all mental afflictions and suffering.

**Profound Understanding of Existential Reality**
An Arhat possesses a direct and thorough perception of the source of the five aggregates and has comprehended the true nature of life. This deep insight into the fundamental components of existence is a core attribute of their attainment.

**The Arhat's Classification as a Saint**
The Arhat is explicitly categorized as a type of saint. This designation confirms their revered status within the spiritual hierarchy as one who has achieved supreme sanctity.
