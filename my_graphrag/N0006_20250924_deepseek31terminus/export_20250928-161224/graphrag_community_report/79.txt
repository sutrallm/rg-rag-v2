**Report on the Practice and Outcomes of Wisdom Observation (智慧观照)**

**Summary**

The text describes 智慧观照 (wisdom observation) as a core practice for achieving liberation. This method involves using correct knowledge (正知见) to observe physical and mental phenomena (身心现象) to perceive their impermanent, illusory, and empty nature. Through this practice, a practitioner (修行者) abandons the delusions of a permanent self and possessions (我所), along with habitual greed and aversion. The ultimate outcomes include transcending duality and grasping, realizing dependent origination and emptiness, and attaining a mind symbolized by a clear mirror (明镜), which reflects reality without attachment.

**Key Findings**

**The Method of Wisdom Observation**
智慧观照 is the practical application of wisdom within the threefold training of morality, concentration, and wisdom. It is a practice where a practitioner uses correct knowledge and view (正知见) to observe the activities and manifestations of the body and mind (身心现象). The concentration developed through meditation serves as the foundational support for this practice.

**The Object of Observation: The Five Aggregates**
The primary focus of this practice is the observation of the five aggregates (五蕴). The practitioner observes these physical and mental phenomena to gain insight into their虚幻无常 (illusory and impermanent) nature and to understand that they are not a permanent self.

**Abandoning Delusion Through Direct Insight**
The practice is designed to eliminate fundamental delusions. By observing phenomena correctly, the practitioner directly sees that the belief in a permanent, independent self is a delusion. This insight also extends to abandoning the concept of "mine" (我所), which is an attachment to possessions rooted in the self-view. Furthermore, the practice allows for the abandonment of habitual tendencies of greed and aversion.

**Realizing Dependent Origination and Emptiness**
Through the consistent application of 智慧观照, the practitioner gains a correct insight into the fundamental doctrine of dependent origination. This is the deep understanding that all phenomena arise based on conditions and are inherently empty of a permanent, independent nature.

**The Outcome: A Liberated Mind**
The successful practice leads to liberation (心解脱). A practitioner who has achieved this liberation possesses the ability of 智慧观照. Their mind is no longer moved by duality and grasping, having transcended such attachments. This liberated state is symbolized by the "明镜" (bright mirror), representing a mind that is clear, unobstructed, and reflects all phenomena accurately without any stain of attachment. This mind has realized the ultimate truth.
