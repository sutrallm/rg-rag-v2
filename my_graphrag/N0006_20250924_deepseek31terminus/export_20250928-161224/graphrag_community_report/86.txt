**Report on the Attainments and Characteristics of a Liberated Being**

**Summary**

This report details the definitive state of a fully enlightened being, known as a Liberated Being or Sage. Such an individual has permanently eradicated the fundamental afflictions of greed, hatred, and delusion, and is no longer subject to the cycle of birth and death. They abide immovably in Nirvana, a state of ultimate peace and silence, completely unaffected by worldly concerns or dualities. This final achievement is the result of successfully completing a specific path of practice.

**The Eradication of Fundamental Afflictions**

A Liberated Being has completely extinguished the fires of the three poisons: greed, hatred, and delusion. These are identified as the primary causes of suffering. Their eradication is a fundamental characteristic of this ultimate state, representing the cessation of all defilements.

**Immunity to Worldly Conditions**

A Liberated Being or one who has Attained the Fruit cannot be disturbed by the Eight Worldly Winds: gain and loss, fame and disgrace, praise and blame, and pleasure and pain. This immovability signifies a mind that is no longer influenced by external conditions or personal fortune, demonstrating a state of profound stability.

**Abidance in Ultimate Peace**

The definitive dwelling place of a Liberated Being is Nirvana, described as a state of ultimate silence and peace that transcends all suffering and dualistic concepts. This condition is the final result of spiritual practice, where the mind is completely purified, akin to the undisturbed nature of space.

**Freedom from the Cycle of Existence**

A Liberated Being exists outside the realm of birth and death, having broken free from the continuous cycle of existence. This liberation means they are no longer subject to the sufferings associated with birth, aging, sickness, and death.

**The State of Non-Attachment and Desirelessness**

A Liberated Being does not cling to the past, crave the future, or grasp at present experiences. This includes non-attachment even to the state of enlightenment itself. This freedom from desire and attachment is described as a state of ease and mastery, achieved by severing all craving.

**The Path to Attainment**

Becoming a Liberated Being is the ultimate goal for a practitioner. This achievement is realized through diligent mental cultivation and the specific practice of meditative concentration, which leads to the liberation of the mind. Successfully completing this path of practice allows an individual to cut off all latent tendencies of affliction and attain a state from which there is no regression.

**The Role of Disciplines and Realization**

The Sage is a Liberated Being who possesses both discipline and wisdom. Maintaining complete moral discipline aids a practitioner in abiding in Nirvana. Furthermore, a Liberated Being can clearly perceive the fundamental truth of the arising and passing away of all worldly phenomena. Correct view is also identified as supportive for abiding in ultimate peace.

**Characteristics of the Fruition State**

One who has Attained the Fruit is characterized by being in a state of "no outflows," meaning they are impervious to afflictions. Attaining mental liberation is a key characteristic or a precursor to reaching this fruition state. The ultimate achievements of a Liberated Being and a Sage are synonymous.
