**Title: The Path to Liberation Through Inner Letting Go and Mindful Practice**

**Summary:**  
Liberation from the cycle of birth, death, and rebirth is attainable only through sustained internal transformation. This transformation arises from disciplined practice of mindfulness and chanding, which quiet the mind and dissolve attachment and aversion. No external means, rituals, or shortcuts can produce this result. True realization comes from direct wisdom that perceives existence as fundamentally free from arising and ceasing, accessible only when craving and conceptual grasping are fully relinquished.

**Chanding Cultivates the Conditions for Liberation**  
When practiced with consistent effort, chanding calms the mind and creates the inner stability required for direct perception. This calming is not an end in itself but a necessary foundation for revealing the true nature of reality, which remains obscured by mental agitation and distraction.

**Liberation Requires No External Solution**  
There is no remedy outside the self—no ritual, invocation, or external agent that can deliver liberation. All attempts to bypass internal work fail because the root of bondage lies in craving, resistance, and conceptual clinging. Release must emerge from within, through persistent unraveling of these patterns.

**Direct Wisdom Reveals Non-Arising and Non-Ceasing Reality**  
The ultimate truth is not learned through doctrine but perceived directly. When attachment and aversion dissolve, the mind no longer imposes the illusion of birth and death onto experience. This perception arises naturally, unmediated by thought, and confirms the nature of existence as beyond temporal change.

**The Only Path Is Non-Attachment and Letting Go**  
All teachings converge on a single method: releasing hold on desire, resistance, and the mind’s tendency to grasp at concepts. This inner surrender is not passive resignation but an active, disciplined unwinding of conditioning. It is the sole means to realize the state of no-birth-no-death, where samsara ceases to bind.
