**Title: The Threefold Training and the Path to Liberation**

**Summary**  
The path to ultimate liberation is structured as an integrated sequence: moral discipline, followed by mental concentration, culminating in wise insight. Each stage is indispensable and builds directly upon the prior one. Liberation arises only when wise insight fully manifests, as it is the direct force that eliminates ignorance — the root of suffering. True spiritual attainment is defined by the clear perception of reality, free from delusion.

**Moral Discipline as the Foundational Beginning**  
The journey toward liberation begins with moral discipline, established as the first and essential pillar of the training. Without this ethical grounding, the subsequent stages cannot unfold properly. It provides the necessary stability and sangha-aligned conduct that creates the conditions for mental development.

**Concentration as the Necessary Preparation**  
Mental focus, developed through chanding, is the second stage and serves as the indispensable foundation for wise insight. This mental stability is not an end in itself but a prerequisite that allows the mind to settle sufficiently to perceive the true nature of phenomena — impermanence and selflessness — without distraction.

**Wise Insight as the Liberating Culmination**  
Wise insight is the final and decisive stage, emerging only after moral discipline and concentration have been fully cultivated. It is the direct cause of liberation, eradicating ignorance — the source of all suffering. The full manifestation of this insight defines true sainthood, marked by unobstructed clarity and freedom from delusion.

**Interdependence of the Three Stages**  
Each component — moral discipline, concentration, and wise insight — is inseparable and sequentially ordered. No single element operates in isolation; the path is complete only when all three are practiced in their proper order. The absence of any one renders the entire structure ineffective in achieving liberation.
