**Title: The Path to Liberation Through Self-Observation and Direct Experience**

**Summary:**  
Liberation is achieved not through belief, doctrine, or intellectual knowledge, but through continuous, non-judgmental awareness of bodily and mental processes. This practice reveals the inherent characteristics of all phenomena—impermanence, suffering, and non-self—directly and personally, forming the essential foundation for full realization.

**Self-Observation Unveils Impermanence**  
By maintaining continuous, non-judgmental awareness of body and mind, one directly perceives how all phenomena are in constant flux. This observable change is not theoretical but experiential, forming the most immediate evidence of impermanence in lived reality.

**Direct Experience Alone Enables Liberation**  
Belief systems and doctrinal acceptance do not lead to full realization. Only when truths about existence—impermanence, suffering, and non-self—are encountered through personal, lived awareness can the transformative state of liberation occur.

**Self-Observation Reveals Suffering and Non-Self**  
The practice of observing internal processes without attachment or aversion exposes the presence of suffering as an inescapable feature of clinging to transient states. It also dismantles the notion of a fixed, independent self by showing how thoughts, sensations, and emotions arise and pass without a central controller.
