**Title: The Path to Ultimate Stability Through Self-Nature Clarity**

**Summary:**  
Ultimate stability arises solely through spiritual practice, with meditation, diligence, and wisdom as the means to this end. True freedom of mind and the clarity of self-nature are not separate conditions but different expressions of the same awakened state. This state emerges naturally when the mind ceases to cling to or reject experiences.

**Self-Nature Clarity and Mind Liberation Are Equivalent States**  
The liberation of the mind and the revelation of self-nature clarity are not two distinct outcomes but two ways of describing the same awakening. When one is realized, the other is inherently present—neither precedes nor follows the other; they coexist as inseparable aspects of full realization.

**Ultimate Stability Emerges When the Mind Ceases to Grasp or Resist**  
Stability at the deepest level is not achieved through effort alone, but through the natural unfolding of self-nature when attachment and aversion cease. As the mind no longer seeks to hold onto or push away experiences, its inherent clarity and calm manifest without condition.

**Spiritual Practice Serves as the Sole Gateway to This State**  
All forms of practice—including meditation, disciplined effort, and discerning wisdom—are directed toward one purpose: the realization of ultimate stability. None exist as ends in themselves; each is a means to dissolve the conditions that obscure the mind’s innate clarity.
