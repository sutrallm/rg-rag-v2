**Title: The Path to Liberation Through Self-Enlightenment**

**Summary:**  
Liberation is not attainable through external rituals, teachers, or formal practices. It arises solely from personal, continuous, and diligent inner effort. The primary barriers to progress—greed, hatred, and delusion—must be actively confronted through unwavering self-exertion and truthful self-observation. Spiritual advancement depends entirely on individual resolve, not external intervention.

**Continuous Self-Exertion Is Essential**  
No external force, ceremony, or authority can instigate true spiritual awakening. Sustained personal effort is mandatory to break free from tendencies of laziness and complacency. Progress is maintained only through relentless inner work, not passive reception of teachings or practices.

**The Three Obstacles Must Be Actively Overcome**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion consistently impede spiritual movement. These are not temporary conditions to be avoided, but persistent forces requiring constant vigilance and disciplined action. Their influence leads directly to stagnation unless met with conscious, unwavering opposition.

**Awakening Is an Internal Realization**  
True awakening emerges from direct, honest self-observation, not from imported doctrines or prescribed methods. The process is deeply personal, grounded in one’s own discernment and perseverance. External means serve no substitutive role in this transformation.
