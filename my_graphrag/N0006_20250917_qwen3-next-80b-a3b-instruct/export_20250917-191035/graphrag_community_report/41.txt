Title: The Core Wisdom of Non-Attachment in Spiritual Practice

Summary:  
The text reveals that true liberation from attachment arises when one perceives all phenomena as illusory. This perception is not merely intellectual but is activated through conscious awareness, which naturally dissolves clinging. The mechanism of this transformation lies in the direct recognition of impermanent, unreal nature of experience.

Perceiving All Phenomena as Illusory  
When all experiences are understood as transient and without inherent substance, attachment does not take root. This recognition does not require forceful suppression of desire or aversion; instead, it renders clinging irrelevant by revealing the foundationless nature of what is clung to.

Awareness as the Instrument of Liberation  
Conscious observation functions as the means through which the illusory quality of reality becomes visible. This awareness is not passive; it actively illuminates the deceptive solidity of phenomena, causing emotional and mental grip to release spontaneously. The transformation occurs not through effort to change experience, but through seeing it clearly.
