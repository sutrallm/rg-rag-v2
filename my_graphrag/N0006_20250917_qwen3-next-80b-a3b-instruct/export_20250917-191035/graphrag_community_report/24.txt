**Title: Chaoshi and the Path of the Enlightened Sage**

**Summary**  
True liberation is achieved not through the accumulation of spiritual experiences—such as meditative bliss, supernatural powers, or stages of enlightenment—but through the unwavering application of Chaoshi, the wisdom that sees all phenomena as impermanent and without self. The enlightened sage embodies this wisdom daily, sustaining mental stillness and moral purity while extinguishing all afflictions. Clinging to any state, even those considered spiritual, perpetuates bondage; freedom arises only through non-attachment, grounded in the direct realization of impermanence and non-self.

**Chaoshi is the Foundation of True Liberation**  
The wisdom of Chaoshi enables the dissolution of clinging by revealing that all phenomena—including meditative states, supernatural abilities, and spiritual accomplishments—are transient and devoid of inherent self. This understanding disrupts attachment not only to worldly desires but also to spiritual achievements, making it the indispensable basis for freedom from suffering and the cycle of birth and death.

**Supernatural Powers and Spiritual Attainments Are Not Ends in Themselves**  
Psychic abilities and stages of enlightenment, though they may arise through deep practice, are not signs of final realization. To hold onto them is to introduce new objects of attachment, which distract from the goal of complete release. Their transient nature means they cannot serve as stable grounds for lasting peace.

**Mental Stillness Must Be Guided by Transcendent Wisdom**  
Daily practice of mental stillness and awareness, while essential, is insufficient on its own. Without the guiding force of transcendent wisdom, such practice risks reinforcing attachment to peaceful or blissful states. Only when stillness is informed by the recognition of impermanence and non-self does it become a vehicle for true liberation.

**The Enlightened Sage Embodies Non-Attachment as a Way of Being**  
The virtue of the sage is not merely moral conduct or emotional balance—it is the continuous manifestation of Chaoshi. This includes the complete extinction of afflictions, the absence of clinging to any experience, and the quiet stability of a mind no longer deceived by illusions of permanence or self.

**Impermanence and Non-Self Are the Core Realizations Grounding All Virtue**  
The understanding that all things arise and pass away—without a fixed, independent core—is what enables moral purity, equanimity, and freedom from disturbance. These two principles are not abstract concepts but the living foundation upon which wisdom operates, dismantling every form of clinging, whether to pleasure, pain, power, or spiritual identity.

**Liberation Is the Extinction of Clinging, Not the Attainment of States**  
True freedom is defined by the absence of attachment—not by the presence of extraordinary experiences. When afflictions are extinguished like burnt-out wood, no desire remains to grasp at anything, including the very notion of enlightenment. Complete release is found only in letting go of all targets, even the most revered.
