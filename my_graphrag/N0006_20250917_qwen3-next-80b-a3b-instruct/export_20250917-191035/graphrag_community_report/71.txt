**Title: The Triadic Path of Jie, Ding, and Hui in Spiritual Liberation**

**Summary:**  
Spiritual liberation is achieved through a structured, sequential progression rooted in moral discipline (jie), supported by meditative concentration (ding), and finalized by liberating wisdom (hui). Each element is indispensable and mutually reinforcing, with jie establishing the necessary conditions for ding to arise, and ding enabling hui to emerge. Without moral purity, the mind remains distracted and incapable of deep concentration or wisdom. Hui is the decisive force that severs the root of suffering, while jie and ding serve as its essential preparatory foundations.

---

**Moral Discipline is the Foundational Step**  
Moral discipline (jie) is explicitly named as the base or groundwork of the spiritual path. It purifies behavior, ensures stability, and prevents mental distraction. Pure ethical conduct is not merely a recommendation but a prerequisite for any further progress, as the mind cannot attain depth or clarity without it. The absence of jie renders cultivation ineffective, as scattered thoughts block the emergence of higher states.

**Meditative Concentration Enables Wisdom to Arise**  
Meditative concentration (ding) serves as the stabilizing force that calms and focuses the mind, making it a receptive instrument for wisdom. It is described as essential fuel or support for developing higher understanding. Without ding, the mind remains unsteady and unable to sustain the clarity required for wisdom to take root, even if moral discipline is present.

**Liberating Wisdom Severs the Root of Suffering**  
Liberating wisdom (hui) is the culmination of the path, acting as the decisive key that cuts through ignorance—the origin of cyclic existence. It is the final and transformative element, but it cannot arise independently. Its effectiveness is entirely dependent on the prior establishment of moral discipline and meditative concentration, which together create the mental conditions necessary for its emergence.

**The Three Components Form an Interdependent System**  
The progression from jie to ding to hui is not linear in isolation but functions as an indivisible triad—like the three legs of a tripod. Each supports the others, with jie providing ethical stability, ding offering mental clarity, and hui achieving ultimate release. No single element can stand alone; their interdependence ensures the integrity and efficacy of the entire path to liberation.
