**Title: The Three Poisons and the Cycle of Birth and Death in Buddhist Liberation**

**Summary:**  
The three poisons—greed, hatred, and delusion—are identified as the core mechanisms that sustain the cycle of birth and death. Liberation is achieved only when these poisons are fully removed, a state marked by spiritual perfection. Wisdom is the direct and sole means of dismantling the poisons by exposing the false nature of self and permanence.

**The Three Poisons Fuel Rebirth**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion are the foundational causes of the cycle of birth and death. They generate karma and perpetuate rebirth, making them the driving force behind continued suffering and spiritual entrapment.

**Spiritual Perfection Requires Eradication of the Three Poisons**  
A person who has attained complete liberation is defined by the total removal of greed, hatred, and delusion. Their state of perfection is not measured by external achievements but by the absence of these internal afflictions.

**Wisdom Is the Direct Antidote to the Three Poisons**  
The cultivation of wisdom does not simply suppress or manage greed, hatred, and delusion—it actively dismantles them. This is accomplished through the clear realization that the self and all phenomena lack inherent permanence, thereby removing their basis in illusion.
