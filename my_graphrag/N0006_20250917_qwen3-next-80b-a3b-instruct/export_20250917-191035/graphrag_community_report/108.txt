**Title: The Liberation of the Saint: Elimination of Greed, Anger, and Ignorance**

**Summary:**  
The Saint achieves complete liberation by severing the three fundamental afflictions—greed, anger, and ignorance. These are not merely suppressed but entirely uprooted, marking the endpoint of psychological bondage. Ignorance is identified as the root cause of both greed and anger, leading to misperception of reality and emotional reactivity. The Saint’s state is defined not by external achievement but by the total dissolution of these internal chains, resulting in clear perception and freedom from suffering.

**Greed is fully eradicated as a sign of purity and liberation**  
The Saint no longer clings to objects, experiences, or identities, having completely severed attachment. This cessation of greed is not a partial restraint but a final and irreversible termination, serving as a definitive marker of spiritual attainment.

**Anger is completely abandoned as an expression of transcendence**  
The Saint no longer reacts with hostility or resentment toward unpleasant conditions. The absence of anger demonstrates mastery over emotional distorting forces, confirming the Saint’s freedom from the cycle of aversion and suffering.

**Ignorance is the root source of all psychological bonds**  
All forms of attachment and aversion arise from a fundamental misunderstanding of reality—the misperception of the five aggregates as a permanent, independent self. This delusion generates both craving and hostility, making ignorance the foundational cause of every binding force.

**The Saint transcends all bonds through direct wisdom**  
No residual attachment, aversion, or confusion remains. The Saint’s realization is not theoretical but transformative, replacing delusion with unerring insight into the true nature of phenomena, thereby dissolving every source of bondage at its origin.
