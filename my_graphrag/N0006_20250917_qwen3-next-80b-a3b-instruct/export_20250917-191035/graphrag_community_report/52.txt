**Title: Shengrong and the Eradication of the Three Poisons**

**Summary**  
Shengrong represents a state of complete liberation achieved through the total removal of greed, hatred, and delusion—the three fundamental afflictions that trap beings in the cycle of samsara. Those who attain this state are free from rebirth and serve as inspirational figures, even to celestial beings. Liberation is not partial but absolute, defined solely by the absence of these three mental disturbances.

**Complete Eradication of Greed, Hatred, and Delusion Defines Liberation**  
The state of Shengrong is not marked by partial progress or temporary calm but by the full and irreversible elimination of greed, hatred, and delusion. These three afflictions are identified as the root causes of bondage to samsara, and their absence is the sole criterion for true freedom. No other qualities or achievements are cited as necessary—their eradication alone constitutes liberation.

**Shengrong Individuals Transcend the Cycle of Rebirth**  
Those who embody Shengrong are no longer subject to samsara, the continuous cycle of birth, death, and rebirth. This transcendence is the direct result of eliminating the three poisons, which otherwise fuel karmic continuity. Their existence is described as a settled, free state—beyond suffering and deterministic forces.

**Shengrong Inspires Beyond Human Realms**  
The presence of Shengrong beings carries weight beyond the human sphere, influencing even celestial entities. Their state of purity and freedom evokes reverence not merely due to moral conduct, but because it embodies a condition wholly detached from the afflictions that govern all conditioned existence.
