**Title: Liberation from Samsara Through the Eradication of Tanchendi**

**Summary**  
Liberation from the cycle of birth and death—samsara—is achieved only through the complete removal of greed, hatred, and delusion. These three root afflictions bind sentient beings to suffering, and their persistence ensures the continuation of the cycle. The pathway out consists of meditative absorption, supported by mindfulness, diligence, and wisdom. Those who have fully severed these roots are distinguished by their definitive liberation.

**Meditative Absorption Breaks the Chain of Conditioned Suffering**  
The practice of meditative absorption serves as the direct means to dismantle the structure of conditioned suffering. It is not merely a technique but the essential practice through which the hold of samsara is undone, enabling movement beyond its confines.

**Greed, Hatred, and Delusion Are the Foundational Bindings to Samsara**  
The three root afflictions—greed, hatred, and delusion—are identified as the sole and sufficient causes maintaining the cycle of birth and death. Their presence sustains suffering; their absence is the condition of release.

**Liberation Is Defined by the Complete Severance of the Three Afflictions**  
True freedom from samsara is not partial or provisional. Those who have attained this state have entirely eliminated greed, hatred, and delusion. This total eradication is the definitive marker of liberation, distinguishing them from those still entangled in the cycle.

**Laxity and Ignorance Lead to Entrapment in Samsara**  
The conditioned realm of suffering is entered through neglect and lack of understanding. Without mindfulness and wisdom, beings succumb to the influence of the three afflictions, reinforcing their bondage and perpetuating suffering.
