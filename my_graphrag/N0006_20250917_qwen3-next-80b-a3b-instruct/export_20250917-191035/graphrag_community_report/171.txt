**Title: Chao Lie Zhu Zhe’s Path to Liberation Through Ethical Conduct, Concentration, and Wisdom**

**Summary:**  
True liberation arises from the integrated practice of ethical behavior, deep concentration, and wisdom that recognizes impermanence and non-self. These elements mutually reinforce one another, dissolving attachment and affliction. Practitioners who demonstrate full realization do not seek or cling to meditative experiences, supernatural feats, or recognition; instead, their attainment is evident in humble, fearless, and unwavering presence in ordinary life.

**Ethical Conduct and Mental Clarity Are Interdependent**  
Clean ethical conduct purifies the mind, creating the conditions necessary for sustained concentration. In turn, deep concentration nourishes wisdom, which brings mental clarity. Neither can fully develop without the other, forming a foundational cycle that supports the entire path.

**Concentration Enables Non-Grasping Observation**  
Deep concentration allows one to observe all phenomena—including meditative states—without clinging. This non-attachment is not passive but active discernment, rooted in the recognition that even profound experiences are transient and lack inherent self.

**Wisdom Directly Dissolves Attachment**  
Understanding the impermanent and selfless nature of all things removes the basis for clinging. This wisdom, not mere technique, is what enables the practitioner to release attachment to outcomes, experiences, or identity—seeing nothing as “me” or “mine.”

**True Realization Is Manifest in Daily Life**  
The highest attainment is not marked by extraordinary experiences or public recognition. It is revealed in quiet humility, fearlessness, and the steadfast embodiment of ethical behavior and mental clarity within everyday actions, free from the desire to attain or display anything.
