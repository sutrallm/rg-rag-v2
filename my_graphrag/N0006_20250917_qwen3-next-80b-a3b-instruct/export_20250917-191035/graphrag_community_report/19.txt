Title: Wulian and Shenguo: The Path to True Liberation

Summary:  
True liberation is achieved not through spiritual attainments, but through the complete absence of desire for any outcome — including enlightenment. Spiritual achievements, when held onto, become obstacles to freedom, reinforcing ego and attachment. Non-seeking is not a supportive practice but the defining condition of liberation.

Non-attachment as the Foundation of Liberation  
Liberation hinges entirely on the absence of desire for results. Any pursuit of outcomes, even those labeled as spiritual, undermines true freedom. The state of non-seeking is not optional or incidental — it is the essential condition that defines liberation.

Spiritual Attainments as Hidden Bondage  
Achievements in spiritual practice, if clung to, transform from markers of progress into sources of ego and entrapment. The very act of holding onto enlightenment or realization creates attachment, which directly opposes the state of non-seeking and thus prevents true freedom.

Contradiction Between Attainment and Non-Seeking  
The moment one clings to spiritual realization, it contradicts the principle of non-seeking. True freedom demands the relinquishment of all outcomes, even the most exalted ones. One cannot attain liberation while still desiring to have attained it.
