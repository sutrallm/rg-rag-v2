**Title: The Cycle of Samsara and Its Roots in Fangyi, Delusion, Greed, and Hatred**

**Summary**  
The cycle of continuous birth, death, and rebirth is sustained by delusion, greed, hatred, and fangyi (laxity or indulgence). These factors collectively generate karma that binds beings to suffering and spiritual regression. Liberation is possible only through wisdom, mindfulness, and ethical conduct that dismantle these core afflictions.

**Delusion as the Foundational Cause**  
Delusion, defined as ignorance of true reality, engenders false perceptions of self and permanence. These misconceptions are the origin of all unwholesome actions, making it the root from which all other afflictions arise and sustain the cycle of rebirth.

**Greed as a Primary Driver of Rebirth**  
Attachment to sensory and mental experiences fuels greed, which generates karma that directly conditions future existence. This attachment does not merely reflect desire but actively creates the karmic force that ensures continued presence within the cycle.

**Hatred as a Cause of Suffering and Rebirth**  
Hatred produces negative karma and disturbs mental equilibrium. Its presence conditions rebirth in states of suffering and ensures the continuation of the cycle by reinforcing patterns of aversion and agitation.

**Fangyi as the Reinforcement of Bondage**  
Fangyi—the abandonment of vigilance—allows the mind to be overtaken by desires and distractions. It does not act independently but strengthens craving and ignorance, thereby locking the mind into samsara and preventing spiritual progress.
