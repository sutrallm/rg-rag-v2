**Title: Ji Jie and the Tri-fold Conduct as the Foundation for Meditation and Wisdom**

**Summary**  
Ethical conduct spanning body, speech, and mind is indispensable for the development of stable meditation and authentic wisdom. Without this integrated moral discipline, meditation risks distortion and wisdom remains superficial. The tri-fold structure is not optional but the necessary framework through which ethical practice sustains deeper mental and spiritual progression.

**Moral Discipline Underpins Stable Meditation**  
A consistent and disciplined ethical life—governing physical actions, verbal expression, and mental states—is required to establish a solid base for meditation. Without this foundation, meditative experiences lose their purity and may become unstable or misaligned with deeper understanding.

**Ethical Conduct Prevents the Distortion of Wisdom**  
Wisdom cannot flourish independently of ethical behavior. When moral discipline is absent, even advanced mental states or intellectual insights fail to attain depth or truth, remaining fragmented or superficial. The integrity of wisdom depends directly on the integrity of conduct.

**Tri-fold Practice Forms the Complete Structure of Ethical Discipline**  
The ethical framework is not partial or selective; it must be applied holistically across all three dimensions—body, speech, and mind. Each aspect supports and reinforces the others, and neglecting any one compromises the entire system. This triad is the essential and comprehensive structure through which moral discipline is realized.
