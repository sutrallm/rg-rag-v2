**Title: Wuqiu and Chaowuzhuo: The Path of Non-Attachment in the Shengzhe**

**Summary**  
The state of wuqiu—non-seeking and non-attachment—is achieved through the deep realization of chaowuzhuo, the wisdom that all phenomena are impermanent and lack inherent self. This wisdom arises from understanding no-self (wuwo) and is cultivated through practices like zhengnian and xiuxin, which foster non-reactive awareness. The shengzhe exemplifies this condition, living without aspiration or aversion, in pure presence. When attachment dissolves, seeking naturally ceases, leading to a mind like a clear mirror and a bliss inaccessible to worldly pursuits.

**Non-Attachment Arises from the Realization of No-Self**  
The foundation of letting go of all attachments is the understanding that there is no permanent, independent self. When the illusion of a self is seen through, the impulse to cling to favorable states or resist unfavorable ones loses its root. Without a sense of “I” that needs to gain or avoid, attachment cannot take hold.

**Wisdom in Impermanence and Emptiness Naturally Ends Seeking**  
Recognizing that all experiences and states are transient and without inherent substance removes the motivation to pursue or escape them. This direct perception leads to the automatic cessation of striving—whether for supernatural powers, divine signs, or relief from dullness—because nothing is seen as worth grasping or rejecting.

**Zhengnian Sustains Non-Attachment in Daily Life**  
Through the cultivation of non-reactive awareness, one remains undisturbed by personal preferences or aversions. This continuous practice steadies the mind, making it possible to meet each moment without interference from desire or aversion, thereby maintaining the clarity of wuqiu.

**Xiuxin Cultivates the Wisdom of Non-Attachment**  
The practice of refining the mind trains the capacity to observe thoughts, feelings, and sensations without clinging or rejecting. This disciplined observation develops the deeper understanding that underpins chaowuzhuo, allowing detachment to arise not through force but through clear seeing.

**Wuqiu Is the Natural Expression of Chaowuzhuo**  
When one truly perceives all phenomena as impermanent and selfless, the urge to seek or avoid dissolves. Wuqiu is not an effort to attain peace but the effortless state that follows when the mind no longer grasps at outcomes. It is the spontaneous condition of a mind unbound by desire.

**The Shengzhe Embodies the Absence of Striving**  
The perfected state is lived without aspiration for personal gain, without any agenda beyond pure presence. This is not a passive state but the active expression of a mind fully aligned with reality—neither reaching nor retreating, simply reflecting what is.
