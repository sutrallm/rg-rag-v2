Title: The Five Aggregates and Anatta Observation in the Path to Liberation

Summary:  
The five aggregates—form, feeling, perception, mental formations, and consciousness—are the sole objects of meditative observation in the practice of anatta. Continuous awareness of their transient nature dismantles the illusion of a permanent self, leading to transcendent wisdom, moral discipline, and inner peace. This process is central to the realization of liberation by saintly beings.

Observation of the aggregates as empty of self is the foundation of moral discipline and inner tranquility.  
The direct meditative practice of tracking the arising and passing away of the five aggregates dissolves the false notion of a fixed, independent identity.

Anatta practice is the established method through which transcendent wisdom arises and is realized.  
The five aggregates serve as the exclusive focus for this practice, functioning as the means by which the illusion of a permanent self is systematically unmade.

Saintly beings attain moral discipline and inner peace through the direct perception that the five aggregates lack inherent self-nature.
