**Title: The Saint and the Three Poisons: Liberation, Radiance, and Reverence**

**Summary**  
A Saint is defined by the total eradication of greed, hatred, and delusion, which are the root causes of suffering and rebirth. This purification leads to unshakable inner tranquility, free from attachment, aversion, and ignorance. The resulting state is marked by radiant peace that transcends external conditions and naturally elicits reverence—even from celestial beings who, despite their bliss, remain bound by samsara. Liberation is not merely freedom from suffering but the permanent cessation of the mental defilements that fuel cyclic existence.

**The Eradication of the Three Poisons Defines Liberation**  
The complete removal of greed, hatred, and delusion is not one among many qualities of a Saint but the sole defining criterion. These three forces sustain suffering and rebirth; their absence marks the end of bondage. A Saint no longer experiences mental defilements because they have uprooted ignorance about reality, extinguished aversion, and dissolved attachment—making liberation not a goal but a condition of being.

**Inner Tranquility Arises from the Absence of Self and Defilements**  
True peace is not the absence of noise or external order but the absence of an “I” that can be disturbed. When the mind is free of the Three Poisons, no internal unrest arises—even amid chaos. This stillness is not cultivated through suppression but emerges naturally when greed, anger, and delusion are fully erased, leaving no foundation for disturbance.

**Radiance Naturally Inspires Reverence Across Realms**  
The purified mind of a Saint emits a quality of inner radiance, the result of complete moral discipline and wisdom. This quality is not forced or displayed; it is an inevitable byproduct of eradicating defilements. Even celestial beings, who dwell in heavenly bliss but remain within samsara, recognize and honor this state. Their reverence stems not from power or status but from the unmistakable depth of a being who has transcended all that binds them.

**Celestial Beings Remain Bound Despite Their Bliss**  
Though celestial beings experience pleasure and longevity far beyond human capacity, they have not eradicated the Three Poisons and thus remain trapped in cyclic existence. Their respect for Saints arises not from superiority but from the contrast: they witness in Saints what they themselves cannot attain—complete freedom from the roots of suffering. Their position highlights that spiritual attainment is not measured by pleasure, but by the absence of the causes of bondage.
