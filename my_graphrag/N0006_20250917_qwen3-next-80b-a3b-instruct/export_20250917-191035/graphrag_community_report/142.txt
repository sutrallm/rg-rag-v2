**Title: Liberation Through Wisdom: The Path of Chazhuo, Chan Ding, and Zhili**

**Summary:**  
True freedom from suffering and the cycle of rebirth arises not through external forces or extraordinary abilities, but through inner transformation rooted in wisdom, persistent effort, and deep observation of reality. The core obstacle is attachment—especially to the illusion of a permanent self. Only by cultivating diligent practice and meditative concentration, guided by profound understanding of impermanence, non-self, and interdependence, can one achieve complete liberation. Supernatural abilities, though possible, distract from this goal and reinforce the very clinging they seem to transcend.

---

**The Root of Suffering is Attachment**  
Clinging to experiences—past, present, or future—is the primary cause of entrapment in cycles of rebirth and suffering. Liberation begins only when this attachment is fully ceased, including attachment to the notion of a separate, enduring self.

**Liberation is Achieved Through Self-Awakening, Not External Means**  
Freedom from samsara is not granted by divine intervention, altered states, or supernatural forces. It is realized internally through the complete cessation of delusion and attachment, resulting in a state of non-abiding, non-acquisition, and non-dependence.

**Wisdom is the Direct Path to Freedom**  
Deep understanding of reality—non-self, impermanence, and interdependence—supersedes all other spiritual achievements. This wisdom directly severs worldly bonds and is the only means to true enlightenment, making it infinitely more valuable than any extraordinary ability.

**Meditative Concentration Enables Observation of Attachment**  
Chan Ding provides the mental clarity needed to observe the arising and dissolution of clinging. While essential, it is insufficient alone; without wisdom, concentration cannot lead to liberation.

**Persistent Effort Sustains the Path to Liberation**  
Swisheng—the continuous, vigilant cultivation of the right path—is the foundation upon which meditative concentration is built and maintained. Without this steady energy, ignorance and laziness prevail.

**Interdependence Reveals the Illusion of Independence**  
All phenomena arise only through causes and conditions. Recognizing this interconnection dismantles the false belief in isolated, permanent entities, weakening the structural basis for attachment.

**Impermanence and Non-Self Are Key to Dissolving Delusion**  
Recognizing that all things change and that no enduring self exists is critical. These realizations break the illusion of permanence that fuels desire and clinging, making liberation possible.

**Supernatural Powers Distort the Path**  
The emergence of extraordinary abilities during practice does not indicate enlightenment. Pursuing them reinforces ego and attachment, deepening delusion rather than transcending it.

**Liberation Embodied by the Sage and Saintly Person**  
Those who have fully severed all bonds of attachment and ignorance—referred to as sages or saintly persons—are living expressions of complete liberation. Their state is not external or miraculous, but the result of inner transformation.
