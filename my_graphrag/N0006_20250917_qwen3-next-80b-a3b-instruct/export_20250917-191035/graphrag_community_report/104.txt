**Title: The Role of Wisdom and Non-Attachment in Overcoming Suffering**

**Summary**  
Suffering is the foundational condition of existence, rooted in attachment to self and permanence. Liberation is achieved not by avoiding suffering but by transcending it through direct understanding of its causes. The path to freedom begins with recognizing impermanence and the absence of a fixed self, supported by accurate perception of the Four Noble Truths. This understanding leads to cessation of craving, elimination of distress, and the natural dissolution of suffering.

---

**Impermanence is the Source of Suffering**  
All conditioned phenomena are transient, and the clinging to stability or permanence in a constantly changing world generates distress. Recognizing this instability is the initial step toward releasing attachment and diminishing suffering.

**Attachment to Self is the Core Cause of Suffering**  
The belief in a permanent, independent self—along with attachment to what is perceived as “mine”—directly produces grief, anxiety, and dissatisfaction. This self-centered clinging fuels desire, aversion, and inner unrest.

**Liberation Consists in Complete Transcendence of Suffering**  
The purpose of spiritual practice is not merely to manage pain but to fully surpass it. Liberation is defined by the total cessation of suffering, achieved only when its root causes are eradicated.

**Wisdom Emanates from Understanding the Four Noble Truths**  
True clarity arises from seeing suffering, its origin, its cessation, and the path to its end as they fundamentally are. This accurate perception forms the basis for ethical conduct and mental freedom.

**Suffering Arises Through Dependent Origination and Clinging**  
Distress emerges not from external conditions alone but from how one responds to them—specifically through grasping. When one sees the causal chain clearly—that “this exists, therefore that exists”—the pattern of clinging unravels, and suffering ends.

**Wisdom Enables Non-Attachment and Ends the Cycle of Distress**  
Through deep understanding, one no longer flees from suffering or clings to fleeting experiences. This shift in perception removes the need for evasion or possession, allowing natural release from the cycle of unease.
