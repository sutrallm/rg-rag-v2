**Title: Liberation Through Inner Observation: The Path from Samsara to Jiepo**

**Summary**  
Liberation (jiepo) is achieved solely through personal inner observation and the cultivation of wisdom, not by external aid or faith. It involves the complete eradication of greed, hatred, and delusion, resulting in freedom from the cycle of rebirth (samsara). This state is reached by directly understanding impermanence, non-self, and emptiness through meditation and moral discipline, which together form the means to transcend ignorance and craving.

**Direct Inner Observation Leads to Liberation**  
Liberation is not granted by outside forces or divine intervention but emerges from personally observing the transient nature of bodily and mental phenomena. This practice reveals the absence of a fixed self, replacing blind faith with clear understanding, and is the foundation for breaking free from the cycle of birth and death.

**Wisdom, Meditation, and Moral Discipline Are the Path to Liberation**  
True liberation arises through zhihui—the direct realization of impermanence, non-self, and emptiness. These realizations are cultivated by sustained meditation and adherence to moral discipline, forming an integrated path that dismantles ignorance and craving, the root causes of rebirth.

**Liberation Means Complete Eradication of Fannao**  
Jiepo is not a state of temporary control or suppression of greed, hatred, and delusion, but their total removal. Only when these mental defilements are wholly extinguished can one be fully freed from samsara and its endless cycle.

**Liberation Ends the Cycle of Rebirth**  
Achieving jiepo terminates the process of rebirth, which is sustained by ignorance and craving. The freedom from samsara is not metaphorical but a fundamental shift in being, attained exclusively through the deep understanding developed via inner observation and wisdom.
