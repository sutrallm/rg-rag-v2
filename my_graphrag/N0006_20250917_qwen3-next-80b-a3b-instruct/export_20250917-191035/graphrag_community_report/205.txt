**Title: Juecha and the Transformation into a Holy Being Through the Eradication of the Three Poisons**

**Summary**  
Sustained mindful awareness, known as Juecha, is the essential practice that enables the eradication of greed, hatred, and delusion — the three root causes of suffering. This process cultivates moral discipline and wisdom, allowing one to remain undisturbed by external conditions and break free from karmic cycles. The transformation into a holy being is not an external achievement but the result of inner refinement rooted in Juecha.

**Juecha is the foundational practice for uprooting ignorance**  
Mindful awareness, or Juecha, begins with observing breath, thoughts, and emotions. This direct perception dismantles ignorance — the fundamental misunderstanding of reality — which otherwise fuels craving, aversion, and entrapment in cyclic existence. By consistently practicing Juecha, the conditions that sustain ignorance are dismantled at their source.

**The three poisons drive karmic cycles and must be eradicated**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion are identified as the primary forces perpetuating suffering and karmic recurrence. These distortions of perception arise from ignorance and manifest as conditioned habits. Their persistent presence traps individuals in repetitive patterns of suffering, making their removal not optional but necessary for liberation.

**A holy being is defined by inner transformation, not external conditions**  
The qualities of a holy being — moral discipline, wisdom, and undisturbed composure — emerge solely from the internal eradication of the three poisons. This state is not achieved through avoidance of external circumstances but through the deep, sustained application of Juecha, which fortifies moral conduct and discernment while extinguishing the roots of suffering.

**Juecha cultivates Chanding and supports the development of wisdom**  
Mindful awareness does not operate in isolation; it is the primary means of developing meditative concentration, known as Chanding. This refined state of focus, born from Juecha, enables deeper insight and stabilizes wisdom, which together sustain moral discipline and ensure the lasting absence of greed, hatred, and delusion.
