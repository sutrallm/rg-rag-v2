**Title: An Zhi, Cheng Jie, and Sheng Zhe: The Path to Liberation from Svenng**

**Summary**  
Mental stillness cultivated through continuous mindfulness leads to inner tranquility, which serves as the foundation for achieving full liberation from the cycle of birth and death. This liberation, marked by the complete eradication of greed, hatred, and delusion, defines the enlightened one—the sage—who resides in enduring peace and inspires reverence. Transcendent wisdom is the essential pathway to this ultimate spiritual goal.

**Continuous Mindfulness Cultivates Inner Tranquility**  
The practice of sustained mindfulness directly results in an undisturbed, peaceful state of mind. This stillness is not incidental but foundational, forming the necessary mental condition from which deeper spiritual progress emerges.

**Liberation Requires Transcendent Wisdom**  
Freedom from attachments, delusions, and the cycle of rebirth is not attained through passive withdrawal or ritual alone. It is the full development of transcendent wisdom that enables the dissolution of ignorance and the cessation of karmic perpetuation.

**The Enlightened One Embodies Complete Liberation**  
One who has eradicated greed, hatred, and delusion is defined by the absence of these hindrances and the presence of unwavering peace. This state, known as the enlightened one, is synonymous with full emancipation from the cycle of birth and death.

**The Sage Sustains Unbroken Stillness**  
The sage does not temporarily attain tranquility but abides in it continually. This enduring state reflects the completeness of liberation, where mental stillness is no longer a practice but a natural mode of being.

**Liberation from Svenng Is the Paramount Objective**  
All spiritual effort, from mindfulness to wisdom cultivation, converges on the single aim of escaping the endless cycle of rebirth. This release is not metaphorical but the recognized culmination of the path.
