Title: 持续修习与精进修行的本质统一

Summary:  
持续修习是精进修行的外在实践形式，二者在本质上无差别，均以不间断的修持为核心。这种修持方式被比喻为添油点灯，only through continuous replenishment can the inner light be sustained and strengthened.

Insights:  
**Sustained Practice Maintains Inner Light**  
The analogy of添油点灯 illustrates that the inner light—representing spiritual clarity or awareness—cannot be preserved without ongoing effort. Just as a lamp dims without oil, so too does spiritual progress fade without consistent practice.

**Continuity Defines True Advancement**  
True spiritual development is not measured by sporadic effort but by unwavering continuity. The equality between sustained practice and earnest cultivation signifies that progress arises not from intensity alone, but from irreducible persistence.
