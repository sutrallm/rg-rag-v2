Title: Dangxia, Shengling, and Zhengnian: The Path to Present-Moment Realization

Summary:  
True realization arises exclusively through direct, unmediated awareness of the present moment. This state is not attained by pursuing future outcomes or external conditions but through the dedicated practice of observing breathing, feelings, and thoughts as they unfold now. The method of mindful observation is the sole gateway to this realization, and without full engagement in the present, awakening cannot occur.

Present-Moment Awareness Is the Sole Condition for Realization  
Realization cannot be reached through anticipation, distant goals, or abstract concepts. It emerges only when attention is fully anchored in the immediacy of experience — the breath, sensations, and mental activity as they appear and dissolve in real time. Any movement away from this immediate awareness, even in pursuit of liberation, prevents the emergence of true understanding.

Mindful Observation Is the Only Access Route  
The practice of mindful observation — noticing phenomena without judgment or attachment — is the structured means by which one enters the awakened state. There is no alternative pathway described; conceptual analysis, emotional pursuit, or spiritual seeking outside the present moment are not supported as valid methods. Only through this continuous, clear observation does the state of realization become possible.

Awakening Cannot Be Separated from the Here and Now  
The state of realization is inseparable from the present moment. It does not arise through effort directed toward future attainment, sacred places, or transcendent conditions. Even the intention to achieve awakening elsewhere invalidates the condition required for it. Full presence, without seeking, is not a technique but the very enactment of realization itself.
