**Title: Jeongjin and Chixu Xiulian as Foundational Paths to Liberation**

**Summary:**  
Diligent effort (Jeongjin) and continuous practice (Chixu xiulian) are inseparable, mutually reinforcing disciplines essential for liberation from ignorance and the cycle of birth and death. Jeongjin drives persistent action, while Chixu xiulian operationalizes this through daily, moment-to-moment mindfulness. Their combined manifestation sustains focus, prevents regression, and transforms small, consistent acts of stillness and goodness into a cumulative force leading to liberation.

**Jeongjin as the Driving Force of Persistent Action**  
Jeongjin is defined as the unwavering, sustained effort applied to the path of liberation. It is not a passive state but an active commitment that fuels progress. Without this relentless dedication, the journey toward overcoming ignorance cannot be maintained. This effort is not occasional or situational but must remain constant, forming the core motivation behind all practice.

**Chixu Xiulian as the Daily Manifestation of Effort**  
Chixu xiulian translates into the necessity of continuous, moment-to-moment mindfulness practiced each day. It is the tangible, lived expression of Jeongjin, where even minor acts of goodness and stillness, when repeated without interruption, accumulate into a transformative momentum. This daily discipline guards against the re-emergence of old habits and ensures steady, unbroken advancement.

**Interdependence of Sustained Focus and Continuous Practice**  
The practice of Chanding—sustained focus—depends entirely on Jeongjin to remain undisturbed by distraction. In turn, Jeongjin finds its form and endurance through Chixu xiulian. Neither can function effectively alone; the one provides the intention, the other the consistent action. Together, they establish the necessary conditions for liberation by aligning intention with persistent, mindful behavior.
