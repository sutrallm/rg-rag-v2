Title: Liberation Through the Eradication of Mental Defilements

Summary:  
Complete freedom from suffering is attained only through sustained, disciplined practice that fully eliminates greed, hatred, and delusion. When these roots are exhausted, the mind operates without attachment, mirroring experience without retention, and manifests as boundless awareness.

—

The mind achieves clarity through non-clinging perception  
When greed, hatred, and delusion are entirely removed, the mind no longer holds onto or identifies with passing thoughts or emotions. It reflects experience as a mirror does — present without accumulation, untouched by temporary conditions.

Liberation requires relentless, energetic practice  
The roots of mental defilements cannot be uprooted by passive observation or intermittent effort. Only continuous, vigorous practice leads to their complete exhaustion and the realization of freedom.

Eradication of defilements produces boundaryless awareness  
With the full cessation of mental contamination, consciousness is no longer confined by attachment or aversion. The resulting state is described as mirror-like and limitless — unconditioned by external or internal stimuli.
