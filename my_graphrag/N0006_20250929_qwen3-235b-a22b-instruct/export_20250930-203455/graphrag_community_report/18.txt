**Title: The Nature and Attainment of Liberation: Characteristics and Pathways of the Liberated Being**

**Summary**  
The text outlines the state of the liberated being — one who has transcended生死 (birth and death), severed all afflictions, and attained unshakable inner stability and freedom. This state is achieved through disciplined practice of meditation and wisdom, resulting in a mind that is clear, unattached, and undisturbed by external conditions. The liberated being embodies tranquility, non-attachment, and complete freedom from cyclic existence, with inner qualities symbolized by a mirror-like clarity and an expansive, unobstructed awareness akin to space.

---

**Path to Liberation Through Meditation**  
Meditation serves as the essential method by which the liberated being attains mental stability and profound insight. Through sustained practice, the liberated being completely eliminates afflictions and reaches a state of unimpeded freedom. The culmination of this practice is a mind that is settled, perceptive, and fully at ease — a condition directly resulting from the disciplined cultivation of meditation.

**Wisdom as the Foundation of Liberation**  
The liberated being realizes the truths of impermanence and non-self through wisdom, leading to a mind free from clinging and disturbance. This realization severs all mental bindings and allows the being to remain unmoved by external circumstances. Wisdom is not merely intellectual understanding but a direct experiential realization that underlies the liberated state.

**Continuous Mindfulness in Daily Life**  
The liberated being maintains unwavering mindfulness in all activities — walking, standing, sitting, or lying down. This persistent awareness ensures that the mind remains undisturbed by internal or external stimuli, reflecting a constant state of presence and equanimity.

**Nirvana as the Embodied State**  
The liberated being has realized Nirvana, a condition characterized by profound peace and unobstructed freedom. The inner state of the liberated being mirrors the qualities of Nirvana — stillness, absence of agitation, and complete release from suffering.

**Unattached Action in the World**  
The liberated being operates in the world without attachment or fixation, embodying a state of “non-abiding action.” This outward expression reflects an inner condition of complete non-attachment to phenomena, allowing the being to engage with the world without being bound by it.

**Mirror-Like Clarity of Mind**  
The mind of the liberated being is likened to a clear mirror — it reflects all phenomena without clinging to or being stained by them. This quality signifies the ability to perceive reality as it is, without distortion or emotional entanglement, maintaining perfect clarity and non-reactivity.

**Spaciousness and Inclusiveness of Awareness**  
The liberated being’s awareness is compared to open space — vast,包容 (inclusive), and unobstructed. This symbolizes a state of complete freedom from fixation, where all experiences are held without resistance or attachment, allowing for boundless adaptability and peace.

**Complete Freedom from Suffering and Rebirth**  
The liberated being has entirely eradicated afflictions and is no longer subject to the cycle of birth and death. This liberation represents the ultimate freedom from suffering and the attainment of a permanent, stable state beyond cyclic existence.

**Unshakable Equanimity**  
The liberated being remains unaffected by external conditions, maintaining inner calm regardless of circumstances. This equanimity arises from the complete absence of craving, aversion, and ignorance, allowing the being to dwell in unchanging peace.
