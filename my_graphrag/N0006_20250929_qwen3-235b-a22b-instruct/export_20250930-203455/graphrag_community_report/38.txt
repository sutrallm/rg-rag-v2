**Title: The Path to Inner Purity and Liberation: Practices and Manifestations in Spiritual Cultivation**

**Summary**  
This report outlines the conditions, practices, and observable outcomes associated with the attainment of inner purity and liberation as described in the text. Key practices include meditation, mindfulness, and the cultivation of non-self awareness. The resulting state is marked by emotional stability, freedom from attachment, and an unshakable clarity of mind regardless of external circumstances. Inner purity is not an abstract goal but a measurable condition—evidenced by the absence of greed, hatred, and delusion, and the mind’s ability to remain undisturbed in all situations.

---

**Meditation as the Foundation for Inner Stability**  
Meditation is identified as a primary method for achieving inner purity. When the mind is no longer disturbed by greed, hatred, or delusion, it reflects the successful outcome of meditation. The deepening of meditation is marked by the mind’s ability to rest peacefully on its own, without being swayed by external conditions. This self-sustained calm is both a sign of progress and a stable condition that can be maintained.

**Non-Self Awareness Leads to Detachment from Conditions**  
The practice of non-self awareness enables the mind to cease clinging to internal or external phenomena. As this practice matures, the mind naturally settles into a state of non-abiding—free from attachment to any境 (condition or experience). In this state, the mind remains unmoved whether encountering favorable or adverse circumstances, indicating a profound level of inner freedom.

**Inner Purity Arises from the Elimination of Greed, Hatred, and Delusion**  
The cessation of greed, hatred, and delusion is a prerequisite for the manifestation of inner purity. Once these afflictions are removed, clarity and brightness naturally emerge in the mind. This purity is not artificially induced but is the natural consequence of eliminating the root causes of mental disturbance. The mind’s freedom from these afflictions is evident in its ability to remain calm and undisturbed regardless of external events.

**Clarity of Mind Is Rooted in Wisdom**  
Wisdom that perceives reality as it is allows the mind to no longer be pulled by false thoughts or illusions. This is the fundamental cause of inner purity. When wisdom is present, the mind operates with clear discernment, free from reactive patterns, and remains steady even in the face of changing conditions.

**The Mind as a Mirror Reflects Without Attachment**  
Inner purity is metaphorically described as a mirror that clearly reflects all phenomena without being stained by them. This mirror-like quality represents the mind’s capacity to perceive reality without clinging, rejecting, or being influenced by what is perceived. It is the ultimate expression of non-abiding—a state where the mind rests in openness and clarity, untouched by external stimuli.

**Stability in All Conditions Validates Spiritual Achievement**  
The ability to maintain inner peace during both favorable and adverse circumstances is presented as a definitive sign of spiritual accomplishment. Those who have reached this state do not react with joy or anger to external events. Their emotional equilibrium is not dependent on external conditions, demonstrating a mature and unshakable inner foundation.

**Liberation Manifests as Natural Freedom and Non-Attachment**  
After liberation, the mind naturally rests in a state of non-abiding. This is not a forced or cultivated condition but the spontaneous result of having freed the mind from afflictions. In this state, there is no clinging to any experience, thought, or sensation. The mind is completely free, operating with effortless clarity and peace, embodying true autonomy and release from suffering.
