**Title: The Path to Liberation: Understanding the State of Non-Attachment for Practitioners**

**Summary:**  
The core of liberation for practitioners lies in achieving a state free from attachment to self, achievement, or outcome. This state, termed non-attachment, is marked by the absence of clinging to “I practice,” “I attain,” or “I realize,” while also not rejecting what has not yet been attained. True liberation is confirmed internally through self-awareness and self-validation, independent of external judgment. The culmination of wisdom practice is this very state of non-attachment, which reflects the cessation of greed, hatred, and delusion, and allows the practitioner to rest fully in the inherent purity of reality.

**Non-Attachment as the Essence of Liberation**  
Liberation is defined by the practitioner’s ability to remain unattached to any state, including the notion of personal achievement or lack thereof. The practitioner does not cling to “I have practiced,” “I have realized,” or “I have attained,” nor do they resist or reject what has not yet been realized. This freedom from fixation is the hallmark of complete ease and liberation.

**Internal Validation as the Sole Measure of Progress**  
The practitioner’s advancement and final achievement are verified solely through self-awareness and self-validation. External evaluations, opinions, or benchmarks hold no authority in confirming the depth or authenticity of the practitioner’s realization. The truth of the state is known internally and cannot be conferred by others.

**Wisdom Practice Culminates in Non-Attachment**  
The full expression of wisdom practice is the realization of non-attachment. This state is not a stage to be reached and held, but the natural outcome of sustained observation and understanding. It represents the completion of the practitioner’s journey, where clinging ceases and the mind rests in its original clarity.

**Non-Attachment Reflects the Cessation of Defilements**  
The attainment of non-attachment signifies the internal eradication of greed, hatred, and delusion. It is not merely a conceptual understanding but a lived condition where the mind no longer reacts to conditions with craving or aversion. This inner transformation is the foundation of true freedom.

**Non-Attachment as the Resting Place in Reality**  
In the state of non-attachment, the practitioner abides fully in the unconditioned nature of reality. This is not a temporary state but the stable, natural condition of the mind when freed from distortion. It is the point where the practitioner no longer seeks, resists, or identifies — they simply are, in perfect alignment with what is.
