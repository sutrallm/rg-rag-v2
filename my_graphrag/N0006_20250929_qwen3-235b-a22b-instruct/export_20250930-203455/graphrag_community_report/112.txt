Title: Heart Autonomy and Its Role in Liberation: A Focus on Inner Freedom and Ethical Choice

Summary:  
The text establishes that true freedom arises from the autonomy of the heart — an internal state free from the influence of afflictions — and is distinct from external indulgence. This autonomy manifests through deliberate ethical choices, specifically selecting wholesome actions and avoiding unwholesome ones. Such behavior is not merely a moral preference but a foundational step in the process of liberation.

Insights:

Heart Autonomy as Liberation’s First Manifestation  
The text identifies the autonomy of the heart as the initial visible stage of liberation. This autonomy is defined by the absence of internal compulsion by afflictions, indicating that liberation begins internally, not through external conditions or achievements.

True Freedom Is Internal, Not External  
The text explicitly contrasts genuine freedom — rooted in the autonomy of the heart — with external indulgence. The latter is presented as incompatible with inner freedom, suggesting that liberation cannot be attained through unrestrained behavior or external gratification.

Ethical Choice as the Practical Expression of Heart Autonomy  
The text links heart autonomy to concrete behavior: the intentional selection of wholesome actions and the conscious avoidance of unwholesome ones. This indicates that autonomy is not abstract but is demonstrated through daily moral decisions, reinforcing its role as both a marker and a mechanism of liberation.
