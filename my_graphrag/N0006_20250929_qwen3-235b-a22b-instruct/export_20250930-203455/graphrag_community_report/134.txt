**Title: The Path to Liberation: How Practitioners Overcome Greed, Hatred, and Delusion**

**Summary**

Practitioners aim to eliminate greed, hatred, and delusion — the root causes of suffering and the primary obstacles to liberation. These afflictions stem from attachment to self and possessions and manifest as mental disturbances that must be systematically purified. Through disciplined practice of moral conduct, mental concentration, and wisdom — supported by the eightfold path — practitioners observe the impermanent, unsatisfactory, and selfless nature of all phenomena. They cultivate mindfulness and insight to recognize and dissolve these afflictions as they arise. The ultimate goal is to fully eradicate these three poisons, thereby achieving a mind that is clear, stable, and free from the cycle of suffering.

**Root Cause of Suffering Lies in Attachment to Self and Possessions**

The afflictions practitioners seek to overcome originate from clinging to the notion of “I” and “mine.” This attachment gives rise to greed, hatred, and delusion — persistent mental distortions that obstruct progress. These are not peripheral issues but the core obstacles that must be addressed and eliminated through sustained practice. Their presence perpetuates suffering and keeps the practitioner bound to cyclic existence.

**Methodology for Purification: Moral Conduct, Concentration, and Wisdom**

Practitioners rely on the triad of moral conduct, concentration, and wisdom — reinforced by the eightfold path — to systematically purify the mind. This includes right view, right intention, right effort, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right mindfulness, and right concentration. These practices enable continuous observation of the mind and the transient, unsatisfactory, and selfless nature of the five aggregates. Through this, practitioners gradually weaken and dissolve the grip of afflictions.

**Mind as a Mirror: Clarity Through Practice**

Through disciplined meditation and mindfulness, the mind becomes like a clear mirror, reflecting reality without distortion. This clarity arises as practitioners remove the mental impurities of greed, hatred, and delusion. Just as turbid water settles to reveal its natural clarity, the mind, when freed from agitation and defilements, reveals its inherent brightness and awareness. This state allows for direct, unmediated perception of reality.

**Immediate Response to Afflictions: Awareness and Observation**

When afflictions arise, practitioners must immediately recognize and observe their emergence and dissolution. This requires constant vigilance and the application of mindfulness and wisdom. Delayed or passive responses allow afflictions to take root. Prompt observation prevents their proliferation and enables their neutralization, preserving the mind’s purity and stability.

**Ultimate Goal: Complete Eradication of the Three Poisons**

The final aim is the total elimination of greed, hatred, and delusion — the three poisons that sustain suffering and rebirth. This is achieved by dismantling attachment to self and phenomena through sustained insight and meditative stability. Without wisdom to observe sensory experiences, practitioners remain vulnerable to these afflictions. Only through persistent application of right mindfulness and wisdom can these poisons be uprooted, leading to a state of enduring peace, freedom, and liberation.
