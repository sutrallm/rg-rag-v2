**Title: The Nature of Ultimate Peace in Spiritual Practice**

**Summary:**  
Ultimate peace is the final state achieved through spiritual practice, characterized by the mind’s return to its original purity and stillness. This state is marked by luminous clarity and the complete cessation of mental afflictions and attachments. It is not attained through further cultivation or verification but is realized as an inherent, complete condition.

**Mind Returns to Original Purity**  
The ultimate state reached by practitioners is one in which the mind reverts to its innate, unblemished condition. This return is not an external achievement but the uncovering of an already-present, tranquil essence. The cessation of mental disturbances and clinging is the necessary condition for this restoration.

**Silent Luminosity as the Manifestation of Ultimate Peace**  
The defining quality of ultimate peace is silent luminosity. This is not a physical light but a metaphor for the mind’s clarity and stillness after all agitation has ceased. It signifies the full expression of the mind’s original purity, free from distortion or obstruction.

**Liberation and Ultimate Peace Are Identical**  
Liberation is not a separate outcome but is synonymous with ultimate peace. It is the final destination after the complete removal of afflictions and attachments. In this state, there is no need for further practice or confirmation — it is a self-evident, fully realized condition.

**Luminosity Reflects Pure Awareness**  
The presence of luminosity in ultimate peace indicates the mind’s purified capacity for clear awareness. It is not an added quality but the natural radiance of the mind when freed from obscurations. This luminosity is inseparable from the mind’s inherent purity and serves as its visible sign.
