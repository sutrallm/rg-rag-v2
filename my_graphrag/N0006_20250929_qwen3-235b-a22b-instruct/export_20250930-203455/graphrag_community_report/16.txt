**Title: Addressing Doubt in Meditation: Methods from Dharma Reflection and Guidance**

**Summary:**  
Doubt arises as an obstacle during meditation, specifically manifesting as uncertainty about the teachings. This can be effectively countered through two primary means: reflecting deeply on the meaning of the teachings and seeking proximity to wise mentors. Reflection on the teachings enables the practitioner to resolve uncertainties through reasoned understanding.

**Doubt as a Meditation Obstacle**  
Doubt emerges during meditation as a specific hindrance rooted in uncertainty regarding the teachings. It disrupts focus and undermines confidence in the practice, indicating that clarity about the teachings is essential for sustained meditation.

**Resolution Through Reflection on Teachings**  
Engaging in deep contemplation of the teachings serves as a direct remedy for doubt. By systematically examining the meaning and logic of the teachings, practitioners can dissolve uncertainties and strengthen their conviction, thereby removing a key barrier to meditative progress.

**Role of Association with Wise Mentors**  
Proximity to individuals who possess deep understanding of the teachings provides another effective counter to doubt. Such association offers guidance, clarification, and experiential validation, helping practitioners navigate uncertainty through trusted examples and direct instruction.
