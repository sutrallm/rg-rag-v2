**Title: The Path to Liberation: How Practitioners Attain Nirvana Through Structured Practice**

**Summary**  
Practitioners pursue liberation by systematically applying the threefold training of ethical conduct, meditative concentration, and wisdom. This structured progression enables the complete eradication of greed, hatred, delusion, and self-attachment, leading to a state of unconditioned peace, freedom from suffering, and unshakable mental stability. The practice is grounded in the Four Noble Truths and sustained by daily application of the threefold training, with emphasis on non-attachment and direct experiential realization.

---

**Structured Progression Enables Complete Liberation**  
Practitioners follow a fixed sequence: ethical conduct first, then meditative concentration, and finally wisdom. This sequence is non-negotiable; skipping stages leads to imbalance or regression. Each stage builds upon the previous one — ethical conduct stabilizes the mind, concentration deepens mental clarity, and wisdom dissolves ignorance and clinging. Only through this sequential, integrated practice can practitioners fully sever the roots of suffering and attain liberation.

**Liberation and Nirvana Are Identical Ultimate States**  
The ultimate goal of practice is Nirvana — a state of complete freedom from suffering, self-illusion, and cyclic existence. It is characterized by the total cessation of greed, hatred, delusion, and all forms of attachment. Practitioners achieve this by eliminating the causes of suffering through disciplined practice, resulting in a mind that remains unmoved by external conditions or internal impulses. Nirvana is not a distant ideal but the direct outcome of sustained, correct practice.

**The Four Noble Truths Provide the Foundational Framework**  
All practice is oriented toward understanding and realizing the Four Noble Truths: the reality of suffering, its origin, its cessation, and the path leading to its cessation. Practitioners must directly perceive these truths to break the cycle of rebirth. This framework guides every aspect of practice, ensuring that efforts are aligned with the root causes and solutions to suffering.

**Daily Practice Must Integrate the Threefold Training**  
The threefold training — ethical conduct, concentration, and wisdom — must be applied consistently in daily life. Each component supports the others: ethical conduct creates the conditions for concentration, concentration enables the arising of wisdom, and wisdom leads to liberation. Practitioners must treat this triad as inseparable pillars of their practice, ensuring that each day includes cultivation in all three areas.

**Non-Attachment Is Essential to Realizing Ultimate Freedom**  
True liberation requires complete non-attachment — not only to external conditions but also to internal states, spiritual experiences, and even the concept of liberation itself. Practitioners cultivate a mind like empty space — ungrasping, unobstructed, and free from clinging to any phenomenon. This non-attachment, grounded in the direct realization of non-self, allows the mind to rest naturally in its original purity and freedom.
