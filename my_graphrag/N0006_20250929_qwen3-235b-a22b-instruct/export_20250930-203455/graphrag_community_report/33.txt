**Title: The Path to Ultimate Liberation Through Non-Attachment in Meditation**

**Summary:**  
Meditative achievements serve as milestones on the path to liberation, but attachment to these milestones obstructs final release. True progress is marked by the absence of clinging to outcomes, the absence of seeking states, and the absence of dwelling in any attained condition. Only through sustained non-attachment can one reach ultimate liberation.

**Non-Attachment to Achievements Enables Liberation**  
Meditative accomplishments are interim stages that lead toward liberation. However, fixation on these stages prevents progression to the final state. Liberation arises only when one does not cling to any attained result or condition.

**Absence of Acquisition as the Mark of True Achievement**  
The state of “no gain” — not clinging to any perceived accomplishment or level — is the true indicator of advancement. This non-possessive stance allows alignment with ultimate liberation, as attachment to results impedes the process.

**Absence of Seeking as the Key to Avoiding Attachment**  
The mindset of “no seeking” — refraining from pursuing specific states or outcomes — is essential to prevent attachment. This attitude removes the impulse to grasp at experiences, thereby facilitating the path to ultimate liberation.

**Non-Abiding, Non-Seeking, Non-Gaining as the Essence of True Meditation**  
The core of authentic meditative achievement lies in three conditions: non-abiding (not dwelling in any state), non-seeking (not pursuing outcomes), and non-gaining (not clinging to results). These conditions collectively ensure freedom from attachment, which is necessary to attain ultimate liberation.
