**Title: The Path to觉性 Manifestation: Practices, States, and Outcomes for Practitioners and圣者**

**Summary**  
The text outlines a unified framework through which practitioners and圣者 achieve wisdom and liberation. Central to this framework is the natural manifestation of觉性, which occurs when mental impurities are removed and attachment to sensory experiences is relinquished. Practices such as止观,正念,无常观, and无我观 serve as direct means to this end. The resulting state is one of clarity, stillness, and freedom from affliction — not a passive void, but an active, luminous awareness.圣者 exemplify the complete realization of this state, marked by unshakable peace, compassion, and mastery over karmic influence. External distractions and internal afflictions are identified as obstacles, but their illusory nature can be seen through wisdom, allowing觉性 to emerge spontaneously.

---

**Practices Leading to觉性 Manifestation**

Multiple methods are described as effective in removing mental obscurations and allowing觉性 to appear naturally. These include止观修习,正念,无常观, and无我观. Each method targets specific mental distortions — such as attachment to permanence or selfhood — and, when applied, results in the spontaneous emergence of觉性. The text emphasizes that these practices are not about creating something new, but uncovering what is already present within.

**The Role of Desire and Sensory Distraction**

Desire and sensory engagement are consistently identified as primary impediments to觉性. When practitioners withdraw from sensory cravings, they enter a state of离欲与寂静, which supports sustained awareness. The text clarifies that this is not suppression or numbness, but a dynamic, clear, and unobstructed awareness that remains undisturbed by external conditions. Sensory distractions are explicitly described as veils that must be seen through with wisdom to allow觉性 to shine.

**The Nature of觉性 in Liberation**

Liberation is defined as the direct experience of觉性 in the present moment — a state where afflictions temporarily cease and the mind is pure. This is not a future attainment, but an immediate, experiential reality. The text stresses that觉性 is not something to be acquired externally or postponed until after death; it is inherently present and becomes evident when conditions are right. Its manifestation is synonymous with clarity, stillness, and freedom.

**The State of圣者**

圣者 are portrayed as those in whom觉性 is fully and continuously manifest. This state arises from the complete eradication of afflictions and is marked by unwavering peace, compassion, and insight into karma. Their presence commands reverence from celestial beings, not because of external power, but due to their perfected wisdom and freedom from suffering. Their compassion is described as a natural expression of觉性, emerging effortlessly once self-centeredness is dissolved.

**Wisdom as the Tool Against Obstacles**

Wisdom is presented as the critical faculty that enables practitioners to see through the illusory nature of obstacles — whether they are external forces like死亡魔王 or internal patterns like sensory craving. By recognizing their insubstantiality, practitioners prevent these forces from obscuring觉性. This discernment is not intellectual, but experiential, arising from direct observation and mental stability.

**Karmic Influence and Behavioral Guidance**

Once觉性 is manifest, practitioners gain deeper insight into the workings of karma. They are better able to direct their actions with正念, thereby generating wholesome causes. This implies a shift from reactive behavior to intentional, conscious action — a direct result of heightened awareness and reduced delusion.

**The Inherent Luminosity of Mind**

The text describes觉性 as an inner light that dispels ignorance. This luminosity is not metaphorical but experiential — it is the clarity and awareness that arises when mental obscurations are removed. It is not dependent on external conditions and is always present, though often obscured.

**The Quality of离欲寂静**

离欲寂静 is not a state of emptiness or inactivity, but one of vibrant clarity and unimpeded freedom. Practitioners in this state are not withdrawn from the world, but are no longer swayed by it. Their awareness remains stable and undisturbed, even amid external stimuli. This state is described as the natural outcome of sustained practice and mental purification.

**The Interdependence of Practice and Realization**

The text presents a clear progression: consistent practice leads to purification, which leads to觉性 manifestation, which in turn deepens wisdom and liberation. There is no separation between method and result — the practices themselves are the path to the realization of what is already inherent. The final stage — complete觉性 manifestation — is the culmination of this process, embodied by圣者 and accessible to all who follow the path with diligence.
