**Title: Key Obstacles to Overcome in the Stages of Spiritual Practice: Focus on Doubt, Self-View, and Wrong Rituals**

**Summary:**

Doubt, self-view, and attachment to incorrect rituals are identified as critical obstacles that must be eliminated during the progressive stages of spiritual practice. These mental hindrances impede the development of wisdom, weaken confidence and effort, and obstruct liberation. Overcoming them requires the cultivation of correct understanding and unwavering faith, particularly in the context of meditation and advancing through levels of realization.

---

**Doubt as a Fundamental Hindrance**

Doubt is consistently described as one of the five hindrances that obstruct meditation and spiritual progress. It undermines confidence and dilutes the energy required for sustained practice. Its removal is essential at specific stages of realization, as it directly impedes the growth of wisdom and the path to liberation.

**Self-View as a Core Mental Obstacle**

Self-view is identified as a fundamental mental affliction that must be severed during the stages of spiritual attainment. It is grouped with doubt and attachment to incorrect rituals as key barriers that prevent the practitioner from advancing toward unconditioned wisdom and ultimate freedom.

**Attachment to Incorrect Rituals as a Barrier to Liberation**

Attachment to wrong or misplaced rituals is recognized as a significant obstacle that must be abandoned during the progression of spiritual practice. Such attachment stems from misunderstanding and prevents the practitioner from attaining liberation, even when other conditions may appear favorable.

**Correct Understanding and Faith as Essential Tools**

The consistent emphasis on using correct understanding and faith to overcome doubt indicates that these qualities are not merely supportive but foundational. They serve as active antidotes to mental hindrances, particularly during meditation and at critical junctures of realization. Their cultivation is necessary to dismantle doubt and progress toward unconditioned wisdom.
