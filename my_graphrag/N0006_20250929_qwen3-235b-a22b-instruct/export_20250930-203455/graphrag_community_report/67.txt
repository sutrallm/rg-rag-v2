**Title: The Path to Liberation: How Practitioners Overcome Greed, Hatred, and Delusion**

**Summary**  
Practitioners aim to eliminate greed, hatred, and delusion—the root causes of suffering and cyclic existence. These mental afflictions stem from attachment to self and possessions and manifest as persistent psychological barriers. Through disciplined practice grounded in ethical conduct, mental focus, and wisdom—particularly via the Eightfold Path and meditative observation—practitioners cultivate awareness of impermanence, suffering, and non-self. This sustained inner work enables them to recognize, observe, and dissolve afflictions as they arise, ultimately achieving a state of mental purity, stability, and freedom from suffering.

**Root Cause of Afflictions Lies in Attachment to Self and Possessions**  
The mental disturbances practitioners seek to overcome originate from clinging to the notion of “I” and “mine.” This attachment gives rise to greed, hatred, and delusion, which are identified as the primary obstacles to spiritual progress. These afflictions are not superficial but deeply embedded, requiring systematic and persistent effort to dismantle.

**Methodology for Overcoming Afflictions Involves Ethical Discipline, Mental Cultivation, and Wisdom**  
Practitioners rely on a structured framework combining moral restraint, concentration, and insight. The Eightfold Path—comprising right view, intention, speech, action, livelihood, effort, mindfulness, and concentration—serves as the core methodology. Through consistent practice, they develop the capacity to observe the transient, unsatisfactory, and impersonal nature of all phenomena, thereby weakening the grip of afflictions.

**Immediate Awareness and Observation Are Essential When Afflictions Arise**  
A critical component of daily practice is vigilance over the mind. When afflictions surface, practitioners must instantly recognize and observe their arising and passing without attachment. This real-time application of mindfulness and wisdom prevents afflictions from taking root or escalating, preserving mental clarity and emotional stability.

**Ultimate Liberation Requires Complete Eradication of Greed, Hatred, and Delusion**  
The final aim is not temporary relief but total elimination of these three poisons. This is achieved through deepening insight into the nature of reality, which dissolves the illusions of self and permanence. As these afflictions are fully uprooted, the practitioner attains a state of unshakable peace, freedom from cyclic suffering, and unobstructed awareness.
