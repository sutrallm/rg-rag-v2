Title: The Unbroken Flow of Practice: Sustained Meditation as the Path to Liberation

Summary:  
Continuous meditation practice, likened to the ceaseless flow of the Ganges River, is essential for maintaining stable concentration and progressing toward ultimate liberation. Interruption in practice undermines this progression, making uninterrupted effort a non-negotiable requirement for spiritual advancement.

Insights:

Uninterrupted Practice Mirrors Natural Continuity  
The analogy of the Ganges River flowing day and night without pause illustrates that meditation must be maintained without break. Just as the river’s flow is constant and unyielding, so too must the practitioner’s effort remain steady and persistent to sustain the depth of concentration required for spiritual growth.

Stability in Meditation Depends on Consistency  
The text links the steadiness of meditation directly to the continuity of practice. Without sustained effort, the meditative state cannot be stabilized. This implies that sporadic or intermittent practice is insufficient to cultivate the mental clarity and focus necessary for deeper spiritual realization.

Liberation Requires Persistent Effort  
The path to ultimate liberation is described as contingent upon uninterrupted practice. This indicates that liberation is not achieved through isolated moments of insight or effort, but through the cumulative effect of persistent, daily engagement with meditation. The necessity of continuity is framed as foundational, not optional.
