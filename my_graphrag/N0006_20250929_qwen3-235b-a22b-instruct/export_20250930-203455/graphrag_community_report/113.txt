**Title: The Nature and Expression of Liberation in自在境界**

**Summary**  
The text describes a state of liberation characterized by inner freedom from the constraints of life, death, gain, and loss. This state is symbolized by emptiness, reflecting an unattached and unstained mind. Those who attain this state live in alignment with circumstances without clinging, embodying completeness through non-attachment.

**Inner Freedom Defined by Absence of Constraint**  
The liberated individual exists in a condition untouched by the dualities of life and death or gain and loss. This state is not achieved through external control but through an internal shift that renders such dualities irrelevant. The absence of disturbance by these forces indicates a fundamental transformation in perception and response.

**Emptiness as Symbol of Unstained Awareness**  
The metaphor of emptiness illustrates the inner condition of the liberated: unburdened, unattached, and free from clinging. It is not a void of absence but a fullness of presence uncolored by desire or aversion. This state resists being bound by the fluctuations of worldly outcomes, reflecting a stable and clear awareness.

**Living Through Alignment Without Attachment**  
The liberated live by responding to circumstances as they arise, without forcing outcomes or holding onto preferences. This way of life expresses a matured state of completeness — not through accumulation or achievement, but through the consistent release of attachment. Action arises naturally, unimpeded by internal resistance or external expectation.
