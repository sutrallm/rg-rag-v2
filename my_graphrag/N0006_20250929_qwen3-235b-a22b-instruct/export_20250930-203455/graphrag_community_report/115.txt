**Title: The Path to Natural Stillness: How the Dissolution of Greed, Hatred, and Delusion Leads to Unconditioned Peace**

**Summary:**  
When greed, hatred, and delusion cease, the mind naturally settles into a state of unconditioned stillness without effort or striving. This state is marked by clarity, freedom from attachment, and the absence of artificial cultivation. The cultivation of mindfulness supports this process by anchoring awareness, preventing the mind from being pulled by external stimuli or sensory impulses. The ultimate condition achieved is one of effortless presence, untouched by gain or loss, and free from the need to attain or maintain any境界 (state).

---

**Natural Settling Follows the End of Afflictive States**  
When greed, hatred, and delusion are extinguished, the mind does not require deliberate suppression or pursuit to enter a state of stillness. The cessation of these afflictions allows the mind to rest naturally in its inherent clarity and unconditioned nature. This is not a result of force or technique, but an organic outcome of inner purification.

**Effortless Clarity as the Mark of Liberation**  
Liberation is expressed as a mind clear and untainted, like a mirror free from dust. This clarity emerges spontaneously when the three afflictions dissolve. It is characterized by the absence of striving, acquisition, or loss — a silent, luminous presence that requires no maintenance or validation.

**Mindfulness Sustains the Ground for Clarity**  
Mindfulness is the practice that enables the mind to remain steady, not carried away by external conditions or sensory distractions. Through consistent attention, the mind is gradually trained to resist habitual reactivity, creating the conditions necessary for the sustained emergence of clarity.

**Unconditioned Stillness Is the Fruit of Non-Attachment**  
The state of unconditioned stillness is not manufactured or achieved through effort. It arises only when there is no clinging to any state or experience. This stillness is timeless, unchanging, and beyond the realm of cause and effect. It is the natural expression of a mind unburdened by attachment.

**Cessation of Afflictions Enables Unconditioned Presence**  
The dissolution of greed, hatred, and delusion directly leads to the mind’s natural repose in unconditioned stillness. This repose is not passive but luminous and alert — a state of being that is free from the need to control, fix, or alter experience. It is the culmination of the path, realized not through striving, but through letting go.
