**Title: The Path of the Holy One: Liberation from the Rebirth Cycle through the Eradication of Delusion**

**Summary**  
The cycle of birth, death, and rebirth is sustained by delusion—the fundamental misperception of reality as permanent, satisfying, and self-existing. This delusion gives rise to craving, attachment, and aversion, which together bind beings to suffering. Liberation is achieved only through the complete eradication of delusion, greed, and hatred, a state realized by the Holy One or Sheng Zhe. This liberation is not passive but requires continuous, mindful effort to cultivate mental clarity and wisdom. Only through unwavering practice can one dismantle the illusion of self, transcend clinging, and attain freedom from rebirth.

---

**Delusion is the Root Cause of Suffering and Cyclic Existence**  
Delusion, or ignorance, is the mistaken belief in a permanent, independent self and in the enduring nature of phenomena. This fundamental misperception leads to clinging to impermanent things as if they were real and lasting, generating attachment and aversion. It distorts perception of reality, making beings unable to see things as they truly are, and is the origin of all unwholesome mental states. Without delusion, craving and hatred would not arise, and the cycle of rebirth would cease.

**Liberation is the Complete Extinction of Delusion**  
The state of nirvana is defined not as a place or condition, but as the absence of delusion. When the misperception of permanence and selfhood is fully uprooted, suffering ends. The Holy One has liberated themselves by realizing that the self is an illusion—an understanding achieved not through theory but through direct observation of non-self. This eradication is total and irreversible, marking the definitive end of rebirth.

**The Holy One Has Eliminated All Three Root Defilements**  
The Sheng Zhe, or Holy One, has entirely eradicated greed, hatred, and delusion—the three defilements that corrupt the mind and sustain bondage. Their freedom is not partial; it includes the absence of ego-clinging even to enlightenment itself. This total purification results in unshakable inner peace, freedom from dualistic thinking, and reverence even from celestial beings, as they no longer respond to worldly conditions with attachment or aversion.

**Mental Clarity Through Chanding is Essential for Liberation**  
Chanding, when combined with wisdom, enables the practitioner to see through the illusions that sustain rebirth. It provides the mental discipline required to recognize the false nature of self and permanence. The Holy One possesses perfected chanding, which grants them stability and clarity. Without sustained, continuous practice, chanding weakens, and the mind succumbs to dullness or distraction, allowing delusion to reassert itself.

**Continuous Effort Prevents Spiritual Regression**  
Spiritual progress is not maintained passively. Only through unwavering, mindful exertion can ignorance and craving be broken. Laziness, mental dullness, and distraction are ever-present threats. Without constant diligence, even hard-won clarity fades, and the patterns of rebirth reemerge. Liberation is not a destination reached once, but a state preserved only through perpetual vigilance.

**Ego-Clinging Anchors Beings in the Rebirth Cycle**  
The primal force trapping beings in cyclic existence is not merely ignorance, but the deep-seated clinging to the notion of a real, enduring self. All attachment, aversion, and craving stem from this core misconception. To be free is to release this attachment—not just to material things, but to spiritual attainments themselves. The Sheng Zhe embodies this supreme non-clinging, having seen through the illusion completely.
