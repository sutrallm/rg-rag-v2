**Title: The Eradication of Fannao and the Path to Liberation Through Wisdom and Mindfulness**

**Summary**  
Mental afflictions (fannao), rooted in ignorance (wuwang) and the false belief in a permanent self, are the sole source of suffering and cyclic existence. These disturbances—primarily manifesting as the three poisons (greed, hatred, delusion)—are sustained by craving and misperception of the five aggregates as a real self. Liberation is achieved only through the systematic cultivation of wisdom and continuous mindfulness, which dissolve the illusions underpinning afflictions. Those who have fully uprooted these disturbances, known as shengzhe, have extinguished all mental defilements and transcended rebirth.

---

**The Three Poisons Are the Primary Manifestations of Afflictions**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion form the core expressions of fannao and serve as the direct causal sources of all mental disturbances. These three poison-like states arise from the same root—ignorance—and collectively fuel attachment, aversion, and confusion, thereby binding sentient beings to samsara. Their presence defines the unliberated condition; their complete absence marks the state of shengzhe.

**Ignorance Is the Foundational Cause of All Afflictions**  
Wuwang, or ignorance of the true nature of reality—particularly the emptiness of self and phenomena—is the origin of every mental disturbance. This ignorance manifests as the mistaken belief that the five aggregates constitute a permanent, independent identity, which in turn generates craving and aversion. Without this foundational misunderstanding, no grasping, no suffering, and no rebirth would occur.

**Wisdom Is the Sole Means to Uproot Afflictions**  
Afflictions cannot be silenced by force, suppression, or external adjustment. They are dissolved only through wisdom (zhihui/chengzhi), which reveals the impermanent, selfless, and illusory nature of all experiences—especially pleasure, ego, and permanence. This direct perception dismantles the basis of clinging, causing greed, hatred, and delusion to lose their power and vanish.

**Continuous Mindfulness Prevents and Thins Afflictions**  
Zhengnian, or unwavering present-moment awareness, acts as a safeguard against the arising of afflictions. When mindfulness is maintained, the mind remains anchored in reality, preventing the reinforcement of habitual patterns of craving and aversion. Over time, this vigilant awareness weakens the hold of fannao by depriving them of the distraction and forgetfulness that allow them to flourish.

**Liberation Equals Complete Extinction of Afflictions**  
The state of jiede, or liberation, is not a passive condition but the total cessation of all mental disturbances. It is defined solely by the eradication of fannao, particularly the three poisons. This final freedom is achieved only when ignorance is fully overcome through wisdom, resulting in the irreversible stillness and clarity of the shengzhe, who are no longer subject to rebirth or emotional turmoil.

**The Illusion of Self Is the Structural Basis of Suffering**  
The mistaken perception that the five aggregates constitute a real, enduring self (wuwo) is the mechanism through which ignorance gives rise to afflictions. This illusion generates attachment (“mine”) and resistance (“not mine”), which manifest as craving and aversion. Seeing through this illusion—not through intellectual understanding but through meditative insight—is what finally breaks the chain of samsaric existence.
