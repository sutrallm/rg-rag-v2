**Title: The Path of Non-Attachment Through Five Aggregates and Fourfold Mindfulness**

**Summary:**  
Sustained observation of the five aggregates—form, sensation, perception, mental formations, and consciousness—as impermanent, unsatisfactory, and without inherent selfhood dismantles the illusion of a fixed identity. This practice, supported by fourfold mindfulness and mental stability, cultivates non-attachment not only to worldly experiences but also to spiritual attainments such as purity, liberation, or psychic powers. The dissolution of ego-clinging through this direct observation is the essential condition for liberation from cyclic existence.

---

**Observation of the Five Aggregates Reveals the Absence of Self**  
The five aggregates—form, sensation, perception, mental formations, and consciousness—are the fundamental components of human experience. Each is transient and conditioned. When observed systematically, none are found to constitute a permanent, independent self. This recognition dismantles the belief in a fixed identity and erodes the foundation of ego-clinging, which is the root of suffering.

**Fourfold Mindfulness Structures the Practice of Non-Self Observation**  
Mindful attention to the body as impure, feelings as suffering, mind as impermanent, and phenomena as non-self provides a comprehensive framework for witnessing the nature of the aggregates. This integrated approach ensures that awareness is applied uniformly across all dimensions of experience, making it possible to perceive impermanence and selflessness consistently and deeply.

**Mental Stability Enables the Emergence of Liberating Wisdom**  
Chanding fosters the mental clarity and stillness necessary to see phenomena as they truly are—impermanent and selfless. Without this steadiness, the mind remains too scattered to penetrate the illusion of self. Stability and observation work together: one sustains the other, allowing wisdom to arise not as abstract thought but as direct perception.

**Non-Attachment to Spiritual Attainments Is Essential for Liberation**  
Even states such as purity, liberation, or psychic powers are recognized as conditioned and transient. Clinging to them, however subtle, perpetuates craving. True freedom arises only when no phenomenon—including spiritual milestones—is clung to. This radical non-attachment is not indifference, but the final release from all forms of grasping.

**Persistent Practice Sustained by Jingjin Leads to the Dissolution of Delusion**  
The enduring effort known as jingjin is required to maintain continuous observation of the aggregates. This persistence gradually wears away the deeply held delusion of a permanent self. Without sustained vigilance, the illusion of self reasserts itself. Liberation is not achieved in a single moment but through unwavering, repeated application of observation.

**Wisdom Beyond Attachment Arises Naturally from Seeing No-Self**  
The clear, direct perception that the five aggregates do not form a self causes clinging to collapse. This is not a forced letting go, but a natural consequence of understanding. When the very basis of selfhood is seen as empty, attachment to anything tied to it—including spiritual goals—ceases spontaneously, opening the way to nirvana.
