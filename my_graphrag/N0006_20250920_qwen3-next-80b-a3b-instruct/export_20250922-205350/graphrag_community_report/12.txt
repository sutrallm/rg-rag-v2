**Title: Shengzhe and the Path to Nirvana: Liberation Through the Threefold Training**

**Summary:**  
Shengzhe are fully liberated beings who have eradicated the three poisons—greed, hatred, and delusion—through the perfected integration of moral discipline, meditative stability, and wisdom. Their state is marked by complete non-attachment, the abolition of self-identification, and unshakable inner stillness. Liberation, or Nirvana, is not an abstract goal but the present reality of Shengzhe, sustained by unwavering diligence, constant mindfulness, and the guarding of the six sense bases. Their conduct is spontaneous, free from striving, and universally revered, not through power or external display, but through the radiance of inner purity.

---

**Perfected Moral Discipline as the Foundation**  
Shengzhe embody flawless ethical conduct, not as rigid rule-following but as the natural expression of inner purity. Their moral discipline is the bedrock upon which concentration and wisdom are built. Without this foundation, mental disturbances arise, and progress toward liberation is impossible. Their actions are unimpaired by desire, aversion, or self-concern, reflecting a life aligned with truth rather than external expectations.

**Unbroken Meditative Stability as Natural Condition**  
The mind of Shengzhe is permanently settled in profound stillness—Chanding. This is not a temporary meditative state achieved during retreats, but the continuous, unshakable condition of their being. It is sustained only through persistent effort (Jijin) and is inseparable from their moral conduct. Their calm exists undisturbed by pleasure, pain, praise, or blame, serving as the stable ground for clear insight.

**Direct Wisdom That Sees Through Illusion**  
Shengzhe possess penetrating wisdom that perceives all phenomena as impermanent, empty of self, and conditioned by causes. This wisdom—Zhihui—is not intellectual but experiential, directly uprooting the misconceptions that bind beings to suffering. They see the five aggregates as transient, the six sense bases as sources of potential attachment, and the notion of “I” as a fabrication. This understanding frees them from clinging, even to the idea of enlightenment itself.

**Complete Cessation of the Three Poisons**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion are not merely suppressed but wholly extinguished in Shengzhe. These three afflictions are identified as the root causes of suffering and the cycle of rebirth. Their eradication is not a partial victory but the defining mark of liberation. Without them, craving, aversion, and ignorance cease to arise, and the mind no longer generates the conditions for future rebirth.

**Non-Attachment to All States and Attainments**  
Shengzhe do not cling to meditative bliss, spiritual achievements, or even the concept of being “liberated.” Their freedom includes relinquishing all forms of possession, including attachment to the state of Nirvana. This utter non-clinging—Wu Zhu—is the final expression of their realization. They neither seek nor guard liberation; they simply dwell in it, effortlessly and without distinction.

**Mindfulness as Continuous Practice**  
Awakened conduct is not confined to formal meditation. Shengzhe maintain unwavering mindfulness—Zhengnian—in every moment, guarding the six sense bases to prevent craving and aversion from taking root. This constant awareness prevents defilements from arising, ensuring that even in ordinary activities, the mind remains undisturbed and unattached.

**Liberation as Present Reality, Not Future Goal**  
Nirvana is not a distant destination but the lived experience of Shengzhe. It is the natural outcome of extinguishing the three poisons, the cessation of clinging, and the realization of non-self. To attain it is not to reach a new state, but to reveal what has always been: peace beyond striving, freedom beyond attainment, and stillness beyond condition.

**Death as the Culmination of Practice**  
The state of mind at the moment of death is not accidental but the fruit of lifelong cultivation. Shengzhe die with a mind free from greed, hatred, and delusion, ensuring liberation continues unbroken. Their final moment reflects the purity and stability cultivated daily—proof that awakening is not a momentary experience, but an irreversible transformation.

**Compassion as Spontaneous Expression**  
Loving-kindness and equal-mindedness arise naturally in Shengzhe, flowing from wisdom, not emotional impulse. This compassion is boundless and undiscriminating, extending to all beings without preference or effort. It is not a practice to be undertaken, but the quiet radiance of a mind no longer confined by self.

**The Threefold Training as Unified Path**  
Moral discipline, meditative stability, and wisdom are not separate stages but inseparable dimensions of a single, perfected life. Shengzhe embody Jietinghui—not as a sequence of steps, but as a unified unity. One cannot be present without the others; each supports and reveals the depth of the whole. Only through their perfect integration can liberation be fully realized.
