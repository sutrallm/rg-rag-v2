**Title: The Enlightened Sage and the Liberation from the Three Realms**

**Summary:**  
The enlightened sage achieves complete freedom from the cyclical domains of existence by eradicating the three root causes of suffering—greed, hatred, and delusion. Through unwavering moral discipline, constant mindfulness, and deep realization of reality, they transcend all conditioned states and are no longer subject to the cycles of birth and rebirth. This state of liberation is recognized and honored by celestial beings.

**Moral Discipline as Foundation**  
Perfect moral discipline is not an external practice but an intrinsic quality of the enlightened sage. It forms the essential groundwork upon which all other qualities of liberation are built, ensuring no action arises from craving or aversion.

**Constant Mindfulness Sustains Inner Stillness**  
The sage maintains unbroken inner stillness and focus, allowing no lapse into distraction or emotional reactivity. This persistent mindfulness prevents the re-emergence of destructive mental states and supports the steadiness of their enlightened condition.

**Wisdom Realizes True Nature**  
True understanding of reality is fully developed in the sage. They perceive all phenomena as they are—impermanent, unsatisfactory, and without inherent self—freeing them from misperception and attachment.

**The Three Poisons Are Extinguished**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion no longer arise within the sage. Their complete absence is not suppression but eradication through wisdom and mindfulness, removing the fuel that binds beings to cyclic existence.

**Transcendence of the Three Realms**  
The sage is no longer subject to the desire realm, form realm, or formless realm. Liberation is not merely a change in condition but a complete departure from the entire framework of samsaric existence, rendering celestial reverence a natural response to their perfected state.
