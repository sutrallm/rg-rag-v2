**Title: The Role of Wisdom in Guiding Meditative Practice**

**Summary:**  
Correct understanding of impermanence and non-self must come before deep meditative absorption to avoid fixation on emptiness or spiritual attachment. Balanced development of concentration and wisdom is necessary to penetrate illusions and realize ultimate truth. Wisdom directs meditation, ensuring it does not devolve into passive stillness or attachment to experiences. The practical application of this principle requires wisdom to lead and shape concentration.

**Wisdom Must Precede Concentration**  
Without prior understanding of impermanence and non-self, meditative absorption risks becoming a fixation on emptiness or an attachment to spiritual attainments. This premature focus on states of stillness, unguided by insight, leads practitioners away from true realization.

**Balance Is Essential for Authentic Realization**  
Meditative absorption and insight must be cultivated together. One-sided development—whether excessive concentration without wisdom or wisdom without sustained absorption—fails to dismantle illusions or lead to the realization of ultimate truth.

**Wisdom Directs the Quality of Meditation**  
The presence of proper wisdom ensures that meditation remains an active process of discernment rather than slipping into passive emptiness or becoming a comfort zone rooted in spiritual experiences. Without this guidance, the practice loses its transformative purpose.

**Practical Application Requires Wisdom to Lead**  
The integration of concentration and wisdom is not theoretical; it must be enacted by allowing wisdom to determine the direction and focus of meditative practice. Concentration alone, unguided, cannot serve as the foundation for authentic realization.
