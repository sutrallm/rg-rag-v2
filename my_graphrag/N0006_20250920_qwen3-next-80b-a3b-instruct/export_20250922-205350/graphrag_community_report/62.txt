**Title: Shengmian, Sinianchu, and Richang Gongke: The Integrated Path to Liberation**

**Summary**  
Continuous, non-reactive awareness — Shengmian — is the foundational skill that enables liberation when applied systematically through Sinianchu and embedded into daily life via Richang Gongke. Sinianchu structures this awareness across four domains — body, feelings, mind, and phenomena — to reveal impermanence, suffering, impersonality, and non-self. Richang Gongke transforms these teachings into practical, uninterrupted practice during ordinary activities, ensuring mindfulness is not isolated to formal meditation but becomes the fabric of everyday experience. Together, they form a unified, actionable path to dissolve ignorance and attachment.

**Shengmian is the Essential Skill for Liberation**  
The core capability required to dissolve ignorance and attachment is continuous, non-reactive awareness of the present moment. Without this sustained attention, distraction and internal agitation persist, preventing progress. This awareness is not passive observation but an active, consistent engagement with experience as it arises.

**Sinianchu Provides the Formal Framework for Non-Self Perception**  
Sinianchu translates abstract understanding into structured practice by directing attention to four specific areas: the body as impermanent, feelings as suffering, the mind as impersonal, and phenomena as non-self. It is the only systematic method described for making the realization of non-self accessible and actionable, turning theoretical insight into direct, experiential understanding.

**Richang Gongke Makes Mindfulness Inseparable from Daily Life**  
Mindfulness is fully realized not in isolation but through integration into routine actions — walking, eating, breathing, and emotional response. This daily application is both the foundation and the culmination of the path, ensuring that awareness does not remain confined to meditation sessions but becomes the natural mode of being throughout the day.

**Sinianchu and Richang Gongke Are Structurally Interdependent**  
Sinianchu supplies the doctrinal structure — the four domains of observation — while Richang Gongke is the practical manifestation of that structure in real-time living. The systematic framework of Sinianchu only becomes effective when enacted continuously through Richang Gongke, and Richang Gongke has no meaningful form without the guiding dimensions provided by Sinianchu. Their union ensures that mindfulness is both intentional and pervasive.
