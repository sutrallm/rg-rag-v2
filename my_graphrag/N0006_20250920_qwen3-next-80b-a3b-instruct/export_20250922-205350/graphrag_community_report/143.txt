**Title: The Nature of Enlightenment: Eradication of Greed, Hatred, and Delusion**

**Summary:**  
Enlightenment is defined by the complete removal of greed, hatred, and delusion—the three root afflictions that bind beings to cyclic existence and suffering. Those who attain this state, whether described as an enlightened being, a saint, or a holy one, share the same defining qualities: unshakable peace, perfect clarity, and freedom from all dualities. Liberation is not achieved through suppression but through direct insight into the impermanent and non-self nature of experience, leading to non-attachment and equanimity. The cessation of greed and hatred is not optional; it is necessary for realizing nirvana and transcending the cycle of birth and death. Delusion, as the root source of both greed and hatred, is overcome only through direct realization of reality as it is.

**Greed is extinguished through non-attachment and insight into impermanence**  
Greed arises from clinging to pleasurable experiences, possessions, or states under the false belief of ownership and permanence. Its persistence binds individuals to suffering and samsara. It is not resolved by force or denial but by observing phenomena as they arise and pass, without craving or resistance. When greed is fully abandoned, the mind is no longer drawn to sensory or spiritual pleasures, and possessive desires cease entirely. This is a non-negotiable condition for liberation.

**Hatred is dissolved through equanimity and the end of aversion**  
Hatred manifests as anger, fear, or rejection in response to unpleasant experiences or beings. It disturbs inner peace, fuels suffering, and reinforces cyclic existence by creating resistance to reality. Its removal requires complete transcendence of aversion, not mere control. Saintly and enlightened beings remain undisturbed in all conditions, no longer reacting with hostility or fear, because they perceive all phenomena without distortion.

**Delusion is the foundational cause of greed and hatred**  
Delusion—the misperception of impermanent, unsatisfactory, and non-self phenomena as desirable, lasting, or inherently threatening—is the root from which both greed and hatred grow. It causes beings to mistake fleeting sensations for things to be clung to or avoided. Overcoming delusion requires direct insight into the true nature of reality, leading to the collapse of both clinging and aversion. Without dispelling this fundamental ignorance, liberation is impossible.

**Enlightenment is characterized by unshakable peace and total freedom from mental defilements**  
The state of an enlightened being is defined not by external achievement but by the complete absence of the three root afflictions. This absence results in radiance, clarity, and stability of mind, untouched by conditioned experiences. Such a being abides in a state beyond dualities—neither attached nor averse—having realized non-self and the transient nature of all phenomena. This condition is the culmination of wisdom, moral discipline, and mindfulness, and is the sole marker that distinguishes the liberated from the bound.
