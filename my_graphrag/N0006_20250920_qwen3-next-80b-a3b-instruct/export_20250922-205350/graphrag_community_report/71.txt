**Title: Liusan, Wuchang, and the Path to Jiede: Core Teachings on Liberation**

**Summary:**  
The cycle of birth, death, and rebirth is sustained by greed, hatred, and delusion, which fuel clinging to transient phenomena. Liberation is achieved only through direct, sustained observation of impermanence in all physical and mental experiences, dissolving the illusion of a fixed self. Without active cultivation of mindfulness and wisdom, one remains bound in ignorance—metaphorically dead despite biological life. True freedom arises not from theory, but from continuous practice that sees all things as conditioned, fleeting, and devoid of inherent existence.

**Contemplation of impermanence dissolves the illusion of self**  
Direct, experiential observation of constant change in the five aggregates reveals that no stable, enduring entity can be found. This recognition dismantles the belief in a permanent self, which is the root of attachment and suffering. The practice of viewing all phenomena as transient aggregates is not abstract but must be cultivated moment by moment.

**Impermanence is the gateway to liberation**  
Recognizing that all conditioned phenomena—bodily forms, feelings, thoughts, and external conditions—are fleeting like dew or lightning undermines clinging. This clarity weakens the grip of greed and delusion, breaking the chain that binds beings to cyclic existence. Liberation is not attained through intellectual understanding alone, but through direct perception of this ever-changing nature.

**The three poisons bind beings to cyclic existence**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion are the driving forces behind karma and confusion, sustaining rebirth and suffering. These mental afflictions arise from misperceiving impermanent phenomena as stable and worth clinging to. Their complete eradication defines the awakened state and is the only condition under which the cycle of birth and death ends.

**Non-practice is spiritual death**  
To neglect the cultivation of mindfulness, wisdom, and ethical conduct is to remain enslaved by ignorance, even while physically alive. Authentic life—true immortality—is found only in diligent practice. Without it, one is functionally dead, trapped in the same patterns of delusion that perpetuate suffering.

**Sustained observation supports compassionate action**  
Continuous practice of non-self observation ensures that compassion does not stem from illusory attachments or emotional identification. When one clearly sees all phenomena as transient and selfless, compassion becomes unconditioned and grounded in reality, free from the distortions of ego.

**Wisdom arises precisely from seeing impermanence**  
The essential component of liberating wisdom is the direct perception of transience. This is not a conceptual belief but an experiential realization cultivated through sustained attention to the arising and passing of all phenomena. It is this vision that transforms the mind and severs the roots of samsaric bondage.
