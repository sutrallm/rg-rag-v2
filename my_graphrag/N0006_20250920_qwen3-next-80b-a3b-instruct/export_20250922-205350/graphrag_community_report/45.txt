**Title: Transcending Attachment and Heart Liberation in the Path of the Sages**

**Summary:**  
The path to ultimate mental freedom involves a systematic unraveling of clinging — from coarse sensual desires to subtle attachments to spiritual achievements. This process is not driven by suppression but by direct insight into the impermanent and unsatisfactory nature of all experiences. The sages, having fully extinguished greed, hatred, and delusion, embody a mind unshaken by any condition, free from even the notion of attainment. Transcending attachment is both the method and the condition for heart liberation, which is the lived expression of non-attachment.

**Transcending Attachment as the Path to Freedom**  
True freedom arises when one no longer clings to pleasant meditative states, supernatural abilities, or spiritual results. This release is not forced but emerges through recognizing the impermanent and insubstantial nature of all phenomena. Even the idea of “non-attainment” must be let go of, as clinging to any concept — including spiritual ideals — binds the mind. This dismantling begins with observable cravings and deepens until even the most subtle attachments dissolve.

**Heart Liberation as the Fulfillment of Non-Attachment**  
Heart liberation is the complete cessation of greed, hatred, and delusion, resulting in a mind as open as space and as clear as still water. It is not an abstract goal but an experiential reality — the direct manifestation of non-attachment. One who has reached this state does not seek, hold, or resist any condition; the mind neither arises nor abides, having transcended all dualities.

**The Role of Calmness in Revealing Clinging**  
The stilling of mental turbulence through calming practices unveils the subtle attachments hidden within meditative experiences and spiritual accomplishments. Without this stability, one may mistake temporary states for final attainment. Calmness creates the conditions for seeing through these illusions, enabling the mind to release its grip without resistance.

**Sages as Embodiments of Complete Release**  
Those who have fully realized heart liberation are recognized as sages. They have eradicated all forms of clinging — to the self, to external phenomena, and even to the pursuit of enlightenment. Their freedom is not measured by achievement but by the absence of any attachment, including to the path itself.

**The Gradual Dissolution of Sensual Desire**  
Removing attachment to sensory pleasures is a foundational step in the journey toward final freedom. This is not accomplished by denial or force but through observing the inherent suffering and transience underlying worldly enjoyment. As insight deepens, even the most refined cravings quietly fall away, aligning the mind with the truth of impermanence.
