Title: The Interdependence of Moral Discipline and Wisdom in the圣者

Summary:  
The圣者 achieves liberation through the integrated development of moral discipline and wisdom. Moral discipline establishes the foundation for mental stability, which in turn enables the arising of wisdom. Wisdom, once fully developed, eliminates afflictions. Together, these two qualities form a mutually reinforcing system essential to the圣者’s attainment.

Moral Discipline as the Foundation  
The圣者 must possess complete moral discipline, which serves as the basis for pure conduct and spiritual practice. This discipline creates inner stability, allowing the mind to settle and become receptive to deeper understanding. Without this foundation, the cultivation of wisdom is undermined.

Wisdom as the Culmination  
The defining characteristic of the圣者 is the full development of wisdom, which directly leads to the complete eradication of afflictions and the realization of liberation. This wisdom does not arise in isolation but is dependent on the prior grounding provided by ethical conduct.

Mutual Reinforcement Between Discipline and Wisdom  
Moral discipline and wisdom are not sequential stages but co-dependent elements. Pure conduct supports the emergence of wisdom, while wisdom deepens the integrity and depth of moral practice. Their synergy is essential—neither can function fully without the other in the path of the圣者.
