**Title: Zheng Nian and Wu Min: The Path from Delusion to Clarity**

**Summary**  
The practice of Zheng Nian—mindful awareness of present-moment experiences—directly counteracts Wu Min, the deep-seated misperception of reality as permanent, self-contained, and satisfying. By cultivating this sustained attention, one builds the concentration necessary for profound mental clarity, gradually dissolving the root cause of suffering.

**Cultivation of Awareness Dissolves Misconceptions**  
When an individual consistently observes bodily sensations, feelings, thoughts, and mental states without judgment, they begin to see phenomena as they truly are—impermanent, interdependent, and devoid of fixed self. This continuous observation undermines the false beliefs that underlie suffering, replacing distortions with direct experience.

**Foundational Awareness Enables Deep Concentration**  
The stability of attention developed through Zheng Nian serves as the necessary groundwork for advanced meditative states. Without this clear, non-reactive awareness, sustained focus cannot be achieved, leaving the mind fragmented and easily misled by habitual patterns of thought.

**Delusion as the Source of Suffering**  
The belief in permanence, inherent selfhood, and lasting fulfillment in transient conditions is not merely a passive error—it actively produces dissatisfaction. This misapprehension obscures the nature of experience and prevents any true understanding of reality, keeping the individual trapped in cycles of craving and aversion.

**Mindfulness as the Direct Counter to Misinterpretation**  
Moment-to-moment awareness functions not as a technique to suppress thought, but as a lens that reveals the falseness of deluded perceptions. By naming and observing experience without interpretation, the mind no longer projects illusion onto reality, naturally weakening the grip of false beliefs.
