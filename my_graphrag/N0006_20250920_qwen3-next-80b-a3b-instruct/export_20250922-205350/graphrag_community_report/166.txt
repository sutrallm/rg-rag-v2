**Title: The Non-Abiding Mind, Inner Freedom, and Present-Moment Awareness in the Realization of Nirvana**

**Summary**  
Nirvana is established as the unconditioned, non-attached state of the non-abiding mind—free from clinging to existence, non-existence, gain, or loss. This state is not attained through external circumstances but through inner transformation, enabling freedom regardless of environment. Liberation is not a future goal but an immediate realization, accessed solely through continuous present-moment awareness, which is both the means and the expression of this freedom.

**The Non-Abiding Mind as the Nature of Nirvana**  
Nirvana is not a distant state or destination but the intrinsic quality of a mind that does not cling—neither to what is, nor to what is not. It operates without留下痕迹, moving like clouds or birds, untouched by attachment or aversion. This condition is described as ever-present and unconditioned, meaning it exists independently of change, circumstance, or temporal progression.

**Inner Freedom Arises from Non-Attachment**  
Liberation is not contingent on location, status, or external conditions—it emerges when the mind is transformed through detachment from gain, loss, and all dualistic preferences. Whether in solitude or in wealth, this freedom is experienced the same way: as a deep stability that neither seeks nor rejects its surroundings.

**Present-Moment Awareness Is the Path Itself**  
Liberation is never deferred to a future time or hidden in a sacred place. Each fully mindful instant is the complete realization of freedom—there is no step beyond the awareness of now. This awareness is not a technique for achieving something later, but the actuality of awakening unfolding in every breath, thought, and perception.
