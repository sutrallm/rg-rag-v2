**Title: Wu Zhuo and Wu Zheng Jie in the Path of Non-Attachment**

**Summary:**  
Wu Zhuo and Wu Zheng Jie describe a state of non-clinging rooted in direct perception of reality’s impermanent, suffering, and selfless nature. Wu Zhuo emerges from observing the five aggregates as not-self and dissolves attachment even to profound meditative states and the goal of awakening. Wu Zheng Jie is its practical application in spiritual practice, ensuring the mind remains untainted by desire for outcomes, including enlightenment. Those who embody Wu Zhuo, such as Sheng Zhe, transcend all forms of attachment, even to spiritual achievements.

**Wu Zhuo as Wisdom from Direct Observation**  
Wu Zhuo arises from seeing the impermanent, suffering, and selfless nature of all phenomena. It is cultivated through Wu Wo Guanzhao—observing the five aggregates as not-self—which naturally dissolves attachment. This wisdom enables one to see through the illusion of meditative experiences, including blissful states, and remain unattached to them. It is not merely the absence of desire, but a clarity that reveals the futility of clinging to any phenomenon, no matter how refined or spiritually significant.

**Wu Zheng Jie as the Practice of Non-Attachment to Results**  
Wu Zheng Jie is the specific manifestation of Wu Zhuo in spiritual practice. It involves engaging in practice without desire for results, even enlightenment. This attitude safeguards against spiritual pride and preserves the purity of the mind. By refusing to attach to the fruits of practice, the practitioner avoids subordinating the path to personal gain, ensuring that action remains aligned with truth rather than aspiration.

**Embodiment of Non-Attachment in Sheng Zhe**  
Sheng Zhe exemplify the culmination of Wu Zhuo—they are beyond all forms of attachment, including attachment to enlightenment itself. Their state demonstrates that true non-clinging extends to the highest spiritual goals, confirming that awakening is not an object to be possessed but a realization that transcends all conceptual endpoints.
