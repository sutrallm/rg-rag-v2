**Title: The Three Realms and the Liberation of the Enlightened**

**Summary**  
The Three Realms—Desire, Form, and Formless—are the conditioned structures of samsara where all unliberated beings experience recurring birth, aging, sickness, and death, driven by karma and the three root afflictions: greed, hatred, and delusion. Despite temporary comforts, these realms are inherently unstable and likened to a burning house. Liberation is achieved only through the complete extinction of these afflictions and the realization that the realms lack inherent existence. Those who attain this state transcend rebirth entirely, no longer bound by the mechanisms of karma or the illusion of a fixed self.

**The Three Realms Are a Cycle of Inherent Suffering**  
The Desire, Form, and Formless Realms collectively form the structural framework of samsara, where all beings are bound by karma and afflictions. Even the most elevated states within these realms—such as those enjoyed by heavenly beings—do not escape suffering, impermanence, or the inevitability of decay. These realms are not sanctuaries but are described as a house on fire, filled with danger and instability, emphasizing that no condition within them offers lasting safety or peace.

**Greed, Hatred, and Delusion Sustain the Cycle of Rebirth**  
The three root afflictions—greed, hatred, and delusion—are the primary forces that bind beings within the Three Realms. They are the driving mechanisms behind karmic action and rebirth, perpetuating the cycle of birth and death. These afflictions arise from ignorance and attachment and are the fundamental targets of spiritual practice; their complete eradication is the essential condition for breaking free from conditioned existence.

**Liberation Means Transcending the Three Realms Entirely**  
True freedom is defined as complete departure from the Three Realms, not merely improvement within them. Those who attain liberation—known as the enlightened or圣者—have severed the roots of greed, hatred, and delusion, thereby dissolving the foundation of the illusion of a fixed self. This realization reveals the empty, dependent nature of the realms, leading to the cessation of attachment and fear toward their outcomes. As a result, the liberated are no longer subject to the laws of karma or rebirth and are established beyond the scope of samsaric cycles.
