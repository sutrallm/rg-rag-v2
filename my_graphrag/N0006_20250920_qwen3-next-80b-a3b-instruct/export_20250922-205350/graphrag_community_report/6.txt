**Title: Jietao, Buxiubuzheng, and the Path to Inner Transformation**

**Summary:**  
Liberation arises not through external means but through direct understanding of suffering, impermanence, and non-self. True practice involves letting go of all striving—neither seeking attainment nor avoiding pain. Attachment, particularly to self and experience, is the core barrier to freedom. Inner transformation occurs through continuous awareness and release of greed, anger, and delusion. Liberation is complete only when all three elements of Jiedinghui are fulfilled and practiced without clinging to outcomes.

**Liberation Requires Seeing Reality Without Distortion**  
True freedom emerges only when one directly perceives the nature of suffering, impermanence, and the absence of a fixed self. This perception dismantles ignorance, the root of the cycle of rebirth, and cannot be induced by rituals, divine aid, or external forces. It is an internal recognition that transforms the foundation of experience.

**Practicing Without Goal or Aversion Is the Mature Expression of Wisdom**  
The highest form of practice involves no desire to attain liberation or to escape suffering. It is the放下 (letting go) of dualistic effort—neither grasping at meditative states nor rejecting painful experiences. This non-striving approach reflects a mind that sees reality precisely as it is, unaltered by desire or aversion.

**Attachment Is the Obstacle to Freedom**  
All forms of clinging—whether to pleasure, states of consciousness, identity, or even the idea of spiritual progress—sustain suffering. Liberation is only possible when these attachments, especially those centered on the notion of self, are fully relinquished. No practice yields results as long as clinging persists.

**Inner Change Occurs Through Mindful Letting Go, Not Ritual**  
Genuine transformation happens internally: greed fades, anger ceases, and delusion dissolves not through ceremonial acts but through moment-by-moment awareness and the continuous release of attachment. This process is the medium through which liberation becomes tangible in daily life.

**Liberation Is Complete Only Through the Unified Practice of Jiedinghui**  
The full path to freedom requires the synchronized development of ethical discipline, meditative concentration, and wisdom. Each element is essential, and none suffices alone. Liberation is not an achievement of one aspect, but the balanced fulfillment of all three together.
