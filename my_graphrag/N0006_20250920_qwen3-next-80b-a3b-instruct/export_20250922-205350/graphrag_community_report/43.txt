**Title: Shenshe and the Transcendence of Niuling**

**Summary:**  
Shenshe are individuals who have fully overcome the three root afflictions—greed, hatred, and delusion—through unwavering moral discipline, meditative stillness, and relentless vigilance. Their state is defined by inner clarity, serenity, and freedom from the forces that bind ordinary beings to cyclic existence. This transformation is not partial but absolute, resulting in a life entirely unshaken by the drives they have severed.

**Shenshe Are Defined by Absolute Mastery of Chanding**  
Shenshe live perpetually in meditative stillness and inner clarity, indicating that their state of being is not transient but continuous. This mastery of Chanding is not merely a practice but the defining condition of their existence, enabling them to remain undisturbed by external or internal disturbances.

**Shenshe Uphold Jingjin as a Constant Practice**  
They embody Jingjin—a state of relentless vigilance—not as occasional effort but as an unbroken commitment to preserving moral and mental purity. This unwavering attention sustains their freedom from corruption and ensures that their inner state remains untouched by base impulses.

**Shenshe Have Severed the Roots of Niuling Completely**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion are not merely suppressed but entirely eradicated in Shenshe. These three afflictions no longer hold any power over them, meaning their actions, thoughts, and responses arise from a foundation untouched by the forces that propel suffering in others.

**Shenshe Are Free from the Drives That Bind Sentient Beings**  
Having removed the roots of Niuling, Shenshe no longer move under the influence of the same impulses that drive sentient beings through cyclic existence. Their serenity and wisdom are not the result of temporary calm but of irreversible liberation from the causes of suffering.
