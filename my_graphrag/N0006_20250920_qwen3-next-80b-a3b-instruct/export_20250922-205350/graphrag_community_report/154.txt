**The Three Trainings: Precepts, Concentration, and Wisdom as a Unified Path to Liberation**

**Summary**  
Ethical discipline, meditative concentration, and wisdom constitute an interdependent sequence leading to liberation. Ethical discipline purifies action and serves as the necessary groundwork for meditative concentration, which in turn stabilizes and unites ethics and wisdom. Wisdom arises only after the prior two trainings are established, completing the path by transforming them into liberating understanding.

**Ethical Discipline Establishes the Foundation**  
Moral purity in bodily and verbal conduct is the essential starting point. Without this foundation, further development in concentration cannot take root or remain stable. The precepts are not optional but the initial and indispensable layer upon which the entire path is built.

**Meditative Concentration Unifies the Path**  
Chanding acts as the central pillar, binding ethical practice and wisdom into a single, cohesive process. It does not function in isolation but requires the prior cultivation of ethical discipline to be effective. Its role is integrative, ensuring that moral clarity and mental focus operate in harmony.

**Wisdom Is the Culminating Transformation**  
Wisdom does not emerge independently but is the result of sustained ethical discipline and meditative concentration. It is the final stage that transmutes the earlier practices into liberating understanding, implying that no level of wisdom can be attained without the preceding stages.
