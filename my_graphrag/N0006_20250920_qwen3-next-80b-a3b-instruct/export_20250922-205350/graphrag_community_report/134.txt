**Title: The Staged Sage and the Cessation of the Three Poisons**

**Summary**  
A staged sage, described as spiritually realized and ethically pure, exists in continuous meditative stillness as a natural result of having fully eliminated greed, hatred, and delusion. This eradication is not merely an attribute but the very condition that defines liberation, enlightenment, and nirvana. All elevated states—whether termed saintly, enlightened, or liberated—are uniformly characterized by the complete uprooting of these three mental afflictions, which are identified as the root cause of suffering and cyclic existence.

**Continuous Meditative Stillness Arises from the Purification of Mind**  
The staged sage’s unbroken tranquility is not imposed through effort but emerges naturally from a mind that has been thoroughly purified. This stillness, referred to as chanding, is not an external practice but an internal condition—the direct expression of a mind free from greed, hatred, and delusion. The absence of these afflictions allows for a stable, unshaken presence that requires no external cultivation.

**Complete Elimination of Greed, Hatred, and Delusion Defines Liberation**  
Every description of an enlightened, liberated, or saintly being converges on one indispensable criterion: the total eradication of the three poisons. Whether framed as nirvana, enlightenment, or spiritual realization, none of these states are achieved without the complete removal of greed, hatred, and delusion. These afflictions are not merely obstacles to be managed but fundamental roots to be uprooted, and their absence is the sole marker of transcendence.

**Perfect Wisdom Is Grounded in the Perception of Impermanence and Non-Self**  
The staged sage’s flawless understanding arises from seeing all phenomena as impermanent and devoid of inherent self. This perception is not abstract knowledge but the experiential foundation of their unattached state. It is this wisdom, rooted in direct insight into the nature of reality, that enables the irreversible dissolution of the three poisons and sustains their liberated condition.
