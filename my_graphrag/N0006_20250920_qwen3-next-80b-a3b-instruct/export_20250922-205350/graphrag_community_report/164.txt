**Title: The Path to Nirvana Through Mind Purification and Sustained Mindfulness**

**Summary:**  
Spiritual liberation is achieved exclusively through the eradication of greed, hatred, and delusion via continuous mindfulness and unwavering effort. The mind is the origin of all actions and their consequences; thus, purifying it halts the creation of new karmic imprints and exhausts existing ones. Without persistent awareness and diligence, the mind succumbs to distraction and regression, preventing progress toward the state of nirvana, which is synonymous with inner purity.

---

**Sustained Effort is Essential to Maintain Meditation Foundations**  
Without consistent and rigorous application, the foundation of chanding cannot be established or sustained. The mind naturally inclines toward slackness and distraction, making deliberate, ongoing effort indispensable to prevent spiritual regression and ensure steady progress.

**Mindfulness Enables Awareness of Intentions and Prevents Harmful Karma**  
Continuous, non-judgmental awareness of bodily and mental activities allows practitioners to observe the emergence of intentions. This awareness creates the space to choose wholesome actions, thereby preventing the generation of negative karma rooted in greed, hatred, or delusion.

**The Mind is the Sole Source of Karma and Liberation**  
All intentional actions—thoughts, speech, deeds—stem from the mind and produce corresponding consequences. Since the mind is the root of karma, its purification is the only means to cease generating new karmic imprints and ultimately attain liberation.

**Purity of Mind Equals Nirvana**  
The state of nirvana is not external or ritualistic but is defined entirely by the absence of greed, hatred, and delusion. This inner transformation, free from mental defilements, is both the condition and the result of enlightenment.

**Eradicating the Three Poisons is the Sole Path to Spiritual Freedom**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion are the fundamental afflictions binding beings to suffering and cyclic existence. Their complete removal is the only true path to spiritual purity; external practices or appearances hold no transformative power without this inner change.
