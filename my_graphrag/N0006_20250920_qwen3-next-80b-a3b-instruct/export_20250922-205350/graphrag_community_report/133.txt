**Title: Saintly Qualities and the Realization of Non-Self**

**Summary:**  
A being who embodies saintly qualities has transcended rebirth, karma, and inner afflictions through the complete internalization of the non-self view. This realization dismantles clinging by revealing the body, feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness as transient and impersonal. Moral discipline, meditative absorption, and wisdom form the foundational pillars sustaining this state. Equanimity and the cessation of new karmic actions arise directly from this deep understanding, which is sustained by continuous present-moment awareness.

**Moral Discipline, Meditation, and Wisdom as Foundational Pillars**  
The three essential qualities that define a saintly being are moral purity in body, speech, and mind; meditative absorption, which cultivates inner stillness and purity; and wisdom—the profound understanding of the emptiness of the five aggregates. These are not separate practices but interdependent foundations that together support the full manifestation of saintliness.

**Cessation of Karma and Equanimity Stem from Non-Self Realization**  
The eradication of greed, hatred, and delusion, along with the stopping of new karmic actions, occurs not through effort alone but as a natural consequence of fully seeing the body, feelings, perceptions, mental formations, and consciousness as not “I” or “mine.” This dissolution of clinging removes the root cause of suffering and eliminates the conditions that generate new karma, resulting in unshakable emotional equanimity.

**Liberation Requires Non-Self as a Lived Experience, Not a Concept**  
True freedom from the cycle of rebirth and all afflictions is only achieved when the non-self view is no longer a theoretical understanding but a constant, embodied perception. This transformation occurs through present-moment awareness, which continuously reveals the impermanent and impersonal nature of all experience, reinforcing the collapse of identity-based clinging.

**Saintly Qualities Are Manifestations of Transcendence**  
The observable traits of such a being—freedom from rebirth, inner peace, reverence from celestial beings, and alignment with truth—do not arise from external behaviors alone. They are the direct expression of having fully transcended the illusion of self, thereby ending the cycle of suffering and the drive to accumulate karma.
