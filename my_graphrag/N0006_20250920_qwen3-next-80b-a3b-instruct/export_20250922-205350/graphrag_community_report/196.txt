Title: 业力 and 善业 in the Practice of 正念

Summary:  
Intentional actions shape future consequences through the law of moral causation. Wholesome actions, performed with mindfulness and wisdom, actively purify past karmic imprints and reduce suffering. Mindfulness enables awareness of thoughts and actions as they arise, preventing the creation of harmful karma and facilitating transformation toward liberation.

Wholesome actions are not merely moral behavior but are intrinsically linked to the purification of past karmic patterns. This purification is sustained through continuous mindful awareness in daily life, creating a reciprocal relationship between ethical conduct and mental clarity. The process supports liberation by replacing harmful tendencies with beneficial ones.
