**Title: The Root of Suffering and Liberation in the Three Poisons**

**Summary**  
The fundamental cause of suffering and cyclic existence is ignorance—specifically, the mistaken belief in a permanent self and ownership. This ignorance, termed 痴 (no míng), is the origin of greed and anger, and underlies all attachment, aversion, and misperception of reality. Liberation arises solely from personally dismantling this ignorance through direct wisdom that perceives impermanence, non-self, and suffering as they truly are. The path to freedom does not depend on external salvation but on individual effort to eradicate the three poisons, with the destruction of ignorance being the decisive and final condition for release.

**Ignorance is the Origin of All Distress**  
The core error underlying suffering is the failure to recognize the true nature of existence: impermanence, absence of a fixed self, and the pervasiveness of dissatisfaction. This ignorance manifests as the illusion of permanence and ownership—"I" and "mine"—which fosters clinging to transient phenomena. Such misunderstanding is not merely a lapse in knowledge but the foundational error from which all other forms of distress branch.

**Attachment and Aversion Stem from Misperception of Reality**  
Greed and anger are not independent forces but direct consequences of this foundational ignorance. Clinging arises when one误认为无常为恒常, and anger emerges in resistance to anything that threatens the fabricated sense of self. Neither can be understood or resolved without addressing the root misperception that there exists a stable, independent identity to protect or possess.

**Liberation Requires Personal Dissolution of Ignorance**  
Freedom is not granted by an external agent but must be realized through one’s own discernment. The Buddha serves as a guide, not a savior; the dismantling of the chains of attachment must be undertaken individually. This requires cultivating a clear perception of reality—seeing all phenomena as conditioned, transient, and devoid of inherent self—thereby uprooting the delusion that fuels desire and aversion.

**Eradicating Ignorance is the Sole Condition for Release**  
All suffering and rebirth are sustained by this single root: ignorance. While greed and anger manifest as visible afflictions, they are symptoms, not causes. True liberation is attained only when ignorance is completely dissolved through wisdom that sees things as they are. No other action—no ritual, devotion, or external intervention—can substitute for this inner transformation. It is the final and definitive step beyond cyclic existence.
