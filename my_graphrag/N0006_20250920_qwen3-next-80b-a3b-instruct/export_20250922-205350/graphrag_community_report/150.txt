**Title: Chijie, Bufangyi, and Jingjin as Foundations for Mental Clarity and Wisdom**

**Summary:**  
Ethical discipline (Chijie) establishes the necessary conditions for mental stability by curbing harmful actions, speech, and intentions. This clarity allows non-negligence (Bufangyi)—mindful presence in daily routines—to take root and act as a protective barrier against mental afflictions. Diligent effort (Jingjin) manifests through this same consistent mindfulness. Together, these practices create a structured, undisturbed mental environment essential for achieving deep concentration and wisdom, with ethical discipline serving as the indispensable first layer.

**Chijie Enables Mental Stability Through Ethical Restraint**  
By refraining from harmful bodily actions, speech, and wrongful intentions, Chijie eliminates external and internal disturbances that agitate the mind. This restraint does not function as mere rule adherence but as a systemic clearing of obstacles, allowing the mind to settle into a state where concentration can arise and endure without disruption.

**Bufangyi Sustains Presence Through Mindful Routine**  
Non-negligence operates in the ordinary moments of life—walking, breathing, eating—by maintaining continuous awareness without fixation on the past or craving for the future. It functions as a vigilant guard, preventing the mind from slipping into afflictions, and relies entirely on the clarity established by Chijie to remain effective.

**Jingjin Expressed as Uninterrupted Mindfulness**  
Diligent effort is not an isolated act of willpower but is demonstrated through the sustained, unbroken practice of non-negligence. It is the energetic continuity of mindful presence in daily life, rooted in the boundaries set by ethical discipline and aimed at preventing mental decline.

**Chijie Is the Indispensable Base for Wisdom**  
Without the grounding provided by ethical discipline, the mind remains turbulent, reactive, and incapable of deep focus. Wisdom cannot emerge from a state of inner chaos; Chijie must precede and support all higher mental development, creating the stable soil in which wisdom can grow.
