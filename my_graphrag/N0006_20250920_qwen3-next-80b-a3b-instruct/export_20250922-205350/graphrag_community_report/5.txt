**Title: Chizhe, Wuzixing, and Chanding in the Path of Liberation**

**Summary**  
The path to liberation hinges on the interdependent roles of meditative concentration (Chanding), the wisdom of non-attachment (Chizhe), and the understanding of emptiness (Wuzixing). Chanding provides the mental clarity necessary for Chizhe to arise, which directly perceives the empty, dependently originated nature of the five aggregates. This perception dissolves clinging to self and phenomena, leading to a state where neither attainment nor avoidance is sought—reality is seen as it is, without distortion.

**Chanding Enables the Emergence of Chizhe**  
Meditative concentration establishes the calm and focused mind required to observe phenomena directly. Without this stability, the deeperseeing of impermanence, non-self, and suffering cannot occur. Chanding is not an end in itself but the necessary foundation upon which Chizhe is built.

**Chizhe Arises from Direct Perception of Wuzixing**  
The wisdom of non-attachment is not conceptual but experiential, emerging when the mind sees through the illusion of inherent existence. This seeing is directed at the five aggregates—each one recognized as dependently originated, without fixed essence. Attachment dissolves not through effort to suppress, but through the clear recognition that nothing possesses independent reality.

**Wuzixing Is the Object of True Non-Attachment**  
Non-clinging is not a passive letting go but an active realization: phenomena are empty. The five aggregates, often mistaken for a self, are the primary objects through which this emptiness is revealed. When their dependent, illusory nature is seen, the grasping that fuels suffering naturally ceases.

**Buxiubuzheng Represents the Maturation of Chizhe**  
In its most refined expression, wisdom no longer strives for liberation or flees suffering. It rests in undistorted perception, neither grasping at meditative states nor resisting pain. This is the natural outcome of seeing all phenomena as empty—no longer needing to change or control experience, because reality is accepted as it arises.
