**Title: Chaoshi, Jiemonti, and the Path to Unshakable Liberation**

**Summary**  
True liberation arises through the threefold training of moral discipline, meditative concentration, and transcendent wisdom, with wisdom being its ultimate fruit. This wisdom, known as Chaoshi, emerges only after sustained moral grounding and mental stability. It is not merely intellectual understanding but a direct realization that all phenomena are impermanent, unsatisfactory, and without inherent self. This realization dissolves attachment—including to meditative states and liberation itself—leading to unshakable freedom. Continuous effort, driven by Jingjin, sustains progress through each stage, culminating in the embodiment of this wisdom by Saintly Beings.

**Moral Discipline Precedes Mental Stability**  
The foundation of the path begins with moral discipline, which creates the conditions for Chanding—meditative concentration—to develop. Without this ethical grounding, the mind cannot attain the clarity and stability required for deeper inquiry.

**Meditative Concentration Enables Profound Wisdom**  
Chanding, the second stage, provides the mental stillness necessary for Chaoshi to arise. It is not an end in itself but a vital preparatory condition, allowing the mind to perceive the true nature of phenomena without distortion.

**Wisdom Is the Culmination, Not the Starting Point**  
Chaoshi is the final outcome of the threefold training, appearing only when moral discipline and meditative concentration are fully developed. It is not acquired through study alone but through the integrated practice of the entire path.

**Wisdom Dissolves All Forms of Attachment**  
Chaoshi goes beyond ordinary understanding by recognizing the emptiness and impermanence of all things—including meditative achievements and the concept of liberation. This leads to complete non-clinging, turning even the goal of freedom into a non-object of attachment.

**Effort Sustains Progress Across All Stages**  
Jingjin, the driving force, operates at every level of the path. Without persistent energy, moral discipline falters, concentration weakens, and wisdom fails to mature, halting the movement toward full emancipation.

**Saintly Beings Manifest Unshakable Understanding**  
Those who have fully realized Chaoshi perceive all existence as empty, transient, and selfless. Their being reflects this understanding constantly, free from clinging, fear, or dualistic distinctions.
