**Title: Nie Pan Jing Ji and She Li: The Path to Mental Peace Through Non-Attachment**

**Summary:**  
Mental peace, known as Nie Pan Jing Ji, arises not through physical withdrawal or elimination of existence, but through the inner release of attachment—termed She Li. This state is reached by fully embracing the truths of impermanence and non-self, leading to the cessation of craving and mental disturbance. Liberation is defined as mental non-clinging, not external renunciation, and is likened to a mirror that reflects without holding.

**She Li is the internal mechanism of liberation**  
True freedom is cultivated through the voluntary relinquishment of attachment to all experiences—positive or negative—without requiring physical separation. This release is not an act of avoidance but a transformation of relationship, where one no longer retains emotional or psychological hold over people, objects, or circumstances.

**Nie Pan Jing Ji is the natural result of embodied truth**  
The state of mental peace is not a mystical void or annihilation, but the quiet ending of craving and inner turmoil. It emerges only when one fully internalizes and lives in alignment with impermanence and non-self, making it the realized expression of the third Mark—not a goal to be attained, but a condition that unfolds when clinging ceases.

**Liberation is characterized by reflective awareness**  
The quality of mental non-clinging is compared to a mirror that receives images without retention. This metaphor underscores that liberation is not the absence of experience, but the absence of ownership over it—allowing all phenomena to arise and pass without disturbance.
