**Title: Attachment to the Five Aggregates and the Path to Liberation**

**Summary:**  
Attachment to the five aggregates—form, feeling, perception, mental formations, and consciousness—is the root of suffering and the cycle of rebirth. This clinging arises from the delusion that these transient phenomena are permanent, satisfying, or part of a self. Even pleasant meditative experiences, sensory pleasures, and the notion of spiritual attainment become traps when clung to. Liberation arises not through pursuit of pleasure or states, but through wisdom that sees clearly their impermanent, unsatisfactory, and selfless nature. True freedom comes only when attachment to all phenomena, including the self, is dissolved.

---

**Clinging to the Five Aggregates Sustains the Illusion of Self**  
The five aggregates constitute what is mistaken for a permanent, independent self. Attachment to them—as if they belong to or define a lasting identity—reinforces the false sense of self, which in turn binds beings to ongoing suffering and cyclic existence. Recognizing these aggregates as impermanent and lacking inherent substance is essential to breaking this deception.

**Meditative States Are Not Escapes but Potential Traps**  
Experiences such as joy, clarity, or tranquility arising in meditation are conditioned and fleeting. When approached with desire or aversion, they become objects of attachment, reinforcing the same patterns of clinging that produce suffering. The teaching explicitly warns against holding onto even the most pleasant meditative states, as they remain part of conditioned reality and cannot lead to ultimate freedom.

**Delusion as the Foundation of Attachment**  
The belief that phenomena are permanent, satisfying, or self-owned is a fundamental misperception. This delusion causes beings to grasp at experiences, identities, and outcomes, mistaking them for lasting truths. Without this error, attachment would not arise; therefore, the correction of misunderstanding is the starting point for release.

**Wisdom Dissolves Attachment by Revealing Impermanence and Non-Self**  
Direct observation of the five aggregates as impermanent, unsatisfactory, and without inherent selfhood dismantles attachment. Wisdom does not suppress desire but removes its basis by showing that nothing can be truly possessed or relied upon. This understanding allows release from clinging—not through effort to control, but through seeing reality as it is.

**Attachment to Life, Not Death, Is the True Source of Suffering**  
Fear of physical death is secondary to the deeper, ongoing suffering caused by attachment to life itself—rooted in greed, delusion, and hatred. True liberation is not about avoiding death, but ending the psychological cycle of craving and aversion that repeats with every moment of clinging. This continuous grasping constitutes the “true death,” a mindset that persists beyond physical demise.

**Desire for Sensory Pleasures Is a Core Mechanism of Bondage**  
Craving for pleasant sensations—sight, sound, taste, touch, and mental objects—is identified as a primary force that traps beings in suffering. This form of attachment arises because pleasure is misunderstood as a source of lasting fulfillment, when in fact it is fleeting and conditioned. Letting go of this attachment is non-negotiable for freedom.
