Title: The Nature of Liberation and the Manifestation of Compassion

Summary:  
True liberation is characterized by the spontaneous expression of compassion, rooted not in emotion but in profound wisdom. Those who have attained liberation surpass even celestial beings in moral and awakeness, earning their reverent homage.

Compassion arises naturally from wisdom, not from emotional impulse, serving as a sign of complete awakening.  
Those who are truly liberated have transcended cyclic existence and eradicated all afflictions, elevating their virtue and awareness beyond that of divine beings, thereby commanding deep reverence from celestial entities.
