**Title: The Role of Sustained Effort in Spiritual Realization Among Saints**

**Summary**  
Sustained, unwavering effort is essential for maintaining moral purity and wisdom while transcending suffering. Those who have eradicated greed, hatred, and delusion through this effort are regarded as perfected beings, revered by both humans and celestial beings. Without continuous diligence, progress in meditation collapses, and distractions such as greed and ignorance prevail. Saints uphold their state not through temporary achievement but through persistent practice.

**Sustained Effort Prevents Regression into Distraction**  
Without constant diligence, meditation cannot be sustained, and the mind becomes vulnerable to greed, ignorance, and other distractions. This indicates that progress is not static; it requires ongoing action to prevent regression into states of attachment and delusion.

**Perfected Beings Are Defined by the Eradication of Core Afflictions**  
Those who have completely removed greed, hatred, and delusion are recognized as embodying the highest spiritual ideal. Their status is not based on ritual or appearance but on the thorough elimination of these three fundamental obstacles.

**Saints Maintain Their State Through Continuous Practice**  
Even after attaining profound wisdom, saints do not rely on past achievements. They actively preserve their pure conduct and inner steadiness through unceasing effort, remaining unaffected by shifting worldly conditions.

**Reverence for Saints Is Rooted in Observable Inner Transformation**  
The veneration bestowed upon saints by both humans and celestial beings arises from their embodied freedom from afflictions and their consistent manifestation of moral and mental purity, not from status, lineage, or doctrine.
