**Title: Saintly Qualities and the Path to Liberation: The Eradication of the Three Poisons and the Cultivation of Chanding**

**Summary:**  
A saintly being, known as Sheng Zhe, embodies the complete eradication of greed, hatred, and delusion—defining traits of enlightenment. This state arises from the integrated practice of ethical conduct, meditative stability (chanding), and wisdom, culminating in liberation from the cycle of rebirth. Such a being remains unshaken by sensory experiences, free from attachment to outcomes or spiritual attainments, and radiates inner peace that transcends even celestial realms. Liberation is achieved not through suppression, but through direct insight into impermanence, non-self, and the illusory nature of desire and aversion, all sustained by unwavering, continuous effort.

---

**Moral Conduct as the Foundation of Inner Purity**  
Saintly qualities emerge only when ethical discipline is fully cultivated. This foundational practice governs behavior and creates the conditions necessary for mental clarity and the later development of wisdom. Without moral purity, the mind remains agitated and incapable of sustaining the depth of awareness required to uproot defilements.

**Chanding as the Stabilizing Force for Insight**  
Chanding, cultivated through concentration (samadhi), stabilizes the mind and generates the clarity needed to perceive reality without distortion. It is not passive stillness but an active, vigilant, and non-lax effort that prevents regression into distraction or dullness. When established, chanding allows for the unobstructed observation of phenomena, enabling detachment from meditative states and even the concept of enlightenment itself.

**Wisdom as the Liberation of Perception**  
Wisdom arises through chanding and reveals the true nature of all phenomena: impermanent, unsatisfactory, and without inherent self. This direct insight dismantles the illusion of permanence and selfhood—delusion’s core—as well as the craving and aversion that follow from it. It is not intellectual understanding but experiential realization that extinguishes the root causes of suffering and ends the cycle of rebirth.

**The Three Poisons as the Root of Suffering and Rebirth**  
Greed, hatred, and delusion are interdependent afflictions that bind beings to cyclic existence. Greed arises from clinging to transient experiences as sources of lasting satisfaction; hatred emerges as rejection of what is perceived as unpleasant; delusion—the fundamental misunderstanding of reality as permanent and self-existing—is the root from which both arise. Their complete eradication is the singular criterion of liberation.

**Non-Attachment as the Manifestation of Freedom**  
Saintly beings display non-attachment not through denial but through deep seeing. They no longer identify with or cling to sensory pleasures, spiritual achievements, identities, or outcomes. This freedom is not an absence of experience but a state of non-reactivity—remaining undisturbed even by the most compelling internal or external stimuli, including divine manifestations.

**Continuous Effort as the Sustaining Condition**  
Spiritual progress is impossible without disciplined, unrelenting exertion. Laziness, mental dullness, and complacency lead to regression into samsara. Only through sustained mindfulness and vigilance can chanding be maintained, wisdom deepened, and the habitual patterns of craving and ignorance broken. There is no static attainment—liberation is preserved only through ongoing presence.

**The Interdependence of the Three Studies**  
Ethical discipline, meditative stability, and wisdom function as an inseparable triad. Morality prevents harm and calms the mind; concentration steadies it for clear seeing; wisdom dissolves ignorance. None can achieve liberation alone. Their integrated practice is the complete pathway, and only when all three are perfected do saintly qualities fully manifest.

**Delusion as the Architect of Cyclic Existence**  
The mistaken belief in a permanent self and enduring phenomena creates attachment and aversion, which fuel karma and rebirth. Delusion alone is sufficient to sustain the cycle, as it underlies every unwholesome mental state. Its eradication through direct insight into non-self and impermanence is not a step toward liberation—it is liberation itself.

**The Transcendence of Dualities**  
An enlightened being abides beyond life and death, pleasure and pain, self and other. This is not a state of emotional numbness but the full realization that all dualities are mental fabrications arising from delusion. Freedom is found in the absence of resistance or craving—remaining unmoved by any condition, internal or external.

**The Holy One as a Refuge Beyond Worlds**  
Those who have extinguished the three poisons and transcended the reactivity of the senses become a source of unwavering peace and reverence—even for celestial beings. Their presence is not charismatic or magical, but a quiet radiance born of complete inner finality: no more clinging, no more aversion, no more confusion. They are the living expression of nirvana—not as a place, but as the absence of all that binds.
