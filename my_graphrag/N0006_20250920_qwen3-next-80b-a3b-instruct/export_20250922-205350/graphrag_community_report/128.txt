**Title: Saints and the Nature of Liberation Through Non-Attaching Wisdom**

**Summary**  
Saints are fully realized individuals who have eradicated greed, hatred, and delusion by cultivating pure moral conduct and direct insight. Their state is defined not by attainment or goal, but by the complete release of all conceptual attachments — including self, Dharma, and enlightenment itself. This release is not passive but an active manifestation of wisdom that arises from seeing the true nature of phenomena. Liberation is the natural consequence of this non-clinging, resulting in unshakable peace beyond conditioned existence.

**Complete Release of Conceptual Attachments Defines Liberation**  
Liberation is not an endpoint to be reached but a state of total non-abiding — the abandonment of all conceptual frameworks, even the idea of being a practitioner or attaining enlightenment. Saints exemplify this by no longer holding onto any notion of self, goal, or spiritual achievement. True freedom emerges only when even the most refined spiritual concepts are let go.

**Wisdom Is the Mechanism of Release**  
The ability to relinquish all conceptual attachments stems from wisdom — a deep, direct understanding of phenomena as they are. This wisdom does not build or accumulate; it dismantles clinging by revealing the emptiness of all constructs, including the desire for enlightenment. Without this discernment, release remains incomplete.

**Saints Embody the Total Eradication of Defilements**  
Saints are not merely morally upright but have extinguished greed, hatred, and delusion at their root through insight. This eradication is not forced or temporary but stable and irreversible, allowing them to dwell in unshakable peace regardless of internal or external conditions. Their existence reflects the natural outcome of this inner purification.

**Mastery of Chanding Sustains Unmoved Stability**  
As part of their practice, saints have perfected chanding — a state of stillness that remains unshaken by disturbance. This mastery is not an act of suppression but a natural expression of their non-attached wisdom, enabling them to exist peacefully without reaction or resistance to the flow of experience.
