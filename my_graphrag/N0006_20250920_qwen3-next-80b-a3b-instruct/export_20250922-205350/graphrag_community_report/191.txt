**Title: 清净戒行与圣者解脱之路**

**Summary:**  
清净戒行是圣者内在德行的核心基础，为心志安定、禅定深入与智慧生起的必要前提。缺乏此基础，即便行为端正或专注力强，仍无法突破轮回束缚，智慧亦停留于理论层面。清净戒行与心理杂染互为对治关系，其存续是稳定禅修、实现真正解脱的唯一可靠支持。

**The Foundation of Stable Meditation**  
Meditative concentration collapses without the stabilizing anchor of moral discipline, as illustrated by the metaphor of a candle in the wind. Mental distractions and afflictions easily extinguish focus when ethical conduct is absent, rendering meditation fragile and unsustainable.

**Ethical Grounding as the Condition for Liberation**  
Virtuous conduct and meditative concentration, when detached from liberating wisdom, produce only temporary, conditioned blessings that remain entangled in samsara. True release requires more than disciplined action—it demands a foundation that enables wisdom to transcend attachment.

**Moral Discipline as the Antidote to Mental Defilements**  
Clean ethical practice functions as the direct countermeasure against mental impurities. It is not merely a restraint on behavior but an active force that purifies consciousness, clears obstacles to inner stillness, and enables the arising of unshakable peace.

**The Core Trait of the Awakened**  
The fully realized being is defined by the possession of pure moral conduct. This is not an optional practice, but the essential and distinguishing characteristic that supports their freedom from anxiety, their abiding in stillness, and their capacity to cultivate lasting wisdom.
